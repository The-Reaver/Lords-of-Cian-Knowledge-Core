# Canon inventory: social venues, roles, record forms, and gaps (for the account craft standard)

Read-only survey of canon-ledger.json (2,693 rules) and all 1,508 Chronicle and interstitial files,
2026-10-04. Feeds the account craft standard (how each VB-067 account type is told, and where).

## Bottom line

The canon has very rich institutional vocabulary (the Maw, the SBD, Ashkeel, the Weregildd, the
cults) and almost no named social venues. Taverns, inns, and bars appear only as generic nouns.
There is exactly one named mainline drinking place, the Anchor and Anvil, and it has no ledger rule.
The homage World has no named bars, no sport, and no Policy-street vocabulary.

## 1. Eating and drinking places

**Mainline Cian.** Generic words in use: tavern, inn, galley, mess. No in-world category word.

- **The Anchor and Anvil**: manuscript Chronicles III, VI, VIII; Kanja Chronicle I header; no rule.
  The early Rebellion's gathering place, with a back room, a kitchen door, a long table, oil lamps,
  and tea. Recruits "followed the rubbings to the Anchor and Anvil," and Ezio first walked in there
  at 15. Never stated outright to be a tavern.
- **Tavern doors as notice boards**: Chronicle VI. Scrip-Forge charcoal rubbings were posted on
  "tavern doors and market stalls."
- **Unnamed harbor tavern**: MCD-825. Kanja, anonymous at 108, hears three retellings of his own
  legend.
- **Neutral dockside tavern** (the-merchant-who-changed-his-trade) and **dockside inn** (Kanja
  meets an envoy).
- **The inn at Greyfen**: MCD-1886. Draconis "heard the story at the inn," and a tavern four days'
  ride away retells it.
- **Tavern basements**: MAW-142, the low end of the Pits.
- **Ship, guard, and house spaces**: galley, forecastle, guardhouse mess, mess hall.
- **Noble meals**: MCD-1746, 1768, 1805, 1822.
- **Ashkeel**: unnamed "halls" (ASH-011).

**Homage World.** Generic words in use: cantina, bar, barbershop, print shop, church hall, clinic
kitchen.

- **Xaragua**: the room above the dockside cantina (MCD-337), and Arturo's house with its annual
  remembrance and three empty chairs (MCD-1024).
- **Aztlán**: an unnamed bar (PH2-030).
- **Areíto**: the barbershop back room (MCD-339) and Kwame Ade's print shop.
- **Borikén**: the church hall.
- **Jibaro**: the seminary kitchen and clinic.
- **Hekalu**: the Common Table (PH2-055).
- **Sankofa**: dice games.

## 8. "The Lifestyle" / LS

Neither "Lifestyle" nor "LS" exists in canon. The closest material is Ashkeel (ASH-001 to ASH-057):

- **Framework**: a neutral sanctuary built on consent and contract law, with registered dynamic
  bonds (ASH-021).
- **Social structure**: tiers (ASH-031) and collar ranks (ASH-034).
- **Four chartered clubs, each with a house rule** (ASH-045):
  - the Black Mirror (silent service)
  - the Gilded Knot
  - the House of the Velvet Vise (masks)
  - the Thermal Baths of Forgetting (no weapons or commerce)
- **Places**: the Grand Promenade / Obsidian Spine, the Ember Galleries, the Basalt Strand, and the
  Velvet Labyrinths (ASH-032, 043).
- **Corpus status**: Ashkeel never appears in a Chronicle file.
- **Flags**:
  - ASH-025 to ASH-030 are explicit adult material, so the child-safety hard stop applies.
    Ashkeel's age floor is 30 (ASH-016, ASH-036).
  - ASH-007 and ASH-048 describe electrical turbines, which sits uneasily with the pre-industrial
    tech level.

## 10. Gaps, and words a new name must not collide with

**Mainline Cian has no name for:**
- a category word for a tavern
- any named tavern besides the Anchor and Anvil
- a dock hiring hall
- the crew's word for its mess
- a fighters' ready room or waiting cell
- a Reckoner's booth, or a betting slip
- crowd slang for fighters or bouts
- the Trust's public-notice form
- the SBD witness-statement format
- wake or funeral customs
- a named gambling house

**The homage World has no name for:**
- any bar, cantina, barbershop, club, or after-hours spot
- the five individual Families
- a Policy bank, runner, slip, or number
- lookouts, stoop crews, or corner crews
- sport or games (no boxing, stickball, or dominoes register)
- church-basement or union-hall gatherings
- wake or homegoing customs

**Ashkeel has no name for:** its everyday drinking halls.

**Words already taken or overloaded:**
- **Ledger**: Black, Dark, Cinnabar, Central, the Ledger Record, the Ledger Office, the Unchained
  Ledger, Hask's ledger.
- **Table**: the Quiet Table, the Common Table, No Blood at My Table.
- **Hollow**: the Hollowed, Keldane Hollow, the Hollow Shogunate, the Hollow Stand, Renn Hollow,
  Ostrey Hollow.
- **Crucible**: the Crucible Market, the Dravos Crucible, two Ashkeel Crucibles.
- **Exchange**: the Offcut, Undercroft, and Compliance Exchanges.
- **Compact**: the Shapers', Slab, and Writedown Compacts.
- **Quiet**: the Quiet Rooms, the Quiet Hand, the Quiet Table.
- **Gallery**: the Handler's Gallery, the Mirror Gallery, the Ember Galleries.
- **Others**: Hall (Storm Hall), Mirror, Knot, Vise, Rest, Slab, Pit, Circuit, Cut/Offcut,
  Standard, Register, Stone, Anchor and Anvil.

**Hard bans and conventions:**
- **Banned words** (VB-013, VB-050): arena (as a venue), gladiator, bookmaker, Colosseum,
  god/deity, magic.
  - Flag: already-locked rules CC-021, CC-085, CC-090, MCD-234, WGD-001, WGD-006, and WGD-008
    still use "arena" or "gladiator."
- **"Cian" reuse**: rename any non-crew use.
- **Homage World** (PH2-034, PH2-049):
  - Names: invented only, from Taíno, Yoruba, Akan, Kikongo, Nahuatl, or Swahili roots; no real
    place or institution names.
  - Tech: no firearms, engines, or broadcast media.
  - Kanja stays unnamed.
  - Near-collisions to avoid: Guaní/Guanín, Kasa/Kasi, Tunji/Tunde.
