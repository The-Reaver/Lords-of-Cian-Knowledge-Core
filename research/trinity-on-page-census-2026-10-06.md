# Trinity On-Page Census, 2026-10-06

Read-only census of every entry file under `docs/lords-of-cian/chronicles/` (1,508 files) for Kanja's Trinity (Mafesto, Onyx of Oblivion, Obsidian Malice) and their named powers. Nothing was edited. This file is the only output. Classes follow Abad's brief: GATE-IN-USE, GATE-TRACK, OPEN-MENTION, AMBIGUOUS. A fifth bucket, "No Trinity reference", holds files the search terms hit by accident (the word "blade" meaning an ordinary blade). Those files need no gating on this ground.

Headline: of the 285 files that hit a search term in their narrative body, **175 are GATE-IN-USE** (3 of those are withdrawn Chronicles IX-XI, where Onyx narrates), **10 are GATE-TRACK** (the 7 Kanja-version entries, counting Chronicle II, which has no Trinity term in its body, plus manuscript Chronicles III, VI, VIII), **76 are OPEN-MENTION**, **5 are AMBIGUOUS**, and **20 are false positives**. Three files are ERA-ERROR candidates (section 3).

## 5. Method notes

- **Scope.** All 1,508 `.md` files in `docs/lords-of-cian/chronicles/`. Header and continuity notes were skipped by script: the narrative body is the text after the first `---` line and before a final `---` that opens an italic "Continuity notes"/"Notes" block. Files with no `---` separator had the title and first italic paragraph skipped.
- **Grep pass.** One script (kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repo) searched the body for: Mafesto, Onyx, Obsidian Malice, "Trinity", Kinetic Transfer, Cadence Ruin, Veil Piercer, Soulbound Edge, Whisper of Shadows, Black Ledger, Void-Lattice, Last Ward, Lunar Resonance, Heartline, Dark Ledger, "the blade", a line-start "ONYX:" and "CODA". 285 files hit in the body. A further 145 files mention a term only in their header or continuity notes and are not in this census. No file had Last Ward, Lunar Resonance or Heartline in its body. "CODA" matched nothing; "ONYX:" matched only manuscript Chronicle VIII.
- **Classification.** Every one of the 285 hits was read in context by me (sentence-level snippets, and the whole scene where the snippet was unclear). The scripted part picked the evidence quote only; the class was decided by hand. A quote is the deciding sentence, trimmed with ellipses.
- **The "blade" noise.** "The blade" is not an Onyx marker in Alias, Territory, Daba, Lauris, Ozmund or Sankofa entries, where it almost always means an ordinary blade. In the Kanja-version track and manuscript Chronicles it is the Onyx narration marker ("The blade records.") and is read that way. Twenty files hit only on ordinary "blade" and are listed as false positives.
- **IN-USE threshold.** A piece counts as in use if it is engaged on the page, even when it fails or is only partly used (Mafesto worn and reading a flood but useless; Onyx used as a plain edge; the Black Ledger invoked). A piece that is carried, racked, left behind, or explicitly not drawn is OPEN-MENTION with a note. One-clause uses ("the Trinity engaged", "Onyx ending it in under two minutes") count as in use. Counterfactual lines ("a discharge would have done nothing but spread the fire") do not.
- **Mixed entries.** A few entries show Trinity use only in a short passage (for example `two-sites-one-night`, `the-settlement-that-was-never-there`, `the-debt-the-sea-called-in`). They are GATE-IN-USE because the rule is per entry.
- **Rule IDs.** Found by scanning every ledger rule (statement, source, note fields) for the filename. All rows have one, except the three manuscript Chronicles (III, VI, VIII), which have no ledger rule referencing their file. The three withdrawn Chronicles map to MCD-331/332/333 (marked withdrawn in the ledger).
- **Era.** Taken from the header and the ledger statement only (ages, "Rebellion era", "Long Mask"). 85 of the 175 GATE-IN-USE files state no era at all; for them the alias default (Rebellion for every alias except the Scourge, Long Mask; Captain mixed) is shown and no era claim should be drawn from it. Of those, none shows Trinity gear beside post-Mafesto gear, so no further era error was detected by script; `the-call-he-got-wrong` is the one flagged for a human check.
- **Not covered.** Manuscript Chronicles I, II, IV, V, VII are not in this repo folder. Entries in other folders (`docs/lords-of-cian/interstitials/`, `drafts/`, character-profile files) were not scanned.
- **Counts check.** 175 + 10 + 76 + 5 + 20 = 286 rows: the 285 body hits plus Kanja-version Chronicle II.
