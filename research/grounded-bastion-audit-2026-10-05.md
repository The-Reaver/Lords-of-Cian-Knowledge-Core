# Grounded Bastion audit: places Kanja reinforced, and what canon already says

Superseded 2026-10-05 by Batch 378 draft revision 3: Kanja is 313 at the Fulfillment Ceremony, on the last day of that age, and the Pi-Awakening falls on the sixth day after his 314th birthday, not on the birthday.

Read-only research, 2026-10-05, prompted by Abad: "all of these things will tie in together when they
revisit places that they were before and it's now so much more fortified... we should be highlighting
how those places are really messed up and we don't know what those places are yet." Nothing here is
canon. Sources: `canon-ledger.json` (v37.9), the 1,508 entry files, `master-to-do-list.md`,
`approval-list-2026-10-03.md`. Age figures assume Book 1 at Kanja ~314 (see the Batch 378 draft:
the Ceremony falls on the last day of his 313th year, the Pi-Awakening on his 314th birthday).

## 0. Three findings that change the question

1. **The Bastion started before the Long Mask.** `MCD-142`: running "three hundred years" by Book 1.
   `MCD-208`: about two centuries by Kanja ~214. Start is about Kanja age 14. All Rebellion sites
   (18-30) are in scope; the Daba mentorship grounds may be. No rule says when Kanja first wore the
   Talisman; he wears it at the Black Trench, age 19 (manuscript Chronicle III, lines 149, 347).
2. **The mechanic Abad describes is not in the ledger.** The CONFLICT-001 resolution exists only in
   `master-to-do-list.md` (line 13) and the Drive `MASTER CANON DECISIONS.docx`: overflow "grounds
   through contact into whatever's physically nearest (floor, foundation, terrain) and accumulates there
   as reinforcement, unnoticed until people return"; the Gilded Lighthouse foundation "cracked rather
   than shattered" when Kanja fell (fall staging, `master-to-do-list.md` line 89). Locked `MCD-061`
   keeps only "cause and effect within it."
3. **Locked canon says the reinforcement can't be seen.** `MCD-142`: "None of this is detectable to the
   unaided eye." `MCD-144`: undetectable without tools in Book 1, macro-visible from Book 4. "So much
   more fortified" needs a ruling against both.

## 1. What the locked rules say

- **Structure.** `MCD-060`: Stage 2, recipient the Planet. Sub1 Heavy Hand ("density transfer through
  contact"); sub2 Foundry Anvil ("radiance channeled into subterranean strata"); sub3 the Gravity-Fetter
  pendant and Architectural Reinforcement/Lattice-Coupling. `MCD-061`, `WC-014`, `MCD-292` confirm.
  `MCD-147`: power shared across all three stages at once, so Stage 2 keeps running after age 200.
- **Mechanism (`MCD-142`).** Fully autonomous for three hundred years, without Kanja's knowledge; he
  believes it is "merely a synchronization server with a density suppressor." Purpose: stop his
  radiance detonating outward. Tiers:

  | Tier | What it does | Area |
  |---|---|---|
  | Foundry Anvil | compensatory tuning pulses into the crust | ~2,000 km of any high-density combat event |
  | Heavy Hand | micro-reinforcement of surfaces and structures in direct contact with the Avatars | any surface any of the 19 Avatars touches (`MCD-140`) |
  | Architectural Reinforcement | gradually converts existing constructed structures (bridges, harbor walls, defensive walls) toward Living Drakma resonance | builds over time; no location stated |

- **Tie to presence.** `MCD-208`: "around Kanja's operational presence." `MCD-147`: deposits
  "increasingly reinforced around Kanja's operational presence." `MCD-143`: "crust, bedrock, harbor
  walls, and atmosphere." `MCD-278`: also answers Titan strain (~1,000 years).
- **Strength (`MCD-146`).** Detection ~0.04 s. Pre-Stress Reinforcement ~200 m radius, compensating
  30-60% by local Living Drakma density. Distributive Absorption: a 2 m crater spreads as strain over
  ~15 m. Re-tuning 4-96 hours. `MCD-145`: failure ceiling ~42,000x sustained >0.4 s, "never crossed
  on-page (Avatars strike each other, not the planet)"; doubles at the Broken Meridian.
- **Start.** `MCD-142` (300 years), `MCD-208` (~200 by ~214), `MCD-256` (first transition Kanja himself
  perceives at 100), `MCD-252` (age 70, it stabilized Stormbreaker on contact).
- **Persistence.** Not stated outright; implied by "gradually converts... over time" and "self-repairing"
  (`MCD-142`) and by `MCD-147`: "the harbor floor at Karkosa still holds T.D.K.'s landing because three
  centuries of pre-loading kept its local capacity above threshold."
- **Who notices.** Not the unaided eye (`MCD-142`). The Blueprint Eye (amber signatures), Orlok's
  Density Sight, Legbara's Death-Impression Reading. `MCD-148`/`MCD-207`: Lauris feels paving stones
  stiffen in Kesmara, ~240 locations by her fifth decade. `MCD-208`: "double magnitude in the
  Karkosa's home harbor" (~Kanja 214); only Lauris and Sephtis hold the finding, Ezio knows it exists,
  Kanja does not. `MCD-144`: Book 1 tools only; Book 2 Blueprint Eye and Karesian body-sense; Book 3
  Density Sight; Book 4 ordinary sight; Book 5 visibly at capacity. `MCD-216`: first spoken on the
  page in Book 3; Kanja learns in Book 4.
- **Resource limits (`MCD-147`).** Diffuse trace, not ore; heaviest under Jicome (Mao), the Voskharen
  Wetlands, the Broken Meridian, certain coastal seams; thinnest under the Trust interior; 99.9%
  utilized. Book 5's 48-hour shortfall: Karkosa's harbor floor holds; elsewhere a Voskharen-coast island
  partly submerges, a Trust coastal cliff with a fishing village collapses, two aging bridges fail,
  ~400 dead. `MCD-1889`: the Tide Line opens under reduced coastal hardening. `MCD-1895`: no Living
  Drakma in the homage World.
- **Not Stage 2.** `MCD-291` Bone-Tempering routes force into the ground. The Shimmer is Stage 3
  (`MCD-269`, `MCD-272` 800 m at 313, `MCD-277` visible to 1,200 m at 310).
