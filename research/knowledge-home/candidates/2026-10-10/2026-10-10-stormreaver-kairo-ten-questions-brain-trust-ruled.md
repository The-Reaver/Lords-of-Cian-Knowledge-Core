## Rulings

1. **Coup year.** 5-0: left unset. Locked bound: in his childhood, after his birth 476 years before
   Kanja's, and before the Parley of the Raptor's Nest (`MCD-259`) (bound adopted 3-2; Oluwole and Bink
   gave the birth bound only, not opposing).
2. **The betrayer.** 5-0: unnamed; identity, method and the placement of the betrayer's death are held
   for the Book 3 outline. Guardrails: a separate person from Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (Abad's
   "carried out by another"); no entry set before Book 1 names the betrayer or touches the death; the
   death does not fall inside `MCD-1913`'s Kairo events or at the `MCD-218` fight (Bink, Sentinel,
   Celestina; drafter's recommendation); when named, the betrayer gets a new, collision-checked name and
   no existing named character takes the role without a full gate pass (Oluwole, Sentinel). Dissent on
   timing: Celestina would place the death inside Book 3; not carried (1-4).
3. **Flight or after.** 5-0: left open. Guardrail: Stormreaver escaped as a child; no other member of his
   family is alive at Book 1 or after, and none reappears alive in any entry set after their death.
4. **Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam's killer; the Sin-Eaters' fate.** 5-0: the hand is held for the Book 3
   outline. Guardrails: they die by the end of Book 3 and after the Sin-Eaters interrupt the Kanja and
   Ozmund fight (`MCD-218`) (3-2: Oluwole, Bink, Celestina); `MCD-218` and `MCD-100` stand unchanged. The
   Sin-Eaters number seven in total and fight in Book 3 (`MCD-218`, `MCD-322`); their fate is held; no
   rule implies a count accumulating over the program's 1,089 years. The Shogunate's turn to the alliance
   needs an on-page cause, set at the Book 3 outline. Not carried: "not by Kanja's hand" (Oluwole,
   Celestina, 2-3) and "not by Stormreaver's hand" and "no new Sin-Eater after their deaths" (Celestina,
   1-4).
5. **Their ages.** 5-0: unset. 4-1: in `POL-109` "the same power" is the faction as an institution;
   whether the two who rose at the coup led it when the program began is not set. Bink's condition (any
   later age must fit their leading the faction at the program's start, if they did) is compatible and
   recorded.
6. **His biology's maturing.** 5-0: specifics come from the blueprint mining batch. Frame now (3-2:
   Oluwole, Celestina, Sentinel; the other two not opposing): a maturing of his existing Aero Variant
   biology (`CC-062`) and kit limits (`ARS-410`), no new biology category, the word "matures" and never
   the Shogunate's augmentation terms (`CC-105`, `POL-106`), no density figure set, and any later figure
   within the per-book register (`MCD-144`).
7. **Book 5 front.** 3-2 for the Line: the Shogunate's forces join the Line as an allied contingent under
   Red Beard's independent command (`MCD-221`); Stormreaver himself stays on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`),
   and the contingent's field commander is unnamed. Sentinel (open, but Line-only if named) and Jasiah
   (open, Line the natural default) dissent on timing only; no seat supports any other front.
8. **Long Mask reveal.** 5-0: placement held for the Book 1 outline. Guardrails: no entry set before
   Book 1 shows it; it falls no earlier than the pendant's severing at the Pi-Awakening (`MCD-1901`)
   (3-2: Oluwole, Bink, Jasiah; not opposed).
9. **Mining batch.** 5-0: yes, `16_Avatar_Two_Tier_Placements` joins `Avatar_Environmental_Blueprint_v2`
   in one batch, run through the full gate. Conditions: neither document is canon; the draft's known-issue
   lists are its pre-flight checklist; the duplicate copy of the second document is diffed first; its
   "Domain Ceiling" Book 2 to 5 frame is reconciled with `MCD-144` and `MCD-1914` before any use.
10. **Crew with powers.** 5-0 in substance: the nineteen Avatars of `MCD-140`, Lauris, and any other
    member of the Lords of Cian crew to whom a locked rule gives variant or other non-baseline biology
    (Damu `CC-100`, Cooper `CC-103`, Valeria Korth `CC-104`, for example). Excluded: standard-biology
    members (Matar, `CC-067`), the founding dock crew, and Onyx (`MCD-140`).
