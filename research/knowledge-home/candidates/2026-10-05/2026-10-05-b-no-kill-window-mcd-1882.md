## Body
MCD-1882 locks the Long Mask's no-kill window. The author's words: "lock in the age 30 and 48 no killing." Batch 356 pinned it ("approved"): ages 31 through 47 are kill-free by his own hand, and his first kill after the Pier is the Pirate Dawn's opening act at age 48, in the weeks before the Night of Black Sails. The nine killed at the Pier (age 30) are the last before the window.

Onyx's Dark Ledger (ARS-437) records no kill jolt across the span. The era's operations (MCD-247 to MCD-249: Unarmed Siege of Maw-3, Crucible Market raid, False Dragon's Wake) stay bloodless by his hand, carried by forged pressure, Standing Order 44-B, and the legend-is-the-weapon doctrine. The window governs Kanja's own hand only; crew and Avatars sit outside it. It binds every track (Alias Chronicles, Kanja-version Chronicles, character Series, Annals); any existing entry showing him killing in that span is reconciled to non-lethal, crew-performed, or re-dated action. A related audit found zero locked violations. Vellacourt's Rule (Kanja Chronicle VI, age 48) was drafted as the first kill after the Pier.
