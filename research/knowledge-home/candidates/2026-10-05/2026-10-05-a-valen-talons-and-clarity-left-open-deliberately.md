## Body
Of the seven Arsenal contradictions, the seventh was resolved by not resolving it. The four Talons of Valen Sinisterblade stay unnamed so that a future Chronicle can earn their names through use, and "the Clarity" stays available prose color rather than a locked third epithet until a scene dramatizes it. This matches the project's general practice for undetailed items (Haku's fate, the Drowning Vault's 120): leave a named gap rather than invent a name with no scene behind it. Any later drafter should not casually use "the Clarity" as a fixed epithet or name a Talon without a dramatizing scene.
