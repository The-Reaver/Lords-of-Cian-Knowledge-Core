## Body
VB-062 (Batch 352) states that any event in Kanja's life may be told from any angle (neutral third-person, crew, enemy, witness, his father) and such accounts may coexist and differ in emphasis without contradicting locked fact; and every significant event always also has an Onyx of Oblivion account. The Kanja-version track is the home of the Onyx accounts; the Alias Chronicles and other-angle tellings are the non-Onyx accounts; a marquee event should normally get both.

Because Onyx is sealed at L9 for the whole Long Mask (MCD-246), its Long Mask accounts are retrospective: told after the Karkosa Heist reunites it with Kanja in Book 1, openly acknowledging it was not present and reconstructing the years from what it learns once his hand closes on the grip. The author's approval: "go with retrospective, keep the two years, lock it." (the "two years" is Maro's Accords negotiation, locked at "the better part of two years" in Chronicle IV). Batch 357 amended the narration person from first person to "the blade" (see VB-063); Batch 375 qualified it with VB-067's account types.
