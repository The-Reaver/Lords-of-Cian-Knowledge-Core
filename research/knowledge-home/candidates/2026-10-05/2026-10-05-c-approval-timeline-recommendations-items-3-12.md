## Body
Recommendations (all unapproved) from Part 2 of the approval list. Item 3, Kares Prime collapse: the old ~7,200-year onset is impossible against Lauris's 6,000 years and MCD-1552's 75,000-year response; recommended the long scale, onset about 82,000 years before Book 1, with the agent entering 25,000-35,000 years ago after the diaspora left (keeps MCD-151); scope five rules and four files. Item 4, Fermand's joining: MCD-268 has him escaping at Kanja 270 but MCD-194 puts him at Lauris's recruitment near Kanja 114; recommended moving his escape to about Kanja 112, with a Ghost-Lattice courier replacing Ghostwind, who is not recruited until Kanja 130. Item 5, Haryn Dael at 4,200 cannot have founded a 6,000-year-old caldera settlement; recommended about 6,300 years (MCD-316). Item 6, Ezio's true age: CC-028 says 75 but MCD-373, manuscript Chronicle VIII, MCD-194 and MCD-1661 imply more; recommended about 309, five years younger than Kanja (the Batch 378 draft revises this to about 308). Item 8, Matar's recruitment 'four hundred years ago' predates Kanja's birth; recommended about 290 years ago, during the Rebellion, brought in by Sephtis. Item 9, Orlok: seat granted about 2,400 years ago, resigned about 1,000 years ago, left the Zenith about 80 years before Book 2. Item 10: Red Beard met Ozmund before the Ceremony, because MAW-121's secret training and Testament I's outer-command night both require it. Item 11: House Brekka founded about 60 years ago (not 120), Lady Aravel's House about 50 (not 250), Essek Nightfall about 950 years ago. Item 12: the Maw's Era I becomes the Old Dominion's surviving administration after the Deposition, keeping every date and era name.
