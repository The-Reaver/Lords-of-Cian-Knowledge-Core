## Body
A Drive pass surfaced an "Institution Codex expansion" planning prompt that referred to "Archon Meridian (A.M.)" as if the SBD's Executive Director and Archon Un Ra (T.D.K.'s hidden son, `MCD-013`/`122`/`130`) were one person. The author ruled it a shorthand slip, not a reveal: "archon Meridian stays as is." The Director instead received a real, separate name, picked from three candidates (Wyck Talmadge, Casimir Wrey, Ilona Corrance): `SBD-046` locks Ilona Corrance. The initialism "A.M." is locked as standing institutional habit predating her tenure, not concealment, so every existing "A.M." cross-reference (`SBD-030`, `MCD-1727`, `CULT-053` through `057`) stays valid with no rewrite, the same convention that lets "T.D.K." survive alongside Anu Un Ra's real name. The author's approval: "Ilona Corrance ... approving all recommendations." Lesson: when a planning prompt's shorthand appears to equate two entities, check it against locked canon and ask, rather than letting it propagate as a reveal.
