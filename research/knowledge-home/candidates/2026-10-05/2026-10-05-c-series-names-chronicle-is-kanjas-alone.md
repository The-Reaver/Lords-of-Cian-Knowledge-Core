## Body
VB-066 (Batch 375). The author asked that 'Chronicle' be Kanja's alone. Series names: Lauris = the Records, Daba = the Rolls, Ozmund = the Testaments, Ezio = the Exhibits, Anirak = the Collections, the territories = the Annals. Kanja keeps 'Chronicle' for the Alias entries, manuscript Chronicles I-VIII and the Kanja-version track. The launch gate is now named the Series Launch Protocol. Anirak's series was first called 'Tallies' and was renamed 'Collections' after a collision with Kanja's tally method and Daba's own 'Tally' (MCD-1610). The author first approved the draft as presented ("lock it. once you're done we will move on to pyro and the Triad"), it was then revised through seventeen independent reviews, re-presented in full, and approved again ("lock it."). Non-Kanja protagonists propose a series name at the Game Plan step. Ledger afterwards: ledger_version 37.7, 2,695 rules, 375 batches.
