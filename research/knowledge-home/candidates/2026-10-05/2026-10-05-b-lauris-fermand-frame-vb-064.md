## Body
VB-064: Fermand Aurelias's narration of Lauris Letitia's Records keeps the established first-person transcriber frame, each entry opening on a short archive fragment in Lauris's spare voice (MCD-211, MCD-1558) then Fermand as transcriber and witness, in CC-034's Baroque/Zafon-Noir register. For this series it supersedes VB-024's Narrator 4 sheet where they conflict: the third-person-limited instruction and the rule that only Ezio receives Fermand's warmth (his regard for Lauris may show, never as panic or broken composure). The Voice Bible's hard constraints bind every entry, old and new: no balanced antithesis, no contractions in narration, no banned words, the Density Spike never named, violence rendered as physics in an autopsy register, powers shown through sensation. Entries that break a hard constraint or contradict locked canon are corrected; the frame and register are not changed. A flagged follow-up: Lauris's corpus carries roughly 315 antithesis constructions.
