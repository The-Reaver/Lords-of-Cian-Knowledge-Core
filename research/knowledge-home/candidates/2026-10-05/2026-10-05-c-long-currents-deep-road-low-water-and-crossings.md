## Body
Under One World, Many Shores (Batch 369), the only crossings are: the Long Currents, which run one way with hard-to-read return loops, with Kares Prime sitting where several currents converge and a current running from the homage World to Cian, the Book 1 route of PH2-048; Kanja's Mar sense makes him one of the few who can cross both ways. The Deep Road is a crushing-pressure abyssal route used by the Vael Kem and T.D.K.'s sea forces. The Low Water is a land bridge that surfaces once a generation and is held in reserve. This is how the Great Migration theme of the homage era is expressed literally as migration between worlds without any space travel, and how Lauris's diaspora arrival and travel between worlds (MCD-149, MCD-151, MCD-313) stay consistent.
