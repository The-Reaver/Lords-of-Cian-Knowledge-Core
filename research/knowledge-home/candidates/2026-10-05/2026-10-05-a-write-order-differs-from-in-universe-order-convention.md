## Body
A recurring project convention: a Chronicle may be drafted after others yet set earlier. Xaragua Chronicle II (`MCD-337`) precedes Xaragua Chronicle I chronologically; Xaragua III is the earliest of all by decades. In Batch 313, Kanja Chronicle II ("The Lesson He Carried Alone", `MCD-1867`) was written second but set before Chronicle I and before Onyx's age-17 bonding (`ARS-020`), so it has zero Onyx presence because no blade exists yet in Kanja's life. It dramatizes the Daba/Kanja mentorship (`MCD-1568`/`1570`) from Kanja's side, using a distinct training night rather than restaging Daba's own Chronicles. A footbridge exercise plants the undramatized root of the Dredge-Line Ambush's terrain-as-weapon logic. The convention has a cost: Batch 338 flagged that write order and in-universe age disagree about how much Onyx should show, once the remaining 27 or more of the Twenty-Two Victories fill in out of age order. That "age-band convention" for Onyx's presence is an open question for the author.
