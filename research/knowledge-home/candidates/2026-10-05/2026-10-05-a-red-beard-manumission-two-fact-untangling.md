## Body
The Arsenal source claimed Red Beard was "granted manumission". Primary-source checking found two separate facts that had been conflated: (1) he did cross the Directorate's genuine 3:1 financial manumission threshold, already locked at `MAW-079`, at roughly the time of the Maw-3 championship bout; (2) the Cestari Cleaver was a different thing, a right-of-victory prize. He never formally activated or claimed his eligibility and stayed inside the system until his real, exercised freedom came through the Book 1 Unchained Legion defection (`CC-023`). Locked at `ARS-424` with zero invented reversal beat. The case shows why adjudication judges that verify against locked text outperform those reasoning from precedent: the strongest result came from two judges who opened `MAW-079` rather than trusting the summary of the contradiction.
