## Body
This item is a draft and is pending the author's approval; nothing in it is locked canon. Flagged reading 1 of the Batch 378 draft (docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-05-pact-and-long-mask-timeline.md). If the Pier had fallen on Kanja's 30th birthday the Long Mask would be exactly 284 years; the author's 'just over 283 years' therefore requires the Pier to fall late in age 30. The draft proposes 30 days before his 31st birthday, which gives a Long Mask of 283 years and 30 days (103,325 days) and Accords that held 283 years and 29 days (103,324 days), on VB-063's 365-day year. At the treaty's hour these are 8,927,280,000 and 8,927,193,600 seconds of Onyx's count. 'Just over 283 years' holds for any Pier date late in age 30; the exact 30 days only makes every count exact, and another number of days would change MCD-1901's day counts, the three Onyx seconds-counts and which entries fall after an anniversary. It also sits inside the no-kill window of MCD-1882. A span of N whole years from the Pier is complete on the Pier's anniversary, 30 days before Kanja's birthday at age 30 + N, and an entry stating such a span is placed after that anniversary.
