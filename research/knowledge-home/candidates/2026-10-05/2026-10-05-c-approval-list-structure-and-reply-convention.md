## Body
docs/lords-of-cian/approval-list-2026-10-03.md lists the items that need a new fact or a ruling between two locked facts. Each item gives the problem, a recommendation, one alternative, and the scope. The author can lock the whole list with one reply such as "approve all recommendations" or "approve all except N, and for N do X" (convention stated on the list itself). Parts: 1 Foundations (items 1-2, ruled first because other items depend on them), 2 Timeline (3-12), 3 Institutions and structure (13-19), 4 Atlas (20-26), 5 Names (27-30), 6 Character placement (31-34), 7 Technology level and the manuscript (35-37), plus two items added later: 38 (Sephtis's staged death against his open place in the crew, added 2026-10-04) and 39 (Fermand narrating K-Theta material against MCD-193). Item 1 has since been taken up by the Batch 378 draft, which is itself unlocked. Character-gate questions (whether the Triad were made at the Gate or are an ancient clade, what Pyro knows about his father) are deliberately not on the list because they are answered inside each character's Psychological Profile. Everything on the list is a recommendation, not canon.
