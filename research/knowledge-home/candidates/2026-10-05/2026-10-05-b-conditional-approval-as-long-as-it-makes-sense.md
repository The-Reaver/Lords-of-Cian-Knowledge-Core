## Body
The approval phrasing recorded in the Batch 360-361 notes is: "as long as it makes sense I approve" (Batch 360, Ozmund CXXI and Daba LIX and the Lauris gate material), followed in Batch 361 by "anything that needs correction is mandated to be corrected everything has to make sense. above all things everything has to connect to everything else logically the connective tissue must be Flawless that is the number one priority above all things."

Reading for future sessions: an approval phrased this way is not a blanket waiver of review. It authorizes lock only if the connective-tissue gate is clean, and it pre-authorizes corrections to anything found wrong, in already-locked material as well as new drafts. The approval quote must still be recorded verbatim in the batch note, per the first non-negotiable rule.
