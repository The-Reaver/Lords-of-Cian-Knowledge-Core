## Body
Roster and opponent for the Tide Line front (Batch 368). The unit: Anirak's Chain Harbor core, now named Edda, Hamund and Odile, then seven of the pirate crew (Torian, Stormbreaker, Azar, Voidbreaker, Ghostwind, Stormreaver and Zora) and Ren at her side (MCD-1890). Her opponent is Sereth Vaul, 'the Silencer', set against her Siren's Voice; the outcome is deliberately reserved and not locked. The Chain Harbor core naming was later reused by CC-163 (Batch 371), which states the unit is Anirak leading Edda, Hamund and Odile, and that Ren is not a member of it.
