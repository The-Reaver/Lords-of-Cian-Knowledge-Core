## Body
Approval-list item 13, the single largest structural question surfaced by the institutional review. Eighteen CULT rules frame T.D.K. as returned and immediately contained at the Great Breach, which conflicts with the locked Book 2-5 arc in which he is still acting. Recommended (unapproved): a new rule, CULT-201, the 'Breach-era containment'. The Pi-Awakening wakes T.D.K. and at the same moment disrupts his Exchange Protocol, as Haku's did. His own life-support architecture holds his person only; he still acts through the Champions, proxies, the Crown-Scar and the Warbody. That makes the cults' 'post-containment' present the Book 2-4 world, and Book 5's containment stays the final one, achieved by the heroes. The proposal states that every piece is already locked. Scope: one new rule plus five cross-reference amendments. Alternative: re-date the cults' present to after Book 5, which rewrites about 14 rules and still breaks CULT-191.
