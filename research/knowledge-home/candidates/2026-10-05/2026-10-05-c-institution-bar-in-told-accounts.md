## Body
Part of VB-068 (Batch 376). No new entry or scene states a fact about an institution that no locked rule holds. Reliable narration of a locked entry, cited by rule ID, counts as held. This keeps told accounts from smuggling new institutional canon past the draft-then-approve-then-lock rule, since a character's confident telling is otherwise an easy place to introduce an unlocked fact. It pairs with the teller bound of VB-067: what a teller may say is limited both by what that teller could know and by what the ledger already holds about the institution.
