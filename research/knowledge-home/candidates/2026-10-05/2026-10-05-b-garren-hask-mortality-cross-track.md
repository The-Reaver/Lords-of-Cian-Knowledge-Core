## Body
The Phase 5 running tally calls this the single most load-bearing unresolved cross-track contradiction: Garren Hask (and by extension Efa Gol and Pell Ostra) is locked dying of old age around Kanja age 50-55 on the Captain Alias Chronicle track but shown alive and active through Kanja age 300-314 on the Scourge track and in several Lauris Strand W entries. It surfaced independently in the Lauris review and in two MCD-core chunks (451-900, 901-1350), and the tally notes it needs one ruling (which track controls, or whether the name does double duty for two people) rather than three separate fixes. The 2026-10-03 approval list traces it to a missing foundation: no rule states how long ordinary humans live. Item 2 recommends about 300-400 years, moving Hask's death to late in Kanja's 313th year (MCD-1422, MCD-1423, MCD-1486 move; about 16 statements and 12 entries touched). The author has not ruled.
