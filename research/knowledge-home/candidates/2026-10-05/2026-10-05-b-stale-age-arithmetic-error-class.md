# Stale age arithmetic was the most frequent error class in the character-track reviews, tracing back to figures that later rulings had outgrown

- id: 2026-10-05-b-stale-age-arithmetic-error-class
- type: finding
- status: candidate
- class: confirmed
- source: Canon session 2026-09-20 to 2026-10-05 (raw archive research/knowledge-home/raw/2026-10-05-canon-session-batches-303-377.jsonl), Batches 335-338
- confidence: high -- the same class recurs in the Ozmund, Lauris, Kanja, and Ezio batch records
- verified: 2026-10-05
- tags: lords-of-cian, review, error-class, ages, timeline

## Body
Across Batches 332-338 the same pattern repeated: an early figure (often from the Character Codex era) persisted in prose after a later locked ruling changed the arithmetic. Examples: Ozmund's "roughly N years before the Ceremony" framing (impossible against CC-015/CC-090's age ~201 at the Ceremony); about thirty Lauris entries still using pre-correction age arithmetic after MCD-1533 set her age at 6,000; Fermand's tenure written as "thirty years" instead of the locked two hundred (MCD-194/MCD-271); Kanja Chronicle III's header mis-dating the Gale Straits by two years and giving Sephtis an age roughly 200 years too old; Ezio's "75" at CC-028 outgrown by later tracks. The remedy applied: reword to age-framing tied to the locked source, and queue any needed number for the author. Check ages, spans, and counts against the latest locked correction, not the original.
