## Body
Three items from the hard-constraints audit were deliberately not corrected: (1) the manuscript's own uses of "spike" in Chronicle III; (2) whether PH2-049's "pre-industrial" constraint (set for the separate homage World) also binds mainline Cian, since the manuscript itself has artillery, Crawlers, and cruisers; (3) MCD-1535's photographs (the Twin Anomaly photographs), against the sketches ruling applied to three other places per MCD-258. Each needs a new decision on tech-level scope or on exempting the manuscript from a Voice Bible ban, so each belongs on the author's approval list rather than a direct edit.
