## Body
During Ozmund's Game Plan the author caught that the first three candidate Chronicle I pitches drifted into Book-1-era material: the Accession Games exist only because the throne is vacant after Aethelgard's murder. He set a standing constraint that the launch wave stays strictly pre-Book-1, meaning before the Fulfillment Ceremony (`MCD-025`), until he explicitly reopens that window. The two Book-1-era pitches were deferred, and the deferral is flagged directly inside the profile file's Game Plan section; the file itself is the "fire and remind me" mechanism the author asked for. Three fresh strictly-pre-ceremony pitches were drafted instead, and the author picked the Draconis one ("love all of them. we will start with B"). The constraint governed all later Ozmund waves (Batches 304-306): no entry may foreshadow the Ceremony. Two further pre-Book-1 pitches remained queued after Chronicle I ("What Never Had to Be Learned", Aethelgard/discipline; "Her Son, Not Her Line", Val Mirel Kareth). A parallel boundary applies to Ezio (the whole Book 1 investigation and Book 2's Crown-Scar discovery, `MCD-279`, are reserved).
