## Body
The author's standing directive, given with the Batch 361 lock and quoted exactly: "anything that needs correction is mandated to be corrected everything has to make sense. above all things everything has to connect to everything else logically the connective tissue must be Flawless that is the number one priority above all things." Operating split: every contradiction gets corrected; a mechanical correction that adds no new fact is applied directly; a resolution that needs a new fact is drafted, presented in full, and locked only on the author's approval in his own words. The split is why the 2026-10-03 approval list exists: the four reconciliation batches (364-367) applied everything that only realigned text with a locked fact, and left 37 items (later 39) that each needed a new fact or a choice between two locked facts that no precedent settled. The directive also made the Connective-Tissue Gate the third non-negotiable rule at the top of CLAUDE.md, with scripts/connective_tissue_check.py as its mechanical half.
