## Body
`MCD-092` named each reckoning in one line (Silence by Red Beard, Ghost by Anansi/Valeria, Blade by Valen). Batch 310 read `Beloved_and_Blade_Ronin_Victims.docx` and locked mechanisms. The Silence (Decimus Korr, `CC-144`): Praetorian-expulsion backstory authorized by Dhampir Black (`POL-102`) and a demonstrative-kill methodology (staging bodies into positions of failure) that explains why his trophy cord (`CC-125`) does not distinguish soldiers from civilians. The Ghost (`CC-145`): no remembered name, about 200 identities, density kept low to evade Density Sight; Anansi's Ghost-Lattice and Valeria Korth's Thread-Perception (`CC-104`) find him by detecting the absence of causal threads where a person should be, and he is captured, not killed, the one outcome an identity built on formlessness cannot survive. The Blade (`CC-146`, Serai Noth): a Celestial Zenith origin (the tradition that produced Orlok, `POL-105`), expelled for premeditated murder using a technique exploiting a 0.04-second guard-transition window; Valen's Precision Variant biology (`CC-035`) processes combat geometry faster than her cultivated 0.8-second ceiling, so he is already inside her window. `MCD-1853` locks the SBD wet-work team's tradecraft at the Fulfillment Ceremony (a planted false-conspiracy narrative misdirecting Ezio's Book 1 investigation, a memory-suppressing compound in the ventilation) and the Ghost's Book 3 infiltration (communication trail sanitized through Lawless Reaches relay points that Ezio later finds clean).
