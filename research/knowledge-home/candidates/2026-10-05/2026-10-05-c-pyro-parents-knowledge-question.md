Cost noted by the profile: anyone who knew of the marriage would know the paternity, so the marriage was known only to those who know (Kanja, Sephtis per CC-110, whichever of Stormbreaker and Azar knows), or CC-079's 'suspect' needs a clarifying clause; two outside records still name her Kanja's wife (CC-046, SBD-041). Option 2: he knows neither parent and was raised as the ship's foundling. Option 3: he knows she was the Captain's wife and has been given a different father, which turns CC-079's 'hides' into an active lie. That she lives, fused into the Gate (MCD-131), stays reserved under every option. Proposed profile reading: his core wound is the night he was born, his defining throughline 'the meal before the fight', and no pre-Book-1 entry stages the Last Breakfast (MCD-277).
