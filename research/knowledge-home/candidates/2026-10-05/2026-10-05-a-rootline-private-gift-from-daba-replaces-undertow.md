## Body
Rannic Sorvell's defeat by the Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea (`MCD-1858`) originally used Undertow (`ARS-388`), a Book-2-era Moonvault gift, though Sorvell's era is the Long Mask's Pirate Dawn, decades earlier. The author's direction: "something Daba gave him... should fit in with the long mask's pirate Dawn." `ARS-436` locks the Rootline: an iron-and-woven-rope grappling rig forged with the Rexmar smithing principles Kanja taught Daba (`MCD-1568`). It has no power of its own; the grounding comes from Kanja's Mar-bloodline tide-reading senses (`MCD-295`) reading the exact moment to deploy it, not a Living-Drakma current mechanism. It is never publicized and never folds 1804 into the crew's credit, consistent with `MCD-1569`. Six candidate names were collision-checked first. `MCD-1858` was amended accordingly. The same pattern (use Kanja's own bloodline sense instead of a later artifact) was used in Batch 348 (headed Batch 347 in CLAUDE.md) for `MCD-951`; the Phase 5 running tally flagged a broader sweep for other Moonvault-gift anachronisms on the Sovereign Ghost track, and Batch 366 cleared the stale claims from Sovereign Ghost rules.
