## Body
MCD-1881 is a standing writer's-guide rule for pre-Book-1 material. Marquee is a named, fully dramatized kill or defeat that becomes legend and may be referenced in Book 1, reserved to the anchor heroes. Notable is a real dramatized victory that does not define how the world speaks of the hero, for every other hero. Ledger is background tallies. Working target: roughly 15-20 marquee kills across the anchors, each with a named victim and a full scene, never a body count.

Per-anchor constraints: Daba/1804's marquee kills are fully dramatized in his own series but publicly unattributed (MCD-1569), legend only inside 1804's record, and network-executed except for at most one personal kill. Ozmund's stay strictly pre-Fulfillment-Ceremony, at most one, unwitnessed by Draconis, so his Book 3 escalation is not spent early. Kanja's Long Mask marquee kills use post-Mafesto gear only, Onyx sealed. The first two under the rule were Harek Vondel (MCD-1878, Daba) and the Sovereign Pier (MCD-1880, Kanja). The author's direction behind it: Kanja should carry devastating kills across the Rebellion and even more across the Long Mask, cultivated alongside 1804, Ozmund, and Lauris, with lesser victories spread among everyone else.
