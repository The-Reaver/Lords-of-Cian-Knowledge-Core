This item is a draft and is pending the author's approval; nothing in it is locked canon. Proposed MCD-1903 (category talisman-of-mao) in the Batch 378 draft (docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-05-pact-and-long-mask-timeline.md), from the author's ruling of 2026-10-05. Through the late Long Mask, Kanja reads the Governor's Shackle's buildup (MCD-060 Stage 3, CC-007, WC-014) as age: visibly slowed by 205 (MCD-260), no longer dodging, turning fast or running by 240 (MCD-262), unable to stand unaided on bad days by 300 (MCD-271), voluntary movement at about 15% of peak at the Countdown Annotation at 310 (MCD-277). He has Valen fitted for the Forge-Coat as the era's next legend-bearer at 312 and by the end believes he is dying (the Gilded Lighthouse staging the author confirmed on 2026-08-13). He knows the Talisman only as a synchronization server with a density suppressor (MCD-142, MCD-208), and his crew shares his reading. The belief is a misconception: the decline is the Shackle's hold building toward the Pi-Awakening, and his own Rexmar biology spends its span slowly (MCD-309). On Day 0 the pendant is severed and the Shackle released; the Pi-Awakening shows him what he felt was the Shackle's weight. Pre-Book-1 entries show the decline and the reading of it, none confirms he is aging out, and none states the truth. Flag 14: MCD-260's '114-year countdown' and MCD-277's Countdown Annotation are read as the record's figures, not knowledge Kanja holds.
