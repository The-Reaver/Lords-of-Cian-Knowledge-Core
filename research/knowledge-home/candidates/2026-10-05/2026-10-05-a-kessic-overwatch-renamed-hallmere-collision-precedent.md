## Body
`MCD-432` locks the Kessic Overwatch garrison falling only to a full-Trinity assault (gate broken, formation shattered by Onyx), while `MCD-1102` described the same name surrendering cleanly on amnesty terms six weeks earlier ("honest capitulation, no tricks"). They cannot be the same event. Resolution: rename the second, contradictory installation to a distinct garrison, Hallmere, "the same resolution pattern already used elsewhere in the project for this exact class of collision". Later sweeps reused it: Kessara became Varrow in a Scourge Chronicle, then Callow (Batch 331) after a one-letter collision with the antagonist Lord Varro Dominael; Ferrenline became Orencliff; Kessic-region misuse in two Bane Chronicles (`MCD-681`/`683`) became "the Brinemoor salt flats". Rule of thumb: rename the later or less-load-bearing usage, and run a near-collision check (including one-letter differences with locked names) on the replacement.
