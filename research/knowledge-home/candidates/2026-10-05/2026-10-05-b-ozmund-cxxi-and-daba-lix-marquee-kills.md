## Body
Batch 360 (MCD-1886, MCD-1887) locked Ozmund Chronicle CXXI, "The Night the Dike Held": his single pre-Ceremony kill, Marek Draye (a Branded deserter extorting the fen hamlet of Lowmere), killed with one open hand while levering a dike's sluice chain toward a breach, unwitnessed by Draconis, legend "the night the dike held itself". It was the first Ozmund entry written to the Voice Bible's Red Beard sheet. It also locked Daba Chronicle LIX, "The Count at the Bottom of the Stair": Captain Edran Brannick killed at a stair-foot as he moves a lit match to a charge under twenty-two people; the Rookery link surfaces only afterward from Brannick's own service book ("Rookery. Correction. Stairs first. Complete."); unattributed per MCD-1569. Both honor MCD-1881. Approval: "as long as it makes sense I approve." A later approval-list item (27) recommends renaming "Marek Draye" because of near-collisions with Harek, Maren, and Maret.
