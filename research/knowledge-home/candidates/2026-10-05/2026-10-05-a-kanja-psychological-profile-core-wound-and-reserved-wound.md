## Body
The Rules Walkthrough for Kanja (`docs/lords-of-cian/character-profiles/kanja-haku-rexmar.md`) was the first profile file for a protagonist who had only existed as shared psychology behind the 11 alias masks. The proposed profile: core wound the debased Scrip-note itself (an eighteen-year-old with every reason to claim his birthright instead treating the system's lie as a problem to solve); defenses (arithmetic as armor, deferring his own naming to others); values (no killing as doctrine, amended in Batch 352 to a costed default rather than an absolute when `CC-161` locked; evidence over violence; people over material); holds contradiction as the most calculating person in the room and the source of the most mythic public image; trust as demonstrated competence; breaks when the ratio has no clean answer because both sides are people; throughline "the function, not the name". The author confirmed the core wound, then added: "his father's death is the most devastating blow, but it awakens the urge to destroy his enemies". That is recorded as a second, larger, explicitly reserved wound outside this track's window, walled off from anything drafted here and flagged for a future Book-1-era profile. He confirmed the rest with "the rest lands, keep going." The throughline later gained a deliberate counterpart in Daba's "the name, not the number".
