## Body
Of the eleven Batch-312 villain defeats, ten stay with their already-tagged Alias or Character tracks. Renfel Auberon's (`MCD-1865`) is the one genuine crossover: a Kanja-crew team-up (Onyx, Sephtis and Ironbane fielding `CULT-197`'s three-source Anti-Resonance countermeasure together) rather than a single alias's solo act, and because Onyx participates directly it must sit before age 30, inside the Kanja-version window. Kanja Chronicle III ("What the Dark Could Not Keep", age 27) dramatizes it: six captured Ever-Haunt entities collapse or disperse, and Auberon is captured alive already partly Green-Mark-contaminated. Before drafting it was checked that Auberon's stock is wild, independent Ever-Haunt population predating T.D.K. (`WC-019`'s "originless" framing), not escapees from the Great Breach, which occurs in Book 1's epilogue decades outside the window. Later review (Batch 336, headed Batch 338 in CLAUDE.md) removed a "post-Breach anomalies" custodial reference from `MCD-1865` for the same reason, and left open the question of naming the "older, quieter apparatus" Auberon is handed to. The author picked all three offered Chronicle I candidates as the track's opening run, in order, under: "continuously, uninterrupted, until completion. this includes rigorous testing, committing, pushing to main origin."
