## Body
Batch 369 replaced space-based explanations with planetary ones. Kares Prime's 4.7x gravity comes from the planet's densest mass lying under its continent. Its 'binary star' is a parhelion, a doubled sun formed by mineral crystal in its dense air. Living Drakma comes from one impact at Mao, focused antipodally; it lies under three continents and is absent from the homage World (the same absence MCD-1895 records). WC-013, MCD-147 and MCD-315 were reconciled to this range in the same batch. This also fixes the Talisman of Mao's planet (MCD-142) as the shared one.
