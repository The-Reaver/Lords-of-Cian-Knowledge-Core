## Body
The author's words: "Singularity is way older than everybody else I would say that we need to give Singularity at least over 150,000 years." The Atlas's own roughly 4,800 years for Soledad Keme (First Seat) undersold her against the setting's age ceiling: Anu Un Ra / T.D.K. at 30,000+ years (`CC-053`) and Orlok at 76,003 years (`CC-058`). `MCD-1851` locks her as the oldest confirmed-aged being, over 150,000 years, which also explains why her account of Old Dominion events about 50,000 years before Book 1 (`MCD-326`) reads as firsthand. Her age does not conflict with the Astral Archipelago's roughly 5,000-year founding (`POL-104`): she predates the nation and became its First Seat at or after founding. Later reconciliations updated `CC-058`'s age ranking (Batch 341, headed Batch 340 in CLAUDE.md) and changed `MCD-1851` to "Merak-era" wording (Batch 367), so any rule ranking the oldest beings must be checked against this ruling.
