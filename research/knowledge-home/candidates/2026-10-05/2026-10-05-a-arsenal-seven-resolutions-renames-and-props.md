## Body
Resolutions locked in Batch 308: (1) Dead Drakma Small Arms (`ARS-426`): reframed, not rejected; `PH2-049` already pre-authorized this future addition; real SBD-issue sidearms, culturally coded as institutional distrust or cowardice, mechanically incapable of harming any density-scaled combatant at any tier (even baseline, per the floor "regardless of sophistication"), effective only against unrated civilians and property. (2) "Cadence Ruin" became "the Void Wake" (`ARS-395`), resolving a triple collision among Onyx's blade power, Varruk's disruption ability and Sereth Vaul's Green Mark aura by folding Sereth's version into the Ever-Haunt's "Void-" naming family; Onyx keeps Cadence Ruin, and Varruk's use was renamed Cadence Break later, in the Phase 5 review (ledger Batches 341-344, `CC-098`). (3) `MCD-302`'s thin "Old Dragon" Rexmar-lineage item was renamed "the Elder Wyrm" rather than touch Valen Sinisterblade's newly drafted twin Living Drakma sword, which keeps "Old Dragon" (`ARS-422`). (4) Fermand's Palimpsest: `MCD-268` stands, Kanja forged it; the source's outside-bladesmith claim is rejected, and the "rare act of personal investment" survives as Fermand personally asking Kanja for it and specifying its design (`ARS-415`). (5) Ezio's Archive-Key housing became his own concealed-carry prop, the Cipher Cane (`ARS-404`), kept distinct from Lauris's Attia's Rite (`ARS-359`). Rule of thumb applied: rename the thinner, less-used or later-conflicting item, and keep the item already carrying more locked weight.
