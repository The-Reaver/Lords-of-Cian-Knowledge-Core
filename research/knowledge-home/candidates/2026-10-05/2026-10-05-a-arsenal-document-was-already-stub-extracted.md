## Body
Batch 308 mined `Arsenal_of_Cian_Definitive_Edition_v2` (about 200,000 characters, 31 sections) with six parallel agents. They discovered the document had already been extracted once at name-only stub level (`ARS-010` through `ARS-340`, an older pre-batch-log pass with uppercase `LOCKED` status). The work was therefore drafting mechanics behind roughly 30 already-named but undetailed items, not inventing items. One section, Lauris Letitia's Section XXII, was fully redundant with the already-locked `ARS-357` through `374`, confirmed word-for-word in places, and was logged as confirmed-redundant with no draft, matching the Batch 44 precedent. The batch locked 35 rules (`ARS-392` through `ARS-426`) and amended `MCD-302` in place. Lesson: before mining a source document, grep the ledger for its item names; a prior stub pass changes the task from invention to elaboration and changes the collision profile.
