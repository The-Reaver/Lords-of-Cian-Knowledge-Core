## Body
A3: the Kares Prime collapse timeline (MCD-153, 156, 158, 162) is arithmetically impossible against Lauris's age of 6,000 (MCD-1533); the old "~7,200 years before present" onset needs replacing (approval-list item 3 recommends a long scale, onset about 82,000 years before Book 1, keeping MCD-151). A4: Lauris's and Fermand's joining dates contradict across MCD-267, MCD-175 to MCD-195/MCD-194, and MCD-268, giving three different implied timelines (item 4 recommends moving Fermand's escape to about Kanja 112 with a Ghost-Lattice courier instead of Ghostwind, who is not recruited until Kanja 130). A5: Haryn Dael's age (about 4,200) versus the Moonvault's own age (6,000+): he cannot have founded something older than himself (item 5 recommends about 6,300 years). None is locked.
