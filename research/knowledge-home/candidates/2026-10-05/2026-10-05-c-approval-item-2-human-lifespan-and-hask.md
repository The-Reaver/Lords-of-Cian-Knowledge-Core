## Body
Approval-list item 2. The Captain Alias track locks Garren Hask dying of old age around Kanja age 50-55, while the Scourge Alias track and several Lauris entries show him alive and active through Kanja 300-314. The cause is that no locked rule ever says how long ordinary humans live. Recommended (unapproved): ordinary humans live about 300-400 years, reaching adulthood on a normal schedule and declining slowly over centuries, which MCD-414, PH2-048 and Tomas Grieve at 190 (CC-124) already imply. Hask's death then moves to late in Kanja's age 313: MCD-1422 (death), MCD-1423 (memorial) and MCD-1486 (the Crow King echo) become the Captain track's last beats; MCD-1408 gives the ledger to its keeper; the old hand in MCD-958 becomes centuries old; the 'Hask is dead' references in MCD-1514 to 1522 come out; the ledger Hask keeps in MCD-1252 is clarified as his private Scourge-era one; and the Lauris dockside entries (LV-LIX, CII, CVIII, CIX) read as set between about Kanja 260 and 313. Scope: two new rules, about 16 rule statements, about 12 entries, three docs. Alternative: he dies the morning after the last coat, which needs no Scourge edits but lands in Book 1's own year. The Batch 378 draft restates the recommendation against its clock: Hask dies in the last month of age 313, after MCD-1408 and before the Ceremony. This was recorded as the single most load-bearing unresolved cross-track contradiction in the earlier review tally.
