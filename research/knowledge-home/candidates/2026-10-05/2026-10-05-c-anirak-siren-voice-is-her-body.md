## Body
ARS-438 (Batch 370) resolves the Anirak walkthrough's question of whether the Siren voice is body or forged gear: it is her body. The Siren's Voice is her own sub-audible, felt hum. The Siren Gorget carries it and shapes it into a 10 m cone. It reaches the deafened and does nothing to the undead. This is the fact later relied on when the Tide Line sets her Siren's Voice against Sereth Vaul, and when Ren, whose face the Siren never catches, joins her crew.
