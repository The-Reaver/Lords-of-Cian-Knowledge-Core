## Body
Item 7: CC-027, WC-016 and CC-111 close the list of people who know Ezio's classified combat capability. The recommendation is 'no': Fermand knows a cover exists but not the capability, with one clause added to CC-027 making that distinction and the two unlocked Ezio drafts' headers tidied. Also held under item 7 is locked Lauris Record IX (MCD-1626), where Fermand narrates that Ezio's true capability has not yet been forced into view; VB-067 holds it open for the author's ruling rather than correcting it directly. Item 39, found by the eighth review of VB-066 and VB-067: MCD-193 locks that only Sephtis and Ezio know of the K-Theta cave system ('Kanja and the wider crew do not know'), yet Lauris Record VIII (MCD-1625) and other Strand L entries such as MCD-1712 have Fermand narrating it firsthand ('she has told me', 'I was permitted to accompany her'). Options: (a) Fermand becomes a third knower and MCD-193 gains his name, (b) the K-Theta material is rewritten so Fermand narrates only what Lauris lets him see, (c) the entries stand and the gap stays unexplained. Recommended (unapproved): (a), because it matches how Fermand already transcribes her archive (VB-064) and changes one rule instead of several entries. Both items have the same shape, a conflict between a locked fact and what a teller could know, which is why they sit together.
