## Body
The gear draft's `ARS-429` (the Unraveling Thread, a Living Drakma filament-line from Anansi letting Valeria strike a perceived structural point at range) originally described itself as her "first personal weapon ever". That contradicted `CC-104`'s already-locked Weaver's Kit and Compass Needle, a dagger she has used 73 times in 210 years. The draft was corrected in the scratchpad before the merge: `ARS-429` now extends her existing kit with a ranged offensive option. The catch came from a final consolidated cross-agent check, which confirmed the three drafting agents' new names did not overlap and surfaced this contradiction. Lesson: superlatives like "first ever" and "only" in drafts are the most likely statements to contradict locked canon, so grep for the character's existing gear before accepting them.
