## Body
Recommendations (all unapproved) from Parts 4-6, complementing the Atlas and near-collision notes already filed. Item 22: MAW-078's 'Keldane to the Frontier in about 3 days' is about 1,400 miles, so about 3 weeks. Item 23: MCD-094's 75/15/10 matches the Atlas by land area, so a new GEO rule records that the shares measure land area and WC-012's '30% of the Shattered Kingdoms' landmass' becomes roughly 40%. Item 24: the Southern Seaboard (MCD-112) covers both coasts, Jicome's Southern District coast and the Trust Domain's coast, with its own Southern Maw network (new GEO rule). Item 26: keep both Ironhold (the Lawless Reaches' capital) and the Iron Hold (House Dravos's compound) with a one-line disambiguation. Item 29: the Kareth artifact keeps the name 'the Patient Stone'; the Maw curriculum becomes 'the Stonehand Primer' and the epithet 'the Stonehand's Heir'. Item 30: Vael Korr-Drennen is she/her (the corpus is split 3-2), which also separates her from the male Vael Threnarr-Karth. Item 31: three Lauris Strand W entries cannot be set before Book 1 (XLVIII and CIV have Ozmund speaking of the crown he refused; LII includes Orlok, who joins in Book 2), so they are read as off-page evenings during the Books and CIV drops the 'Verehimu' surname. Item 32: Ironbane joined during the Rebellion, by age 27, matching Kanja Chronicle III. Item 33: Sephtis's own private network (MCD-196), explicitly not the SBD, holds Renfel Auberon after capture (alternative: the SBD's Coldline detachment). Item 34: Orin's phonograph is replaced by a scribe's written transcription performed by hired players, which also fixes a pronoun slip.
