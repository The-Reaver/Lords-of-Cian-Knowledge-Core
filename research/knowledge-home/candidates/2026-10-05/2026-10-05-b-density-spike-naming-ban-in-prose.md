## Body
The Voice Bible's Red Beard sheet (and VB-064's hard constraints) forbid naming the Density Spike in prose; VB-063 extended the ban on the word "spike" to every prose passage (including Heartline signals, reworded "jolt"). At Batch 360 a flag was raised that 44 of Ozmund's 120 older Chronicles name the Spike in prose and run looser than Red Beard's sheet; Batch 363 corrected 22 places in his track to "the density in his blood" or "the density in him". Ozmund Chronicle CXXI (Batch 360) was the first Ozmund entry written to Red Beard's sheet from the start. The ban explains why prose uses the phrase "the density in him" for a power the rules call the Density Spike.
