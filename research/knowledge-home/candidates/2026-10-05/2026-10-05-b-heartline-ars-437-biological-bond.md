## Body
Prompted by the author raising that Onyx is biologically tethered to Kanja, Batch 353 locked ARS-437, named the Heartline to avoid a fifth "tether" in canon (alongside the Metabolic, Scrip-, Whalebone, and Crown-Scar root-access tethers). At bonding (age seventeen, ARS-020) the blade's Living Drakma lattice keyed itself to his blood, pulse, and nervous system. Across distance the channel runs one way: Onyx receives, Kanja does not. It carries body signals only (heartbeat, strain, wounds and illness, sleep, the jolt of a kill, density surges that Onyx records but never names) and never words, sights, places, or thoughts; full exchange happens only through the grip.

It does not break when Onyx is sealed at L9; it holds for the whole 284-year Long Mask. In the dark Onyx keeps the Dark Ledger, a section of the Black Ledger recording only what it felt, logged against its running seconds-count with no causes. At the Book 1 Karkosa Heist reunion, grip contact restores the full channel and Onyx reads 284 years out of his body, reconciling each Dark Ledger entry. Limits: it cannot locate or warn Kanja or act. Kanja does not know the bond survived until the reunion. The author's approval: "Both Heartline / Dark Ledger. keep all three, lock it." (The word "spike" was later replaced by "jolt" in the rule text under VB-063. The unlocked Batch 378 draft would amend both 284-year figures to 'just over 283 years' under the author's 2026-10-05 ruling; until it locks, ARS-437 reads 284.)
