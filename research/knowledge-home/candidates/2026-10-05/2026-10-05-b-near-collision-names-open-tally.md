## Body
The Phase 5 running tally lists near-collision names flagged but not renamed: Guani/Guanin, Kasa/Kasi, Tunji/Tunde, plus roughly a dozen further character-name near-collisions in the institutional blocks, the question of whether "Torvald" (a possible Old Norse name outside the project's naming palette) needs renaming in the Detroit Kazi track, and the cross-block "Patient Stone" naming collision. Item 27 of the approval list (docs/lords-of-cian/approval-list-2026-10-03.md) proposes thirteen mainline renames, each checked clean against canon, for example Marek Draye to Ulvar Corlay, Kaerith Vossen to Gessar Ostrin, Joren to Quenby, Serath to Navene, Hesk to Wyle. None is applied until the author rules.
