## Body
Recommendations (all unapproved) from Parts 4-7. Atlas: item 20 keeps the Verehimu Wetlands (House Verehimu's inland freshwater fens with Greyfen, Sennick, Lowmere, Aldenmoor) and the Voskharen Wetlands (coastal tidal marsh and trench in the Trust Domain) as two distinct places; item 21 renames the crew's ship, currently called 'the Karkosa', *The Balance* (fits Hask's run The Audit, The Receipt, The Ledger; 11 rules and 3 Lauris files); item 22 changes MAW-078's Keldane-to-Frontier 'about 3 days' to about 3 weeks; item 23 records that MCD-094's 75/15/10 shares measure land area; item 24 makes the Southern Seaboard both coasts; item 25 places Portside in Atlas cell C09 with Killane, plus the naval theaters, Kesmara and the Voskharen province; item 26 keeps Ironhold and the Iron Hold with a disambiguation line. Names: item 27 lists thirteen mainline renames (for example Marek Draye to Ulvar Corlay, Kaerith Vossen to Gessar Ostrin, Serath to Navene, Drennan to Tolland), item 28 homage-era renames (Tunde to Chike, Kasi to Haraka, Nzila to Kiala, Torvald to Ekow), item 29 renames the Maw curriculum 'the Stonehand Primer', item 30 sets Vael Korr-Drennen as she/her. Placement: items 31-34 (three Lauris Strand W entries read as off-page evenings during the Books, Ironbane's Rebellion-era joining by age 27, Sephtis's own network holding Renfel Auberon rather than the SBD, Orin's phonograph replaced by a scribe's transcription). Tech level: item 35 recommends that PH2-049's 'firmly pre-industrial' binds the homage world only, with mainline Cian keeping its locked baseline (siege and naval artillery, Crawlers, the Hymn-Engine) and nothing beyond it; item 36 keeps Lauris's photographs as Directorate imaging, the single sanctioned exception; item 37 changes 'heartbeat spiked' to 'heartbeat climbed' in manuscript Chronicle III and keeps 'cargo spike' as a named tool.
