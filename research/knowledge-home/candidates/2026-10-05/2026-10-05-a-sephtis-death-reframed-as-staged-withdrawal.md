## Body
Batch 325 amended `MCD-982` and four downstream rules to describe Sephtis's departure from the Storm That Walks school as a staged withdrawal rather than a real death, since he is alive and active elsewhere (Ezio's tutor, with Lauris, in Anirak's Collection era). Later batches carried the framing (Batch 348, headed Batch 347 in CLAUDE.md, amended `MCD-1054`/`1055`/`981`). It was applied as mechanical reconciliation, not a new fact. However, the Batch 372 review of Anirak's Game Plan found Sephtis openly with the crew in Ren's recruitment years (`MCD-195`, `MCD-206`), and the older clash between `MCD-982`'s staged death and his open presence became approval-list item 38 awaiting the author. Treat the staged-withdrawal wording as provisional and do not draft new scenes that depend on it either way.
