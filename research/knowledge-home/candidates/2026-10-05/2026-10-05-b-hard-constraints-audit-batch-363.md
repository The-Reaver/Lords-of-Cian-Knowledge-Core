## Body
The audit covered narrative prose only (headers and continuity notes skipped) across all 1,505 Chronicle files. Edits applied: 22 places in Ozmund's track that named the Density Spike (now "the density in his blood" or "the density in him"); 7 lowercase "spike" uses; 21 banned words (the Iron Bastard priest arc's "god" became "the one they prayed to"); one "arena"; one rule ID leaked into prose; era-impossible technology (engines on sailing ships, steam drive, sonar, a gun turret, three photographs turned to sketches per MCD-258, homage-world newspapers turned to broadsheets per PH2-049); and five self-referential uses of the word "Chronicle". Every edit was a realignment with locked fact or a banned-word removal, so it ran under the direct-correction mandate.
