# Agents sharing one placeholder batch number caused 29 rules to cite the wrong correction batch, and a 144-rule wave-wording drift needed a pattern fix

## Body
In Batch 347, 29 rules across five Alias Chronicle tracks carried a wrong "Corrected Batch 321" citation, because the parallel fix agents in Phase 1 had all used the shared "321" placeholder and the orchestrator renumbered the scripts but not every citation inside statements. They were renumbered per track: Trench Monarch to 323, Crow King to 324, Iron Bastard to 326, Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea to 327, Lord of Embers to 330. In Batch 346, a recurring "wave-wording" leak (writers'-room phrasing such as "wave N of ten" in rule statements) was corrected as a pattern across MCD-561 to MCD-890, 144 wording corrections, plus three Chronicle prose files, one of which used the alias name "Trench Monarch" before that alias existed. Lesson: when agents number their own work, citations embedded in text drift; renumbering must be a sweep, not just the script header.
