## Body
This item is a draft and is pending the author's approval; nothing in it is locked canon. The Batch 378 draft (docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-05-pact-and-long-mask-timeline.md) records the author's rulings of 2026-10-05, in his own words: "Kanja is 313 years old when his father dies." On the Pi-Awakening at 314 (CC-006, MCD-003, WC-005, MCD-260): "this is correct... I have established this." And, on the principle behind the batch: "Everything has to make sense... numbers more importantly than anything... we must always have our time correct." The proposed rule MCD-1901 puts the murders of Maro Rexmar and Aethelgard Verehimu at the Fulfillment Ceremony (MCD-025, MCD-091) on the last day of Kanja's age 313, the day before his 314th birthday. Day 0 of the Pi-Awakening is his 314th birthday, the day after the murder, early in Book 1's 10-Day Interregnum, and WC-022 is amended so the Interregnum counts from the murder. Consequences proposed: Pyro is 23 at Book 1 (CC-101 to '23', CC-110 from 24 to 23 years), Ezio about 308, and the Eve of Awakening (MCD-272) and the coat night (MCD-1022) move to age 313. 'The last day of Kanja's 313th year' is read as the last day of his age 313, because MCD-272 calls age 313 'the 314th Year'.
