## Body
The author's request was for memorable, formidable, defeatable pre-Book-1 enemies, since the power balance only starts turning in Book 2 and is not even by Book 5. Checking how Book 1 will be written (item 3 of his list) was answered by recording that Book 1's investigative-noir structure is locked (`MCD-070`: two kings dead, Ezio and Fermand investigate) and the Voice Bible's Zafonian Gothic Noir pillar (`VB-001`/`003`) governs atmosphere. That shaped the villain tiering: institutional or mid-tier threats, never mastermind-tier material that would compete with Book 1's own reveals. The eleven (`CC-147` through `CC-157`, `MCD-1855` through `MCD-1865`) span five registers: Directorate/Trust command (Harek Vondel, Orven Castellan, Halveth Ashcombe, Rannic Sorvell), the Maw circuit (Brakon Skevik), Weregildd and slaver economics (Ilsevet Sorrenta, Vex Thurlow, Ossa Drem, Kruger Sennit), institutional corruption (Callas Modrin), and an independent Ever-Haunt trafficker (Renfel Auberon). Each defeat is assigned to an existing track. Deliberately left alone: the PH2- track (homage-era antagonists stay unnamed by convention, read as load-bearing) and Archon Meridian (left ungeared pending his alignment as an unresolved third force, `MCD-099`). Chronicle prose for the defeats is a later wave, following the Kazi Tunji/Femi precedent of locking characters first.
