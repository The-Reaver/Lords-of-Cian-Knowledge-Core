## Body
The Atlas lists Master Void-Cusp (Second Seat, domain the dead, about 3,200 years old, Baron Samedi homage) and the Event Horizon (Fifth Seat, domain crossroads, no age, Elegua homage) as apparently distinct entries, while `MCD-095` already fuses both epithets into one person, Legbara Kalunga (3,181 years). Rather than choose a side, `MCD-1852` locks a compatible reading: he genuinely holds both seats simultaneously, explained by his rank as the Singularity's Champion, which the nine-seats-nine-holders structure does not otherwise accommodate. His two-part name is functional: "Legbara" (Legba/Elegua) names the crossroads seat and "Kalunga" (the Kikongo threshold between living and dead) names the death seat. The near-exact age match (about 3,200 vs 3,181) and the Event Horizon's missing age both corroborate one person. Batch 307 had deliberately left the question open per the author's "draft the rest now"; Batch 309 closed it after re-fetching the source passage in full rather than working from a summary. The same compatible-reading technique (accept both facts, add a mechanism that makes them coexist) recurs across the project.
