## Body
This item is a draft and is pending the author's approval; nothing in it is locked canon. Approval-list item 1 asked whether the Fulfillment Ceremony falls 284 or 296 years after the Sovereign Pier. MCD-091 and ARS-010 say 'roughly 296 years', but every rule that locks Kanja's age at the Pi-Awakening and the end of the Long Mask (age 314, CC-006, WC-022, MCD-260) and the 284-year Long Mask (CC-005, ARS-437 and others) implies 284; 296 would put the Ceremony after the Pi-Awakening, which WC-022 places inside Book 1. The earlier Batch 56 ruling that MCD-091's 296 controls rested on a mistaken belief that the 284 years were a different interval from the Long Mask. The unlocked draft docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-05-pact-and-long-mask-timeline.md (Batch 378, merge script merge_batch378_pact_long_mask_timeline.py, dry run clean) resolves it under the author's rulings of 2026-10-05, replacing both numbers with 'just over 283 years'.
