## Body
Batch 307 mined the `Shattered_Kingdoms_Political_Atlas`. Its "Voss Labyrinth, First Patriarch" collided with the already-locked current First Patriarch of the Obsidian Prefecture's Senate of Twelve, Severin Ebonrath (`POL-100`, Batch 301). The author ruled directly: "Voss Labyrinth is a predecessor." Locked at `POL-102`: Voss Labyrinth becomes real institutional history, a long predecessor tenure (Jupiter homage), rather than a competing claim on the current seat. `POL-102` also adds two non-conflicting Patriarchs, Dhampir Black (military commander, Fourth Patriarch) and Iron-Gore (Chief Engineer). Dhampir Black later authorizes the Silence's Praetorian expulsion in `CC-144`. The pattern is the project's standard for a source document's title colliding with locked canon: the locked fact stands and the source figure becomes history or a subordinate. Batch 307 also locked `MCD-1850` (the first Verehimu as a Root-Born Aethel-Gard general elevated by T.D.K., the mechanism behind `WC-012`'s one-line origin) and `CC-141` through `CC-143` (Orlok's Codex extension), with zero new proper-noun collisions.
