## Body
Six Arsenal agents surfaced seven contradictions or naming collisions and resolved none unilaterally. The author's direction was "let the fleet render a verdict": five independent background-agent judges, deliberately given no visibility into each other's reasoning, each read the full project-instructions history and rendered a verdict on all seven items against the project's precedents. Verdicts were consolidated by plurality, weighted toward judges who verified claims against primary locked-source text rather than reasoning only from precedent. Two judges independently pulled `MAW-079` and found it already locks Red Beard as one of fewer than 200 Cestari in 5,000 years to cross the genuine manumission threshold, which sharpened that item. The consolidated resolution was presented in full before drafting, and the author approved: "lock it." Vote strengths: small arms 5/5, Cadence Ruin rename 5/5, Old Dragon 4/5, Palimpsest 4/5, Cipher Cane prop 5/5 (cane form 4/5), Valen's Talons 4/5 and "the Clarity" 3/5 left open. The approach is reusable when several contradictions need rulings and the author prefers a verdict to a menu.
