## Body
While checking the Chronicle tracks tracker before drafting villain defeats, the author observed that Onyx is supposed to take over from the main Chronicles, which are much longer than the side Chronicles. Direct investigation (word counts plus close reading of the eight manuscript chapters fetched from the "My Rivals Distance" Drive folder) confirmed it: `VB-026`'s progressive handoff is genuinely implemented in the manuscript (an unlabeled coda of about 230 words by Chronicle I at age 18, a labeled "CODA: THE LEDGER OF ONYX" by Chronicle II, growing codas through III and VI, a labeled "ONYX:" section by Chronicle VIII at age 22) and is absent from all 990+ Alias Chronicles and the Territory and Character tracks, including entries set decades past age 30. A corpus-wide grep for `"ONYX:"` and "the blade rests/arrives/knows/holds/records" matched only the manuscript files. The author's resolution: the existing Alias, Territory and Character Chronicles are not fixed and stay as "the regular accounting"; a new additional track, "the Kanja version" (Kanja as protagonist, Onyx-narrated with the progressive handoff), is built forward. Scope: a single continuous sequence for the Rebellion only, ages 18-30, ending at the Trinity's surrender. The Rootline lock, Daba's profile and the Captain Trinity fix were pinned during this launch.
