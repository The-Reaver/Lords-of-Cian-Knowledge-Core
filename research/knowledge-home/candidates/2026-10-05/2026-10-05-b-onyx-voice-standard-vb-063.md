## Body
VB-063 (Batch 357) governs every Onyx-narrated or Onyx-voiced passage in any track, using the Voice Bible's Narrator 1 sheet and the Voice Progression Sheet (mirrored at docs/lords-of-cian/voice/). Four rulings where the documents and the manuscript differ: (1) Onyx calls itself "the blade" ("us" permitted), never "I"; the single exception is Kanja Chronicle IV's closing line at the sealing. (2) Articles follow the manuscript codas: kept, with the telegraphic effect carried by very short sentences, fragments, and refrains rather than article-dropping. (3) The word "spike" is banned from all prose, including the Density Spike and the Heartline's signals. (4) The seconds-count is exact, never hedged, on a 365-day year from the Sovereign Pier treaty.

The author's verbatim picks: "Self-reference: "The blade" (Recommended). Articles: Follow the manuscript (Recommended). "Spike": Ban it everywhere (Recommended). Count: Exact (Recommended)." Also carried from the documents: no equivocation, no balanced antithesis, no textbook explanation of powers or gear, no narrator emotional display (loyalty shows as precision); Onyx speaks in a scene only through the grip and only in utilitarian fragments.
