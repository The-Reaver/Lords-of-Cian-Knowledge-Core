## Body
The Phase 2 review of Ezio's track found three UNLOCKED/PENDING-APPROVAL drafts (`ezio-exhibit-ii` through `-iv.md`) drafted earlier and never presented to or approved by the author. They were corrected for internal consistency (dangling numeric spans reworded, an incorrect header claim about Lauris Chronicle I's content corrected, a `CC-108` overstatement softened) but not locked, because the draft-then-approval rule outranks any review pass. Review fixes also applied to locked Chronicle I (`MCD-1876`): a settlement-count contradiction (prose said three settlements and nine levies, later "eleven") reconciled to three throughout, and a dangling "seventy-five years of work" reworded non-numerically pending the age ruling. The profile doc's three Industrial Myth appearance counts (84/31/35) measure different things and got one clarifying sentence rather than a change. `ARS-404` was amended to remove a real-world-term leak ("the way a doctor reads an X-ray").
