This item is a draft and is pending the author's approval; nothing in it is locked canon. Proposed MCD-1902 (category book1-structure) in the Batch 378 draft (docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-05-pact-and-long-mask-timeline.md). The murders at the Fulfillment Ceremony end the Sovereign Pier Accords (CC-009, MCD-085, MCD-1901). Whoever the Book 1 investigation names for the killing, Kanja holds it as the Sovereign Trust's breaking of the Accords: at thirty he had the Trust's system on the ropes, it survived only because his father brokered the Accords and asked him to stand down (MCD-245, MCD-1880), and that man is now dead. After the Pi-Awakening on Day 0, Kanja chooses to reclaim the Trinity he surrendered to the L9 vault in the Karkosa Complex (MCD-246): Mafesto, Onyx of Oblivion and Obsidian Malice. The reclamation is Book 1's Karkosa Heist, where grip contact restores the Heartline's full channel (ARS-437). It is the turn CC-161 and the Kanja profile reserve for Maro's death. Reserved: no pre-Book-1 material may draft, foreshadow or reference the murder, the breaking of the Accords, or the choice to reclaim the Trinity. The draft's flag 3 reads 'Take Macana' as Obsidian Malice, the war club, and asks the author to confirm.
