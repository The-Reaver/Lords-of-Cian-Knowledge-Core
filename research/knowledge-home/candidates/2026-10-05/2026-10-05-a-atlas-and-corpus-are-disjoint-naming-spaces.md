## Body
The Phase 1.5 review of `GEO-001` through `GEO-006` found that of about 70 live-sheet Gazetteer names only six appear anywhere in the Chronicles, while the corpus has built its own unplaced geography: Portside and its canal districts (the Rebellion's unnamed home city), the Kessic region, Voskharen, Kesmara, naval geography off Jicome (Gale Straits, Kothrane Narrows, Salt Keep) and House Verehimu's seat. Applied directly in Batch 331: `GEO-003` gains the Rathaan Prime capital site; `GEO-006` clarified that Killane and Ash Harbor are additional to, not included in, the 52 free-to-rename Holds and Settlements; two Bane Chronicles (`MCD-681`/`683`) fixed the Kessic collision; "COINTELPRO" removed from Sankofa Chronicle V prose. Left for the author: whether the Verehimu Wetlands (`MCD-147`) and Voskharen Wetlands are the same, whether "the Karkosa" the crew keeps an archive aboard is the Karkosa Complex itself, the Teeth's placement, `MCD-094`'s percentages as area vs political weight, `MCD-112`'s Southern Seaboard definition, and a full enrichment pass. Several were mechanically settled in Batches 364-367, the others remain queued.
