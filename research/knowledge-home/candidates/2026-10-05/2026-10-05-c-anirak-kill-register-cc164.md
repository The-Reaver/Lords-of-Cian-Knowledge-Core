## Body
CC-164 (Batch 371), on the CC-162 pattern. The register runs from the fleet's arrival at Chain Harbor onward. Necessity kills only. Terms are given once where there is time, and anyone who sits or runs is not struck. Giving ground inside a fight is not fleeing. Foreclosure is lethal only to those still coming (ARS-441 amended: it strips weapons from the dropped and kills only those who keep coming). At Flood State she cannot make these judgments, so Ren carries them. The original Foreclosure wording contradicted the approved register, which is why the batch needed six reviews. This is the third necessity-kill register after Lauris's CC-162 and Kanja's CC-161.
