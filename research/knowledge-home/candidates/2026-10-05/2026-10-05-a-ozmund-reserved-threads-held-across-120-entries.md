## Body
By the end of Batch 306 the Ozmund series stood at 120 entries (I-CXX, `MCD-1730` through `MCD-1849`), and each wave's note records that these threads stayed closed: (1) Draconis never learns, or approaches learning, that Ozmund is a Gravity Titan (the purity test, `CC-085`); (2) Lucius Blackthorne and Grulak appear nowhere; (3) the Crown-Scar's true siphon / root-access-tether nature (`MCD-290`) is never revealed; (4) `CC-071` and `MCD-319` are untouched; (5) `MCD-318`'s strangers-until-adulthood constraint is respected throughout the Val Mirel strand, so even the oblique war-sister hint toward Val Saeryn Kareth never names her; (6) no entry foreshadows the Fulfillment Ceremony. The house-politics strand additionally excluded Cassius Verehimu entirely after a single cameo (Chronicle XVII), kept deliberately unremarkable. Reserved threads are the part of a series most likely to be broken silently by parallel drafting agents, so each agent was told them up front and each wave was swept afterward. Later review (Batch 336) found one leak anyway, the Crown-Scar nature referenced in Chronicle XC, and removed it, proving the sweep step is needed.
