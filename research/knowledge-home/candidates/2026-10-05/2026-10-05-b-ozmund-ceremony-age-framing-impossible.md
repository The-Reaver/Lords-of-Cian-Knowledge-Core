## Body
The Batch 336 Ozmund review found the Val Mirel strand rules MCD-1741 to MCD-1745 gave "Set roughly N years before the Fulfillment Ceremony" framing, impossible against CC-015/CC-090 which place the Ceremony at roughly Ozmund age 201. They were reworded to "Set strictly pre-Fulfillment-Ceremony, Ozmund age N". The same batch reconciled MCD-1806's age (26 to 25), stripped a Crown-Scar-siphon leak from MCD-1819 and Chronicle XC (the Crown-Scar's true nature is reserved, MCD-025/MCD-290), and fixed citations for Osric, Cobb, and Aldenmoor. Deliberately left for the author: the ~190-year gap between Ozmund's youth and Red Beard's "decades later" interview framing; the MCD-138 versus MCD-1850 tension over Drakmund's age; Chronicle I's unstated age; the Karkosa Atlas-queue dependency in two entries; whether to lock a Book-2 duration number in Chronicle XII.
