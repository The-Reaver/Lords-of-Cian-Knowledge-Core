## Body
The Phase 5 tally calls this the single largest structural question surfaced in Phase 5: the CULT block's framing that T.D.K. returned and was immediately contained at the Great Breach conflicts with the locked Book 2-5 arc, where he is active and Book 5 is the final containment. The tally says 16 rules, approval-list item 13 says 18 CULT rules. The recommended resolution: a new rule CULT-201, the "Breach-era containment": the Pi-Awakening wakes T.D.K. and at the same moment disrupts his Exchange Protocol as Haku's did; his life-support architecture holds only his person, and he still acts through the Champions, proxies, Crown-Scar, and Warbody; the cults' "post-containment" present is then the Book 2-4 world, and Book 5's containment stays the final one. Alternative: re-date the cults' present to after Book 5, rewriting about 14 rules and still breaking CULT-191. Awaiting the author.
