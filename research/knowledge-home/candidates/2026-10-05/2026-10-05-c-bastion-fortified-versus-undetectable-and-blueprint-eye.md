## Body
This is read-only research and nothing in it is canon. From research/grounded-bastion-audit-2026-10-05.md. Finding 3: locked canon says the reinforcement cannot be seen. MCD-142: 'None of this is detectable to the unaided eye.' MCD-144: undetectable without tools in Book 1 and macro-visible from Book 4. 'So much more fortified' therefore needs a ruling against both. Errors and tensions rated REAL: the Blueprint Eye shows the reinforcement as amber signatures (MCD-142) and is Kanja's own tool (MCD-289, MCD-246, ARS-350), used on reinforced structures such as the Pier Nine seawall (MCD-1521) and the Glass Reef (MCD-1232), yet he never sees them, so a masking or misreading mechanism is needed; and hardening older than the Talisman (MCD-207's anomalies about 1,950 years before Book 1) has no locked cause (candidates: MCD-145 Ionic Rite hardening, MCD-278, coastal seams). Rated SOFT (compatible reading exists but is unstated): Kanja's 'unaware' state against MCD-256 and MCD-252, Lauris detecting it before Book 2 against MCD-144, collapses Kanja caused while the Bastion ran (sharpest MCD-243, the Voskharen Span). A ready seed: MCD-1037 (Killane, about Kanja 40) credits a gallery he shored to workmanship, 'Not because it was special', with an unexplained 'discoloration in the stone'. Revisited Rebellion places show no hardening, and none needs a fix under current rules.
