## Body
VB-067 (Batch 375). The author asked for reliable and unreliable account types for every character. Account types: the Series, plus Comrade Account, Adversary Account, Dossier and Hearsay. Every teller is bounded by what they could know. Unreliable tellers may state falsehoods, which are locked only as said and traced to the truth. Pre-lock entries stay reliable and are not relabeled. From VB-067's lock onward every new entry's header carries its account type and its teller. VB-062 was amended in the same batch. The full craft rules for how these accounts are written are in VB-068.
