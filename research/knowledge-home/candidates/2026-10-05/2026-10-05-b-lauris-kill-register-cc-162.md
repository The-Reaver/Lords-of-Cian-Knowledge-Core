## Body
CC-162: Lauris's forty Directorate contracts, including terminations of cornered or non-resisting targets (CP-414, MCD-187; Hellem Veth-Kovan by agreement, MCD-192) and the killing of Selene's killer, stand exactly as locked. From the Defection (Operation 40, MCD-192) onward every kill by her own hand is a necessity kill: the person is an active immediate threat to life, and where there is time she gives one warning. She disables rather than kills whenever disabling suffices (Attia's Rite exists for that), never executes a surrendering or fleeing person, never kills for deterrence. It parallels CC-161 without merging with it. Her combat-joy (CC-134) lives in the body's full capacity, never in the kill. Approved with the conditional phrasing "as long as it makes sense I approve".
