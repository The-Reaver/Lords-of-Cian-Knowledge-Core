## Body
The author's direction at Batch 306 was: "let's make sure each of these entries have 20 total entries. logically woven into our rules and batches." It was read as bringing each of the six existing strands (Draconis, Aethelgard, Val Mirel, house politics, wider household/guard, coming-of-age) up to 20 entries, so 120 total, up from 50, adding 70 entries (`MCD-1780` through `MCD-1849`). The reading was not re-confirmed with the author before the run; it is recorded here as an interpretation that held because no correction followed. Eight parallel agents ran, one per strand block (the two largest strands split into 8/7 sub-waves to match proven per-agent capacity). The phrase "logically woven into our rules and batches" was honored by merging as ledger rules in one batch with file references verified to resolve. An ambiguous scale instruction of this kind is worth restating back to the author in one line before a large run.
