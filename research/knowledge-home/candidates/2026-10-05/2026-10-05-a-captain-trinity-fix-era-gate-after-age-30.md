## Body
Rules Walkthrough backfill for the Captain alias found `MCD-1058`, `1091`, `1367`, `1374`, `1381`, `1386`, `1515`, `1518` and `1521` describing "full-Trinity combat showcases" (Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System, Onyx's named powers, Obsidian Malice) in scenes set well inside the 284-year Long Mask, after the Trinity's age-30 surrender (`MCD-246`). The finding was verified against the primary rule text rather than the walkthrough agent's characterization. Nine parallel agents (one per file) rewrote each Chronicle with the correct Long-Mask-era kit: the seven-piece post-Mafesto system (`ARS-344` through `356`: Forge-Coat, Sovereign Eyes, Breath Collar, Ironhand Gauntlets, Ironfall Boots, Smoke System, Mend-Line) plus the Rexmar Machete, wielded through trained swordsmanship and Kanja's instinctive Rexmar-Mar tactical sense, never a named power. Substitution map: Mafesto momentum redirection became Forge-Coat plus Ironfall Boots grounding; Obsidian Malice discharge became Ironhand Gauntlets leverage (explicitly not a discharge); Onyx's reading of a fight became Machete plus instinct, with Sovereign Eyes and Smoke System covering perception and stealth beats. Every scene, outcome and line of dialogue stayed; each file's italic header note was corrected citing the batch; ledger statements were then updated centrally from each agent's swap-map. The era rule: Trinity gear before the age-30 surrender, post-Mafesto kit after, and Book-2 Moonvault gifts never before Book 2.
