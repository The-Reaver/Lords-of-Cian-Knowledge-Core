## Body
The Ezio Rules Walkthrough found `CC-073` ambiguous about whether Ezio knows Nadea loves him and whether a reconciliation should be built toward. Asked which reading he preferred, the author delegated: "lets go with what makes the story more rich and stays true to Ezio's character." Resolution: Ezio already knows (a man whose profession is reading what people do not say, with 750 years of wisdom-equivalence under Sephtis and the Socratic Trap, `ARS-405`, missing it for years would undercut his characterization) and has chosen deliberately and repeatedly to say nothing and stay. `MCD-1875` (category `book1-structure`) locks the reconciliation as one act of chosen vulnerability at Book 1's climax, the Karkosa Heist act: Nadea steps from patron-at-a-distance into direct field collaboration at genuine personal risk (a defector whose departure already dissolved the Mirrored Chorus, `MCD-021`), and Ezio breaks his lifelong pattern of managed information once, for her alone, to say plainly that he has always known. It is deliberately not a resolved romance; what follows stays open, matching `MCD-216`'s precedent for flexible book-level framing. Approval: "lock it." The approval-list later carries a Nadea Thren proposal moved to `CC-165`.
