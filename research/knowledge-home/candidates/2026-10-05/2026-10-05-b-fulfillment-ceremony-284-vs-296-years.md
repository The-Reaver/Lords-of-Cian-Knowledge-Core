## Body
Finding A1 of the MCD-1-450 review: MCD-091 and ARS-010 say 296 years between the Sovereign Pier Accords and the Fulfillment Ceremony, but every rule locking Kanja's age at the Pi-Awakening (age 314) and the 284-year Long Mask implies 284. Several rules (MCD-226, MCD-279, MCD-320) silently assume one answer or the other. Approval-list item 1 recommends 284 (Kanja 314 at Book 1) because 296 would put the Fulfillment Ceremony after the Pi-Awakening that CC-006 sets at age 314; ten or more rules already assume 284, including CC-005, ARS-437 ("Onyx reads 284 years"), MCD-269, CC-110. It would reverse the Batch 56 ruling that MCD-091 controls, which rested on a belief that 284 years were a different interval from the Long Mask. Scope: MCD-091, ARS-010, CC-101 ("24-36" becomes "24"), seven docs. Awaiting the author.
