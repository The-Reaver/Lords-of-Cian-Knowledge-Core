## Body
Recurring classes found across the Alias Chronicle tracks: (1) Trinity-era gear (Mafesto, Obsidian Malice, Onyx's named powers) in scenes after the age-30 surrender (`MCD-246`) without being swapped for the post-surrender kit (`ARS-344` through `356`); (2) Obsidian Malice (a war club, `ARS-030`) described as a bladed or sheathed weapon; (3) its "two years of dormant charge" (a one-time pre-Black-Trench bank, `ARS-342`) claimed as a fast-regenerating reserve; (4) Voice Bible characterization labels, such as `VB-060`'s "Already-Finished Negotiation", leaking into narrative prose as in-world quoted phrases; (5) writers'-room terms (batch, wave, Chronicle numeral) and literal rule IDs leaking into prose; (6) duplicate Chronicle numerals within a track; (7) stale `chronicle-tracks-status.md` counts (93 to 102); (8) further Corren Halst / Danne Sok pronoun misses; (9) cross-alias timeline impossibilities (an Iron Bastard lesson referenced before that alias existed). Representative per-track fixes: Industrial Myth's Furnace District Strike age corrected 19 to 21 and the alias-name circulation reconciled (`MCD-230`, `VB-061`); Trench Monarch Dredge-Line kill-count conflict with the no-killing doctrine; Sephtis death handled as staged withdrawal; Blue-Collar Titan's Sewer War reconciled into a two-phase campaign (`MCD-1877`, the "resistance command" clarified as the Rebellion's own senior-crew council, never a superior hierarchy). Use as a pre-lock checklist.
