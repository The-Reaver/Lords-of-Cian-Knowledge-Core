## Body
Standing directive from Batch 361: mechanical corrections that add no new fact (realigning a passage with an already-locked fact, removing a banned word, fixing a wrong citation or rename) are applied directly under the author's correction mandate, but still pass gate steps 1-3. A resolution that needs a new fact, or a choice between two locked facts that no precedent settles, is drafted, presented, and locked only on approval.

Practical effect in Batches 363-367: four read-only reviews (timeline, crew and tracks, institutions, Atlas) were split into "no new fact" items, applied immediately as scripted batches with exact-match asserts and zero duplicate IDs, and "needs a new fact" items, collected into docs/lords-of-cian/approval-list-2026-10-03.md (37 items) to await the author. The split is the mechanism that lets cleanup run at speed without the process silently inventing canon.
