## Body
Source: docs/lords-of-cian/character-profiles/triad-guardians.md, Section 2 (proposals in review; gate not cleared; shared thread versus three series undecided), pending the author. TA1, made at the Gate or an ancient clade: recommended an ancient clade bonded and released at the Gate (the last survivors of a clade the Vael Kem hunted, MCD-040, MCD-041, ARS-200), with the forge sealing the bond; this needs clarifying clauses on WC-017 and CC-046; making them at the Gate would break MCD-277 (Varruk seen at Kanja 248) and arguably MCD-040. TA2, how the bond completed and how a Guardian seen at 248 is still juvenile at 290: recommended a keyed bond and an open juvenile window (the mother keyed the three before the curse; imprint completed at the birth event; imprint closes the window and starts maturation), at the cost that no unimprinted Dhar-Kael ever matures; alternatives are a long juvenile stage or a different Oath-Raptor at the Dog Watch. TA3, Sorya's Oath: recommended that it is the keeper's vow, made when she keyed the bond, which keeps CC-097's 'the Oath itself, not Pyro' distinction and gives Sorya her missing bond expression (Varkul holds through will, Varruk through the path, Sorya through the vow); whether she knows Kanja is Pyro's father stays open. Proposed new fact: none of the three has speech beyond Mimic Speech (CC-096), so the profile describes drives, not inner monologue. Recommended one shared profile in three registers (Varkul holds, Sorya remembers, Varruk finds the way).
