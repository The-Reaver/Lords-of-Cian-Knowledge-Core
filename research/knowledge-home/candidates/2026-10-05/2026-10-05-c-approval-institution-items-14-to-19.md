## Body

Item 17: rename 'Aethelgard Kinetic Radiance' to 'Kinetic Radiance' because it shares a name with Ozmund's father and the nation of Aethel-Gard (five rules plus pyro.md). Item 18: read the two near-identical 40% wage statistics (MCD-240 and MCD-286) as an echo, one skim formula applied to soldiers too. Item 19: leave the Iron Bastard priest arc's congregation deity unnamed ('the one they prayed to'). Item 13, the T.D.K. 'contained at the Great Breach' framing, is covered in its own earlier note.
