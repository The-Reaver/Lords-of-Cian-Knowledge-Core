## Body
The Phase 5 tally lists "VB-020/VB-023 vs CC-034's standing Ezio-narrator conflict" as open. Approval-list item 16 recommends: Ezio narrates the Books' investigation chapters, alternating with Fermand (VB-020/VB-023), while Fermand narrates every chapter centered on Ezio himself (CC-034), "because the man who carries the crew's deception cannot be his own witness". The alternative is Fermand narrating all Ezio material. This needs the author's ruling because it affects how the Series Launch Protocol's narrator confirmation for Ezio is worded; Ezio's own Game Plan already confirmed Fermand as narrator under CC-034/VB-024. VB-064 settled the Lauris series' version of the same frame.
