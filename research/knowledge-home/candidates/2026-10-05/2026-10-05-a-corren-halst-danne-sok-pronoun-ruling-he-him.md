## Body
A corpus-wide grep found he/him for both characters throughout all of Bane's track but she/her in seven Captain and Blue-Collar Titan entries (`MCD-1459`, `1056`, `1091`, `1367`, `1379`, `1456`, `1400`) plus six more true positives beyond the pilot's sample (`MCD-1380`, `1389`, `1402`, `1369`, `1451`, `1452`). Resolved he/him for both, the clear majority usage, matching the Maret Vos / Dol Maren resolution (Batch 226, the author's ruling "He/him" there). New dossiers: `CC-158` (Corren Halst) and `CC-159` (Danne Sok); Batch 323 later added `CC-160` for Maret Vos and amended 158/159 with a "found each other on the docks" clause. Care was taken to leave untouched the she/her pronouns of unrelated nearby women (Efa Gol, Danne Sok's own daughter, visiting scholars and children) that the same grep surfaced as false positives. Later fixes in the review sweep also corrected the fourth-generation Storm That Walks apprentice to he/him and Deryn Kettel (Daba track) to she/her, so pronoun consistency is tracked per character, not per track.
