## Body
The Phase 3 review of Kanja Chronicles I-III left four creative rulings unresolved: (1) a standing convention mapping Rebellion age bands to Onyx's VB-026 presence growth, since write order and in-universe age currently disagree once the remaining 27+ of the Twenty-Two Victories fill in out of order; (2) whether narrator codas should be first-person or "the blade" third-person by age band (partly answered later by VB-063); (3) locking Ironbane's (and possibly Soulreaver Zora's) Rebellion-era joining date, since Chronicle III is the only place putting Ironbane in Kanja's company before the Long Mask; (4) naming, or ruling out the SBD as, the "older, quieter apparatus" to which Renfel Auberon is handed.
