## Body
Recommendations (all unapproved) from items 8-12 of docs/lords-of-cian/approval-list-2026-10-03.md, complementing the A3-A5 items covered separately. Item 8, Matar's recruitment: 'four hundred years ago' predates Kanja's birth; recommended about 290 years ago, during the Rebellion, brought in by Sephtis (scope CC-067, CC-102, Lauris XCIX). Item 9, Orlok's dates (CC-091, CC-141 and CC-143 disagree): seat granted about 2,400 years ago, resigned about 1,000 years ago, left the Zenith about 80 years before Book 2 (three rules). Item 10, when Red Beard met Ozmund: before the Ceremony, because MAW-121's secret training and Testament I's outer-command night both require it; the 'twenty years' lines in Ozmund I and L are softened and the meeting is locked as a narration convention; the alternative (they met only in Book 1) means amending MAW-121 and recasting Testament I's frame. Item 11, Maw dates: House Brekka founded about 60 years ago, not 120, which predated its own harbor; Lady Aravel's House about 50 years ago, not 250, since she is 72; Essek Nightfall about 950 years ago, so Gorren does not out-serve Red Beard's 'longest career'; recommended all three. Item 12, the Maw's eras (MAW-091, MAW-110, MAW-111, MAW-150) date the Haku war to about 3,000 years ago and give T.D.K. direct control during his dormancy; recommended that Era I becomes the Old Dominion's surviving administration after the Deposition, keeping every date and era name (four rules).
