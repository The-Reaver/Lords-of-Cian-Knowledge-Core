## Body
The Codex defines the Chain-Strike doctrine: Stack is the fuel and Fury, her heart rate, is the engine; Stack drains when she stands still. Debt Collection is her hook-and-pull family of techniques. Her carry is the Fangs on forearm chains and the Morning Star across her back. Named combinations are organized by Fury state, undersea, with partners, and with the Tide Line crew. This extends MCD-251's momentum-accumulation biology and the four escalation states Warm, Hot, White and Flood (ARS-370).
