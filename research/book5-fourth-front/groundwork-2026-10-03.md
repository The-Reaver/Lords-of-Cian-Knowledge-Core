# Anirak's Fourth Front: Groundwork Report

Read-only research against `canon-ledger.json` (`ledger_version` 36.9, 2,668 rules) plus
`docs/lords-of-cian/character-profiles/*.md` and the Chronicle corpus, 2026-10-03.
Abad's direction being researched: *"there are three battlefronts in the final book [Book 5, The
Miner's Son] we will create a fourth specific Battlefront that she [Anirak] will take on on her own
with her own specialized unit which will be the rest of the pirate crew."*

Nothing here is drafted rule text. It records what's locked, where the seams are, and three
candidate shapes for the front.

---

## 0. Headline findings

1. **Canon already implies a fourth theater.** `MCD-328`: "The Obsidian Prefecture commits 200,000
   legionaries to the alliance's western front." `POL-100` and `POL-102` repeat it. "The western
   front" is not one of `MCD-221`'s three fronts, and no rule says which front, if any, it is. Its
   direction is also ambiguous. The Prefecture is a locked antagonist (`POL-040`, `POL-108`), broadly
   sympathetic to T.D.K. (`CULT-067`), and Lady Vestige steers its timing (`POL-100`). That reads as
   committing the legions *against* the alliance's western front, but the sentence can also be read
   as joining it. **This needs a ruling whichever candidate is chosen.**
2. **Book 5's geography is almost entirely unlocked.** No rule places the Engine, Gate, or Line
   fronts on the map. The fixed points:
   - the Gate is "the Broken Meridian entrance" (`MCD-098`), and the Broken Meridian lies "beneath
     what later became the Old Dominion's foundations" (`WC-010`);
   - the Old Dominion's former capital is now the Ash Maw/the Scar in the Lawless Reaches
     (`GEO-007`);
   - T.D.K. lands at "the harbor floor at Karkosa" during the 48-hour deficit (`MCD-147`).

   "Karkosa" is itself an open Atlas question: the Trust capital versus the crew's own base
   ("the Karkosa's home harbor," `MCD-208`; Sereth Vaul's "314 years preparing an operation against
   the Karkosa," `MCD-226`).
3. **T.D.K. has real sea-going assets in locked canon.** None of them is assigned to a Book 5 front:
   - **Ever-Haunt naval forces.** `MCD-279` Act III has "Kanja's naval engagement against Ever-Haunt
     forces," and Sereth Vaul "redirects Ever-Haunt naval forces" (`MCD-281`).
   - **Tide-Wraiths.** These are engineered Ionic-Rite sea predators, not a natural species
     (`MCD-185`, `MCD-1545`). Lauris destroyed two production substrates (the Voskharen Trench and
     the Far Trench, `MCD-185`/`MCD-191`). `MCD-191`'s Op 36 finding, that the tradition "built
     distributed redundancy into its facilities generally," leaves room for a third.
   - **The Vael Kem (the Hollow Tide).** A deep-ocean civilization with pressure-based biology that
     "consumed Dhar-Kael as a food source," the Triad's own clade. They are "scattered, not destroyed"
     (`MCD-041`).
   - T.D.K.'s "hidden coastal corner"/"dead ground," the Peninsula (`WC-012`, `WC-020`, `GEO-002`
     DK, `GEO-003` Dark Spire, `POL-101`).
4. **Anirak's Line-front tie is already internally strained** (`anirak.md` finding 12):
   - Ren has a Line-front role (`ARS-369`, `ARS-372`) but isn't on `MCD-221`'s roster.
   - `ARS-374` puts Lauris in a shared Book 5 engagement "extend[ing] MCD-221's Line-front roster,"
     while `ARS-386` puts Lauris on the Engine front.
   - `ARS-374` invokes the Spine's pressure wave, which `ARS-365`/`ARS-366` restrict to the
     Meridian's enclosed corridors.

   Moving Anirak to her own front is a chance to repair this seam, not only a cost.
5. **Who "the rest of the pirate crew" can be.** Roughly a dozen named Long Mask-era crew have **no
   Book 5 assignment at all**: Torian, Stormbreaker, Dreadlord Azar, Voidbreaker, Ghostwind,
   Stormreaver, Zora, Ren, Damu, Cooper, Matar, and Sephtis, plus Valen (whose Blade reckoning has no
   location) and the Triad. That pool is large enough for a real specialized unit without touching
   any locked front assignment.

### 1b. Anirak's own unit

- `MCD-251`: "Establishes her four-person unit as the first sub-crew loyal to a lieutenant rather
  than to Kanja directly."
- `MCD-1883` / Kanja Chronicle V: three freed prisoners hold the eastern passage at her back unasked.
  They "do not join the crew. They join her," and look at her first when Valen asks whose orders they
  take.
- **No names locked anywhere.** It is ambiguous whether "four-person" means Anirak plus three, or
  three plus a later fourth (`anirak.md` finding 14). Whether Ren is ever part of the unit is also
  unaddressed.
- **Recommendation:** her Book 5 "specialized unit" is the natural maturation of this sub-crew. The
  Chain Harbor three (named at last) become the unit's core, with the unassigned Avatars and pirate
  sailors around them. This is the only lieutenant-loyal command structure in canon, which is
  exactly what "on her own, with her own unit" needs.

### The Talisman's 48-hour deficit (`MCD-145`, `MCD-146`, `MCD-147`)
- The Orlok-Vakas collision at the Gate nearly breaches it. The Talisman reallocates, and the rest of
  the planet's hardening runs at a deficit for about 48 hours, "the exact window T.D.K. times his
  climactic attack around." Re-tuning latency is a hard floor (`MCD-147`).
- **Best protected:** Jicome/Mao, the Voskharen Wetlands, the Broken Meridian, and "certain coastal
  seams."
- **Thinnest:** the Sovereign Trust interior.
- **Karkosa harbor holds** (three centuries of pre-loading).
- **Off-page failures:**
  - "a small island off the Voskharen coast partially submerges"
  - "a Sovereign Trust coastal cliff collapses with a fishing village on it"
  - "two aging bridges fail"
  - "roughly 400 dead combined, reported off-page."
- **Implication:** any front fought outside Jicome, the Voskharen, and the Meridian during those 48
  hours is fought on ground that won't absorb S-tier collisions. That is a coastal and island
  vulnerability, and a real tactical reason a sea front matters.

## 4. Anirak's abilities relevant to choosing her front

- **Underwater superiority** (`CC-113`): "measurably faster and more powerful underwater than on
  land." The mechanism is torque through resistance, and there is a hydro-sensitive sonar-like sense.
- **Ambient ocean perception** (`ARS-367`): she reads "waves, weather, submersibles" before any
  instrument.
- **Active sonar** (`ARS-371`): about 300m at Hot state or above, Book 5 only.
- **Flood State** (`ARS-368`, `ARS-369`, `ARS-373`): reached only after the series' *longest
  sustained engagement*. Her ceiling is duration-gated, not biology-gated. A front defined by
  endurance, such as holding for the whole 48-hour deficit window, fits it best.
- **Siren:**
  - The gaze is always on and captures friend and foe alike (`CC-112`).
  - The Voice is a ~10m cone with a 15-20% distance-misjudgment effect (`ARS-411`), and it has no
    effect on undead (`ARS-373`). Her front should be against **living** enemies.
  - Voice/gaze sync becomes one compound effect at Flood State (`ARS-370`).
- **Lantern-Star** (`ARS-411`, `CULT-197`): strobe mode is part of the crew's light toolkit against
  the Ever-Haunt. It causes involuntary dispersal in lower tiers and degraded function in higher
  ones.
- **Twin Fangs and chain thermal transfer** (`ARS-372`): structural weakening. That is
  anti-hull and anti-structure work, the "Surface Storm" half of the pair.
- **Ren pairing** (`CC-114`, `CC-112`): "Surface Storm / Deep Pressure."
  - Ren is from a "deep-ocean pressure-adapted community on the Jicome continental shelf"
    (`CC-101`).
  - He has ~15km of pressure-sense early warning against a sub-surface team (`CC-138`).
  - He is the one person who sees her clearly.
  - Together they make an undersea pair no one else in canon matches.
- **Naval and Maw history:**
  - Recruited at the Chain Harbor holding port, age 55 (`MCD-251`, `MCD-1883`).
  - Southern Sweep campaign era (`MCD-255`).
  - Maw-raised since 12, and Maw-11 was her former facility (`MCD-264`).
  - No other locked naval engagement names her. Her sea credentials rest on biology and kit, not on
    a battle record. That is a gap a pre-Book-1 Chronicle wave could fill to set the front up.
- **Hard constraints that bind any front** (`ARS-373`): no stillness, no sniping, no clean kills.

## 5. Candidate concepts for the fourth front

### Candidate A: "The Deep Front" (undersea and sea-lane front during the deficit)

- **Theater:** open sea and the undersea approaches to T.D.K.'s Karkosa-harbor landing. This covers
  the trench country (Voskharen and Far Trench waters) and the coastal seams left exposed when the
  Talisman reallocates (`MCD-147`).
- **Enemy:**
  - Sereth Vaul's Ever-Haunt naval forces (`MCD-279`, `MCD-281`), which is his "314-year operation"
    (`MCD-226`) finally launched;
  - a third, previously unknown Tide-Wraith production substrate, waking on cue, consistent with the
    distributed-redundancy finding (`MCD-191`) and `MCD-185`'s "engineered" ruling;
  - optionally, the Vael Kem (`MCD-041`) drawn up from the deep. They are the Dhar-Kael's ancient
    predators, which gives the front stakes for Pyro's Triad without moving the Triad off the
    Engine.
- **Objective:** keep T.D.K.'s seaborne forces from reaching the Engine front's flank or the harbor
  during the 48 hours. Optionally, sever undersea Ionic-Rite kennel loops (`CULT-198`) so the
  Ever-Haunt at sea lose cohesion.
- **Why only she can hold it:**
  - underwater superiority (`CC-113`) and sonar (`ARS-371`);
  - Ren's deep-ocean origin, plus the Lantern-Star against the Ever-Haunt;
  - Flood State reached because 48 hours is unambiguously "the series' longest sustained
    engagement";
  - mostly living enemies (Wraiths, Vael Kem, Sereth himself), so the Siren works.
- **Unit:** the Chain Harbor three as core, plus Ren, Zora (atmospheric), Stormreaver (aerial
  spotting), Voidbreaker, Cooper (logistics), Damu (medic), and the Foldtide and escorts or the
  Merchant Accord hulls.
- **Keeps intact:** `MCD-099`, `MCD-327`, `MCD-145`, `MCD-146`, `MCD-147`, `MCD-098`, `MCD-220`,
  `MCD-221`'s Engine and Gate, `MCD-319`, `ARS-367`, `ARS-370`, `ARS-372` (Ren stays with her),
  `CC-114`, `CULT-197`.
- **Amends:**
  - `MCD-097` and `WC-022` (three to four);
  - `MCD-221` (remove Anirak from the Line, add the fourth front);
  - `ARS-368` and `ARS-373` (front name);
  - `ARS-369` (front name; the Red Beard 30m clause either drops or becomes the one moment the two
    fronts touch);
  - `ARS-371` (Ironbane can stay as the Line-fleet recipient of her relayed contacts, preserving the
    clause; rename only the "Line front's" warning-system wording);
  - `ARS-374` (re-scope away from the Line roster, which also fixes the `ARS-386` conflict).
  - It also needs new rules: a third Wraith substrate and Sereth's Book 5 front. Optional: tying the
    Vael Kem to T.D.K.
- **Strength:** most "only she could hold it." **Cost:** introduces new enemy commitments.

### Candidate B: "The Western Front" (adopt `MCD-328`)

- **Theater:** the Obsidian Prefecture's western coast: Vance, Vanegate, and the Drowning Floor at
  Praetura/Maw-15 (`POL-040`, `MAW-063`), where the water level shifts mid-fight and favors aquatic
  fighters.
- **Enemy:** the Prefecture's 200,000 legionaries (`MCD-328`) under Dhampir Black (`POL-102`), all
  **LIVING**, so the Siren works at mass scale. Vestige is behind the timing (`POL-100`). Possibly
  Prefecture-allied Shogunate vessels.
- **Objective:** stop the legions from turning the alliance's western flank and rolling into the
  Line's rear while the deficit leaves the coast unprotected. Severin's political and epistemic
  defeat (`POL-100`) happens behind it, unchanged.
- **Why only she can hold it:**
  - Maw-raised, so the Drowning Floor is her ground;
  - she can come from the sea against a land army;
  - she's an anti-mass-infantry weapon (Split-Crown, `ARS-411`; a 10m cone);
  - longest engagement: 200,000 men.
- **Keeps intact:** **`MCD-328`, `POL-100`, `POL-102`, `CULT-067`, `POL-040`** (it gives the orphan
  "western front" a home), plus everything Candidate A keeps.
- **Amends:** the same set as A (`MCD-097`, `WC-022`, `MCD-221`, `ARS-368`, `ARS-369`, `ARS-371`,
  `ARS-373`, `ARS-374`).
- **Requires one ruling:** `MCD-328`'s direction. If the legions are committed *to* the alliance,
  B inverts into Anirak keeping a volatile, Vestige-manipulated ally from turning. That's still
  viable but weaker.
- **Strength:** least new invention. **Weakness:** it's mostly land against infantry, so it uses her
  underwater superiority least, and the Drowning Floor is a Maw venue, not a battlefield, until a
  rule makes it one.

     `MCD-328`'s "western front" is named as hers.
- **Connective tissue:**

  - Red Beard's 30m (`ARS-369`) becomes the one beat where the Tide Line and the Line meet at the
    coast. It is kept, not cut.
- **Keeps the most:**
