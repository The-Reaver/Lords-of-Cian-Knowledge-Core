# PART 1 — Complete Chronicle of Kanja Haku Rexmar (87,701 words)

## Key overall finding
Only a small number of Gazetteer place names actually appear in the manuscript verbatim: **Jicome** (region, ~51 mentions — the entire story's home turf), **Karkosa** (Sovereign Trust Domain capital, 2 mentions), **Maw-15** (Prefecture, matches Gazetteer's "Maw-15 Drowning"), **Maw-7** (1 vague mention), **Stormshelter Cove** (Sovereign Trust Domain, 2 mentions), and passing non-directional references to **Old Dominion**, **T.D.K.**, and **Shattered Kingdoms** as historical/cultural background. Every other named location in the manuscript (Portside, Killane, Ash-Wharf, Ghost Harbor, Gale Straits, Kothrane Narrows, Furnace District, Free Quarter, Fort Tidewall, Ironport, Saltmarsh, Vastok, Keldane Province/Reach/City, Callister, Vostane Mines, the Gilded Lighthouse, Sovereign Pier, etc.) is an invented sub-cell location not present in the master Gazetteer at all — expected per the atlas's own Note 5, but worth surfacing as a real coverage gap.

## 1. Contradictions found

### C1 — "Jicome Eastern Seaboard" vs. Jicome's actual coastal orientation (HIGH CONFIDENCE)
Quote: *"The Jicome Eastern Seaboard was serviced by nine Blight Frequency relay towers, positioned at intervals of approximately fifty kilometers along the coastal ridge."* (line ~559)

Places: Jicome's coastline / ports.
Grid: Jicome's only two Gazetteer settlements typed "Port" are **Cindreth (A08)** and **Sulvane (A05)** — both in column A, the westmost column on the entire 20-column grid. Jicome's column-C settlements (Emberward C06, Straithe C03, Orrum C04) are inland-type sites (Ringkeep, Wardtower, Mine-town), not ports.
Conflict: The Gazetteer places Jicome's coast on its **west** edge, not its east. Elsewhere the manuscript consistently and repeatedly calls this same coastal warzone the "Jicome Southern Seaboard" / "Southern District" (rows 5-9, the southern part of Jicome's own row 1-9 span, which does fit the Gazetteer). This single "Eastern Seaboard" instance looks like either an internal manuscript slip (vs. its own dominant "Southern Seaboard" naming) or a genuine mismatch against the atlas — either way it conflicts with the grid.

### C2 — Maw-15 as part of "the Southern Seaboard" (MODERATE-HIGH CONFIDENCE)
Quote: *"Maw-15 was the last major Maw on the Southern Seaboard."* (Ledger, Maw Cascade campaign)
Grid: Gazetteer lists **Maw-15 Drowning** in **The Prefecture**, cell **B16**. Jicome's southernmost named settlements sit around row 8 (Cindreth A08, Vaelkarn B08). B08→B16 = 8 rows = **~1,600 miles due south**, in a wholly separate mapped region (The Prefecture, not Jicome/Sovereign Trust territory).
Conflict: The manuscript's "Southern Seaboard" is established (repeatedly, e.g. "Jicome Southern Seaboard," "Jicome Southern District") as Jicome's own home coastline, and the Maw Cascade campaign groups Maw-15 with Maw-6, Maw-9, Maw-11 as the same regional Maw system. Placing a Prefecture site ~1,600 miles south inside that same "Southern Seaboard" campaign conflicts with the grid. (Caveat: "Southern Seaboard" is never formally defined as Jicome-exclusive in every instance, so there's room to read it as a broader colonial coastal band — noted as the counter-reading.)

### C3 — Stormshelter Cove as "on the Southern Seaboard" (MODERATE-HIGH CONFIDENCE)
Quote: *"Stormshelter Cove was a natural harbor on the Southern Seaboard's most protected stretch of coastline."* (The Orphan's Harbor, age 250)
Grid: Gazetteer places **Stormshelter Cove** in **Sovereign Trust Domain**, cell **G15**. From Jicome's southern settlements (~B08) that's ~5 columns east and ~7 rows south (~1,700 miles southeast), and the two territories are not contiguous — the entire Lawless Reaches region (columns D-H, most of rows 8-18) sits between them on the grid.
Conflict: Same "Southern Seaboard = Jicome's coast" naming pattern as C2, now applied to a site in a distant, non-adjacent region. This is the stronger of the two "Southern Seaboard" findings because of the non-contiguity.

Note: C2 and C3 share a root cause — by the back half of the manuscript, "Southern Seaboard" appears to have drifted from "Jicome's coast" (its consistent meaning in Volume I) to a looser, colony-wide label that also absorbs Sovereign Trust Domain and Prefecture territory (reinforced by "creating a continuous Blight field from the Southern Capes to the Keldane Reach," line ~3335, where Keldane echoes the Gazetteer's Sovereign Trust Domain settlement Keldane Hollow). This is worth a continuity pass by the author even if not every instance is a hard "error."

## 2. Atlas-internal ambiguities encountered

- **Karkosa duplicate listing (Note 2).** The manuscript's Karkosa references ("the deepest vault of the Karkosa Complex... the Sovereign Trust's most secure containment facility," lines ~1125-1145) clearly imply Karkosa belongs to the Sovereign Trust's own seat of power. The Gazetteer lists Karkosa twice, once under Sovereign Trust Domain (its capital, Wallcrown City, tier L) and once under Lawless Reaches, both at G16. The prose context resolves this in favor of the Sovereign Trust Domain listing. Not a chronicle error, it's the atlas's own known duplication (per Note 2), now specifically attributable to the Sovereign Trust Domain entry rather than Lawless Reaches, per the prose.
- **Maw-7 duplicate listing (Note 2).** The one vague Maw-7 mention ("He had won his manumission at Maw-7 at age twenty-eight, walked out of the Maw, and disappeared into the interior," line ~1903) gives no directional or regional detail specific enough to resolve which of the two Gazetteer Maw-7 Slab listings (Sovereign Trust Domain vs. Lawless Reaches, both G17) is intended. Flagged as unresolved atlas ambiguity, not a chronicle error.
- **"Southern Seaboard" scope drift** (see C2/C3 note above) sits at the boundary between a chronicle continuity issue and an atlas-scope question, since the atlas itself doesn't define seaboard/coastal-band groupings across regions.

# PART 2 — The Long Mask Chronicles (T3 Corrected, ~58,000 words)

## Method note
Cross-referenced every Gazetteer place name (60+ entries) against the manuscript, then ran exhaustive regex sweeps across all 2,496 lines for compass-direction words (139 instances reviewed), distance/travel phrasing (15+ instances), and border/adjacency language. Full-file coverage, not a sample.

## Headline finding
This volume's actual named locations (Ironport, Portside, Gallows Bay, Gilded Lighthouse, Fort Gallan, Keldane Reach/City/River, Chain Harbor, Meridian, Gale Straits, Raptor's Nest, Vostane Mines, Brasswater, Saltmarsh, Callister, Vastok, Brinewell, Coppermouth, Tideglass, Ashcoral, Windbreak, etc.) are almost entirely absent from the Gazetteer. Only a handful of Gazetteer names surface at all, and none are used with an explicit compass direction or numeric distance relative to another Gazetteer-listed place. Net effect: effectively zero hard, checkable place-to-place spatial claims in this volume, a different profile than Part 1, because this book's prose almost never puts two Gazetteer-named places in the same sentence with a direction or distance between them.

## 1. Contradictions found
None met the bar for a confirmed contradiction (explicit, unambiguous grid conflict). One item flagged conservatively as a possible tension rather than a contradiction:

- **Maw-15 grouping.** Prose: *"Maw-15 was the last major Maw on the Southern Seaboard."* (line 2159), grouped narratively with Maw-3, Maw-7, Maw-9, Maw-11, Maw-12 as one continuous coastal "Southern Seaboard" Maw system.
  Gazetteer: Maw-15 "Drowning" sits at B16 (The Prefecture). Maw-3 "Belly" sits at K13 and Maw-7 "Slab" at G17 (both Lawless Reaches / Sovereign Trust Domain cluster).
  Grid math: B16→G17 is ~5 columns / ~1 row ≈ 1,000 miles; B16→K13 is ~9 columns / ~3 rows ≈ 1,900 miles.
  Not called a hard contradiction because "the Southern Seaboard" is not a defined Gazetteer term and the prose never states a direction or distance between Maw-15 and the other Maws specifically, it's scene-setting, not a precise claim. But if the author intends these Maws to be reachable within one fleet campaign along a contiguous coast, worth knowing Maw-15's atlas cell puts it in a different named region roughly 1,000+ miles from the Maw-3/Maw-7 cluster. This echoes C2 from Part 1 almost exactly.

## 2. Atlas-internal ambiguities encountered

- **Karkosa duplicate (per Note 2).** "Karkosa Complex" (deepest vault, holds the sealed Trinity weapons) is used in a context implying Sovereign Trust territory/authority. Prose context favors the Sovereign Trust Domain listing, consistent with Part 1's resolution of the same ambiguity.
- **Maw-7 Slab duplicate (per Note 2).** Same G17 duplication (Sovereign Trust Domain vs. Lawless Reaches). Azar's manumission "at Maw-7" (line 751) doesn't specify a controlling region, so the ambiguity carries through unresolved, same as Part 1.
- **Undefined region code "RA" on the World Overview Grid.** Rows 19-21, columns H-K show the code "RA" in territory that Gazetteer entries (Ash Maw Scar J19, Rathaan Prime J20) assign to Lawless Reaches, but "RA" doesn't appear anywhere in the Region Code Key (only "LR" is defined for Lawless Reaches). Looks like it should read "LR" but is worth the author double-checking against the source workbook tab directly, since it isn't explained by the capital-marker-override logic that explains other short codes.
- **"Jicome" naming overlap.** The manuscript uses "Jicome" pervasively (~19 times) as Kanja's homeland/nationality and as effectively synonymous with the Sovereign Trust-controlled "Southern Seaboard", e.g. "The most dangerous man in Jicome," "Jicome's Southern elite," "any Jicome noble could request census data," "the slowest communication network in Jicome." But the Gazetteer's "Jicome" is a separate, distinct region (capital Rexhaven at B05, far northwest of the map) from "Sovereign Trust Domain" (capital Karkosa at G16), two regions with no stated adjacency and, on the grid, several thousand miles apart. Every actual named location tied to the plot (Karkosa, Stormshelter Cove, Maw-7) sits in Sovereign Trust Domain territory, not the atlas's Jicome. Not a hard contradiction (no explicit direction/distance stated), but a naming collision worth attention: either "Jicome" is meant loosely as a demonym/adjective untethered from the atlas region of the same name, or scenes described as being "in Jicome" are actually set in what the atlas calls Sovereign Trust Domain. This is the single most consequential finding across both volumes, since it recurs in Part 1 too (Part 1's C2/C3 "Southern Seaboard drift" is arguably a symptom of this same Jicome/Sovereign-Trust-Domain conflation).

# COMBINED TAKEAWAYS (both volumes)

1. **Three confirmed contradictions**, all in Part 1: Jicome's coast called "Eastern" when the atlas puts it on the west (C1); Maw-15 grouped into Jicome's "Southern Seaboard" when the atlas places it ~1,600 miles away in The Prefecture (C2); Stormshelter Cove called part of the same "Southern Seaboard" when the atlas places it in non-contiguous Sovereign Trust Domain territory (C3).
2. **The biggest structural issue isn't a contradiction, it's a naming collision**: "Jicome" is used throughout both volumes as the name of the whole home nation/setting, but in the atlas "Jicome" is one specific region (capital Rexhaven, far northwest) distinct from "Sovereign Trust Domain" (capital Karkosa), where the actual plot geography (Karkosa, Stormshelter Cove, the Maws) sits. Worth a direct decision: either rename the in-story homeland to match Sovereign Trust Domain, rename Sovereign Trust Domain to Jicome in the atlas, or explicitly define "Jicome" in-world as a broader cultural/national label that spans both mapped regions.
3. **Coverage gap dwarfs the contradictions**: across ~145,000 words of manuscript, the overwhelming majority of named locations (Portside, Ironport, Keldane City, the Gilded Lighthouse, Gale Straits, Fort Gallan, and dozens more) have no atlas entry at all. The atlas currently maps the world at 200-mile resolution with ~80 named sites; the manuscript operates at a street/harbor/district level the atlas was never built to capture. Closing this gap (adding these places to the Gazetteer, even at approximate cells) is the highest-leverage next step if book-to-book consistency matters.
4. **Two unresolved atlas-internal duplicates** (Karkosa and Maw-7 Slab both double-listed under two regions at the same cell) and **one likely grid typo** (region code "RA" used on the grid but never defined in the key, probably meant to be "LR").
