# World crossing: the full menu of options

*UNLOCKED -- options for Abad's ruling, 2026-10-03. His direction: "i want all of your recommendations
when it comes to world travel. give me some simple, some clever, some outside the box. as long as it
adheres to the rules and only if it adheres to the rules."*

## The rules every option must pass

- **No space travel** (Abad, 2026-10-03).
- **Mythic Realism:** "if you cannot describe it in terms of mass, sound, pressure, or chemistry, it
  does not exist." No magic, nothing mystical.
- **`MCD-313`:** the homage World is a separate World, "not a Dimension, and not a region of Cian
  itself," and travel between worlds exists in-fiction.
- **Kares Prime stays as locked:**
  - high gravity, 100,000-year lifespans, and an amber, doubled light
  - the diaspora that left 122,000 years ago and "rarely returned" (`MCD-151`, `MCD-158`)
  - Lauris's arrival at Kesmara 2,000 years ago (`MCD-175`)
- **Book 5's T.D.K. landing at the harbor floor stays intact.**

Every option below passes all of these. The options that failed are listed at the end with the
rule each one breaks.

## Simple

### 1. One World, Many Shores

All the worlds are far shores of one great planet. Cian is one land-world. The homage World lies
across the World-Sea. Kares Prime is a continent-world on the far side. The sea between them is vast
and lethal, so each is a separate world to the others in every way that matters: language, law,
sky, history.

- **Physics:** distance and water.
- **Why it works:** it is how the real "Old World" and "New World" were one planet. The homage era's
  migrations become sea crossings, carrying the weight of every diaspora it honors.
- **What it changes:** "orbit," "planetfall," and "planet" become "shore," "landfall," and "world"
  in about 6 rules and 9 Lauris lines.

### 2. The Long Way Round

The same frame as option 1, on a far larger scale. The planet is enormous, and Cian's known world is
a small fraction of it. The other worlds lie months or years away by sea and land. Few people ever
make the journey, and fewer come back.

- **Physics:** distance alone.
- **Trade-off:** it is simplest to state, but the crossings become rare events rather than something
  a crew can run repeatedly. That limits Kanja's guest appearances.

## Clever

### 3. The Long Currents (recommended as the crossing mechanic)

The World-Sea is crossed only along a few vast ocean currents. Each runs **one way**: a ship rides it
across in weeks, but cannot sail against it. Coming home means finding a return loop, a second
current that bends back, and that is hard to read and moves with the seasons.

- **Physics:** pressure, salinity, and temperature driving deep ocean currents. Real oceans work this
  way.
- **Why it is clever:**
  - **It explains `MCD-158` exactly.** Karesian women "leaving and rarely returning" now has a
    physical cause: the current carried them out, and almost no one could read the way back.
  - **It makes Kanja one of the few who can cross both ways.** His Mar bloodline's locked navigation
    tradition reads barometric pressure through the inner ear and tastes salt content (`MCD-295`).
    That is exactly the sense the return loops require.
  - **It makes him a recurring guest in the homage World across every era, and explains why the
    Trust and the SBD cannot follow.** They can ride out, but they cannot come home.
  - **It makes migration literally one-way for most people,** which gives the homage era's theme
    real weight.

### 4. The Low Water

Every few decades the moon's long tidal cycle drags the World-Sea down so far that shoals and land
bridges surface between worlds for a season. Then the water returns.

- **Physics:** lunar tides, already locked as real force in this world. Living Drakma is forged only
  under lunar conditions (`WC-013`), and the moon is Ossenmere, the Vaultlight (`COS-001`).
- **Why it is clever:** a crossing becomes an event a whole generation waits for. A walking
  migration fits the Great Migration homage closely.
- **Best use:** layered on top of option 3, as the rare overland alternative to the currents.

### 5. The Doubled Sun and the Dense Shore (Kares Prime's explanation, works with 1-4)

- **Gravity:** Kares Prime's continent sits over the planet's densest mass, so gravity there is
  literally higher. That is density physics, which the series already runs on.
- **The "binary-star light":** Kares Prime's dense atmosphere splits the one sun into a permanent
  second image, a real optical effect called a parhelion. Its amber light stays.
- **Bonus fix:** Living Drakma formed from a celestial impact (`WC-013`). On one planet, that impact's
  debris fell on Jicome, the Moonvault caldera, and Kares Prime. This explains why all three hold
  Living Drakma, and resolves `WC-013`'s "Jicome-exclusive" wording against Kares Prime's deposits
  (`MCD-174`).

## Outside the box

### 6. The Deep Road

Beneath the World-Sea runs an abyssal floor-road between the worlds, along trenches like the
Voskharen Trench. It is walkable or sailable only by bodies or hulls that can bear crushing pressure.
It is the home ground of the Vael Kem, the deep-ocean civilization with pressure-based biology,
"scattered, not destroyed" (`MCD-041`).

- **Physics:** pressure and density, the series' own core physics.
- **Why it is outside the box:**
  - The secret crossing is down rather than across.
  - Density-capable beings and pressure-born ones (Ren's deep-ocean origin, Anirak's underwater
    superiority) can use it where ordinary sailors cannot.
  - It ties directly to the Tide Line, the Tide-Wraiths, and T.D.K.'s sea forces.
- **Best use:** a rare, secret, dangerous route layered on top of the main crossing. It is how
  enemies and monsters reach places the currents do not.

### 7. The Blight Belt

The worlds are divided by land, not sea: a continental belt where the air itself is poison, from
Blight chemistry seeping out of the old Dominion wars. Crossing means carrying your own breath.

- **Physics:** chemistry.
- **Why it is outside the box:** Kanja's Breath Collar (`ARS-346`, Environmental Sealing) suddenly
  matters on a world scale.
- **Trade-off:** it sits awkwardly with an island-and-sea Kares Prime. It works best as the barrier
  for one specific crossing, for example between two homage-World regions, rather than as the
  universal mechanism.

## My recommendation

**Use option 1 as the frame, option 3 as the crossing, and option 5 for Kares Prime.** Then layer
option 6 on top as the secret route the enemy uses.

- **The frame:** the worlds are far shores of one planet.
- **The crossing:** the Long Currents carry people out one way, and only a rare few, Kanja's Mar
  senses foremost, can read the return loops.
- **Kares Prime:** its gravity and its doubled sun come from density physics.
- **The Deep Road:** the abyssal route the Vael Kem, the Tide-Wraiths, and T.D.K.'s forces use. It
  pays off in the Tide Line.

Option 4 stays available as a rare generational event, if you want a walking migration scene in the
homage era.

## Rejected because they break a rule

- **Density Strata** (worlds layered in the same place at different densities): reads as a
  Dimension, which `MCD-313` rules out.
- **Ancient gates** connecting distant places directly: cannot be described in mass, sound,
  pressure, or chemistry without hand-waving.
- **A tidally locked planet**, with worlds on a twilight ring: breaks locked day/night cycles and
  lunar phases.
- **Flight or airships:** breaks the locked technology level; no engines beyond the Hymn-Engine and
  the Meridian Engine.
- **A hollow-world interior:** breaks basic gravity physics, and the homage cities need a sky.
- **Time-displacement:** not mass, sound, pressure, or chemistry.
