## Part 1 — The Tide Line (Book 5's fourth front)

**MCD-1889 (new, book5-structure).** The Tide Line is Book 5's fourth front. It is held by Anirak
alone in command, with her own unit drawn from the Long Mask pirate crew. It opens in the Talisman's
48-hour reallocation deficit (`MCD-145`-`147`), when the planet's coastal hardening runs at reduced
capacity, and covers the sea approaches and the western shore that the other three fronts leave open.

It runs in two phases inside those 48 hours:
- **Undersea.** The pirate crew and fleet hold the sea approach against Sereth Vaul's Ever-Haunt
  naval forces and engineered Tide-Wraiths from a third production site. That site is the
  distributed redundancy already implied at `MCD-191`; it is not the third Operation 38 facility.
- **Ashore.** The front then moves onto the western shore, the "alliance's western front" of
  `MCD-328`.

The Tide Line is the longest sustained engagement in the series. That is why Anirak reaches Flood
State there and nowhere else (`ARS-369`). Its success keeps the sea and the western coast closed
while the Engine front recovers the Meridian Engine fragment (`MCD-327`). It touches the Line exactly
once, where the shore phase meets the Line's flank. Her active sonar relays naval positions to
Ironbane's fleet on the Line throughout (`ARS-371`).

**MCD-1890 (new, book5-structure).** The Tide Line's roster:
- **Command.** Anirak, Blades Fury, in sole command.
- **Her core.** Her original Chain Harbor unit (`MCD-251`, `MCD-1883`): Edda, Hamund, and Odile,
  the three prisoners she freed by hand in the eastern passage, Maw-raised and loyal to her before
  any loyalty to Kanja. They are the first sub-crew in the Lords of Cian loyal to a lieutenant.
- **The pirate crew.** Torian (Bloodreaver), Stormbreaker (Kaelen), Azar Dreadlord, Voidbreaker
  (Jax), Ghostwind (Sylas), Stormreaver (Kairo), and Soulreaver Zora. All of them have no other locked
  Book 5 placement, and most were recruited during the Long Mask and the Pirate Dawn.
- **Ren (Abyss).** Ren holds his locked role at her side as aftermath-radius manager (`ARS-369`,
  `ARS-372`, `CC-114`).
- **The fleet.** The Long Mask fleet the pirate crew sailed under the Scourge.
- **On the Line, not here.** Ironbane stays on the Line with his fleet (`MCD-221`). Zora, his partner
  (`CC-063`), fights on the Tide Line. The two fronts' only standing link runs through them and through
  Anirak's sonar relays.

Valen's reckoning with the Blade (`CC-146`, `ARS-385`) keeps no fixed front.

**MCD-1891 (new, book5-structure).** The Obsidian Prefecture's 200,000 legionaries (`MCD-328`) arrive
on the western shore as the alliance's committed reinforcement. Lady Vestige's perception-warfare
(`MCD-282`, `POL-100`) turns them mid-battle: forged orders, engineered optics, and manipulated
signals make the legions read the alliance as the enemy. Severin Ebonrath's Senate believes the
commitment was its own patient calculus playing out.

The turned legions are living soldiers, so Anirak's Siren's Voice and gaze work on them (`ARS-373`).
Her always-on attention-capture (`CC-112`) is the one force on the shore that competes directly with
Vestige's perception field. Anirak and the pirate crew hold 200,000 turned legionaries on the shore
until the deception breaks. How the turn breaks, and what it costs, is reserved for Book 5's own
drafting.

**MCD-1892 (new, book5-structure).** Sereth Vaul, "the Silencer," is Anirak's named Book 5 opponent on
the Tide Line's undersea phase. The Silencer meets the Siren's Voice:
- He is the only being who stays functional inside an Ever-Haunt Anti-Resonance field (`MCD-281`).
- Her Lantern-Star is a locked Ever-Haunt countermeasure (`ARS-411`, `CULT-197`).
- Both of them hunt by sound.

The outcome of their engagement is reserved for Book 5's own drafting. No pre-Book-5 material may
assert or imply it.

## Part 2 — How the worlds connect, with no space travel

**The problem.** Canon currently uses space travel in a few places:
- Kares Prime's "orbital trade-points" (`MCD-1550`, `MCD-1688`).
- Its "orbital-travel infrastructure" (`MCD-158`).
- Lauris's "planetfall at the orbital descent station of Kesmara" (`MCD-175`).
- Kares Prime's "binary-star light" (`MCD-1549`).
- `MCD-313`'s "a distinct, physically reachable planet" for the homage World.
- Nine Lauris Chronicles say "orbit."

All of it has to be replaced.

### Recommended: "One world, many shores"

The worlds are the far shores of a single great planet.
- **Cian** is one land-world.
- **The homage World** lies across the World-Sea.
- **Kares Prime** is a continent-world on the far side of the planet.

Each is a world to the others because the water between them is so vast and so lethal that almost
no one crosses it. A crossing is possible only in narrow passages, open only in tidal-and-lunar
windows. People "wind up in another world" the way people always have: they cross the water.
- **Why it is simple.** It is how real people have always reached another world: the "Old World" and
  the "New World" were one planet. It needs no new physics.
- **Why it is clever.**
  - **Kares Prime's high gravity.** Its continent sits over the planet's densest mass, so gravity
    there is literally higher. That is density physics, which this series already runs on.
  - **The "binary star."** It becomes a doubled sun: one sun, split by Kares Prime's dense atmosphere
    into a permanent second image (a parhelion, a real optical effect). Its amber light stays.
- **Why it fits the homage.** Migration between worlds becomes a sea crossing. That carries the full
  weight of every diaspora the homage era honors, including the Great Migration and the crossings
  that came before it.
- **Why it fits Kanja.**
  - Kanja is a sailor and pirate captain for 284 years, and his fleets know the crossings. That is
    how he becomes a recurring guest in the homage World across eras.
  - It also means the Trust and the SBD cannot simply follow him: the windows are few, and the water
    kills the unprepared.
- **What changes.** About six rules (`MCD-158`, `MCD-175`, `MCD-1549`, `MCD-1550`, `MCD-1688`,
  `MCD-313`, plus a phrase in `MCD-151`/`174`) and nine Lauris Chronicle lines.
  - "Orbital trade-points" become crossing-harbors on Kares Prime's shelf.
  - "Planetfall at the orbital descent station" becomes "landfall at Kesmara's crossing-harbor."
  - "Planet" becomes "world" or "shore."

### Alternative A: "The Density Strata"

The worlds occupy the same place at different densities, stacked like layers of oil and water. You
cross at thin places where the layers touch, and only by matching the other layer's density. This is
elegant with the series' physics. But it reads as a Dimension, which Abad ruled out in `MCD-313`.

### Alternative B: "The Old Crossings"

Ancient physical structures, the Living Gate and the Meridian works, connect distant shores. The
Mythic Realism rule ("if you cannot describe it in terms of mass, sound, pressure, or chemistry, it
does not exist") makes this hard to explain without hand-waving. It could still layer on top of the
recommendation as rare, ancient shortcuts, if wanted later.

**Recommendation: "One world, many shores."** Once it is ruled, the homage World gets its own name and
its own Atlas sheet, and the crossings are placed on the new Atlas the census is building.
