# Mirza: planning notes (not canon)

*Superseded by the locked rules `MRZ-001` to `MRZ-007` and `VB-070` (Batch 381; draft at
`docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-11-mirza-rules.md`). Where these notes differ, the locked rules control:
no institution takes "of Mirza", and no Archive or Tribunal exists. Abad's direction, 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-10.*

## What Abad asked for

A new state, Mirza, with a dedicated military that studies the sciences, and a formidable name for that
army. Everything the state names is referred to as "of Mirza".

## Placement, ruled

Abad, 2026-10-10: "Mirza's crown signs the pact while its science army tries to buy the doctrine. This is
the richest option, and it gives Mirza an inner conflict from the start."

- **The crown** is the unnamed foreign sea nation already in canon: its envoy honored the Sovereign Ghost's
  fleet (`MCD-490`), and three years later signed the joint anti-slaving pact aboard *The Ledger*
  (`MCD-1040`).
- **The science army** stands behind the foreign sovereign's envoy who offered land, title and exclusive
  teaching rights to weaponize the Iron Bastard doctrine against civilian housing, and was refused
  (`MCD-1414`). That envoy spoke for a "royal corps"; the rule to be drafted has the army acting in the
  crown's name and against the crown's own course.
- Collision check: "Mirza" appears in no rule and no entry.

## To check when drafting

- The eras of `MCD-490`/`MCD-1040` (Sovereign Ghost) and `MCD-1414` (Iron Bastard, wave 31) must fit one
  timeline for the crown and the army.
- `MCD-1414` leaves the depot-engine mystery's culprit unresolved; whether the army is behind it stays
  Abad's call and is not assumed.
- Mirza sits within ordinary sailing reach of Cian and is not the fourth continent (`MCD-1893`).
- Its science works inside this world's physics (density, resonance, Drakma, acoustics, chemistry) and its
  tech level; approval-list item 35 is still open.

## Abad's choices, 2026-10-10 (not yet drafted as rules)

- **All three uses of "of Mirza" at once.** Every institution carries it (the Axiom of Mirza, the Archive of
  Mirza, the Tribunal of Mirza). Outsiders cannot tell the state from the army ("Mirza is marching").
  And it is an earned personal title, in Abad's words: "A person's official title that they MUST he
  adressed as if introduced in formal settings amd ceremonies and these people proudly introducethemselveswith
  this title: only proven scholar-soldiers may add it to their name ("Tessaly of Mirza")."
- **Army names.** Abad listed all four (the Axiom, the Theorem, the Fulcrum, the Prism of Mirza). Proposed
  reconciliation, to be confirmed: the Axiom of Mirza is the army (a soldier is an Axiom, recruits are
  Postulants, commanders are Theorems, a campaign is a proof, a won war is proven); the Fulcrum of Mirza
  (mechanics) and the Prism of Mirza (optics) are its two schools. "The Theorem of Mirza" stays a rank,
  not a body, so it does not collide with the commanders' title.
