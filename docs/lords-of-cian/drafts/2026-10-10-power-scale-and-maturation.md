### 1.2 Proposed threat tiers (P)

Many characters have no density figure. For them I use a **threat tier (PT)**, rated at best sustained output in that book. PT is a planning tool and does not add a stat to anyone.

| PT | Meaning | Density equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| PT0 / K | Non-combatant or key specialist. Counted as essential, not as a Mighty Hero | under 500x or not density-based |
| PT1 | Elite specialist | 500x to 1,000x |
| PT2 | Branded-class | 2,200x to 4,900x |
| PT3 | Proven-class | 5,000x to 16,000x |
| PT4 | Sovereign-class | 16,000x to 22,000x |
| PT5 | Apex, Titan, Spike or Warbody class | above 22,000x |

**Mighty Hero (P)** = PT3 or higher: someone who can stand against a Proven-class or Sin-Eater-class opponent on the day.

### 1.3 Reading notes

- Lauris's output climbs with fight length (MCD-209), so her rank depends on duration. Rankings use best sustained output over roughly thirty minutes. The Spike-holders (Kanja, Ozmund) are the opening-second exception: a connected Spike ends a fight at once (MCD-209).
- Kanja's Book 1 figures (MCD-227) are read as post-Awakening, since the Awakening falls inside Book 1 (CC-005, MCD-1901) and the Density Spike is post-Awakening only (CC-002). **The author should confirm this reading.**
- Kanja's Book 2 and Book 4 numbers are interpolations (P). Locked points are Book 1, Book 3 and Book 5.

## 4. Maturation schedule (P)

### 4.1 Design rules

- The pool is the eighteen crew members who are not yet at final form: fifteen Avatars plus Damu, Cooper and Valeria. Pyro, Anirak and the Triad are in it.
- Already at final form before Book 1: **Lauris, Ozmund** (growth is refinement, MCD-219), **Red Beard**, **Valen, Sephtis**, and the standard-biology members **Matar, Ezio, Fermand**, who sit outside any progression rule. Orlok and Legbara are allies and mature on their own paths.
- **The Triad are young.** They were juveniles when they imprinted on Pyro at his birth (MCD-270, MCD-040), and Pyro is 23 at Book 1 (MCD-270, MCD-1901). So before Book 1 they are already formidable (the SBD rates all three OMEGA-PRIME, SBD-020 to SBD-022, SBD-044) but not yet grown. No locked rule gives their growth stage or lifespan (section 9). Their full maturity is placed in Book 4 (P), once most of the crew has matured, which is when the author wants them to become lethal in their own specific way (section 6.4).
- A member realizes in the book whose story needs that capability. Non-density specialists realize early (Book 1 is a Branded-register book, MCD-144, and a stealth Heist suits non-density power). Combat members realize as the register climbs.
- No member waits for Book 5 for its own sake. Book 5 holds only the two whose locked beats sit there.

## 5. The Talisman as nurturer

The "amulet" is the Talisman of Mao, a shifting Living Drakma sphere (WC-014). The Gravity-Fetter pendant is a separate object, the Shackle's physical interface, and it is severed at the Awakening (CC-007). The Talisman itself persists.

### 5.1 Locked

| What | Source |
|---|---|
| Stage 1, the Sovereign Umbrella, has the Crew as recipient: Shadow-Pulse, Blight Immunity, Kinetic Buffer | MCD-060 |
| The Umbrella protects nineteen Avatars; it is a synchronization server for them | MCD-140, WC-014 |
| Stage 2 hardens the planet and micro-reinforces surfaces touching the Avatars; it has run autonomously for ~300 years | MCD-142, MCD-208 |
| Stage 3 has the Captain as recipient and holds the Governor's Shackle; Damu tracks its 99.9% drain | MCD-060, CC-065 |
| The world's hardening mirrors Lauris's body: adaptation by ordinary survival pressure, not engineering | MCD-143 |
| Broadcast power is one shared budget at 99.9% use, a 0.1% margin; the Awakening redistributes it; re-tuning latency is a hard floor; Book 5 has a 48-hour deficit | MCD-147, MCD-146, MCD-145 |
| Kanja does not know what it does; he learns it from Sephtis in Book 4 | MCD-142, MCD-208, MCD-1903, MCD-216 |
| The Awakening gives remote Drakma communication and environmental tuning | WC-005 |

So the **protecting** half is locked. The **cultivating** half is not: no locked rule says the Talisman matures anyone's biology. Only Stage 2 changes things over time, and that is the planet.

### 5.2 New, needs its own rule

"The Amulet nurtures and cultivates the crew" is a new fact. It needs a rule that does not contradict the above.

### 5.3 How it could work (P)

1. **The Umbrella is a trellis, not a roof.** The Kinetic Buffer (MCD-060) caps each Avatar's harm per event at a survivable fraction and leaves the remainder as load. The Avatars' biologies adapt to that load the way Lauris's lineage did (MCD-143). This reuses a locked mechanism for people.
2. **Why now, and why staggered.** Maturation needs accumulated high-density load. The combat register rises each book (MCD-144), so the load available rises with it. Different biologies cross the threshold at different books.
3. **A shared budget.** Maturation draws on the same power pool (MCD-147). The Awakening redistributes it (MCD-147), and releasing the Shackle ends Stage 3's steady draw (CC-007). I propose the freed margin funds crew maturation. The pool's limit is then the reason maturations come in groups and not all at once. **Risk:** Stage 3 demand may rise with Kanja's growing Spike, and CC-065's 99.9% and MCD-147's 99.9% may not be the same figure. A reviewer must confirm.
4. **Before Book 4 it is blind; after, it can be steered.** The Awakening gives Kanja remote Drakma communication (WC-005). He does not know about the Talisman until Book 4 (MCD-1903), so Books 1 to 3 maturations happen without his hand. In Books 4 and 5 he can deliberately tune, which suits the last members (Pyro, Anirak).
5. **Limits.** It matures existing biology only. It adds no new category (as draft MCD-1913 says of Stormreaver) and it keeps every locked limit: Cooper's ~4,500x (CC-103), Ironbane's bracer (CC-041), Varkul's ceiling unshown (SBD-044), Pyro's peak sealed (MCD-223).
6. **It does not nurture everyone.** Lauris, Damu, Cooper, Valeria, Matar, Ezio, Fermand and Red Beard are not among the nineteen (MCD-140). The author's phrase "nurturer of all the crew" is wider than the locked roster. Options: widen the roster, which conflicts with MCD-140; or let those members mature by other means. I recommend the second: Cooper already draws mass from Living Drakma (CC-103) which Stage 2 now diffuses through the crust (MCD-147), and Lauris is the proof of concept (MCD-143), already mature.

## 6. The Triad arc (P)

### 6.1 Heavy lifting early

Before the crew matures, only the Triad and a few veterans can hold both individual threats and groups. Book 1 puts the Triad in the second attempt, "Kanja, Pyro and the Triad unleashed", where the attackers' attack turns "disastrous for the attackers" (MCD-1907). In Book 2 Varkul and Varruk carry the naval work while Torian, Kaelen and Zora are still maturing.

### 6.2 The myth that they are the edge

Why enemies believe it, grounded in locked material:

- The SBD rates Varkul OMEGA-PRIME and "a living preservation clause" it cannot capture or bond (SBD-020, SBD-044). It rates Varruk OMEGA-PRIME (SBD-021) and Sorya OMEGA-PRIME conditional (SBD-022).
- The world regards Varkul as the strongest non-human and the three as one posture package (SBD-044).
- Those same files are unreliable: the Pyro Birth file is false (SBD-041), Varruk's suite is overclaimed (SBD-045), Sorya's confidence is overstated (CC-136), and the Oracle Conflict Map exists because reality keeps contradicting the SBD (MCD-1727, CULT-200).
- **Jealousy, propaganda and fear (P):** an enemy that loses to a crew of people prefers to say it lost to beasts. The Dhar-Kael are the last three of their clade (MCD-040), which makes them a ready legend.

The claim is half true, not false: Varkul really is formidable (SBD-044). The myth is about **cause**, not strength.

### 6.3 How it fades

| Book | What happens (P) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The myth already exists before Book 1, in the SBD's files and among those who have faced the crew. The second attempt is fought away from witnesses and its attackers stay unidentified (MCD-1907), so it feeds only the SBD's own internal reading: a failed cell and a broken animal team, recorded as beaten by the beasts |
| 2 | The Kingdom repels Baryon through Ozmund's command architecture (MCD-280); Kanja's naval engagement is crew-fought |
| 3 | The Kanja v. Ozmund fight and Kairo's stand put the principals on display (MCD-218). Varkul begins discovering the SBD's apparatus around him (SBD-044) |
| 4 | Varkul turns aggressive; Archon's dismantling of the SBD intersects (SBD-044, MCD-097). The SBD's own Oracle posture is "Tighten" (CULT-200) |
| 5 | With the crew realized, no enemy can credibly say the Triad is the edge |

### 6.4 The role they take once everyone is realized

Their lethality is **against organization**, not against single champions. The matured crew now handles the champions, which frees the Triad for what only they can do:

- **Varruk** reconstructs the whole field from fragments (CC-098) and breaks enemy coordination at ~200 m (ARS-413). The enemy's strongest tools are coordination: the Legacy Lattice's delegated commands (ARS-396), the Crownless Host (MCD-283), the Sin-Eaters' collective approach (POL-106), the Warbody's gaps (MCD-329).
- **Sorya** tastes broken vows and delivers evidence into a mind (CC-096). That counters deceit, such as Lady Vestige turning 200,000 legionaries on the western shore (MCD-1891). The Witness Shriek buys three seconds of chaos (ARS-414).
- **Varkul** collapses a group's discipline with the Harrow Presence (CC-094) and anchors a front.

On the Engine front they go with Pyro (bond, CC-095). The Vestige turn on the Tide Line shore is a possible use of Sorya, but that needs a detachment from Pyro, which CC-097 resists. The author decides.

## 8. Conflicts with the drafted rules, and what else needs a rule

### 8.1 MCD-1914 (draft) versus a staggered schedule

The draft says each progression "begins some time after Book 2 or 3" where no locked rule sets an earlier start, and that "at the end of Book 5 each of them reaches the maturity of their biology". It conflicts in four ways:

1. **Start.** Book 1 realizations (Sylas, Jax, Damu) and Book 2 realizations (Torian, Kaelen, Zora, Cooper) begin before "after Book 2 or 3". None is set earlier by a locked rule, so the draft forbids them.
2. **Finish.** "At the end of Book 5 each reaches maturity" contradicts members who finish in Books 1 to 4.
3. **Scope.** It sweeps in Lauris, who is already mature, and Anirak, whose Hot and White begin in Book 3 (ARS-442) but whose Flood State is Book 5 only (ARS-369).
4. **Stormreaver.** Draft MCD-1913 says he reaches maturity "by the end of Book 5", which accommodates finishing in Book 4. Only MCD-1914's "at the end of Book 5" conflicts.

**Replacement wording (P):**

> Every crew member with powers, meaning the nineteen Avatars of MCD-140, Lauris, and any other member of the Lords of Cian crew to whom a locked rule gives variant or other non-baseline biology (Damu CC-100, Cooper CC-103 and Valeria Korth CC-104, for example), follows their own specific progression, and those progressions are staggered across Books 1 to 5. Some members are at the full maturity of their biology before Book 1, some reach it in Books 1, 2, 3 or 4, and the last reach it in Book 5. None is held to Book 5 for its own sake. Where a locked rule sets a start or a finish, that rule controls. By the end of Book 5 every one of them has reached the maturity of their biology; that maturity is reached and is not necessarily shown at its maximum on the page. Standard-biology members (Matar, CC-067), the founding dock crew and Onyx (MCD-140) are outside this rule. Characters already made powerful, Lauris, Anirak and any others, remain powerful in every book. The per-member dates are the series' maturation schedule and are not fixed by this rule; it fixes no mechanism or number. The per-character cases already locked stand: [keep the existing list].
