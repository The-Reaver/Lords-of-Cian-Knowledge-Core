birthday and lasts just over 283 years, so the Pier fell late in his age 30. If the Pier had fallen on his
30th birthday, the Long Mask would be exactly 284 years; any later Pier date inside age 30 gives 283 years
and some days. The draft proposes 30 days before his 31st birthday: the Long Mask is then 283 years and 30
days, and the Accords, broken the day before, held 283 years and 29 days (365-day years, `VB-063`).

After the Pi-Awakening on Day 0 (CC-006, MCD-1901), Kanja chooses to reclaim the Trinity he surrendered to the L9 vault in the Karkosa Complex (MCD-246): Mafesto, the armor (ARS-010); Onyx of Oblivion, the sword (ARS-020); and Obsidian Malice, the war club (ARS-030). Reserved: no pre-Book-1 material may draft, foreshadow, or reference the murder, the breaking of the Accords, or the choice to reclaim the Trinity (ARS-437, CC-161, VB-062).

**MCD-1903** (category: talisman-of-mao). The aging misconception; Abad's ruling, 2026-10-05. He knows the Talisman only as a synchronization server with a density suppressor and does not know what it is doing to his body (MCD-142, MCD-208), and his crew shares his reading ('the old man doesn't get up,' MCD-1885). The belief is a misconception: the decline is the Shackle's hold on his body as it builds toward the Pi-Awakening, and his own Rexmar biology spends its span slowly (MCD-309). On Day 0 the pendant is severed and the Shackle released (CC-007, MCD-1901); the Pi-Awakening shows him that what he felt was the Shackle's weight, and that knowledge gives him confidence. Pre-Book-1 entries show the decline and his and the crew's reading of it. None confirms that he is aging out, and none states the truth, which arrives at the Pi-Awakening in Book 1; the physical preview already locked at MCD-262 and MCD-1885 stands.
