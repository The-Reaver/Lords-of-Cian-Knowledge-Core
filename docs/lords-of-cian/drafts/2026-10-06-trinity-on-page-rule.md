## (a) New rule, in full

Status on lock: `locked`. Source: "Abad's rulings in conversation, 2026-10-06; drafted from the Trinity on-page
census (research/trinity-on-page-census-2026-10-06.md)". Next free ID verified: the highest `VB-` ID in the ledger
is `VB-068`, so the rule is `VB-069`.

**Prefix.** `VB-` and not `MCD-`. The rule governs how entries are written and filed. The standing writer's-guide
rules `VB-062` (the two-account rule), `VB-063` (the Onyx voice standard and its gated check), `VB-066`
(series names), `VB-067` (account types) and `VB-068` (the account craft standard) all sit under `VB-` for that
reason, and `VB-069` extends that family. The world facts it leans on (`MCD-246`, `MCD-1902`, `MCD-1907`,
`ARS-437`) stay where they are. `MCD-1881` is a writer's-guide rule that sits under `MCD-`, so the choice is not
forced. The recommendation is `VB-`. Category `voice-bible`, the category of `VB-066`, `VB-067` and `VB-068`.

**VB-069** (category: voice-bible). The Trinity on-page bar and the Book 1 unlock tier. Abad's rulings, 2026-10-06: 'the trinity doesn't get used until after book one is published which means we may have to keep some of the Chronicles locked in the archive'; 'all recommendations, run the census' (answering a list whose item 7 recommended that pre-Book-1 entries may describe what the weapons do as legend, and whose item 8 was the census), an answer given before the census ran, accepting the recommendation that preceded the census; 'all yes on yiu 6 questions' ('yiu' reads 'you'; answering six questions whose sixth asked whether to draft Batch 379), which confirmed only that Batch 379 be drafted. (1) The bar. Before Book 1 is published, no entry open to readers shows the Trinity (Mafesto, ARS-010; Onyx of Oblivion, ARS-020; Obsidian Malice, ARS-030) in use on the page. A piece is in use when a scene the narration shows has it worn and engaged, wielded, discharged, or exercising one of its named powers. A piece that fails or acts only in part is in use, a one-clause use is in use, and Onyx speaking through the grip is in use. A piece that is carried, racked, left behind, worn dormant, or stated unused is outside the bar. The Talisman of Mao, the Aegis-Talisman, the Rexmar Machete and the post-Mafesto kit (ARS-344 through ARS-356) are outside it. (2) What an open entry may do. It may name the Trinity and say what it is. It may carry it through legend, rumor, aftermath, survivors' accounts and SBD Dossiers (VB-067), any of which may be wrong; a false account is locked only as said (VB-067). Legend may describe what the weapons do, at the level of effect and in the teller's own terms, within what the teller could know (VB-067) and within the Voice Bible's ban on textbook descriptions of how an ability works. A told or recorded account gives results and what its teller saw. It does not stage the use as a scene, whatever the frame, and a storytelling scene inside an entry (VB-068) is held to the same line. MCD-1902's reservation of the reclaiming stands, and so does ARS-437's reservation of Maro's death reaching the blade. (3) Onyx narrating. An entry narrated by Onyx of Oblivion (VB-020, VB-021, VB-026, VB-062, VB-063) counts as the Trinity appearing, with or without a piece in use. The Kanja-version track gates as a whole, its Long Mask accounts told after the Karkosa Heist included (VB-062), and the eight manuscript Chronicles I through VIII gate as a set, since they carry the Onyx codas and the Trinity's first deployments (MCD-232). (4) The Book 1 unlock tier. An entry that shows the Trinity in use, and an entry that, read in full, cannot be decided either way (MCD-729), are listed by name in the manifest and filed in the Book 1 unlock tier, as is every entry of the two sets in (3). A withdrawn entry is not filed. The tier keeps them in the archive's vault, and each becomes eligible to go live when Book 1 is published, subject to the archive's own review (the Phase 5 cascade of the archive game plan). The tier is a book placement, distinct from the archive's clearance levels and from the T0 to T4 content classification. The manifest at docs/lords-of-cian/archive/book1-unlock-trinity-manifest.md, with its .json copy, lists every entry in the tier with its rule ID, track and reason, and an entry is added to it in the batch that locks it. Entries in the tier are not rewritten to satisfy this rule. (5) New entries. An entry drafted after this rule's lock for release before Book 1 shows no Trinity in use. An entry that needs the Trinity in use, and every new Kanja-version entry, is written for the tier. (6) First on the page. In Book 1's present-day narrative the Trinity's first use is at the Karkosa Heist, where it is reclaimed (MCD-1902, MCD-070); where Book 1's own Rebellion chapters (VB-026) fall is left to Book 1. Its first full showing in combat in the present-day narrative is the second attempt (MCD-1907). (7) Standing. This rule binds archive publication and the content of new entries. Each entry in the tier keeps the standing it has: locked canon, or for the manuscript Chronicles I through VIII a reliable part of Kanja's Series, cited by number (VB-067, VB-068). Its events stay in force and its rule ID, where it has one, stays citable. A marquee kill dramatized in the tier (MCD-1881: the Pier, MCD-1880; Vellacourt, MCD-1884), and any kill dramatized there (the Black Trench, MCD-232), stay referenceable as legend in open entries under (2). (8) Era errors. An entry set after the Trinity's surrender at the Sovereign Pier (MCD-246, MCD-1880) and before the Karkosa Heist that shows the Trinity in use is an era error (MCD-246). It is corrected to the gear Kanja held at that age (MCD-246; ARS-344 through ARS-356), as Batch 314 and Batches 324 to 330 did, and is not filed in the tier. The Heartline and the Dark Ledger (ARS-437), which run through the seal, and Onyx's retrospective narration (VB-062) are not use in this sense.

### Cross-check of the rule against what is already locked

- **`VB-062`, the two-account rule: agrees, with one consequence.** `VB-062` says every significant event in
  Kanja's life also has an Onyx account, that the Kanja-version track is the home of those accounts, and that the
  Long Mask accounts are retrospective, told after the Karkosa Heist reunites Onyx with Kanja. The tier delivers
  exactly that order: the Onyx accounts reach readers when Book 1 is published. The consequence is that `VB-062`
  names the Alias entries as the "non-Onyx accounts", open to readers, and 173 of them now sit in the tier for
  showing the Trinity in use or, for one, not being readable either way. The Alias entries are still non-Onyx accounts; the tier follows from the Trinity on the
  page, and a narrator's presence sets only the two sets of rule item 3. `VB-062` takes a pointer note.
- **`VB-026`, the narrator handoff: agrees.** It applies to the Kanja-version track and Book 1. The track gates as a
  whole and the manuscript Chronicles carry the same handoff, so the handoff reaches readers in the tier. It is
  unchanged. `VB-026` takes a pointer note.
- **`MCD-1881`, the marquee-kill tiers: agrees, with a limit on visibility.** A marquee kill is "a named, fully
  dramatized kill or defeat that becomes legend and can be referenced in Book 1". Kanja's two existing marquee
  kills, the Sovereign Pier (`MCD-1880`, Kanja Chronicle IV) and Vellacourt (`MCD-1884`, Kanja Chronicle VI), are
  both dramatized only in gated entries, and the kills of the Black Trench (`MCD-232`) are dramatized only in
  manuscript Chronicle III, also gated. The marquee kills and the Black Trench kills stay referenceable in open
  entries as legend, rumor, aftermath, survivors' accounts and SBD Dossiers (rule item 7), and by rule ID. The
  tiering is unchanged. `MCD-1881` takes a pointer note.
  See flag 3 for what readers will and will not see before Book 1.
- **`MCD-1902`, the Accords broken and the Trinity reclaimed: agrees and extends.** `MCD-1902` already bars any
  entry set before Book 1 from dramatizing or foreshadowing the murder, Kanja's reading of it, or his choice to
  reclaim the Trinity. Rule item 2 repeats that bar for legend, so legend of the weapons cannot point to their
  return. Rule item 6 adds the Trinity's first use in Book 1's present-day narrative. `MCD-1902` takes a pointer note.
- **`MCD-1907`, the second attempt: extends.** It puts Kanja, Pyro and the Triad against the Quiet Hand with the
  Trinity reclaimed, after the Heist. Rule item 6 names it the first full showing in combat in the present-day narrative. It takes a pointer
  note. The Heist's own fighting stays open (flag 2).
- **`MCD-246`, the surrender at the Pier: agrees.** The Trinity is sealed at L9 from the Pier until the Heist. Rule
  item 8 turns that into the test for an era error, and corrects to the gear Kanja held at that age.
- **`MCD-1882`, the no-kill window, and `CC-161`, necessity kills: no interaction.** Kills inside gated entries
  stay as locked.
- **`ARS-437`, the Heartline and the Dark Ledger: agrees.** The Dark Ledger and Onyx's retrospective accounts appear
  only in Kanja Chronicles V to VII, all gated, and no open entry mentions the Heartline (census section 5). Rule
  item 8 says the Heartline, the Dark Ledger and Onyx's retrospective narration are not use in the rule's sense. The
  gate also keeps "Kanja does not know the bond survived" from reaching open readers early. `ARS-437`'s reservation
  of Maro's death reaching the blade is restated in rule item 2.
- **`VB-063`, the Onyx voice standard, and the Voice Bible's ban on textbook descriptions of how an ability
  works: constrains item 2.** The ban binds narration. Legend that explains a mechanism would break it, so the rule
  limits legend to "the level of effect and in the teller's own terms". That limit is drafted here; Abad's
  "legend may describe what the weapons do" did not state it (flag 5).
- **`VB-067`, account types, and `VB-068`, the account craft standard: agrees and constrains.** Dossiers and
  Hearsay may state what is false, and a false account is locked only as said; every teller is bounded by what they
  could know. A frame could be used to stage the use of the Trinity as a scene in a Comrade Account or a Dossier, so
  rule item 2 says a told or recorded account gives results and does not stage the use, whatever the frame (flag 6).
- **`MCD-144`, the openly seen register: no interaction.** Legend describes effect, and the Book 1 ceilings stay
  as locked.
- **The archive's unlock model: agrees, with a naming risk.** The archive game plan
  (`docs/lords-of-cian/lords-of-cian-archive-game-plan.md`, Phase 5, "The Book 1 unlock cascade") already marks
  Book 1 as published in `admin_settings`, makes Book-1-tagged vault items eligible to go live "subject to author
  review", and keeps Book 2 to 5 content vault-locked. The tier is that book placement. The archive also carries
  four clearance levels (0 to 3) and the Phase 2 era's T0 to T4 content classification, which the Brain Trust review
  of 2026-09-13 kept separate and warned could drift when a third "tier" appears. Rule item 4 says in words that the
  tier is a book placement and distinct from both (flag 1). The CLAUDE.md line about a "gamified five-tier
  reader-unlock model" for the Phase 2 era is the T0 to T4 classification and is untouched.
- **The Series Launch Protocol and the template: extends.** The template's gate step gains one bullet.
