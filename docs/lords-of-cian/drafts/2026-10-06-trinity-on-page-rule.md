## (a) New rule, in full

Status on lock: `locked`. Source: "Abad's rulings in conversation, 2026-10-06; drafted from the Trinity on-page
census (research/trinity-on-page-census-2026-10-06.md)". Next free ID verified: the highest `VB-` ID in the ledger

**Prefix.** `VB-` and not `MCD-`. The rule governs how entries are written and filed. The standing writer's-guide
rules `VB-062` (the two-account rule), `VB-063` (the Onyx voice standard and its gated check), `VB-066`
(series names), `VB-067` (account types) and `VB-068` (the account craft standard) all sit under `VB-` for that
reason, and `VB-069` extends that family. The world facts it leans on (`MCD-246`, `MCD-1902`, `MCD-1907`,
`ARS-437`) stay where they are. `MCD-1881` is a writer's-guide rule that sits under `MCD-`, so the choice is not
forced. The recommendation is `VB-`. Category `voice-bible`, the category of `VB-066`, `VB-067` and `VB-068`.

**VB-069** (category: voice-bible). The Trinity on-page bar and the Book 1 unlock tier. Abad's rulings, 2026-10-06: 'the trinity doesn't get used until after book one is published which means we may have to keep some of the Chronicles locked in the archive'; 'all recommendations, run the census' (answering a list whose item 7 was 'What pre-Book-1 entries may say. The alternative is that they only mention the weapons exist.' and whose item 8 was 'The census.'); 'all yes on yiu 6 questions' ('yiu' reads 'you'; question 6 was 'Batch 379. Should I draft the Trinity rule, the lock list and the two era fixes once 378 is locked?'). (1) The bar. Before Book 1 is published, no entry open to readers shows the Trinity (Mafesto, ARS-010; Onyx of Oblivion, ARS-020; Obsidian Malice, ARS-030) in use on the page. A piece is in use when a scene the narration shows has it worn and engaged, wielded, discharged, or exercising one of its named powers. A piece that fails or acts only in part is in use, a one-clause use is in use, and Onyx speaking through the grip is in use. A piece that is carried, racked, left behind, worn dormant, or stated unused is outside the bar. The Talisman of Mao, the Aegis-Talisman, the Rexmar Machete and the post-Mafesto kit (ARS-344 through ARS-356) are outside it. (2) What an open entry may do. It may name the Trinity and say what it is. It may carry it through legend, rumor, aftermath, survivors' accounts and SBD Dossiers (VB-067), any of which may be wrong; a false account is locked only as said (VB-067). Legend may describe what the weapons do, at the level of effect and in the teller's own terms, within what the teller could know (VB-067) and within the Voice Bible's ban on textbook descriptions of how an ability works. A told or recorded account gives results and what its teller saw. It does not stage the use as a scene, whatever the frame, and a storytelling scene inside an entry (VB-068) is held to the same line. MCD-1902's reservation of the reclaiming stands, and so does ARS-437's reservation of Maro's death reaching the blade. (3) Onyx narrating. An entry narrated by Onyx of Oblivion (VB-020, VB-021, VB-026, VB-062, VB-063) counts as the Trinity appearing, with or without a piece in use. The Kanja-version track gates as a whole, its Long Mask accounts told after the Karkosa Heist included (VB-062), and the eight manuscript Chronicles I through VIII gate as a set, since they carry the Onyx codas and the Trinity's first deployments (MCD-232). (4) The Book 1 unlock tier. An entry that shows the Trinity in use, and every entry of the two sets in (3), is filed in the Book 1 unlock tier. It stays in the archive's vault and becomes eligible to go live when Book 1 is published, subject to the archive's own review (the Phase 5 cascade of the archive game plan). The tier is a book placement, distinct from the archive's clearance levels and from the T0 to T4 content classification. The manifest at docs/lords-of-cian/archive/book1-unlock-trinity-manifest.md, with its .json copy, lists every entry in the tier with its rule ID, track and reason, and an entry is added to it in the batch that locks it. Entries in the tier are not rewritten to satisfy this rule. (5) New entries. An entry drafted after this rule's lock for release before Book 1 shows no Trinity in use. An entry that needs the Trinity in use, and every new Kanja-version entry, is written for the tier. (6) First on the page. The Trinity's first use on the page for a reader is the Karkosa Heist, where it is reclaimed (MCD-1902, MCD-070). Its first full showing in combat is the second attempt (MCD-1907). (7) Standing. This rule binds archive publication and the content of new entries. Each entry in the tier keeps the standing it has: locked canon, or for the manuscript Chronicles I through VIII a reliable part of Kanja's Series, cited by number (VB-067, VB-068). Its events stay in force and its rule ID, where it has one, stays citable. A marquee kill dramatized in the tier (MCD-1881: the Pier, MCD-1880; the Black Trench Titans, MCD-232) stays referenceable as legend in open entries under (2). (8) Era errors. An entry set between the Sovereign Pier and the Karkosa Heist that shows the Trinity in use is an era error (MCD-246). It is corrected to the post-Mafesto kit, as Batches 314 and 321 did, and is not filed in the tier.

### Cross-check of the rule against what is already locked

- **`VB-062`, the two-account rule: agrees, with one consequence.** `VB-062` says every significant event in
  Kanja's life also has an Onyx account, that the Kanja-version track is the home of those accounts, and that the
  Long Mask accounts are retrospective, told after the Karkosa Heist reunites Onyx with Kanja. The tier delivers
  exactly that order: the Onyx accounts reach readers when Book 1 is published. The consequence is that `VB-062`
  names the Alias entries as the "non-Onyx accounts", open to readers, and 175 of them now sit in the tier for
  showing the Trinity in use. The Alias entries are still non-Onyx accounts; the tier is set by the Trinity on the
  page, not by who narrates. `VB-062` takes a pointer note.
- **`VB-026`, the narrator handoff: agrees.** It applies to the Kanja-version track and Book 1. The track gates as a
  whole and the manuscript Chronicles carry the same handoff, so the handoff reaches readers in the tier. It is
  unchanged. `VB-026` takes a pointer note.
- **`MCD-1881`, the marquee-kill tiers: agrees, with a limit on visibility.** A marquee kill is "a named, fully
  dramatized kill or defeat that becomes legend and can be referenced in Book 1". Kanja's two existing marquee
  kills, the Sovereign Pier (`MCD-1880`, Kanja Chronicle IV) and Vellacourt (`MCD-1884`, Kanja Chronicle VI), are
  both dramatized only in gated entries, and the Black Trench Titans (`MCD-232`) are dramatized only in manuscript
  Chronicle III, also gated. They stay referenceable in open entries as legend, rumor, aftermath, survivors'
  accounts and SBD Dossiers (rule item 7), and by rule ID. The tiering is unchanged. `MCD-1881` takes a pointer note.
  See flag 3 for what readers will and will not see before Book 1.
- **`MCD-1902`, the Accords broken and the Trinity reclaimed: agrees and extends.** `MCD-1902` already bars any
  entry set before Book 1 from dramatizing or foreshadowing the murder, Kanja's reading of it, or his choice to
  reclaim the Trinity. Rule item 2 repeats that bar for legend, so legend of the weapons cannot point to their
  return. Rule item 6 adds the first use on the page for a reader. `MCD-1902` takes a pointer note.
- **`MCD-1907`, the second attempt: extends.** It puts Kanja, Pyro and the Triad against the Quiet Hand with the
  Trinity reclaimed, after the Heist. Rule item 6 names it the first full showing in combat. It takes a pointer
  note. The Heist's own fighting stays open (flag 2).
- **`MCD-246`, the surrender at the Pier: agrees.** The Trinity is sealed at L9 from the Pier until the Heist. Rule
  item 8 turns that into the test for an era error.
- **`MCD-1882`, the no-kill window, and `CC-161`, necessity kills: no interaction.** Kills inside gated entries
  stay as locked.
- **`ARS-437`, the Heartline and the Dark Ledger: agrees.** The Dark Ledger and Onyx's retrospective accounts appear
  only in Kanja Chronicles V to VII, all gated, and no open entry mentions the Heartline (census section 5). The
  gate also keeps "Kanja does not know the bond survived" from reaching open readers early. `ARS-437`'s reservation
  of Maro's death reaching the blade is restated in rule item 2.
- **`VB-063`, the Onyx voice standard, and the Voice Bible's ban on textbook descriptions of how an ability
  works: constrains item 2.** The ban binds narration. Legend that explains a mechanism would break it, so the rule
  limits legend to "the level of effect and in the teller's own terms". That limit is drafted here; Abad's
  "legend may describe what the weapons do" did not state it (flag 5).
- **`VB-067`, account types, and `VB-068`, the account craft standard: agrees and constrains.** Dossiers and
  Hearsay may state what is false, and a false account is locked only as said; every teller is bounded by what they
  could know. A frame could be used to stage the use of the Trinity as a scene in a Comrade Account or a Dossier, so
  rule item 2 says a told or recorded account gives results and does not stage the use, whatever the frame (flag 6).
- **`MCD-144`, the openly seen register: no interaction.** Legend describes effect, and the Book 1 ceilings stay
  as locked.
- **The archive's unlock model: agrees, with a naming risk.** The archive game plan
  (`docs/lords-of-cian/lords-of-cian-archive-game-plan.md`, Phase 5, "The Book 1 unlock cascade") already marks
  Book 1 as published in `admin_settings`, makes Book-1-tagged vault items eligible to go live "subject to author
  review", and keeps Book 2 to 5 content vault-locked. The tier is that book placement. The archive also carries
  four clearance levels (0 to 3) and the Phase 2 era's T0 to T4 content classification, which the Brain Trust review
  of 2026-09-13 kept separate and warned could drift when a third "tier" appears. Rule item 4 says in words that the
  tier is a book placement and distinct from both (flag 1). The CLAUDE.md line about a "gamified five-tier
  reader-unlock model" for the Phase 2 era is the T0 to T4 classification and is untouched.
- **The Series Launch Protocol and the template: extends.** The template's gate step gains one bullet.

## (e) Era errors

An era error is an entry that shows the Trinity in use after the surrender at the Pier (age 30, `MCD-246`) and
before the Karkosa Heist (`MCD-1902`). The census listed three candidates. Each was read in full and checked
against the ledger.

**1. `the-recapture-at-dusk` (`MCD-1238`, Scourge, age 172, V3 gear): confirmed.** Line 25 credits the guards'
collapse to "the Trinity and the loadout together". At age 172 the Trinity is sealed at L9 (`MCD-246`), and the
entry's own continuity note says "Onyx of Oblivion correctly absent per its L9 seal". The line is a leftover and a
sealed Trinity cannot produce a collapse. Fix: one phrase, "the Trinity and the loadout together produced" becomes
"the loadout produced". The entry names no other Trinity piece. The rule statement does not name the Trinity, so it
takes a correction note, not an amendment.

**2. `the-order-he-didnt-question` (`MCD-589`, Storm That Walks, Long Mask): confirmed.** Batch 321 swapped the
showcase to the Long-Mask-era kit (Forge-Coat, Ironfall Boots, Ironhand Gauntlets, Rexmar Machete), but two lines
kept the old framing: "before committing the Trinity to a timing window" and "before the Trinity moved". Fix: two
phrases, to "his kit" and "Kanja", the minimal swap in the style of Batches 314 and 321. The rule statement names the
Trinity only in its Batch 325 history, which is accurate, so it takes a correction note.

**3. `the-call-he-got-wrong` (`MCD-594`, Captain): not an error. It stays, and is listed in the manifest as
IN-USE.** Its header and rule state no era, so the era was established from the ledger and the file:
- The ledger places it as Captain Alias Chronicle XIX, wave 7 of that track (the statement says "wave 7 of ten").
  `docs/lords-of-cian/character-profiles/alias-captain.md` says the Trinity in its crew-protective register is
  confined to the Rebellion-era waves, "waves 1-19 only", and that wave 19 (`MCD-1004`) is set at the Rebellion's
  close. Everything the profile counts as post-surrender begins at wave 20 (`MCD-1056`, about five years after the
  surrender), and all twelve entries that Batches 314 and 321 corrected sit at `MCD-1058` or later.
- The other four Captain IN-USE entries (`MCD-396`, `MCD-429`, `MCD-461`, `MCD-507`) state "Rebellion era".
- The file shows Obsidian Malice's discharge and nothing from the post-Mafesto kit, and Kanja overrules Corren
  Halst on a tide read, a role fully in place in the Rebellion window. Batch 328's Captain review found three
  further post-surrender anachronisms in that track and did not flag this entry.
- Residual doubt, flagged (flag 7): the evidence is positional, and the track's era dating is uneven. The profile's
  era-span paragraph says wave 4 onward moves toward and then past the Rebellion's close; neighbouring entries
  (`MCD-597`, `MCD-614`) span generations; and the same Batch 328 review struck mistaken "Rebellion era" header tags
  from two earlier Captain entries (`MCD-463`, `MCD-508`). The reading rests on the wave number and on the absence of
  any post-Mafesto kit, not on a stated age.

**Fallback, not applied.** If Abad rules `MCD-594` post-surrender, the same minimal swap applies: "Obsidian
Malice's discharge blasting a secondary path through a collapsed wall" becomes the Ironhand Gauntlets' leverage,
with the Forge-Coat and Ironfall Boots grounding him, breaking a secondary path through the collapsed wall.

**A second scan.** All 172 IN-USE files were scanned for post-Mafesto kit, "Long Mask", "post-Mafesto", ages above

## (g) Flags for Abad's ruling

Numbered, each with a recommendation. Flags 1, 2 and 4 to 8 are the items that the CLAUDE.md paragraph lists as
covered only by his final approval. Flag 3 asks for no change now, and flags 9 and 10 are noted and need no ruling.

1. **The name "Book 1 unlock tier".** The archive already has clearance levels 0 to 3 and a T0 to T4 content
   classification, and the 2026-09-13 review recorded one four-versus-five-tier drift already. The rule defines the
   tier as a book placement (`published_books` in the archive plan) and keeps it distinct from both. *Recommend:
   keep Abad's term, with the definition in the rule.* The alternative is "Book 1 unlock set".
2. **"First full showing" and the Heist.** The rule calls the Heist the Trinity's first use on the page and
   `MCD-1907` its first full showing in combat. The Heist's own fighting is not locked, and the reclaiming itself is
   the first use. *Recommend: keep as drafted, and leave open whether the Trinity fights during the Heist.*
3. **What open readers see of Kanja's marquee kills.** The Pier, Vellacourt and the Black Trench Titans are all
   dramatized only in gated entries, so before Book 1 no Kanja marquee kill is dramatized in an open entry. They
   stay referenceable as legend. *Recommend: no change now. If Abad wants them visible, write them later as Hearsay
   or Adversary entries (`VB-067`) that tell the legend without staging the use.*
4. **Three readings of what "gates".** (a) "The 3 AMBIGUOUS entries" means the duel (`MCD-1425`), the twelve locks
   (`MCD-729`) and the well (`MCD-1144`); the other two of the census's five are corrected as era errors.
   (b) Kanja Chronicle II holds no Trinity term and no Onyx (it is set before the age-17 bonding) and gates only
   because the track does. (c) The three withdrawn Chronicles are not listed. *Recommend: all three as drafted. For
   (b), the alternative is to leave Chronicle II open; it gates with its track for a single reading order.*
5. **Legend "at the level of effect".** Abad accepted "legend may describe what the weapons do". The rule adds "at
   the level of effect and in the teller's own terms" so the Voice Bible's ban on textbook descriptions of how an
   ability works still holds. *Recommend: keep.*
6. **No staging inside a told account.** A Comrade Account, Dossier or Hearsay entry could narrate a Trinity fight
   in full and call it a retelling. The rule says a told or recorded account gives results and what its teller saw
   and does not stage the use, in any frame, storytelling scenes included. This is a drafted line, not an answer of
   Abad's. *Recommend: keep.*
7. **`the-call-he-got-wrong` and the unstated eras.** `MCD-594` is read as Rebellion era from its wave position, and
   its header states no era. The Captain profile's era-span paragraph is uneven (flagged in section e). 85 IN-USE
   entries state no era. *Recommend: confirm `MCD-594` as Rebellion era; if so, a later batch can add an era line to
   its header. If Abad rules it post-surrender, the fallback swap in section e applies.*
8. **New entries.** Rule item 5 says an entry that needs the Trinity in use, and every new Kanja-version entry, is
   written for the tier. This lets the Kanja-version track continue to be written and gated, which Abad's text
   implies and does not state. The unlock is eligibility when Book 1 is published, subject to the archive's review,
   which follows Phase 5 of the archive plan. *Recommend: keep.*
9. **Noted, no ruling: the manuscript Chronicles have no ledger rule.** The census found no rule that references any
   of the eight files, so the manifest's rule column reads "none (manuscript)", and the rule's "standing" clause says
   they keep the standing the ledger already gives them (part of Kanja's Series, reliable and cited by number,
   `VB-067`, `VB-068`). A rule per manuscript Chronicle is a separate decision.
10. **Noted, no ruling: pending sync.** The archive repo's Phase 5 plan should name the manifest as its Chronicle
    source for the Book 1 cascade. Canon sessions do not build the archive app, so this is owed, not done. The
    mirrored Voice Bible and Voice Progression Sheet are untouched. The headers of the other three manuscript
    Chronicles (III, VI, VIII) and of the seven Kanja-version entries carry no gate pointer; the manifest is the
    pointer, and adding one to each is optional.
