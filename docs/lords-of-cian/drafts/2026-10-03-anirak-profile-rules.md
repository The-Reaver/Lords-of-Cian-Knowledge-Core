# Rule text: Anirak's Psychological Profile recommendations

*Locking draft, 2026-10-03. Abad approved the profile and its four recommendations ("approved"),
then said "lock it." This file holds the exact rule text, run through the Connective-Tissue Gate
before the merge.*

---

**CC-163 (new, character-crew).** Anirak's origin. She is pressure-born (`MCD-1896`): born free in a
pressure-born deep-water community, she was sold into the Maw circuit at twelve against a debt, which
began her Maw-raised years (`MCD-251`). She is Branded, as every Maw fighter is (`MCD-080`), and she
was never Cestari: the Cestari are Farm-bred and branded within 72 hours of birth (`MAW-010`,
`MAW-070`, `MAW-071`). That keeps her distinct from Red Beard while giving her the same enemy. The
origin fits her underwater superiority (`CC-113`), her ambient sea-sense (`ARS-367`), and her ease on
the Deep Road (`MCD-1896`). Who owed the debt, whether her community was of the Vael Kem (`MCD-041`),
and what became of it all stay open. Her Chain Harbor unit, the four-person unit of `MCD-251`, is
herself with Edda, Hamund, and Odile (`MCD-1890`).

**CC-164 (new, character-crew).** Anirak's kill register, from Chain Harbor (`MCD-251`, `MCD-1883`)
onward. Her Maw years are not governed by this rule. From Chain Harbor on, every kill by her own hand
is a necessity kill: the person is an active, immediate threat to life in that moment. Where there is
time she gives terms once, and anyone who sits or runs is left untouched, the standard she watched
kept in the Chain Harbor yard (`MCD-1883`). She never executes a surrendering or fleeing person and
never kills for deterrence. "No clean kills -- total engagement is doctrine" (`ARS-373`) describes how
she fights, not whom she kills. Foreclosure's sweep (`ARS-441`) disables the men the Voice has
dropped; it does not kill men already down. Flood State is the one exception to terms: on the Book 5
Tide Line her techniques run on instinct alone (`ARS-369`, `ARS-444`), so no terms are possible. Ren's
clearing there (`ARS-369`) is the safeguard. On the shore the men inside her storm are deceived,
turned legionaries (`MCD-1891`), and what breaking the turn costs stays reserved (`MCD-1891`). This
parallels Kanja's `CC-161` and Lauris's `CC-162` without merging with either; `CC-161` and `MCD-1882`
govern only Kanja's own hand.
