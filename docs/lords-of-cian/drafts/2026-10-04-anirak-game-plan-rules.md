**VB-065 (new, voice-bible).** Anirak's Character Chronicles: the voice standard. Close-third on
Anirak, with no designated narrator. Every character who looks at her is subject to the Siren's pull
(`CC-112`), so the narration rides behind her own eyes and holds her doubt about whether the
attention around her is real without resolving it. The Voice Bible's Pillars, hard constraints, POV
cadence, and exclusion list bind every entry. Three rulings apply to this track. (1) Articles and
pronouns are kept. Articles follow `VB-063` ruling (2); the telegraphic effect comes from short
sentences and fragments. (2) Her threat assessments keep the cadence's binary verdict in her own plain terms,
without the words Iron and Rust, on the Red Beard template's precedent. (3) The word "spike" appears
in no prose (`VB-063` ruling (3)). Her biology is momentum, so she is rendered through mass in
motion; the Karesian prose rule applies to the Karesian or high-density characters around her. Her
dialogue states facts and costs, on the model of her two lines in Chronicle V (`MCD-1883`). The Siren
is shown as faces turning and staying turned, and the Voice is felt in the body; neither is named as
a power in narration. Through the Long Mask, Kanja is "the Captain" and speaks in the Scourge
register (Voice Bible Section IV). Onyx is sealed through the Long Mask, so no Onyx passage appears.

**Dropped from the approved recommendations: the `CC-101` Sephtis amendment.** The Game Plan's
option (a) assumed Sephtis was away from the crew in disguise when Ren was recruited (`MCD-982`).
Independent review found that premise false: locked canon has Sephtis openly with the crew from
Lauris's arrival through the present day (`MCD-195`, `MCD-206`, `MCD-212`, `MCD-1565`, `CC-110`).
`CC-101`'s bare "recruited by Sephtis" already fits, so no amendment is needed. The real conflict,
`MCD-982`'s staged withdrawal against Sephtis's open presence with the crew, predates this Game Plan
and goes to Abad's approval list.
