# VB-068 draft and account-craft naming table

*Draft for Abad's approval, 2026-10-04. Nothing here is locked. It is the companion to
`docs/lords-of-cian/voice/account-craft-standard.md`, which marks every vocabulary gap as
**[NAME NEEDED: key]**. Part (b) answers each key. Part (c) lists conflicts with locked canon,
flagged only and not resolved.*

## (a) Proposed rule

**VB-068**. The account craft standard. From this rule's lock onward, every new entry outside a
Series (each Comrade Account, Adversary Account, Dossier, Hearsay entry, and entry of account type
none, `VB-067`) is drafted to the standard at docs/lords-of-cian/voice/account-craft-standard.md,
and so is every new scene inside a Series entry in which a character tells a story aloud. Each new
entry's header carries its account type and its teller as `VB-067` sets out. Entries locked before
`VB-067` are not relabeled. Before drafting a Comrade Account, an Adversary Account, or a Hearsay
entry, the writer fixes its teller state (informed, uninformed, mistaken, or deliberately lying),
its named listener, and its setting module. The page shows the teller state and the listener, and
no label names either. Both are none for an entry of account type none, and neither applies to a
Dossier. A storytelling scene inside a Series entry fixes its teller state, its listener, and its
setting only, since account types govern entries and never scenes. A lie or a misreading is shown
through the pattern of the telling or through contradiction with outside fact or locked canon; an
honest error through contradiction alone. None of them is ever shown through gaze, fidgeting, or
any other body-language tell. Honest drift runs in memory's directions (rounder, larger, more
famous, more vivid). A liar's drift runs toward his own defense. A Hearsay entry makes its number of
removes legible. No account states a fact about an institution that no locked rule holds; such a
fact is drafted as its own rule. The standard governs how an account is told. What an account may
say is set by `VB-067` (its reliability rules, its Comrade Account bounds, its knowledge bound, its
trace rule, and its reserved-rule bar), by this rule's institution bar, and by the Voice Bible's
Pillars, hard constraints, exclusion list, and track voice rulings (`VB-063`, `VB-064`, `VB-065`,
and `CC-034` with `VB-024` for the Exhibits). Where the standard and any of these differ, the
locked rule controls. The standard's research evidence grades are craft guidance and assert
nothing in-world. Its proposed venue and vocabulary names are usable only after each is locked by
its own rule. Its section 7 checklist is part of the Connective-Tissue Gate's independent review
for every entry and scene it covers. A change to the standard needs Abad's approval in his own
words, like a change to any rule. Extends `VB-067`; qualifies nothing in it.

## (b) Naming table

**How each name was checked** (2026-10-04, against `ledger_version` 37.6, 2,693 rules).
- **L and C counts.** Each candidate was matched case-insensitively on word boundaries against
  every rule statement in `canon-ledger.json` and every file under `docs/lords-of-cian/chronicles/`
  (subfolders included). A space in a multi-word candidate matches a space or a hyphen; a hyphen in
  a hyphenated candidate matches only a hyphen. **L** counts matching rule statements. **C** counts
  matching Chronicle files.
- **Near-collisions.** A near-collision is a capitalized name in either corpus that shares the
  candidate's first letter and sits within edit distance 2 of it (for a multi-word candidate, of
  any one of its words). Names counted are in-world proper names and words inside a locked proper
  name (an ability, an institution, an artifact). For words of four letters or fewer, distance 2
  matches most short names, so only one-edit matches are named there. Real-world homage anchors
  named inside `PH2-` rules (Raby, Staten, Sandino and the like) are not in-world names and are not
  listed, except where an earlier draft relied on one.
- **Also noted.** Overlaps the method does not catch (a different first letter, a name contained
  inside the candidate, a sound-alike, a real place) are listed under "Also noted" or "Real place."
- "Generic" means every hit is the ordinary English word, never a proper name.
- "Clean" means L0, C0, and no near-collision by the method.

**Constraints applied.**
- **Mainline.** Germanic or industrial register. The words listed as taken or overloaded in
  `00-canon-venue-inventory.md` §10 are avoided: Ledger, Table, Hollow, Crucible, Exchange,
  Compact, Quiet, Gallery, Hall, Mirror, Knot, Vise, Rest, Slab, Pit, Circuit, Cut, Standard,
  Register, Stone, and the Anchor and Anvil. Also avoided:
  - the banned words of `VB-013` and `VB-050`
  - "Cian"
  - Iron and Rust, Onyx's verdict words
  - Crow (the Crow King)
  - Long (Long Mask, Long Walk, Long Currents, Long Pursuit, Long Correction, Long Tenure)
  - Vigil (the Vigil Standard)
  - Deposition (`WC-020`)
  - Cinder (Cinderhilt, `MCD-1729`)
  - Marker (the Marker Rebellion)
  - Shape (Shaper)
- **Homage World** (`PH2-034`). Invented names drawn from real Taíno, Yoruba, Akan, Kikongo,
  Nahuatl, or Swahili vocabulary. No word-initial clusters English doesn't use (Mb-, Mw-, Ml-,
  Ms-, Mk-, Nz-, Ng-), no Spanish (`PH2-061`'s colonial-name reading), and no real-world proper
  noun used as an in-world name. Every row flags real-place homonyms, and overlaps with existing
  PH2 names (Guaní/Guanín, Kasa/Kasi, Tunji/Tunde are the standing examples). Names already taken
  in PH2 were rejected outright:
  - Kesho, Bohio, and Ile (`PH2-` supporting casts)
  - Kiti (a territory)
  - Batey (the city)
  - Kalunga (`MCD-095`)
- **Homage street terms.** These are street vocabulary, not replacements. Plain English nouns (bar,
  barbershop, runner, corner) stay usable in narration; the terms below are what the people
  themselves say.

## (c) Conflicts with locked canon noticed while reading (flag only)

Re-checked against the ledger after Batch 374, which already fixed the banned Maw and venue
terminology, House Galthorn's collision with Onyx's Black Ledger (now "the Black Dot," `MAW-031`,
`MAW-136`), `ASH-007`/`ASH-048`'s tech level (now convective heat-galleries), and `MCD-825`'s
antithesis. Those items are dropped here. The rest remain open.

1. **Funeral practice.** The Chronicle locked at `MCD-998` describes the crew's deck service as using
   "no formal rites borrowed from any Trust or Kingdom." The Chronicle locked at `MCD-1073` has
   Kanja speak "the words of the rite himself" at sea. The two are compatible if those words are the
   crew's own, but any named general custom (N11) must not be read into either.
2. **Garren Hask's mortality** (already on CLAUDE.md's running tally). He is locked dying around
   Kanja-age 50-55 on the Captain track and shown alive through 300+ elsewhere. No Comrade Account
   with Hask as teller can pass the knowledge-window check until Abad rules.
3. **The Anchor and Anvil** is the most-used named mainline venue (manuscript Chronicles III, VI,
   VIII; the header of Kanja Chronicle I) and has no ledger rule. A lock would let the standard cite
   it by rule.
4. **The Voice Bible against the research on greetings.** `VB-004` and `VB-050` ban phatic talk.
   The research documents greeting rituals as real openers (06 §2.1, §2.2). The standard resolves
   this by carrying openings in action or in lines that carry information (R0.2). Please confirm.
5. **Spanish in homage-World speech.** In the Chronicle locked at `MCD-337`, dockhands call Kanja
   *el que no necesita nada*, while `PH2-061` frames Spanish as the imposed colonial tongue and
   `PH2-034` bars real-world-derived in-world names. The standard allows Spanish as spoken phrase
   and never as an invented name. Please confirm.
6. **Research examples that must not be imported.** These are not canon, but they would break
   canon if copied:
