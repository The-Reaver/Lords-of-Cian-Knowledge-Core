*Draft for Abad's approval, 2026-10-04. Nothing here is locked. It is the companion to
`docs/lords-of-cian/voice/account-craft-standard.md`, which marks every vocabulary gap as
**[NAME NEEDED: key]**. Part (b) answers each key. Part (c) lists conflicts with locked canon,

## (a) Proposed rule

**VB-068**. The account craft standard. Every entry outside a Series is told to the standard at
docs/lords-of-cian/voice/account-craft-standard.md. That covers every Comrade Account, Adversary
Account, Dossier, Hearsay entry, and entry of account type none (`VB-067`). So is every scene inside
a Series entry in which a character tells a story aloud. Before drafting, the writer fixes four
things: the account type, the teller's state (informed, uninformed, mistaken, or deliberately
lying), the named listener, and the setting module, and the page carries each one. A lie, an error,
or a misreading is shown through the pattern of the telling and through contradiction with outside
fact or locked canon, never through gaze, fidgeting, or any other body-language tell. Honest drift
runs in memory's directions (rounder, larger, more famous, more vivid). A liar's drift runs toward
his own defense, and a Hearsay entry makes its number of removes legible. The standard governs how
an account is told, never what it may say. It sits inside `VB-067`'s reliability rules, its
knowledge bound, its trace rule, its reserved-rule bar, and its bar on new institutional facts, and
inside the Voice Bible's Pillars, hard constraints, exclusion list, and track voice rulings
(`VB-063`, `VB-064`, `VB-065`). Where the standard and any of these differ, the locked rule
controls. Its research evidence grades are craft guidance, not in-world fact. Its proposed venue
and vocabulary names are usable only after each is locked by its own rule. Its section 7 checklist
is part of the Connective-Tissue Gate's independent review for every account it covers. A change
to the standard needs Abad's approval in his own words, like a change to any rule. Extends
`VB-067`; qualifies nothing in it.

## (b) Naming table

**How each name was checked.** Each candidate was grepped, case-insensitive and on word boundaries,
against `canon-ledger.json` and every file under `docs/lords-of-cian/chronicles/` (subfolders
included). Each was also checked for near-collision with every capitalized word in both corpora:
an edit distance of 1 for names of four letters or fewer, and 2 for longer names. Results are given
as **L** (ledger hits) and **C** (Chronicle hits).

- "Generic" means the hits are the ordinary English word, never a proper name.
- "Clean" means zero hits and no near-collision.

**Constraints applied.**
- **Mainline.** Germanic or industrial register. The words listed as taken or overloaded in
  `00-canon-venue-inventory.md` §10 are avoided: Ledger, Table, Hollow, Crucible, Exchange,
  Compact, Quiet, Gallery, Hall, Mirror, Knot, Vise, Rest, Slab, Pit, Circuit, Cut, Standard,
  Register, Stone, and the Anchor and Anvil. Also avoided:
  - the banned words of `VB-013` and `VB-050`
  - "Cian"
  - Iron and Rust, Onyx's verdict words
  - Crow (the Crow King)
  - Long (Long Mask, Long Walk, Long Currents, Long Pursuit, Long Correction, Long Tenure)
  - Vigil (the Vigil Standard)
  - Deposition (`WC-020`)
  - Cinder (Cinderhilt, `MCD-1729`)
  - Marker (the Marker Rebellion)
  - Shape (Shaper)
- **Homage World** (`PH2-034`). Invented names drawn from real Taíno, Yoruba, Akan, Kikongo,
  Nahuatl, or Swahili vocabulary. No word-initial clusters English doesn't use (Mb-, Mw-, Ml-,
  Ms-, Mk-, Nz-, Ng-), no Spanish (`PH2-061`'s colonial-name reading), and no real-world proper
  noun used as an in-world name. Two kinds of overlap are flagged where known: with real
  places, and with existing PH2 names (Guaní/Guanín, Kasa/Kasi, Tunji/Tunde are the standing
  examples). Names already taken in PH2 were rejected outright:
  - Kesho, Bohio, and Ile (`PH2-` supporting casts)
  - Kiti (a territory)
  - Batey (the city)
  - Kalunga (`MCD-095`)
- **Homage street terms.** These are street vocabulary, not replacements. Plain English nouns (bar,
  barbershop, runner, corner) stay usable in narration; the terms below are what the people
  themselves say.

## (c) Conflicts with locked canon noticed while reading (flag only)

1. **Banned terminology in locked rule text.** These rules use "arena" or "gladiator(ial)" against
   `VB-013` and `VB-050`:
   - `CC-021` ("gladiatorial economy")
   - `CC-085` ("as a gladiator")
   - `CC-090` ("Accession Games arena")
   - `MCD-234` ("the arena's own load-bearing foundation")
   - `WGD-001`, `WGD-006`, `WGD-008` ("arena and betting circles," "arena stock," "shifting
     arenas")
   - Not on the inventory's list: `ASH-046` ("trial arenas") and `CC-155` ("shifting arenas").
2. **Antithesis in locked prose.** `MCD-825` ("The Meal He Ate as No One"), the standard's own
   tavern precedent, carries a balanced construction: "none of it true, exactly, and none of it
   entirely a lie either." That breaks `VB-010`.
3. **A "Black Ledger" name collision.** `MAW-136` gives House Galthorn the nickname "the Black
   Ledger," the same name as Onyx's Black Ledger power (`ARS-020`, `VB-062`, `ARS-437`). Batch 342
   renamed an Ashkeel register away from this exact collision, and this one was missed.
4. **Tech level.** `ASH-007` and `ASH-048` describe electrical supercritical-CO2 turbines against
   the world's pre-industrial tech level (`WC-012`, `WC-013`; the inventory's flag). The standard
   tells drafters to lean on neither.
5. **Funeral practice.** `MCD-998` locks the crew's deck service as using "no formal rites borrowed
   from any Trust or Kingdom." `MCD-1073` has Kanja speak "the words of the rite himself" at sea.
   The two are compatible if those words are the crew's own, but any named general custom (N11)
   must not be read into either.
6. **A Dossier scope gap.** `VB-067` type 4 names only the SBD and the Trust on Cian. Many other
   locked institutions keep records: the Maw, the Weregildd, Ashkeel, the cults. Listed above as a
   [RULE NEEDED] item.
7. **Garren Hask's mortality** (already on CLAUDE.md's running tally). He is locked dying around
   Kanja-age 50-55 on the Captain track and shown alive through 300+ elsewhere. No Comrade Account
   with Hask as teller can pass the knowledge-window check until Abad rules.
8. **The Anchor and Anvil** is the most-used named mainline venue (manuscript Chronicles III, VI,
   VIII; the header of Kanja Chronicle I) and has no ledger rule. A lock would let the standard cite
   it by rule.
9. **The Voice Bible against the research on greetings.** `VB-004` and `VB-050` ban phatic talk.
   The research documents greeting rituals as real openers (06 §2.1, §2.2). The standard resolves
   this by carrying openings in action or in lines that carry information (R0.2). Please confirm.
10. **Spanish in homage-World speech.** `MCD-337` has dockhands call Kanja *el que no necesita nada*,
    while `PH2-061` frames Spanish as the imposed colonial tongue and `PH2-034` bars Spanish-derived
    in-world names. The standard allows Spanish as spoken phrase and never as an invented name.
    Please confirm.
11. **Research examples that must not be imported.** These are not canon, but they would break
    canon if copied:
    - 03's cross-setting note mentions phone calls in the homage World (`PH2-049` bans electronic
      media).
    - 04 and 05 use a subway train, a radio, and a sawed-off gun for "a 1970s city" (`PH2-049`: no
      engines, broadcast, or firearms).
    - 09's gossip marker "God knows I don't like to say it" uses a banned word (`VB-050`).

    The standard avoids all of them.
12. **"Sitting" and "wake" as naming hazards.** Not conflicts, but traps for any future venue or
    custom name:
    - To sit is to yield in Anirak's track (`CC-164`, `MCD-1883`).
    - "Wake" already names an adult Ashkeel ritual (`ASH-028`), a ship's wake as a battle name
      (the False Dragon's Wake), and two abilities (`ARS-395`, `SBD-045`).
