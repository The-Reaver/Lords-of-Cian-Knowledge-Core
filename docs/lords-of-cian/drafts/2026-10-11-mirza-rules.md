# Draft: Mirza, the state, its army, and the writing rule

*Draft for Abad's approval, 2026-10-11. Nothing here is locked. Follows the planning notes at
`docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-10-mirza.md`. The new prefix is `MRZ-` (a new institution gets its own
prefix, as `ASH-` did for Ashkeel). The writing rule is `VB-070`, the next unused Voice Bible number (the
ledger's highest is `VB-069`; there is no `MRZ-` rule yet). All rules below are written to lock with
`"status": "locked"`, source `"Original invention, chat-drafted 2026-10-10, no source document"`. The
Connective-Tissue Gate still owes its independent review before this is presented as final.*

## 1. Abad's rulings this draft rests on

- Mirza is a state, with a dedicated military that studies the sciences.
- Placement: "Mirza's crown signs the pact while its science army tries to buy the doctrine." The crown is
  the unnamed foreign sea nation of `MCD-490` and `MCD-1040`. The science army stands behind the foreign
  sovereign's envoy of `MCD-1414`, acting in the crown's name against the crown's own course.
- The army is the Axiom. A soldier is "an Axiom"; recruits are Postulants; commanders are Theorems; a
  campaign is "a proof"; a won war is "proven". Two schools: the Fulcrum (mechanics: they move armies by
  finding the one point that tips them) and the Prism (optics and light science: they split a problem into
  its parts before they strike). "Theorem" is a rank only, not a body.
- "of Mirza" is exclusive to one use outside treaties and formal documents: an earned personal title. Only
  proven scholar-soldiers may add it to their name. They must be addressed by it in formal settings and
  ceremonies, and they introduce themselves with it proudly. "Axiom of Mirza" appears only in treaties and
  formal documents; everyday speech says "the Axiom". The state is simply "Mirza". The schools are "the
  Fulcrum" and "the Prism", no suffix.
- "Mirza is marching" is outsiders' speech only, used sparingly in dialogue, never an official name: on
  Cian, frightened people cannot tell the state from its army.
- Writing rule: no comic use of the title or the name in prose; enemies may mock it in dialogue, the prose
  never does; the comic registers `VB-004` as amended allows in told accounts do not extend to the title or
  the name.

## 2. Proposed rules

### MRZ-001

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** Mirza is a sovereign state under a crown. It is the foreign sea nation of `MCD-490` and
  `MCD-1040` (called the Foreign Sea nation in `MCD-1893`): its envoy offered the Sovereign Ghost of the
  Great Sea's fleet safe harbor in its waters when Kanja was 21 (`MCD-490`, Ghost Harbor, Rebellion era), and
  its envoy signed the joint anti-slaving pact aboard *The Ledger* three years later, when Kanja was 24
  (`MCD-1040`). Both entries left the nation unnamed and stand as written; this rule gives it a name. The
  state is called simply "Mirza". The crown named in this rule is the office, and any number of sovereigns
  may hold it across the entries that follow; one sovereign holding it from `MCD-490` through `MCD-1414` is
  possible and is not required.

### MRZ-002

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** Mirza lies within the waters reachable from Cian by ordinary sailing (`MCD-1893`). It is not
  the fourth continent of `MCD-1893`, it is not one of the eleven regions of `GEO-002`, and it holds no cell
  on the Atlas grid (`GEO-003`). It is not one of the five sovereign nations of the Shattered Kingdoms
  (`WC-012`, `POL-108`). Its geography is left open: its coasts, islands, cities, climate and resources, and
  whether Living Drakma occurs there (`WC-013`, `MCD-1895`), are undecided, and no entry may fix any of them
  until a rule does.

### MRZ-003

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom is Mirza's dedicated military, an army whose soldiers study the sciences as part of
  soldiering. A soldier of the Axiom is called an Axiom, and the body is called the Axiom. In treaties and
  formal documents the body is styled "the Axiom of Mirza", and that style appears only there. Everyday
  speech says "the Axiom". Whether the Axiom is Mirza's only armed force, and whether the crown's sea patrols
  of `MCD-1040` belong to it, is left open.

### MRZ-004

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom's own words for its affairs: a recruit is a Postulant; a commander holds the rank
  of Theorem, a rank only, and no body is called the Theorem; a campaign is called "a proof"; a war the Axiom
  has won is called "proven". The Axiom uses these words of itself. The word "proven" in other mouths and in
  narration keeps its ordinary meaning.

### MRZ-005

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom has two schools. The Fulcrum is the school of mechanics: its Axioms move armies by
  finding the one point that tips them. The Prism is the school of optics and light science: its Axioms split
  a problem into its parts before they strike. The schools are called "the Fulcrum" and "the Prism", with no
  suffix and never "of Mirza". Whether every Axiom belongs to one of the two schools is left open.

### MRZ-006

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The crown's own course toward other powers is the one shown in `MCD-490` and `MCD-1040`:
  safe harbor offered in good faith when Kanja was 21; then, when he was 24, a signed bilateral pact of joint
  patrols against slaving routes, a mutual distress-signal system, one shared ledger kept by Garren Hask for
  both powers' patrols, and a clause letting either side suspend the pact on a season's notice. Whether the
  pact still stands in later years, including after the Sovereign Pier (`MCD-246`), is left open.

### MRZ-007

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The royal corps of `MCD-1414` is the Axiom, and the foreign crown and sovereign of that
  entry are Mirza's. The offer there, of land, title and exclusive teaching rights for the Iron Bastard's
  doctrine of diagnostic listening, went out in the crown's name with the Axiom behind it, and the Axiom's
  purpose in it, the breaking of occupied civilian housing in territories that resist annexation, runs
  against the course the crown set in `MCD-1040` (`MRZ-006`). The envoy of `MCD-1414`, a man, is a different
  person from the envoy of `MCD-1040`, and stays unnamed. Kanja refused the offer, and the Axiom did not
  obtain the doctrine from him (`MCD-1414`). `MCD-1414` is set in the Long Mask, in a year the entry does not
  fix, years after `MCD-1040` and possibly many decades after it; the sovereign who ruled when it was made
  may be a different holder of the crown from the sovereign of `MCD-1040`. Whether that sovereign knew of the
  offer or approved it, whether Mirza's crown knows that the Iron Bastard and the Sovereign Ghost's captain
  are one man, and whether the pact stood when the offer was made, are left open.

### MRZ-008

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The depot-engine mystery (`MCD-1308`, `MCD-1412`) keeps its culprit unassigned. No `MRZ-`
  rule places the Axiom, the Fulcrum, the Prism or the crown behind the Crawler design of `MCD-1308` and
  `MCD-1412`, and none rules them out. The envoy of `MCD-1414` cites the mystery as leverage, which shows
  only that the Axiom had read of it.

### MRZ-009

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** "Of Mirza" is an earned personal title. Outside treaties and formal documents, the phrase
  has this one use. A proven scholar-soldier of the Axiom may add it after their name, in the form "[name] of
  Mirza", and nobody else may. The bearer must be addressed by the full title in formal settings and in
  ceremonies, and introduces themselves with it, proudly. No other person, office, school or place takes "of
  Mirza" in speech. The Axiom's own style in treaties and formal documents is set by `MRZ-003`. What counts
  as proven for the title is left open. No bearer is named by this rule.

### MRZ-010

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** "Mirza is marching" is outsiders' speech for the Axiom on campaign. People outside Mirza use
  it sparingly, and only in dialogue. It is never an official name or phrase of Mirza and no Mirzan source
  uses it. On Cian, frightened people cannot tell the state from its army, and the phrase shows that.

### MRZ-011

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom's science works inside this world's physics: density, resonance, Drakma,
  acoustics and chemistry. It fields no firearms; the only small arms on Cian are the Sealbound Directorate's
  (`ARS-426`). Any instrument, engine or device an entry gives it is held within the mainline technology
  level that Abad's ruling on that open question sets (approval-list item 35); until that ruling, no entry
  gives it a device beyond those the locked rules already place on Cian. The Axiom's study of Drakma implies
  nothing about a Drakma deposit in Mirza (`MRZ-002`).

### VB-070

- **category:** `voice-bible`
- **statement:** The name and the title of Mirza are never comic in prose. No narration, in any entry or
  track, uses the name "Mirza" or the personal title "of Mirza" (`MRZ-009`) for a joke, a pun or a laugh.
  This holds for Series narration, close-third narration, Onyx's narration (`VB-063`), and the voice of a
  teller in a Comrade Account, an Adversary Account or a Hearsay entry (`VB-067`). An enemy of Mirza may
  mock the name or the title in dialogue, as that character's own line, and the prose around the line stays
  level. The comic registers that `VB-004` as amended allows in told accounts, and the registers `VB-068`
  names, do not extend to the name or the title. In the Axiom's formal style, "Axiom of Mirza" follows
  `MRZ-003`.

- **One Mirza fits.** The state is one state and the Axiom stands behind both acts, but the entries are
  sequential, not simultaneous. Abad's "while" cannot be literal: the pact comes first, at 24, and the offer
  comes at least seven years later (Long Mask, after 30), and probably decades later, since the offer follows
  three generations of the doctrine's students (`MCD-499`, `MCD-719`, `MCD-967`) and the academy's rise
  (`MCD-551`, `MCD-1307`). The Iron Bastard's Stand itself is age 25 (`MCD-238`), after the pact.
- **Single reign or succession.** A single reign is possible, because long lifespans are normal here, but
  it is not required and cannot be shown. `MRZ-001` and `MRZ-007` therefore make the crown the office, with
  the sovereign of `MCD-1414` possibly a different holder.
- **Different envoys.** The `MCD-1040` envoy is "she" and the `MCD-1414` envoy is "he", so they are two people.
- **No evidence in `MCD-1414` that the crown is Mirza's pact partner.** The entry says only "a foreign
  crown" and never mentions the pact, the Ghost or Mirza; the identification is Abad's ruling, written into
  `MRZ-007`.
- **The pact's status after the Pier is not recorded anywhere.** `MRZ-006` and `MRZ-007` leave it open.

## 6. Questions for Abad (unresolved, not decided in this draft)

1. **The sovereign's part in `MCD-1414`.** The entry's narration (reliable, Series) says "a foreign crown ...
   wanted" the teaching. `MRZ-007` reads that as the offer going out in the crown's name at the Axiom's
   instance, with the sovereign's own knowledge open. If you want the sovereign to have approved it, the
   conflict becomes the crown against its own earlier course, and the Axiom stops being the one acting
   against it.
2. **What counts as "proven" for the title** (`MRZ-009`). Candidates: holding Theorem rank; having led or
   served in a proof that was won; a ceremony. Not invented here.
3. **Does Mirza's crown know the Iron Bastard is the Ghost's captain?** The pact envoy of `MCD-1040`
   negotiated with Kanja face to face, and the Long Mask hides his identity (`MCD-246`). The `MCD-1414` envoy
   calls him only "Bastard". Left open in `MRZ-007`.
4. **Is the Axiom Mirza's only force, and does it include the fleet?** The pact's sea patrols are the
   crown's. Left open in `MRZ-003`.
5. **Do Postulants count as members?** `MRZ-004` calls a soldier an Axiom and a recruit a Postulant, and does
   not say when a Postulant becomes an Axiom. The plural "Axioms" in `MRZ-005` is my own wording.
6. **VB-070 and Adversary Accounts.** `VB-004` as amended treats the teller's voice in an Adversary Account
   as characters' talk. An enemy teller mocking Mirza in that voice sits between "narration never mocks" and
   "enemies may mock in dialogue". The draft lets an enemy character's line stand and keeps the frame around
   it level; you may want a plainer ruling. Also, `VB-070` covers only the name and the title, as ruled. It
   leaves "Axiom", "Postulant" and "Theorem" outside the writing rule.
7. **The planning notes' "Archive of Mirza" and "Tribunal of Mirza".** Your later ruling (the state is
   simply "Mirza", "of Mirza" only as the earned title and in formal documents) overrides the earlier idea
   that every institution carries the suffix. No Archive or Tribunal is drafted. Say if either should exist
   as an institution, under a plain name.
8. **Does the Fulcrum's craft explain the Axiom's wish for the doctrine?** It would tie `MRZ-005` to
   `MCD-1414`. Not written into any rule.
9. **Tech level (approval-list item 35)** is untouched. `MRZ-011` only holds the Axiom to whatever that ruling
   sets.
10. **Unrelated, still open from the planning notes:** the Kairo name (the Hollow Shogunate's capital in
    `GEO-003`, and Stormreaver's true name in `CC-044`, `MCD-259`, `ARS-170`). No Mirza rule uses it.
