# Draft: Mirza, the state, its army, and the writing rule

*Draft for Abad's approval, revision 2, dated 2026-10-10. Nothing here is locked. Follows the planning notes
at `docs/lords-of-cian/drafts/2026-10-10-mirza.md`. Revision 2 follows the first independent review. The new
prefix is `MRZ-` (a new institution gets its own prefix, as `ASH-` did for Ashkeel). The writing rule is
`VB-070`, the next unused Voice Bible number (the ledger's highest is `VB-069`; there is no `MRZ-` rule yet).
All rules below are written to lock with `"status": "locked"`, source `"Original invention, chat-drafted
2026-10-10, no source document"`. The Connective-Tissue Gate still owes its independent review of this
revision before it is presented as final.*

## 1. Abad's rulings this draft rests on

- Mirza is a state, with a dedicated military that studies the sciences.
- Placement: "Mirza's crown signs the pact while its science army tries to buy the doctrine." The crown is
  the unnamed foreign sea nation of `MCD-490` and `MCD-1040`. The science army stands behind the foreign
  sovereign's envoy of `MCD-1414`, acting in the crown's name against the crown's own course.
- The army is the Axiom. A soldier is "an Axiom"; recruits are Postulants; commanders are Theorems; a
  campaign is "a proof"; a won war is "proven". Two schools: the Fulcrum (mechanics: they move armies by
  finding the one point that tips them) and the Prism (optics and light science: they split a problem into
  its parts before they strike). "Theorem" is a rank only, not a body.
- "of Mirza" is exclusive to one use outside treaties and formal documents: an earned personal title. Only
  proven scholar-soldiers may add it to their name. They must be addressed by it in formal settings and
  ceremonies, and they introduce themselves with it proudly. "Axiom of Mirza" appears only in treaties and
  formal documents; everyday speech says "the Axiom". The state is simply "Mirza". The schools are "the
  Fulcrum" and "the Prism", no suffix.
- "Mirza is marching" is outsiders' speech only, used sparingly in dialogue, never an official name: on
  Cian, frightened people cannot tell the state from its army.
- Writing rule: no comic use of the title or the name in prose; enemies may mock it in dialogue, the prose
  never does; the comic registers `VB-004` as amended allows in told accounts do not extend to the title or
  the name.

## 2. Proposed rules

### MRZ-001

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** Mirza is a sovereign state under a crown. It is the foreign sea nation of `MCD-490` and
  `MCD-1040` (called the Foreign Sea nation in `MCD-1893`). Its envoy offered the Sovereign Ghost of the
  Great Sea's fleet safe harbor in its waters when Kanja was 21 (`MCD-490`, Ghost Harbor, Rebellion era).
  The same envoy, "older, less formal", returned three years later, when Kanja was 24, and signed the joint
  anti-slaving pact aboard *The Ledger* (`MCD-1040`): joint patrols against slaving routes, a mutual
  distress-signal system, one shared ledger kept by Garren Hask for both powers' patrols, and a clause
  letting either side suspend the pact on a season's notice. This is the crown's own course toward the
  Sovereign Ghost's fleet. Both entries left the nation unnamed and stand as written; this rule gives it a
  name. The state is called simply "Mirza". The crown named in this rule is the office, and any number of
  sovereigns may hold it across the entries that follow; one sovereign holding it from `MCD-490` through
  `MCD-1414` is possible and is not required. Whether the pact still stands in later years, including after
  the Sovereign Pier (`MCD-246`), is left open.

### MRZ-002

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** Mirza lies within the waters reachable from Cian by ordinary sailing (`MCD-1893`). It is not
  the fourth continent of `MCD-1893`, it is not one of the eleven regions of `GEO-002`, and it holds no cell
  on the Atlas grid (`GEO-003`). It is not one of the five sovereign nations of the Shattered Kingdoms
  (`WC-012`, `POL-108`). Its geography is left open: its coasts, islands, cities, climate and resources, and
  whether Living Drakma occurs there (`WC-013`, `MCD-1895`), are undecided, and no entry may fix any of them
  until a rule does.

### MRZ-003

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom is Mirza's dedicated military, an army whose soldiers study the sciences as part of
  soldiering. A soldier of the Axiom is called an Axiom, and the body is called the Axiom. In treaties and
  formal documents the body is styled "the Axiom of Mirza", and that style appears only there. Everyday
  speech says "the Axiom". Within the Axiom, a recruit is a Postulant; a commander holds the rank of Theorem,
  a rank only, and no body is called the Theorem; a campaign is called "a proof"; a war the Axiom has won is
  called "proven". The Axiom uses these words of itself, and "proof" and "proven" keep their ordinary
  meaning in other mouths and in narration. This sense of "proven" is the Axiom's own. It is unrelated to the
  Proven density tier and the Proven elevation (`WC-024`, `MCD-144`, `MAW-118`), and requires no density
  rating. The Axiom has two schools. The Fulcrum is the school of mechanics: its Axioms move armies by
  finding the one point that tips them. The Prism is the school of optics and light science: its Axioms split
  a problem into its parts before they strike. The schools are called "the Fulcrum" and "the Prism", with no
  suffix and never "of Mirza". Whether the Axiom is Mirza's only armed force, whether the crown's sea patrols
  of `MCD-1040` belong to it, whether every Axiom belongs to one of the two schools, and when a Postulant
  becomes an Axiom, are left open.

### MRZ-004

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The royal corps of `MCD-1414` is a corps of the Axiom, and the foreign crown and sovereign
  of that entry are Mirza's. The sovereign then holding the crown sent the envoy and wanted the teaching
  (`MCD-1414`). The Axiom pressed for it. The offer, of land, title and exclusive teaching rights for the
  Iron Bastard's doctrine of diagnostic listening, aims at the breaking of occupied civilian housing in
  territories that resist annexation, and it runs against the course the crown set in `MCD-1040`
  (`MRZ-001`). The envoy of `MCD-1414`, a man, is a different person from the envoy of `MCD-490` and
  `MCD-1040`, and stays unnamed. Kanja refused the offer, and the Axiom did not obtain the doctrine from him
  (`MCD-1414`). `MCD-1414` is set in the Long Mask, in a year the entry does not fix, years after
  `MCD-1040` and possibly many decades after it; the sovereign who made the offer may be a different holder
  of the crown from the sovereign of `MCD-1040`. Whether Mirza's crown knows that the Iron Bastard and the
  Sovereign Ghost's captain are one man, and whether the pact stood when the offer was made, are left open.
  The depot-engine mystery (`MCD-1308`, `MCD-1412`) keeps its culprit unassigned. No `MRZ-` rule places the
  Axiom, the Fulcrum, the Prism or the crown behind the Crawler design of `MCD-1308` and `MCD-1412`, and none
  rules them out. The envoy of `MCD-1414` cites the mystery as leverage, which shows only that those the
  envoy spoke for had read the scholar's account.

### MRZ-005

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** "Of Mirza" is an earned personal title. Outside treaties and formal documents, the phrase
  has this one use. A proven scholar-soldier of the Axiom may add it after their name, in the form "[name] of
  Mirza", and nobody else may. The bearer must be addressed by the full title in formal settings and in
  ceremonies, and introduces themselves with it, proudly. No other person, office, school or place takes "of
  Mirza" in speech. The Axiom's own style in treaties and formal documents is set by `MRZ-003`. Being proven
  here is the Axiom's own sense of the word (`MRZ-003`). It is unrelated to the Proven density tier and
  elevation (`WC-024`, `MCD-144`, `MAW-118`), and requires no density rating. What counts as proven for the
  title is left open. No bearer is named by this rule. Separately, "Mirza is marching" is the phrase of
  outsiders who fear the Axiom, on Cian among them, who cannot tell the state from its army. It is used
  sparingly, and only in dialogue. It is never an official name or phrase of Mirza and no Mirzan source uses
  it.

### MRZ-006

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** The Axiom's science works inside this world's physics: mass, pressure and mechanics, sound
  and resonance, light as crystal and lens bend it (`MCD-1895`), Drakma, and chemistry. It fields no
  firearms; the only small arms on Cian are the Sealbound Directorate's (`ARS-426`). Any instrument, engine
  or device an entry gives it is held within the mainline technology level that Abad's ruling on that open
  question sets (approval-list item 35); until that ruling, no entry gives it a device beyond those the
  locked rules already place on Cian. The Axiom's study of Drakma implies nothing about a Drakma deposit in
  Mirza (`MRZ-002`).

### MRZ-007

- **category:** `mirza-state`
- **statement:** No `MRZ-` rule identifies any other foreign party in canon as Mirza's. The foreign parties
  of `MCD-573` (a delegation seeking exclusive rights to the storm-timing method), `MCD-972` (a trading
  caravan), `MCD-991` (a neutral delegation), `MCD-1458` (envoys seeking exclusive tunnel-engineering
  instruction), `MCD-1464` (a tribunal of merchant-nations) and `MCD-1471` (a foreign anti-slaving squadron)
  each stay unidentified and open. The island people of `MCD-1204`, to whom the fleet's reputation means
  nothing, are not Mirza's, since Mirza's crown acknowledged that reputation in `MCD-490`.

### VB-070

- **category:** `voice-bible`
- **statement:** The name and the title of Mirza are never comic in prose. No narration, in any entry or
  track, uses the name "Mirza" or the personal title "of Mirza" (`MRZ-005`) for a joke, a pun or a laugh.
  This holds for Series narration, close-third narration, Onyx's narration (`VB-063`), and the voice of a
  teller in a Comrade Account, an Adversary Account or a Hearsay entry (`VB-067`). An enemy of Mirza may
  mock the name or the title in dialogue, as that character's own line and within the registers `VB-004` as
  amended allows that scene, and the prose around the line stays level. The comic registers that `VB-004` as
  amended allows in told accounts, and the registers `VB-068` names, do not extend to the name or the title.
  In the Axiom's formal style, "Axiom of Mirza" follows `MRZ-003`. Qualifies `VB-004` and `VB-068`.

## 6. Questions for Abad (unresolved, not decided in this draft)

1. **The sovereign's part in `MCD-1414`, and the word "while".** Revision 1 left the sovereign's knowledge
   open. The review shows canon forces more: `MCD-1414`'s statement says "A foreign sovereign's envoy", its
   narration says the crown "wanted" the teaching for "its own royal corps", and the seat offered is "close
   ... to the throne". So `MRZ-004` has the sovereign then holding the crown send the envoy and want the
   teaching, with the Axiom pressing for it. The inner conflict is the Axiom pulling the crown off its own
   earlier course. This adjusts the "while" in your placement ruling: `MCD-1414` falls years after `MCD-1040`,
   and its sovereign is not ignorant of the offer. Confirm this reading.
2. **What counts as "proven" for the title** (`MRZ-005`). Candidates: holding Theorem rank; having led or
   served in a proof that was won; a ceremony. Not invented here.
3. **Does Mirza's crown know the Iron Bastard is the Ghost's captain?** The pact envoy negotiated with Kanja
   face to face, and the `MCD-1414` envoy calls him only "Bastard". Left open in `MRZ-004`.
4. **Is the Axiom Mirza's only force, does it include the fleet, and when does a Postulant become an
   Axiom?** Left open in `MRZ-003`. The plural "Axioms" there is my own wording.
5. **VB-070 and Adversary Accounts.** `VB-004` as amended treats the teller's voice in an Adversary Account
   as characters' talk. An enemy teller mocking Mirza in that voice sits between "narration never mocks" and
   "enemies may mock in dialogue". The draft lets an enemy character's line stand, within the registers the
   scene allows, and keeps the frame around it level; you may want a plainer ruling. Also, `VB-070` covers
   only the name and the title, as ruled. It leaves "Axiom", "Postulant" and "Theorem" outside the writing
   rule.
6. **The planning notes' "Archive of Mirza" and "Tribunal of Mirza".** Your later ruling (the state is
   simply "Mirza", "of Mirza" only as the earned title and in formal documents) overrides the earlier idea
   that every institution carries the suffix. No Archive or Tribunal is drafted. Say if either should exist
   as an institution, under a plain name.
7. **Does the Fulcrum's craft explain the Axiom's wish for the doctrine?** It would tie the schools to
   `MCD-1414`. Not written into any rule.
8. **The other foreign contacts** (`MRZ-007`): `MCD-573`, `MCD-1458` and `MCD-1471` echo Mirza's pattern
   (exclusive rights, anti-slaving). Each stays open; say if any should be Mirza's.
9. **Tech level (approval-list item 35)** is untouched. `MRZ-006` only holds the Axiom to whatever that
   ruling sets.
10. **Unrelated, still open from the planning notes:** the Kairo name (the Hollow Shogunate's capital in
    `GEO-003`, and Stormreaver's true name in `CC-044`, `MCD-259`, `ARS-170`). No Mirza rule uses it.
