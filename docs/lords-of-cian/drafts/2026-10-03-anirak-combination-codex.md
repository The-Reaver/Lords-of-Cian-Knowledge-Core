## Foundations: three gaps closed first

**ARS-438 (new).** The Siren's Voice: her body and her gorget. This reconciles `ARS-367` ("baseline
capability") with `MCD-251`/`ARS-130` ("forged at Chain Harbor").

The Siren's Voice is Anirak's own biology: a sub-audible neuroacoustic hum her throat produces. The
Siren Gorget, forged at Chain Harbor (`MCD-1883`), carries and shapes it. Its crystal nodes take up
the hum and focus it into the locked forward cone of about 10 meters, with a 15-20%
distance-misjudgment effect (`ARS-411`). Her voice does the work; the gorget gives it direction.

Without the gorget, the hum is unshaped. It reaches a short radius in every direction and hits her
own side as hard as the enemy. That is why she never fights without it, and why it was the first
piece Kanja forged for her. Underwater, the water carries the hum to the full cone with no loss
(`CC-113`).

Its effect on living minds is unchanged. It has none on the undead (`ARS-373`), and none on anyone
whose eardrums are already ruptured.

**ARS-439 (new).** The Chain-Strike doctrine, which names her style and links her two scales.

The locked doctrine is the frame (`ARS-411`): the Twin Fangs set the perimeter, the Siren's Voice
degrades perception of it, and the Triform Morning Star punishes whoever crosses it. Chain-Strike is
how she runs that frame without a pause. Every strike's recoil is fed into the next motion, so the
chains never come to rest.

Her two locked scales are one system:
- **Stack** is the fuel. It counts consecutive landed strikes and absorbed impacts (`MCD-251`,
  `ARS-411`). It sets the Fangs' reach, about 3 meters at low Stack and 6 at high.
- **Fury** is the engine. It is her heart rate (`ARS-368`). Rising Stack drives her heart rate up,
  and a rising heart rate heats the pulse-keyed Fangs (`MCD-1883`) and moves her from Warm toward
  White.

Stack bleeds away when she stops moving. A few seconds of stillness and it begins to drain. That is
the biological root of her locked "no stillness" doctrine (`ARS-373`): stopping is the one thing
that costs her.

**ARS-440 (new).** Debt Collection: her hook-and-pull technique family (named but undefined at
`ARS-374`).

A Fang's hook catches a limb, a weapon, or a shield. The chain-pull then takes the opponent's own
momentum and turns it into the start of her next strike, adding it to her Stack. Every blow she
takes is a debt she has absorbed (impact is fuel, `ARS-367`), and Debt Collection is how she pays it
back with interest.

The name is Maw and Cestari language. She was raised in a system that sold people by their debts
(`MCD-251`), and she turned its vocabulary into a fighting style. Every combination below that pulls
is a form of Debt Collection.

## The combinations, by Fury state

**ARS-441 (new).** Warm-state combinations. Full fine control and every technique available
(`ARS-368`); these are her precise, deliberate openings.
- **First Payment.** Her opening. A Fang hooks the lead opponent's weapon arm. A Debt Collection pull
  drags the arm across his body into a Grapple-Star catch, and the weapon is gone. She banks her
  first Stack before the second man has set his feet.
- **Compound Interest.** The Fangs run in a continuous crossing figure-eight, left and right in
  turn, so every pass lands on someone. It is built to climb Stack as fast as her body allows, and
  it is the bridge from Warm into Hot.
- **The Lien.** One Fang wraps a shield or weapon and holds it under tension while the other works.
  It binds a strong opponent in place without ending him, and it is her answer to anyone she needs
  held rather than broken.

**ARS-442 (new).** Hot-state combinations. Her reaction time narrows, the Voice and her gaze begin
to fire together on their own (`ARS-370`, first in Book 3), and from Hot upward her sonar is active
to about 300 meters (`ARS-371`).
- **Siren's Draw.** The Voice cone makes distances lie. An opponent steps back to where he judges he
  is safe and lands inside the Fangs' radius. This works only on the living, and only on those who
  can hear.
- **Foreclosure.** The Voice hits a crowd. Vertigo and nausea break its footing. A Split-Crown sweep
  (about 40% wider striking radius, `ARS-411`) clears everyone still standing in the cone. It is her
  answer to massed soldiers, and the core of how she holds a line alone.
- **Echo Cast.** In smoke, darkness, or murk, her active sonar fixes positions she cannot see, and
  she throws the Fangs at what she hears.

**ARS-443 (new).** White-state combinations: her combat ceiling through Book 4 (`ARS-368`). Fine
control is reduced, the Fangs run near forge heat, and the Voice and her gaze fire together every
time. Her shapes get bigger and more committed.
- **Called Debt.** Both Fangs wrap one opponent from opposite sides and pull in opposite directions,
  then a Morning Star blow lands on the point the two pulls have opened. It is the White-state
  finisher for a single hard target.
- **The Long Note.** At near-forge heat the swinging chains scream on one long note (as in
  `MCD-1883`'s yard). She drives the scream and the Voice's hum into one wall of sound, and the
  enemies inside it lose balance, distance, and direction at the same moment.

**ARS-444 (new).** Flood State has no named combinations. It happens only on the Book 5 Tide Line
(`ARS-368`/`369`/`373`, `MCD-1889`), and there her techniques survive only as instinct, never as
choices (`ARS-369`).

What witnesses see is the Chain Storm running with no gap at all. The Voice and her gaze act as one
compound effect (`ARS-370`). Two chain wraps held three seconds weaken whatever they hold (`ARS-372`),
and Ren clears what she leaves (`ARS-369`). No account written before Book 5 may show Flood State, or
any engagement long enough to reach it.

## Water, the Ever-Haunt, and her partners

**ARS-445 (new).** Undersea combinations. She has more torque in denser water (`CC-113`), the water
carries her Voice, and her sea-sense runs ambient below Hot and active from Hot upward.
- **The Drowning Spiral.** Underwater, a full-body rotation drives both chains in one spiral that
  drags a swimmer inward into the Fangs. Water resistance gives her more torque, not less.
- **Lighthouse.** The Lantern-Star's strobe (`ARS-411`) is a locked Ever-Haunt countermeasure
  (`CULT-197`), and in black water it is the only light. She strobes it to scatter the lower tiers
  of the Ever-Haunt and to blind and disorient anything that hunts by sight. Against Sereth Vaul, who
  hunts by sound as she does (`MCD-1892`), it is the one tool in that fight that is not sound. The
  outcome of their engagement stays reserved.

**ARS-446 (new).** Partner combinations.
- **Storm and Depth (with Ren).** This is their locked pairing, "Surface Storm / Deep Pressure"
  (`CC-114`). Her Fangs cut a target's structure, and then Ren's active field (+200% load within
  about 15 meters, `CC-101`) collapses it under its own weight. Inside Ren's field her Siren gaze is
  stripped, so he is the one partner who sees her clearly (`CC-112`).
- **Joint Account (with Lauris, before Book 5 only, `ARS-374`).** Lauris's Triad-Lock breaches
  become failure points that Anirak's Debt Collection pulls open in the following second. Lauris
  grows quieter and Anirak louder, and no instrument can track both.

**ARS-447 (new).** Tide Line crew combinations (`MCD-1890`), each built on the partner's locked kit:
- **Double Draw (with Stormbreaker).** Her chain-pull drags a target into the Equinox's gravity
  corona, which pulls the struck target into the hit rather than knocking it away (`ARS-417`). The
  target is pulled twice, from two directions, by two different physics.
- **Mark and Collect (with Voidbreaker).** He fixes a target's exact position with a Void-Marker
  dart (`ARS-419`), and she hooks it at range before it can move.
- **Two Storms (with Zora).** Zora's Scream is a sonic boom that ruptures eardrums (`ARS-420`). A
  ruptured ear cannot hear the Siren's Voice (`ARS-438`), so the order is fixed: Voice first, then
  Scream. Run backward, it cancels Anirak's best weapon. The crew drills the order until it is
  instinct.
- **The Eastern Passage (with Edda, Hamund, and Odile).** This is the formation from Chain Harbor,
  where three freed prisoners held the passage at her back so she could work (`MCD-1883`). Her core
  holds the line, and she never has to stop. It is the oldest combination in the Codex, built before
  any of the others.
