*Draft for Abad's approval, dated 2026-10-10, revision 4, after his second set of rulings and the review of revision 3 (revision 2 followed the first independent review). Nothing here is locked. Source for all three rules: "Original invention, chat-drafted 2026-10-10, no source document". The rules would lock as Batch 383 with `"status": "locked"`. The ID between `CC-164` and `CC-166` is reserved for a pending proposal and is not used. The IDs used are `CC-166`, `POL-109` and `MCD-1913`.*

## 1. Abad's rulings

### The first ruling

> "Stormreaver is from the shogunate, named after that great City his family helped build that great City his family was targeted and they had to flee that city is storm reavers namesake and he is the heir to that great City as his family protected and governed that great City fairly and the corruption of the current leadership that exists now through treachery forced abandonment when storm River was a child and unable to protect"

Reading: "storm River" is Stormreaver, "unable to protect" is read as unable to protect the city, and "that great City" is Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`). This resolves the open question of why one word is both Stormreaver's true name (`CC-044`, `CC-062`, `MCD-140`, `MCD-259`, `ARS-170`, `ARS-394`, `ARS-410`, `MCD-1890`) and the Shogunate's capital. Both uses stay, and the rules below link them.

> "No one survived. / it was carried out by another (major character death) and Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam, rose through it. / Book 3 Kairo's biology begins to augment. If you check the Canon all of the members of the pirate crew will start developing stronger emphasis on their abilities. / He will not be strong enough, formidable yes, but not yet mature enough in his biology development to fight Oz one on one (very few characters can fight Oz yo the death and expect to live.) which is why Kanja intervenes. / science already existed in the Shogunate while his family governed Kairo fairly. / Kairo recognizes him as heir, we will see how the story flows and see if he remains there after Book 3 as a strategic Ally along with Oz as they build kingdoms to combat the immense numbers they have to face and book 4 and 5."

His clarifications, in answer to follow-up questions:

- On "(major character death)": "Betrayer dies later". Reading: someone other than Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam carried out the treachery. That person is a major character, and that person's death is a major story event later. Who it is stays unnamed.
- On the Book 3 fight: "Yes to both". Reading: Ozmund's prosecution of the Shogunate war (`CC-126`) reaches Kairo; Stormreaver stands against him for his city and cannot win one on one; Kanja intervenes, and that is the start of the already-locked Kanja and Ozmund fight of `MCD-218`.
- On a crew-wide rule: "Lauris and Anirak and any other character I have already made powerful can remain powerful other than that we will go with the progression of each crew member as planned". Reading: no crew-wide Book 3 rule is drafted. Stormreaver's maturing in Book 3 is his own planned progression.
- On wording: "\"Begins to mature\"". Reading: his biology "begins to mature". The word "augment" is not used for him. In canon, augmentation is the Shogunate's synthetic extraction science (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). His maturing is his own Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`) and is a separate thing.

## 2. The rules

### CC-166

- **category:** `character-codex`
- **statement:** Stormreaver's true name, Kairo (`CC-062`), is the name of a city. He is a native of the Hollow Shogunate (`POL-070`) and is named for its capital, the great city of Kairo (`GEO-003`, `POL-109`), which his family helped build and which they governed and protected fairly. The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while his family governed Kairo (`CC-105`, `POL-106`); this rule does not say who held it or whether it was used then. When he was a child and could not protect the city, the Shogunate's corrupt current leadership rose through treachery that took Kairo from his family, targeted them, and forced them to abandon it. No one of his family survived except him. He is its sole survivor and the heir to the city. The fact stands beside his locked profile: his Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`), his recruitment at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest after three years of his own observation (`MCD-259`), his role as the crew's mediator (`MCD-259`), the trust and de-escalation effect of his Insight Lenses (`ARS-410`) and his place on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`). Each reads as consistent with this origin, and this rule assigns none of them a motive. His age and the year of the coup are not set. The rule fixes only that the coup came in his childhood. His three years of observation began near Kanja's age 152 (`MCD-259`), and this rule does not place the coup against them. His Book 3 maturing and his stand for Kairo are at `MCD-1913`.

### POL-109

- **category:** `political-atlas-nation`
- **statement:** Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`), was built in part by one family and governed and protected by that family fairly. The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while that family governed Kairo (`CC-105`, `POL-106`); this rule does not say who held it or whether it was used then. The treachery that ended the family's governance was carried out by someone other than Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam, a major character who is unnamed here and whose later death is reserved as a major story event. The Shogunate's present, corrupt rulers, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (`POL-070`), who rule through the Parasitic Sovereignty defined at `POL-106`, rose through that treachery. No one of the family survived except its heir, Stormreaver, whose true name is Kairo (`CC-166`, `CC-062`). In Book 3 the city of Kairo recognizes him as its heir (`MCD-1913`). This rule does not date the coup, and it does not say when the Sin-Eaters' manufacture began relative to it. The history of the augmentation Vargo Vakas bought (`CC-105`) is left as `CC-105` has it.

### MCD-1913

- **category:** `books-3-5-structure`
- **statement:** In Book 3 (`MCD-097`) Stormreaver's Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`) begins to mature, as his own planned progression. It is a different thing from the Shogunate's synthetic augmentation (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). He is formidable and is not yet mature enough in his biology to fight Ozmund one on one. Very few characters can fight Ozmund to the death and expect to live, and Ozmund's effective ceiling in Book 3 is 22,000x-24,000x (`MCD-219`). Ozmund's prosecution of the Shogunate war (`MCD-097`, `CC-126`) reaches Kairo. Stormreaver stands against him for his city, and Kanja intervenes. That intervention opens the Kanja and Ozmund fight of `MCD-218`, with Kanja's Book 3 figures at `MCD-321`. The city of Kairo recognizes Stormreaver as its heir (`CC-166`, `POL-109`). Whether he remains in Kairo after Book 3, as a strategic ally beside Ozmund while they build kingdoms against the numbers they face in Books 4 and 5, is left open to how the story flows. `MCD-1890` places him on the Tide Line in Book 5, and this rule does not change that. This is Book 3 material. No entry set before Book 1 may dramatize or foreshadow it.

## 5. Open questions for Abad

1. **Stormreaver's age and the year of the coup.** Only his childhood at the coup is fixed. Recommend leaving both open until a Chronicle or Book 3 needs a number.
2. **Did his family govern only Kairo, or the whole Shogunate?** `CC-166` says the city. Recommend leaving it at the city, so a later ruling can widen it without a correction.
3. **The betrayer.** Who carried out the treachery, how, over what span, and where the betrayer's later death falls in the five books. Recommend holding all three for the Book 3 outline, with the death placed so it does not collide with `MCD-1913`'s Kairo events. Also open: whether the betrayer may be an already-locked character, and whether the family died in the flight or after it (the first ruling has them flee, the second has none survive except him; both hold as written). No entry set before Book 1 names the betrayer or touches the reserved death.
4. **When the Sin-Eaters' mass production began relative to the coup** (`POL-106`, `CC-105`). Not set, and Vakas's augmentation is left undated. Recommend leaving both open until Book 3 material needs the order.
5. **Does he remain in Kairo after Book 3, and how does that sit with `POL-108` and `MCD-1890`?** Left open to how the story flows. Recommend deciding at the Book 4 outline, with his Book 5 Tide Line placement as the fixed point and a `POL-108` ruling made at the same time if Kairo becomes an ally.
6. **What changes in his biology as it begins to mature in Book 3.** Nothing is set beyond the fact of it. Recommend deciding when his Series or the Book 3 outline needs it, and using "matures" language, never the Shogunate's augmentation terms.
7. **May a pre-Book-1 entry tell his origin?** `MCD-1913` bars the Book 3 events before Book 1. `CC-166` itself (the family, the lost city, the heirship) has no such bar yet. Recommend allowing the origin only as legend or hearsay under `VB-067`, and holding the heirship and Kairo's recognition of him for Book 3.
