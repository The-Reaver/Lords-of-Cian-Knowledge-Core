## 2. The rules

### CC-166

- **category:** `character-codex`
- **statement:** Stormreaver's true name, Kairo (`CC-062`), is the name of a city. He is a native of the Hollow Shogunate (`POL-070`) and is named for its capital, the great city of Kairo (`GEO-003`, `POL-109`), which his family helped build and from which they governed the whole Shogunate, fairly and protectively. He is 789 years old at the Fulfillment Ceremony, Book 1's opening, when Kanja is 313 (`MCD-1901`), so he is 476 years older than Kanja and was about 631 at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest (`MCD-259`). The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while his family governed (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). The Sin-Eaters' mass production (`POL-106`) began in that time as a hidden program, about 1,089 years before Book 1 and 300 years before his birth, run in secret by a faction outside the family's control (`POL-109`). When he was a child and could not protect the city, that faction's powers, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (`POL-070`), rose through treachery carried out by another, a powerful warrior turned warlord, which took Kairo from his family, targeted them, and forced them to abandon it. The coup came in his childhood, after his birth 476 years before Kanja's and before the Parley of the Raptor's Nest (`MCD-259`), and its year is not set. He escaped as a child. No one of his family survived except him; no other member of his family is alive at Book 1 or after, and none reappears alive in any entry set after their death. Whether the others died in the flight or after it is not set. He is its sole survivor, heir to the city and to the family's governance of the Shogunate. The fact stands beside his locked profile: his Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`), his recruitment at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest after three years of his own observation (`MCD-259`), his role as the crew's mediator (`MCD-259`), the trust and de-escalation effect of his Insight Lenses (`ARS-410`) and his place on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`). Each reads as consistent with this origin, and this rule assigns none of them a motive. His three years of observation began near Kanja's age 152 (`MCD-259`). Before Book 1 his origin may be told only as legend or hearsay (`VB-067`). His friendship with Ozmund, his Book 3 stand for Kairo and maturing, and his governing of the Shogunate as its Protector after Book 3 are at `MCD-1913`.

- **category:** `political-atlas-nation`
