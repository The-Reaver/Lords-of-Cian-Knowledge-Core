# Draft: Stormreaver (Kairo) and the city of Kairo

*Draft for Abad's approval, dated 2026-10-10. Nothing here is locked. Source for both rules: "Original invention, chat-drafted 2026-10-10, no source document". The rules would lock as Batch 383 with `"status": "locked"`. The ID between `CC-164` and `CC-166` is reserved for a pending proposal and is not used. The IDs used are `CC-166` and `POL-109`.*

## 1. Abad's ruling

> "Stormreaver is from the shogunate, named after that great City his family helped build that great City his family was targeted and they had to flee that city is storm reavers namesake and he is the heir to that great City as his family protected and governed that great City fairly and the corruption of the current leadership that exists now through treachery forced abandonment when storm River was a child and unable to protect"

Reading: "storm River" is Stormreaver, and "that great City" is Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`). This resolves the open question of why one word is both Stormreaver's true name (`CC-044`, `CC-062`, `MCD-140`, `MCD-259`, `ARS-170`, `ARS-394`, `ARS-410`, `MCD-1890`) and the Shogunate's capital. Both uses stay, and the rules below link them.

## 2. The rules

### CC-166

- **category:** `character-codex`
- **statement:** Stormreaver's true name, Kairo (`CC-062`), is the name of a city. He is a native of the Hollow Shogunate (`POL-070`) and is named for its capital, Kairo (`GEO-003`, `POL-109`), which his family helped build and which they governed and protected fairly. When he was a child and could not protect it, the Shogunate's current leadership took the city through treachery, targeted his family, and forced them to abandon it. He is the heir to the city. The fact stands beside his locked profile: his Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`), his recruitment at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest after three years of his own observation (`MCD-259`), his role as the crew's mediator (`MCD-259`), the trust and de-escalation effect of his Insight Lenses (`ARS-410`) and his place on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`). Each reads as consistent with this origin, and this rule assigns none of them a motive. His age and the year of the coup are not set. Only the order is fixed: the coup came when he was a child, and he was grown by the time his three years of observation began, which put its start near Kanja's age 152 (`MCD-259`).

### POL-109

- **category:** `political-atlas-nation`
- **statement:** Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`), was built in part by one family and governed and protected by that family fairly. The Shogunate's current leadership holds power in Kairo through treachery, and that treachery targeted the family and drove it from the city. The family's heir is alive and is Stormreaver, whose true name is Kairo (`CC-166`, `CC-062`). The leadership is the one `POL-070` names as the Shogunate's rulers, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam, who rule through the Parasitic Sovereignty defined at `POL-106`. This rule makes the treachery the current leadership's own act or the means of its rise, and does not say which. It does not date the coup, and it does not say when the Parasitic Sovereignty or the Sin-Eaters' manufacture began relative to it. The history of the augmentation Vargo Vakas bought (`CC-105`) is left as `CC-105` has it.

## 5. Open questions for Abad

1. **Stormreaver's age and the year of the coup.** Only the order is fixed. Recommend leaving both open until a Chronicle or Book 3 needs a number.
2. **Did his family govern only Kairo, or the whole Shogunate?** `CC-166` says the city. Recommend leaving it at the city, so a later ruling can widen it without a correction.
3. **The fate of the rest of his family.** The rules say only that they were targeted and forced out, and that the heir lives. Recommend leaving it open, since a living or dead family is a large story choice.
4. **The details of the treachery.** Who acted, how, and over what span. Recommend holding for a Series entry or Book 3.
5. **Did Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam personally carry it out, or only rise by it?** Your words were "the current leadership". `POL-109` ties the treachery to the leadership and takes no side. Recommend leaving it open.
6. **His role in Book 3's Shogunate war** (`POL-108`, `MCD-097`). Nothing is set. Recommend deciding at the Book 3 outline, with `MCD-1890`'s Tide Line placement in Book 5 as the fixed point.
7. **When the Sin-Eaters began to be mass-produced relative to the coup** (`POL-106`, `CC-105`). Not set, and Vakas's augmentation is left undated. Recommend leaving both open until Book 3 material needs the order.
8. **Does the city of Kairo recognize him as heir, and does he claim it?** Not set. Recommend holding for the Book 3 outline.
