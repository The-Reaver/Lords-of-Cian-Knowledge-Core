## 2. The rules

### CC-166

- **category:** `character-codex`
- **statement:** Stormreaver's true name, Kairo (`CC-062`), is the name of a city. He is a native of the Hollow Shogunate (`POL-070`) and is named for its capital, the great city of Kairo (`GEO-003`, `POL-109`), which his family helped build and which they governed and protected fairly. The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while his family governed Kairo (`CC-105`, `POL-106`); this rule does not say who held it or whether it was used then. When he was a child and could not protect the city, the Shogunate's corrupt current leadership rose through treachery that took Kairo from his family, targeted them, and forced them to abandon it. The treachery was carried out by another, a powerful soldier turned warlord (`POL-109`). No one of his family survived except him. He is its sole survivor and the heir to the city. He is older than Kanja (`MCD-1901`). The fact stands beside his locked profile: his Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`), his recruitment at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest after three years of his own observation (`MCD-259`), his role as the crew's mediator (`MCD-259`), the trust and de-escalation effect of his Insight Lenses (`ARS-410`) and his place on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`). Each reads as consistent with this origin, and this rule assigns none of them a motive. His exact age and the year of the coup are not set. The rule fixes only that the coup came in his childhood and does not say whether it came before or after Kanja's birth. His three years of observation began near Kanja's age 152 (`MCD-259`), and this rule does not place the coup against them. His friendship with Ozmund, his Book 3 stand for Kairo and maturing, and his governing of the Shogunate as its Protector after Book 3 are at `MCD-1913`.

### POL-109

- **category:** `political-atlas-nation`
- **statement:** Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`), was built in part by one family and governed and protected by that family fairly. The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while that family governed Kairo (`CC-105`, `POL-106`); this rule does not say who held it or whether it was used then. The treachery that ended the family's governance was carried out by someone other than Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam: a powerful soldier turned warlord, a major character who is unnamed here and whose later death is reserved as a major story event. The Shogunate's present, corrupt rulers, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (`POL-070`), who rule through the Parasitic Sovereignty defined at `POL-106`, rose through that treachery. How they lose power by the end of Book 3 is left open (`MCD-1913`). No one of the family survived except its heir, Stormreaver, whose true name is Kairo (`CC-166`, `CC-062`). In Book 3 the city of Kairo recognizes him as its heir, and after Book 3 he governs the Shogunate as its Protector and refuses its crown (`MCD-1913`). This rule does not date the coup, and it does not say when the Sin-Eaters' manufacture began relative to it. The history of the augmentation Vargo Vakas bought (`CC-105`) is left as `CC-105` has it.

### MCD-1913

- **category:** `books-3-5-structure`
- **statement:** In Book 3 (`MCD-097`) Stormreaver's Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`) begins to mature, as his own planned progression. It is a different thing from the Shogunate's synthetic augmentation (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). Across Books 1 and 2, after Ozmund's Book 1 arc (`MCD-025`) and through the Book 2 journey (`MCD-279`), Stormreaver becomes a close and dear friend of Ozmund. In Book 3 Ozmund's prosecution of the Shogunate war (`MCD-097`, `CC-126`) reaches Kairo, and Ozmund is in an undesirable position, the Crown-Scar fully embraced (`MCD-100`, `MCD-219`). Stormreaver knows it and stands against him for his city anyway. He is formidable and is not yet mature enough in his biology to fight Ozmund one on one. Very few characters can fight Ozmund to the death and expect to live, and Ozmund's effective ceiling in Book 3 is 22,000x-24,000x (`MCD-219`). Kanja intervenes. That intervention opens the Kanja and Ozmund fight of `MCD-218`, with Kanja's Book 3 figures at `MCD-321`. The city of Kairo recognizes Stormreaver as its heir (`CC-166`, `POL-109`), and the friendship with Ozmund holds afterward. After Book 3 Stormreaver stays in a governance capacity. He refuses a crown and governs as the Protector of the Shogunate, inspired by Ozmund's own crown rejection in Book 1 (`MCD-025`, `MCD-051`). Stormreaver's own name for Kanja, which many others also use, is the King of Cian; Kanja holds no formal title by it, and this rule does not say when the name began. The Shogunate is the antagonist in Book 3 (`POL-070`, `POL-108`, `MCD-094`, `WC-012`). After Book 3, governed by Stormreaver as its Protector, it stands with the alliance, and he stays on as a strategic ally beside Ozmund while they build kingdoms against the numbers they face in Books 4 and 5. How Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam lose power by the end of Book 3 is left open. `MCD-1890` places him on the Tide Line in Book 5, and his governing leaves that placement unchanged. This is Book 3 material, and the friendship it rests on forms in Books 1 and 2. No entry set before Book 1 may dramatize or foreshadow any of it.

## 5. Open questions for Abad

1. **Stormreaver's exact age and the year of the coup.** That he is older than Kanja is fixed, and so is that the coup came in his childhood. Recommend leaving the numbers and the coup's order against Kanja's birth open until a Series entry or Book 3 needs them.
2. **Did his family govern only Kairo, or the whole Shogunate?** `CC-166` says the city. After Book 3 he governs the whole Shogunate as its Protector, which is a separate matter. Recommend leaving the family's earlier reach at the city, so a later ruling can widen it without a correction.
3. **The betrayer.** Who carried out the treachery (a powerful soldier turned warlord), how, over what span, and where the betrayer's later death falls in the five books. Also open: whether the betrayer may be an already-locked character, and whether the family died in the flight or after it (the first ruling has them flee, the second has none survive except him; both hold as written). Recommend holding all of it for the Book 3 outline, with the death placed so it does not collide with `MCD-1913`'s Kairo events, and that no entry set before Book 1 names the betrayer or touches the reserved death.
4. **How Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam lose power by the end of Book 3.** Whether by the war, by the betrayer's fall, or by the city turning, and what becomes of the Parasitic Sovereignty (`POL-106`) when they go. Recommend deciding at the Book 3 outline, so the Shogunate's turn to the alliance has a cause on the page.
5. **When the Sin-Eaters' mass production began relative to the coup** (`POL-106`, `CC-105`). Not set, and Vakas's augmentation is left undated. Recommend leaving both open until Book 3 material needs the order.
6. **What changes in his biology as it begins to mature in Book 3.** Nothing is set beyond the fact of it. Recommend deciding when his Series or the Book 3 outline needs it, and using "matures" language, never the Shogunate's augmentation terms.
7. **Does the Protector's title have a formal in-world name?** `MCD-1913` uses "Protector of the Shogunate" descriptively. Recommend keeping it descriptive until you rule.
8. **Confirm the friendship-timing reading.** The rules have Stormreaver becoming Ozmund's close friend across Books 1 and 2, before the Book 3 stand. Recommend confirming it, since it also decides when the two first meet on the page.
9. **How does he govern the Shogunate while serving on the Tide Line in Book 5 (`MCD-1890`)?** Both stand as locked. Recommend leaving the arrangement open until the Book 5 outline, and not answering it with a rule now.
10. **May a pre-Book-1 entry tell his origin?** `MCD-1913` bars the Book 3 events before Book 1. `CC-166` itself (the family, the lost city, the heirship) has no such bar yet. Recommend allowing the origin only as legend or hearsay under `VB-067`, and holding the heirship and Kairo's recognition of him for Book 3.
11. **Mine `Avatar_Environmental_Blueprint_v2` as its own batch?** It holds the book-by-book development arc for all 16 profiles and has never been extracted. Recommend one separate batch, run through the gate, with the three points above (16 against 19 Avatars, three fronts against four, the dropped archetype label) settled for every profile at once.
