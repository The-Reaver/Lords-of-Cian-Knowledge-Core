## 2. The rules

### CC-166

- **category:** `character-codex`
- **statement:** Stormreaver's true name, Kairo (`CC-062`), is the name of a city. He is a native of the Hollow Shogunate (`POL-070`) and is named for its capital, the great city of Kairo (`GEO-003`, `POL-109`), which his family helped build and from which they governed the whole Shogunate, fairly and protectively. He is 789 years old at the Fulfillment Ceremony, Book 1's opening, when Kanja is 313 (`MCD-1901`), so he is 476 years older than Kanja and was about 631 at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest (`MCD-259`). The Shogunate's extraction science already existed while his family governed (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). The Sin-Eaters' manufacture (`POL-106`) began in that time as a hidden program, about 1,089 years before Book 1 and 300 years before his birth, run in secret by a faction outside the family's control (`POL-109`). When he was a child and could not protect the city, that faction's powers, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (`POL-070`), rose through treachery carried out by another, a powerful warrior turned warlord, which took Kairo from his family, targeted them, and forced them to abandon it. The coup came in his childhood, more than 450 years before Kanja's birth, and its year is not set. No one of his family survived except him. He is its sole survivor, heir to the city and to the family's governance of the Shogunate. The fact stands beside his locked profile: his Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`), his recruitment at the Parley of the Raptor's Nest after three years of his own observation (`MCD-259`), his role as the crew's mediator (`MCD-259`), the trust and de-escalation effect of his Insight Lenses (`ARS-410`) and his place on the Tide Line (`MCD-1890`). Each reads as consistent with this origin, and this rule assigns none of them a motive. His three years of observation began near Kanja's age 152 (`MCD-259`), and this rule does not place the coup against them. Before Book 1 his origin may be told only as legend or hearsay (`VB-067`). His friendship with Ozmund, his Book 3 stand for Kairo and maturing, and his governing of the Shogunate as its Protector after Book 3 are at `MCD-1913`.

### POL-109

- **category:** `political-atlas-nation`
- **statement:** Kairo, the Hollow Shogunate's capital (`GEO-003`), was built in part by one family, which governed the whole Shogunate from it, fairly and protectively. The Shogunate's extraction science already existed during that governance (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). About 1,089 years before Book 1, 300 years before the family's heir was born, a faction inside the Shogunate began the Sin-Eaters' manufacture (`POL-106`) as a hidden program, run in secret outside the family's control. That faction is the same power the coup later raised: Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam (`POL-070`), who rule through the Parasitic Sovereignty defined at `POL-106`. The program is the seed of the coup, and the family's governance stays fair throughout. The coup's treachery was carried out by another, a powerful warrior turned warlord, a major character who is unnamed here and whose later death is reserved as a major story event; Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam rose through it. It came in the heir's childhood, more than 450 years before Kanja's birth, and its year is not set. No one of the family survived except its heir, Stormreaver, whose true name is Kairo (`CC-166`, `CC-062`); whether the others died in the flight or after it is not set. In Book 3 the city of Kairo recognizes him as its heir, Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam die by the end of Book 3 (by whose hand is not set), and after Book 3 he governs the Shogunate righteously as the Protector of the Shogunate and refuses its crown (`MCD-1913`). Vargo Vakas's older purchased augmentation (`CC-105`) is left as `CC-105` has it.

### MCD-1913

- **category:** `books-3-5-structure`
- **statement:** In Book 3 (`MCD-097`) Stormreaver's Aero Variant biology (`CC-062`) begins to mature, as his own planned progression (`MCD-1914`), and he reaches the maturity of his biology by the end of Book 5. It is a different thing from the Shogunate's synthetic augmentation (`CC-105`, `POL-106`). Across Books 1 and 2, around the Moonvault Rescue (the cousins' first joint operation, Book 2, `MCD-318`; `MCD-025`, `MCD-279`), Stormreaver becomes a close and dear friend of Ozmund. In Book 3 Ozmund's prosecution of the Shogunate war (`MCD-097`, `CC-126`) reaches Kairo, and Ozmund is in an undesirable position (`MCD-100`, `MCD-219`, `CC-126`). Stormreaver knows it and stands against him for his city anyway. He is formidable and is not yet mature enough in his biology to fight Ozmund one on one. Very few characters can fight Ozmund to the death and expect to live, and Ozmund's effective ceiling in Book 3 is 22,000x-24,000x (`MCD-219`). Kanja intervenes. That intervention opens the Kanja and Ozmund fight of `MCD-218`, with Kanja's Book 3 figures at `MCD-321`. The city of Kairo recognizes Stormreaver as its heir (`CC-166`, `POL-109`), and the friendship with Ozmund holds afterward. Vile-Sire and Hollow-Dam die by the end of Book 3; by whose hand, and the Sin-Eaters' fate, are left open. After Book 3 Stormreaver stays in a governance capacity. He refuses a crown and governs the whole Shogunate righteously as the Protector of the Shogunate, a formal title he holds, inspired by Ozmund's story, his own crown rejection in Book 1 among it (`MCD-025`, `MCD-051`). Stormreaver's own name for Kanja, which many others also use, is the King of Cian; Kanja holds no formal title by it. The name is in use before Book 1: commoners, and those Kanja rescued as Kanja, see him as a king through his legend and his Rebellion, and when the Long Mask is revealed to have been him his legend grows. Before that reveal, those rescued under a Long Mask persona do not know their rescuer was Kanja. When the Long Mask is revealed is not set. The Shogunate is the antagonist in Book 3 (`POL-070`, `POL-108`, `MCD-094`, `WC-012`). After Book 3, governed by Stormreaver as its Protector, it stands with the alliance. He stays on as a strategic ally beside Ozmund while they build kingdoms against the numbers they face in Books 4 and 5, and the Shogunate fields forces in Book 5 under its Protector. Which front they join is open (`MCD-221`). `MCD-1890` places Stormreaver himself on the Tide Line in Book 5, and his governing stands beside that placement. This is Book 3 material, and the friendship it rests on forms in Books 1 and 2. No entry set before Book 1 may dramatize or foreshadow any of it, except that pre-Book-1 entries may use the name King of Cian within the bounds above.

### MCD-1914

- **category:** `books-3-5-structure`
- **statement:** Every crew member follows their own specific progression, beginning some time after Book 2 or 3. At the end of Book 5 every crew member reaches the maturity of their biology. Characters already made powerful, Lauris, Anirak and any others, remain powerful. The details of each member's progression come from that member's own planned progression, and this rule fixes no mechanism, number or per-member specific and changes no locked figure. The per-character cases already locked stand as they are: Anirak's Hot and White states from Book 3 (`ARS-370`, `ARS-442`, `ARS-443`), Lauris's Convergence-driven saturation drop (`ARS-358`, `ARS-361`) and the per-book combat register (`MCD-144`). Stormreaver's case is at `MCD-1913`: his maturing begins in Book 3 and he reaches the maturity of his biology by the end of Book 5.
