# The Anniversary Callum Breck Still Kept

---

Decades on, Callum Breck still marked the date privately, apart from the crew's larger annual
remembrance — a quieter ritual he'd never asked anyone to witness, a walk to whatever stretch of
water was nearest wherever the crew happened to be that year, a few minutes alone before he rejoined
the others as if nothing had happened.

Kanja had noticed it for years without saying anything, respecting that some grief wants to stay
private even inside a crew built on shared remembrance. This year, for the first time, Breck asked
him to come.

"You don't have to say anything," Breck said, once they'd reached the water's edge. "I just — I've
done this alone every year since it happened, and I think I'm ready for it not to be alone anymore.
Doesn't mean I need words. Just means I don't need the silence to be empty this time."

They stood together a long while, saying nothing. Eventually Breck spoke, not performing grief for
an audience the way ceremony sometimes demands, just saying the plain truth of it out loud because
someone was finally there to hear it unrehearsed.

"I used to think going quiet for four months after he died meant I'd failed him somehow. Like real
grief was supposed to look like something, and mine just looked like nothing at all, for months."

"It wasn't nothing. I watched you carry it the whole time, even silent. Silence isn't the absence of
grief. Sometimes it's the only shape grief can take before it's ready to become something else."

"You never once tried to hurry me out of it."

"I never wanted to. It wasn't mine to hurry. It was yours, and you came back to your own voice on
your own time, at Ghost Harbor, when it was ready to come back and not a moment before. I've never
regretted letting you have that."

Breck nodded slowly, something in his posture easing that hadn't eased in this particular way
before. "Thank you for coming this year. I think I needed to know I didn't have to keep doing this
part by myself, even after this long."

"Any year you want company, I'm here. Any year you don't, that's yours to keep as it's always been."
