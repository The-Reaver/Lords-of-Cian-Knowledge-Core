# The Weeks the Chair Sat Empty

*Locked canon, Batch 150, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-475`). Kiti Chronicle II. Homage-era Detroit, Kiti/Owusu.
Territory Chronicle -- Owusu as protagonist, Kanja an unnamed background guest with no command,
credit, or resolution authorship. New standalone material. No new named characters.*

---

A sudden illness put Owusu in recess for six weeks, longer than any absence his tenure had yet
required — and for the first time since the manufactured scandal had proven attrition useless against
him, the political ground beneath his seat behaved the way ordinary political ground behaved for
ordinary officeholders.

Opponents who had spent years failing to gain traction against him made real progress in those six
weeks. A deputy filling his chair in the interim found himself outmaneuvered on votes Owusu would have
held without difficulty. Coalition members who'd never once wavered began, cautiously, testing whether
loyalty built around him personally would hold without him actually present to anchor it.

"It's happening exactly the way it would happen to anyone else," his own chief aide reported,
watching six years of accumulated institutional weight erode faster than she'd believed possible.
"Everything you told me once, about the strength lapsing the instant you left the seat — I always
assumed that was a caution for some distant future. I didn't expect to watch it happen in real time
over something as ordinary as a fever."

Owusu returned to his seat in the seventh week, still visibly recovering, and the ground reversed
itself almost as quickly as it had shifted — the deputy's losses recovered within days, the testing
coalition members falling back into alignment the moment he resumed the chair in person, six weeks of
erosion undone by nothing more than his own physical return to the room.

"I've spent years telling people the strength was institutional, not personal," he told his aide
afterward, still weak but unmistakably himself again. "I don't think anyone fully believed it,
myself included, until we actually watched it prove true. It's a strange thing, learning something
about your own nature only once you've been forced to test its edges."

"Does it worry you? Knowing exactly how fragile six weeks away can make it?"

"It should worry me less than it does. I already know how this ends, eventually — the seat empties
for good, someday, and whatever this is goes with it entirely, the way it's always been going to." He
settled back into the chair that had, for six weeks, meant nothing without him in it. "I'd rather
know precisely what I'm working with now than be surprised by it later, when there's no returning to
the seat to reverse it."

An unnamed man who had been present in the gallery through several of the six empty weeks, watching
without comment, returned once more the week Owusu resumed his seat and said nothing to anyone.
