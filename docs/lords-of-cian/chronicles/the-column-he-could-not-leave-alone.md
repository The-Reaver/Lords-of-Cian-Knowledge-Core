# The Column He Could Not Leave Alone

*Locked canon, Batch 124, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-419`). Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle IV, first entry in
the second wave. Rebellion era. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in
neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

Every prior Iron Bastard engagement had been fought with the field entirely clear behind him — no
civilians, no wounded, nothing to protect but the ground under his own feet — and the armored column
that caught a supply convoy of forty wounded fighters mid-transport was the first time the alias's
doctrine had to answer a question it had never once been tested against: what happens when standing
alone isn't an option, because leaving means abandoning people who can't defend themselves.

"We move the wounded first," the convoy's field medic said, already organizing evacuation routes.
"You hold the column off as long as you can."

"I'm not holding them off from a fixed position this time. That's how the doctrine's worked every
other time, and every other time there was nothing behind me that couldn't move on its own." Kanja
was already reading the column's approach vectors, recalculating everything the alias's usual
open-ground stand assumed. "This time I need the fight to move with the evacuation, not stay in one
place while it happens somewhere else."

The Aegis-Talisman's frequency-inversion lens worked differently in motion than it ever had standing
still — reading each vehicle's resonant signature in the brief window before he passed it rather than
sustaining a fixed broadcast, a rolling wave of structural failure that advanced at exactly the pace
the wounded convoy needed to clear ground behind him. Mafesto absorbed what fire reached him during
the movement, Obsidian Malice discharging into whichever vehicle's shed fittings made it the
column's most immediate threat rather than the most convenient target, the entire engagement
reorganized around a retreat's own pace rather than a fixed stand's.

It took longer than any prior Iron Bastard engagement, and cost more — a graze along his own
shoulder that the fixed-position stands had never once allowed close enough to land — but the
convoy's forty wounded reached safe ground fully intact, the column's own pursuit broken by the same
resonance doctrine adapted, under real pressure, into something it had never needed to be before.

"You changed the whole method mid-fight," the field medic said afterward, treating the shoulder wound
Kanja hadn't mentioned until the convoy was fully clear. "Doctrine's not supposed to bend that fast."

"Doctrine's not the point. The doctrine's just what worked the last time the problem looked a
certain way. This time the problem looked different, so the method had to look different too." He let
her finish the field dressing, first time in the alias's history anyone had needed to. "I'd rather
take a wound holding a moving line than keep a spotless record standing on a fixed one while the
people I'm actually supposed to be protecting get left behind it."
