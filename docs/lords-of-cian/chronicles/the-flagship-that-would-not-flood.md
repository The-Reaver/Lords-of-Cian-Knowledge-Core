# The Flagship That Would Not Flood

*Locked canon, Batch 115, 2026-09-10 (`MCD-393`). Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle II. Rebellion
era, a new naval engagement in the Gale Straits corridor (`MCD-242`, age 29), a detailed showcase of
the Trinity in a shipboard boarding action against Krael's replacement's own flagship. Not a
territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters.*

---

The replacement admiral's flagship carried triple bilge pumps and reinforced below-deck bulkheads
specifically to survive the kind of close engagement the Gale Straits corridor had made famous —
built to take a hull breach and keep fighting rather than founder the way lesser vessels had. She
was, by every specification her builders could point to, unsinkable in anything short of a direct
magazine strike.

Kanja boarded her directly rather than trying to sink her.

Mafesto took the leap across open water between hulls without hesitation, the Kinetic Transfer
System converting the landing's full force into stored charge before his boots found the deck
planking, and the marines who met him in the first thirty seconds discovered that a ship built to
survive flooding had never been built to survive a boarding party of one. Obsidian Malice discharged
into the forward hatch cover in a single strike, not to breach the hull below but to jam the hatch
itself immovably in its frame — sealing the flagship's own marine reserve belowdecks as effectively
as any flood ever could have, without a drop of water reaching the bilges the pumps were built to
answer.

Onyx cleared the quarterdeck in the exchange that followed, Whisper of Shadows turning the storm's
own driving rain and pitching deck into cover no training had prepared the crew to fight through, and
the admiral himself, drawing his own blade at the wheel rather than abandon his post, found Soulbound
Edge waiting the instant their strikes crossed — a duel decided not by force but by the same fraction
of committed intent that had once turned aside an honor blade years earlier on a canal-district
dueling ground.

The flagship never took on a drop of water. She surrendered instead, her marine reserve sealed
uselessly below a jammed hatch, her admiral disarmed at his own wheel in a storm his own weather
service had failed to predict as accurately as Sephtis had predicted it for the other side.

"You built her to survive flooding," Kanja said, afterward, standing on a deck that had never once
been breached. "That's a good design, for the fight you expected. I never needed to flood her. I
just needed thirty seconds belowdecks and a jammed hatch, and every reinforcement you built into her
hull turned out to be exactly the wrong problem to have solved."
