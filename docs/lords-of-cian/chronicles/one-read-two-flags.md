# One Read, Two Flags

*Locked canon, Batch 261, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1302`). Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle LXXXIII, wave 28. A detailed technical-and-political showcase of the joint aqueduct assessment. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

Both delegations arrived at the aqueduct the same morning, Trust engineers on one bank and rebel observers on the other, neither group crossing the water to stand beside the other, watching Kanja walk the span between them as though the read itself were the only neutral ground either side was willing to occupy.

The structure's own signature told a more complicated story than either delegation had expected. Doubled verification confirmed it on the first careful pass: the aqueduct wasn't failing from age or neglect, as the Trust engineers had assumed, nor from deliberate sabotage, as the rebel side had quietly suspected of the other. It was failing because both sides, independently, over years of contested control, had each reinforced their own half of the structure without coordinating with whoever held the other half — two well-intentioned repair efforts, decades apart, that had never been designed to bear load together and had begun, slowly, working against each other's tension instead of with it.

"Neither of you broke this," Kanja said, addressing both banks at once rather than either delegation separately. "You each tried to save it, on your own side, without knowing what the other side had already changed. The aqueduct's been quietly fighting itself for longer than either of you have been fighting each other over the ground it crosses."

The Trust engineers wanted to argue the point until he walked them through the doubled verification himself, hand to stone, letting their own senior engineer confirm the same signature independently. The rebel observers wanted a guarantee no Trust hand would touch the repair once the truth was known, until the successor — present in person, a decision that had cost him political capital on his own side just to make — stepped onto the span himself, unarmed, and stood beside Kanja rather than behind his own engineers.

"I'm not asking your side to trust mine," the successor said, to the rebel bank directly. "I'm asking both sides to trust an account that answers to neither of us. The read already told us neither side sabotaged this. What we do with that truth is the only part left that's actually a political choice."

The repair took eleven days, worked by engineers from both banks under a temporary truce neither command fully endorsed but neither actively broke, coordinated moment to moment by Kanja's own diagnostic listening confirming that each side's contribution answered the other's rather than fighting it, the way the original, uncoordinated repairs never had. Obsidian Malice discharged only once, clearing a single failed section neither side's engineers had trusted their own tools to remove safely without risking the whole span.

When the aqueduct's water ran clean through both banks' settlements again, neither flag flew over it. It stood, by unspoken agreement from both commands, simply as what it had always actually been underneath the years of separate suspicion — one structure, serving both sides of a line that had never mattered to the water crossing it at all.
