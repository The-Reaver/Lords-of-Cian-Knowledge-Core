# The Last Site Before the Trench

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Trench Monarch Alias Chronicle XLIII, wave 15.
Rebellion era, age 18, the final days before the Battle of the Black Trench (`MCD-232`), age 19. Not
a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

Nobody called it the last site before the Trench while it was happening. Nobody could have known
that. But looking back on it afterward, Garren Hask would mark the Coldrace dredge line as the final
entry in the ledger's pre-Trench section — the last ordinary correction, the last unremarkable
handshake, before everything the crew had built on the canal folded into the larger war the Black
Trench announced.

The Coldrace foreman was already halfway convinced before Kanja arrived, the five-district standard
having traveled far enough by then that most sites greeted the crew with resignation rather than
resistance. The correction itself took less than an hour — a straightforward wage discrepancy,
verified by two of the now-established independent clerks, settled without incident.

"This one felt different," Corren Halst said afterward, unable to fully place why. "Not harder.
Just — different."

"I felt it too." Kanja stood at the edge of the site a while longer than the settlement required,
watching the ordinary rhythm of a dredge line that had no idea it was about to become, within days,
a footnote to something considerably larger. "I don't know what it is exactly. Maybe just the sense
that we've been doing this same work, site after site, long enough that it's started to feel like it
could go on being just this. And I don't think it's going to. Something's coming that's bigger than a
wage correction. I can't tell you how I know that. I just do."

"You think the Trust's finally going to answer the five districts with everything, instead of raids
and rerouted manifests."

"I think something's coming that won't look like a raid or a rerouted manifest at all. I don't have a
name for it yet." Kanja looked back at the Coldrace line one more time — ordinary men, doing ordinary
work, believing, reasonably, that tomorrow would look much like today. "I want to remember this
site, exactly as it was. Not because it was special. Because I don't think many more of them are
going to feel this simple, after whatever's coming actually arrives."

He was right, though not one person on that dredge line, watching him leave that afternoon, could
have said why the goodbye felt heavier than the wage correction warranted. Within days, the Black
Trench would take the fight somewhere the tally sheets and the five-district standard had never
had to go — a battlefield, not a negotiation, the first time Mafesto and Obsidian Malice would stand
beside Onyx as anything more than dormant potential.

The Coldrace ledger entry, in Garren Hask's own hand, closed with a line he would not fully explain
to anyone who asked about it later: *Last simple correction before the war found us properly. Kept
exactly as it happened. Nothing more to add.*
