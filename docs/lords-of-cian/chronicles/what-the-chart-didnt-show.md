# What the Chart Didn't Show

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-586`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XLI, wave 14 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The shoal that nearly cost the reassigned squadron a ship had never appeared on any chart Sephtis
worked from — a shifting sandbar, reshaped by the very storm surge the forecast had correctly called,
invisible to a method built to predict weather rather than the seafloor's own response to it.

The lead ship struck it in the storm's final hour, hull grinding against sand that hadn't been there
two months earlier, and for several minutes the engagement's success looked set to end in a grounded
flagship rather than a completed maneuver.

Mafesto reached the strain point first, the Kinetic Transfer System absorbing the hull's own grinding
impact against the sand rather than letting the ship tear itself open further, buying the minutes
needed for the crew to work her free under power rather than force. Obsidian Malice discharged into
the water off the starboard side, not as a weapon but to clear enough loose sand from the ship's
immediate path that the final effort to reverse off the bar succeeded before the tide dropped further
and made the grounding permanent.

The ship came free with damage but no loss, and the near-disaster reached Kanja within the hour, the
same hour the formally reassigned officer's own decades of local knowledge — had he been aboard — might
have flagged the shifted bar before the squadron ever approached it.

"Sephtis's method reads weather," Kanja told the assembled command afterward, refusing to let the
near-miss go unexamined. "It doesn't read what weather does to a seafloor two months after the fact.
That's a real gap, and pretending it isn't because tonight ended without a loss would be exactly the
mistake that costs us a ship for real someday."
