# The Voice Two Generations Removed

*Locked canon, Batch 187, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-548`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle XV, closing the
fifth wave. Rebellion era, age 23. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in
neutral third-person prose. No new named characters beyond the already-locked apprentice singer.*

---

The apprentice who'd once carried three hundred interleaved voices, then grown to redesign the
Hymn-Engine's own constructions past a cryptographer's breakthrough, had taken on a student of her
own — a decision she made without asking Kanja's permission, and one he learned about only once he
found the two of them already deep into a lesson.

"I didn't think to ask first," she admitted, catching his reaction before he'd said anything. "I
should have, maybe. It felt like the right thing to do before I'd thought to make it a question."

"You didn't need to ask. You've earned the craft enough to decide who else deserves to carry it."
Kanja watched the new student practice a construction the apprentice herself had designed, one
generation removed now from anything he'd personally taught. "I taught you the discipline of
listening before cleverness. I never taught you this particular construction. That's entirely yours.
Watching you pass down something I didn't build myself is a different thing than I expected to feel,
and I think it's the right one."

"Does it worry you? The craft moving further from what you actually taught, generation to
generation?"

"No. I think that's supposed to happen, if the craft's actually alive rather than simply repeated."
Kanja watched the new student attempt the same restraint-over-cleverness lesson the apprentice had
once needed to learn herself. "I'd rather the Hymn-Engine grow past anything I personally built than
stay frozen at whatever I happened to understand the day I first used it. Two generations removed
from me is exactly where I'd hope it eventually got to, if the people carrying it forward are as
careful with it as you've been."

The new student's own first solo attempt, weeks later, succeeded using a construction entirely her
own invention — three generations now removed from Kanja's own original craft, each one adding
something the one before it hadn't held, the discipline itself the only thing that had never changed
across any of them.
