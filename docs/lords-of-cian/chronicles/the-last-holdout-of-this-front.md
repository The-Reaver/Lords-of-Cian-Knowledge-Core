# The Last Holdout of This Front

*Locked canon, Batch 256, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1173`). Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle LXXXIX, wave 30. A genuine unresolved closer: an administrator refuses to open his books entirely, and the campaign, called to a new front of the war, must leave the case formally open rather than force it.*

---

He never raised his voice and never once produced a false record, which made him harder to move than any hostile administrator the crew had faced. He simply declined, politely, repeatedly, across nine days, to open the mill's books at all — no legal obligation compelled him to, he pointed out accurately, and no amount of patient testimony-gathering from his workers could substitute for records he alone controlled and had no intention of showing anyone.

Ezio tried every approach the method had ever built. Worker testimony alone, cross-referenced against outside production estimates, produced a plausible but not airtight case. A formal tribunal request, the same route that had worked at MCD-745's chamber years earlier, stalled in a queue with no clear date. Patient waiting at a locked gate, the tactic that had broken the province-wide collusion at MCD-929, produced nothing here — this was one man's quiet, lawful refusal, not a blockade with a crowd behind it capable of breaking it from inside.

On the ninth day, word reached the crew that the front of the war they'd been supporting needed them elsewhere within the week, a demand neither Kanja nor Ezio had the standing to refuse or delay for one stubborn district's sake. They had a choice: force a weaker finding than the method's own standard allowed, built on testimony and estimate rather than the mill's own proven figures, or leave the case exactly as strong and exactly as unresolved as it genuinely was.

"We've never closed a file without an answer," Ezio said, the ledger open to a page with no total written at the bottom of it.

"We're not closing it. We're leaving it open, honestly, which isn't the same thing." Kanja had him write the finding plainly for what it was: worker testimony consistent with a shortfall, an administrator's lawful but total non-cooperation preventing confirmation, no figure the method could stand behind with its usual certainty, and an explicit statement that the case remained unresolved rather than decided in the administrator's favor by default. He told the workers the same thing directly — that the crew was leaving, that the case wasn't proven, and that it wasn't disproven either, and that the record would say so honestly rather than pretend a conclusion the numbers hadn't earned.

"Some other crew might come back to this," Ezio said, packing the tent for the last time in that district. "Or nobody might."

"Then the record says exactly what's true either way. We don't get every case. We get every case we're honest about, whether it ends in a number or a blank page that says why there isn't one yet."
