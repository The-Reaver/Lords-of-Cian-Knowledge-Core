# The Flag From a Foreign Sea

*Locked canon, Batch 155, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-490`). The Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias
Chronicle XII, closing the fourth wave. Rebellion era, age 21, Ghost Harbor. Not a territory
Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters.*

---

A small vessel flying colors from a nation entirely outside Sovereign Trust waters approached the
fleet under open parley flags, its envoy requesting audience rather than fleeing the reputation the
way every Directorate-aligned ship had learned to — the first foreign contact the ghost fleet had
received since its reputation began.

"Word of your fleet has reached our own waters," the envoy said, careful but unafraid, plainly having
prepared for this meeting rather than stumbled into it. "Ships disabled rather than sunk, captives
freed rather than plundered, a reputation for restraint that travels further than most reputations
built on fear alone. My government wondered whether that restraint extended to nations outside the
conflict you're actually fighting."

"It extends to anyone who isn't actively working against what I'm trying to do here. I've no quarrel
with your nation, and no intention of manufacturing one." Kanja received the envoy with the same
plain honesty he brought to every account of the fleet's actual conduct. "What is it your government
actually wants from this meeting?"

"Trade, eventually, once your rebellion resolves one way or another — our merchants have heard the
same accounts everyone else has, and would rather do business with a fleet known for honest dealing
than take their chances with pirates who aren't." The envoy produced a formal letter, sealed and
genuine. "For now, only this: an offer of safe harbor in our own waters, should the fleet ever need
it, extended in good faith rather than obligation."

Kanja read the letter carefully before answering. "I appreciate the offer, and the trust it implies.
I can't promise I'll ever need to use it. I can promise the fleet won't abuse it if we do."

"That's precisely the answer our government was hoping to hear." The envoy prepared to depart,
something like genuine satisfaction in the exchange. "Reputations built on restraint are rare enough
that most nations learn to trust them slowly, if at all. Yours seems to have earned that trust faster
than most."

The letter joined Garren Hask's own growing ghost-fleet ledger that evening, the first formal
acknowledgment from beyond the conflict the fleet had ever fought — proof the reputation's reach had
already outgrown the war it was born from.
