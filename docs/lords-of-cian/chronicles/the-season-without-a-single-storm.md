# The Season Without a Single Storm

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-576`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXI, wave 11 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Sephtis's forecasts for the harbor blockade's first ten weeks agreed on one point consistently: no
system capable of covering an approach would cross the coast for the foreseeable stretch he could
reliably chart, and beyond that stretch his own method admitted no confidence at all.

The blockade held anyway, static and unglamorous, while the harbor's garrison rationed stores it had
stockpiled expecting a shorter siege and Kanja's crew held station on open water with nothing for the
doctrine to offer them but patience.

Morale frayed in ways it hadn't during any storm-timed engagement. A crew built around decisive,
weather-timed strikes found ten weeks of holding station with no forecast to plan around a harder kind
of endurance than any single battle had demanded. Two junior officers requested reassignment. A third
openly questioned, in front of enough of the crew to matter, whether the alias's whole reputation had
been built on weather that simply wasn't showing up when it was actually needed.

"He's not wrong to ask," Kanja told the ship's quartermaster, overhearing it secondhand. "The doctrine
was never a promise that a storm comes when we need one. It's a promise that when one does, we'll know
before anyone else does. Those aren't the same guarantee, and I'd rather my own people understand the
difference than assume a run of quiet weeks means the method's failed them."

He said as much to the crew directly, at the tenth week's close — not a defense of the doctrine, but
a plain admission that the blockade would likely be won or lost on nothing but held discipline for as
long as the sky stayed quiet, and that holding it was its own kind of victory worth respecting on its
own terms rather than as a lesser substitute for the storm that wasn't coming.
