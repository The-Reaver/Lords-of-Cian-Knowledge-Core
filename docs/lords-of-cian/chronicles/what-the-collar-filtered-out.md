# What the Collar Filtered Out

*Locked canon, Batch 259, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1236`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LXXI, wave 24. Age
145, V3 gear. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The cave system ran half a mile back from the coastline, a natural cut the slaving operation had
expanded with pick and blasting powder into a holding depot no naval patrol could ever have spotted
from open water. What the depot's builders hadn't accounted for was the Blight seam their own
blasting had opened along the tunnel's third chamber — a slow particulate seep, invisible, odorless
to anyone without augmented senses, thickening with every hour the poor ventilation trapped it
against the low ceiling.

The guards inside had been breathing it for weeks without knowing why their tempers had gone short
and their hands had started shaking on watch. The captives, chained lower and longer in the worst of
it, had it far worse.

The Breath Collar's filtration membrane — built originally against airborne toxins and particulate
Blight contamination, distinct from and complementing the Talisman's own frequency-level Blight
Immunity — had never been tested against a seep this concentrated in an enclosed space this poorly
aired. It held. Three hours inside the tunnel system, guiding sixty-eight captives back out through
a chamber thick enough with contaminant that torchlight visibly hazed in it, and the collar's
membrane kept every breath he drew clean the entire way, doing in practice exactly what its
seven-category design brief had promised it would in theory decades earlier.

The guards, unfiltered and unaware what had been working against them for weeks, didn't put up the
fight the depot's fortifications suggested they should have. Half of them were too sick to properly
resist by the time the tunnel was cleared. Garren Hask's ledger recorded the liberation as sixty-eight
freed and made a separate note beside it, in different ink, flagging the seam itself for whichever
crew came through the region next — a hazard no rescue could fully close, sealed rock and slow poison
outlasting the operation it had originally been dug to hide.
