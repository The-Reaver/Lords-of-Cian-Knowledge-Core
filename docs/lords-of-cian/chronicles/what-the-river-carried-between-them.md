# What the River Carried Between Them

*Locked canon, Batch 254, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1100`). Bane Alias Chronicle LXX, wave 24. A detailed
full-Trinity combat showcase fought waist-deep in a fast night river crossing, with Obsidian Malice's
discharge deliberately redirected away from the water itself.*

---

The ford was the only crossing wide enough for the column within two days' march, and the Directorate
patrol that caught them mid-crossing knew it as well as Bane did. Half the column was already in the
water when the first arrows came off the far bank, and there was no good ground to retreat to on
either side.

He went in after them rather than pulling back.

Waist-deep, current pulling hard at his footing, Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System had nothing solid
to plant against — until he stopped trying to plant against the riverbed at all and let the current
itself become the leverage. A soldier wading in from the flank met a redirected surge of the river's
own force diverted through Bane's own braced stance, and went under rather than forward, swept twenty
feet downstream before he found his footing again, out of the fight without a blow landed on him.

Sight was nearly useless in the dark water and the spray. Onyx wasn't. Cadence Ruin read the
vibration of coordinated footsteps through the riverbed itself, picking apart an attempted pincer
before either flank realized the other had been read; it didn't need eyes to do it.

Obsidian Malice was the one piece of the Trinity Bane held back from its usual answer. A full
discharge released into open water, with forty of his own people still fording it, could have hurt
more of them than the enemy — so when the moment came that called for it, he raised the blade clear
of the surface and released the charge upward and outward instead, a controlled arc over the water
rather than through it, taking the last organized resistance on the bank without ever letting the
current carry the current.

The column finished crossing under cover of the confusion that bought, no one lost to the river or
the fight.

"You never used it in the water," Danne Sok said afterward, wringing out a sodden sleeve. "Even when
it would've ended things faster."

"Faster isn't the only thing I'm counting." Bane looked back at the ford, quiet now. "Forty of our
own people were standing in the same water I'd have been discharging it into. Fast that costs us
something we didn't have to lose isn't actually fast."
