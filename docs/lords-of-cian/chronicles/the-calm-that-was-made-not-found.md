# The Calm That Was Made, Not Found

*Locked canon, Batch 208, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-906`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XLVI, wave 16. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The second sky-reader called a six-hour window of unusual calm two days out — flat water, slack wind,
conditions clean enough to move the fleet's slower supply hulls through open corridor water without a
storm's cover to hide them, but also without a storm's risk to threaten them. She had called dozens of
windows like it. This one felt, by every measure she knew how to take, exactly the same.

It wasn't the same. A Directorate weather-service defector, embedded three ranks deep in a coastal
signal station, had spent weeks learning to read her network's own relay methods — smoke-column
timing, flag sequences passed hull to hull, the specific cadence of a confirmed call versus a
tentative one — and had spent the two days before the window feeding false confirmations into the
chain at exactly the points the successor's own network trusted without re-verifying.

The calm was real. The six hours were not going to stay calm. A second, faster-moving weather system
the defector's own genuine local knowledge had spotted — and deliberately withheld from the relay —
was closing on the corridor from the north, timed to arrive precisely when the fleet's supply hulls
would be most exposed and least escorted.

"Every signal confirmed clean," the successor said, reviewing the relay chain afterward, once the
deception was understood. "Every single one. I didn't miss a reading. Someone fed me readings that
were never true to begin with."

"That's worse, in a way," Kanja said. "A miss you can study and correct. This isn't a gap in the
method. It's someone standing inside the method, wearing its own voice."

The supply hulls were three hours into the window, committed to open water, when the first wrong
swell arrived.
