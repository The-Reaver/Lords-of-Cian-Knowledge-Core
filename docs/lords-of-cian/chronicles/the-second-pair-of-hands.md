# The Second Pair of Hands

*Locked canon, Batch 278, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1443`). Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle XCV, wave 32. Kanja begins training a field successor for his own testimony-gathering role, extending MCD-483's "replicable rather than personal" theme to the interview side of the method for the first time.*

---

Ezio had trained auditors for years by then — the arithmetic side of the method had already outgrown the two of them, running in districts neither had visited in months. What hadn't been tested was whether the other half could be taught at all: the part that was Kanja himself, sitting first with the worst-treated hand in the yard and letting the testimony come at its own pace before a single figure was written down.

Maret Vos asked to learn it on a slow afternoon between districts, and Kanja almost said no out of the same instinct that had made him gently refuse seven workers who'd once wanted to leave their own lives to follow him. This was different, and it took him a full day of turning it over to see why: Maret wasn't asking to join a crusade. He was asking whether a freed Cestari with no gift for arithmetic could still do the one part of this work that had never been about numbers.

"You've sat across from men holding back the worst of it," Kanja said. "You did it yourself, once, before anyone was writing any of it down."

"That's why I think I can hear it," Maret said. "Not because I'm good at anything. Because I know what it costs to say the true amount out loud."

The test came three weeks later, at a tannery district Kanja deliberately let Maret run alone from the first interview: an old scraper-hand who'd said almost nothing to two prior visitors finally talked for an hour, not because Maret asked better questions but because he waited through silences Kanja himself might have filled too soon. Ezio's later audit of the transcript found nothing to correct.

"He got more out of that man in one sitting than either of us managed in two visits," Ezio said.

"Because he wasn't performing patience. He has it." Kanja set the transcript down beside the pages Ezio had already started keeping of his own trained auditors, the beginning of a matching ledger for a side of the method no one had thought to teach before. "I built the front half of this thinking it needed to be me sitting there. It needed someone willing to actually listen. That was never the same requirement."
