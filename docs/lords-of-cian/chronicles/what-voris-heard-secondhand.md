# What Voris Heard Secondhand

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Crow King Alias Chronicle XLII, wave 14, closing

---

The report on the Voss Corridor withdrawal reached Voris through channels well removed from his own
command, a secondhand account of an engagement he hadn't personally overseen — but the detail that
caught his attention wasn't the tactician's doctrine, nor the draw neither side had won. It was the
single line, buried in the after-action summary, noting that a clear opportunity to rout the
retreating column had gone unused for reasons the report's author plainly hadn't understood well
enough to explain.

Voris understood it immediately, and found himself sitting with the report longer than its length
warranted.

"You look troubled by good news, sir," his own second observed, glancing at the summary over his
shoulder. "He let an entire column walk home. That's a defeat we didn't have to suffer."

"It's not troubling because it's bad news. It's troubling because it's confirming something I've
suspected for years without quite being able to prove." Voris set the report down carefully, the
same way he still handled the scarecrow itself. "I built a career on the theory that his method was
cunning applied without limit — find the seam, exploit it, win as completely as the seam allows.
This says otherwise. This says he found a seam large enough to destroy that column entirely, and
declined to use the full width of it."

"Does that change anything, strategically?"

"Not immediately. But it changes what I've been teaching for years about how to predict him." Voris
considered the report a while longer before setting it fully aside. "I've spent this entire career
studying how far his cunning would go. I never once studied where he chooses to stop it on purpose,
because I assumed, wrongly, that restraint wasn't actually part of the method at all — just an
absence of opportunity. I don't think that's true anymore. I think the restraint might be the harder
half of the craft to counter, because it means he's not simply following the seam wherever it leads.
He's choosing, every time, how far to let it take him. That's not a weakness I can exploit. That's a
discipline I don't think I've ever fully matched myself."
