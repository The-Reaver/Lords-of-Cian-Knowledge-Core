# What He Chose to Share

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-575`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXX, wave 10 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

A second foreign delegation, from a different nation entirely and with no history of the first one's
attempted theft, made a more modest request: not exclusive rights, not the method itself, but advance
warning of storm systems that might affect their own merchant shipping along a stretch of coast they
shared with the Sovereign Trust.

Kanja brought the request to Sephtis directly rather than deciding it alone.

"The last one wanted to own it," Sephtis said. "This one just wants to not lose ships. Those aren't
the same request, and I don't think we should treat them the same."

They settled on a limited arrangement — regional forecasts shared openly, on a delay long enough to
protect any active military advantage, with the underlying method itself still withheld and the
network's own training reserved for those Sephtis chose to teach directly. Not a sale. Not a secret
kept from everyone. Something between the two, decided case by case rather than by a fixed policy
either extreme would have made simpler.

"Why not just teach everyone," Kanja asked, "the way you taught the fishing village's elder?"

"Because she asked to compare what she already half-knew, not to be handed a weapon she'd never
otherwise have had. A nation's shipping office isn't a fishing village. I'll share what keeps people
off rocks and out of storms. I won't hand over the reasoning that turns a fleet's timing into a
weapon, not to anyone who hasn't earned my trust the slow way, the way you did, the way she's earning
it now." He nodded toward the tent where the second sky-reader was working through the delegation's
shipping charts. "That's not me hoarding it. That's me being honest that not every kind of sharing is
the same kind of gift."

The arrangement held, imperfectly but without further incident, for the remainder of the Rebellion.
