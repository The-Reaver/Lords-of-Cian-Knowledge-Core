# The Storm He Chose Not to Use

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-579`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXIV, wave 12 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Two storm systems developed within days of each other, on opposite ends of a coastline where two
separate rebel squadrons — one under Kanja's own direct command, one an allied force he had no
authority over — were each engaged with Trust patrols of comparable strength.

Sephtis and the second sky-reader split the work between them, each tracking one system, and reached
Kanja with the same conclusion delivered from two directions: both storms were real, both usable, but
the resources to fully exploit either — the Trinity's own presence, the coordinated timing a strike
required — existed in only one place at a time.

"I can't be in both storms," Kanja said, reading both forecasts side by side. "Which fleet needs it
more."

"That's not a forecasting question," Sephtis said. "That's yours to answer."

He chose his own squadron's storm, not because it needed the advantage more objectively, but because
it was the one he could commit to with full confidence and full presence, and because splitting the
Trinity's attention between two fights nine hours apart from each other risked doing neither one well.
The allied squadron fought its engagement without the storm-timing advantage its own scouts had hoped
for, and took losses it might not have taken with the doctrine behind it.

"I made the choice I could stand behind," Kanja told the allied squadron's commander afterward, not
offering an excuse for it. "I won't pretend it cost your people nothing. It did. I'd make the same
choice again, because I trust what I can be fully present for over what I'd be dividing myself to
reach, but I won't tell you that makes what your people paid acceptable. It just means I'm not going
to lie to you about why I chose the way I did."

---

*Continuity notes (not narrative): a genuine triage dilemma — two simultaneous storm systems, only
one usable to full effect, with a real cost borne by the fleet not chosen. First entry in the run
where the doctrine's own success in one place is directly paid for by its absence in another. Names
Sephtis and the second sky-reader; opens the twelfth wave.*
