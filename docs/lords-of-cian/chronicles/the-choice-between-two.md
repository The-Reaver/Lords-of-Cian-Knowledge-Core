# The Choice Between Two

*Locked canon, Batch 161, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-506`). Captain Alias Chronicle XIII, first entry in the
fourth wave. Rebellion era. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — the first genuine
no-clean-answer dilemma in this alias's run. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters.*

---

Two separate crew elements came under attack in the same collapsing minutes, miles apart, both
genuinely beyond reach of anything but Kanja's own speed — and even his own speed wasn't enough to
reach both. The choice fell to him alone, with seconds rather than minutes to make it.

He chose the smaller group, the one facing the more immediate, more certain death. The larger group
held its own against worse odds than it should have had to, losing two before reinforcements arrived
without him.

"You made the right call," the crew chief said afterward, reviewing the full account. "The smaller
group would have been wiped out entirely without you there. The larger one had at least a chance on
its own."

"I know the reasoning was sound. I made the same calculation in the moment, and I'd probably make it
again given the same seconds to decide." Kanja didn't dispute the logic, didn't need it repeated back
to him. "That doesn't make the two names from the larger group easier to sit with. I chose to save
one group knowing it meant the other might lose people. That's not the same as a rescue with a clean
outcome. That's a choice with a cost attached no matter which way it went."

"Nobody's blaming you for the choice."

"I'm not asking anyone to blame me. I'm asking that nobody pretend it wasn't a real choice with a real
cost, dressed up as an obviously correct call just because the reasoning holds up under review." He
reviewed the names of the two lost, letting the weight of the decision sit rather than resolving it
into something easier. "The name 'Captain' has to mean I'll make the hard calls honestly, not that
every hard call comes out clean if I make it well enough. Some of them don't, no matter how well I
make them."

---

*Continuity notes (not narrative): the first genuine no-clean-answer command dilemma of Captain's
run, distinct from prior loss entries (an unpreventable collapse, a single-person rescue) in forcing
an active choice between two endangered groups rather than an outcome beyond anyone's control. No
new named characters. First entry in Captain's fourth three-Chronicle wave.*
