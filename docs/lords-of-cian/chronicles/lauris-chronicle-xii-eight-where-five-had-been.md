# Lauris Chronicle XII: Eight Where Five Had Been

*Locked canon, Batch 299, 2026-09-18 (`MCD-1629`). Twelfth entry in Lauris Letitia's own Chronicle
series, the fifth entry of Strand K, drafted as part of a 50-entry wave across eight strand-agents
under Abad's blanket authorization. Dramatizes her arrival at Vask Karth-Ven at age fourteen, her
expanded eight-member Sister-Hold (`MCD-167`), the Vask's mountain-built infrastructure and
sixty-meter Drakma training floor (`MCD-1554`), and her first meetings with instructors Veska
Karth-Ven, Tiramen Karth-Ven, and Voreth Karth-Ven -- including Mira Threnarr-Olmedrin's role making
the introductions, extending her established prior residency at Karth-Ven before her relocation to
Threnarr (`MCD-164`). No new named characters. Full text below.*

-----

*Archive fragment, Vask Karth-Ven, age fourteen: "Five became eight. The floor is stone the mountain
gave up and stone the Vask gave back changed. I am told it has been listening for twenty-eight
thousand years. I do not know yet what it has heard."*

She arrived at Vask Karth-Ven in early autumn by the Threnarr reckoning, though I am told the
smallest of the twelve surviving Vasks kept its own calendar, tied less to season than to the
training floor's own maintenance cycles. Selene stayed the first season, as arranged, and I have
found in her own archive a brief, almost embarrassed admission that she had expected to find the
place forbidding -- a Vask built for combat rather than memory -- and found instead something closer
to reverence than she had prepared herself for.

Vask Karth-Ven held, at the time of Lauris's relocation, roughly four hundred and eighty residents,
the smallest surviving population of the twelve, and every structure I have had described to me was
built directly into the mountain's own spine rather than raised beside it -- training halls cut into
living stone, residence galleries deeper still, deliberation rooms left deliberately unworked, the
rock itself standing in for whatever a grander Vask might have carved into ornament. At its center
sat the training floor: a sixty-meter circle of high-gravity-forged Drakma, in continuous calibration
for twenty-eight thousand years before Lauris ever set foot on it. I have stood on comparable ground
exactly once, on Cian, at a training yard Kanja's own crew maintains, and I did not feel anything
close to what Lauris describes feeling that first morning -- that the floor was not simply surface
beneath her but something closer to an instrument that had, across twenty-eight millennia of
combatants, been slowly learning what a body was.

The Sister-Hold that received her there numbered eight rather than the five she had known at
Threnarr -- Selene, Drenneth, Veth, and Olda retaining their places by correspondence and occasional
visit rather than daily presence, with Mira Threnarr-Olmedrin the one member of her original Hold to
remain fully resident, since Mira had herself trained and taught at Karth-Ven before her own
relocation to Threnarr years earlier for Lauris's early conditioning. It was Mira, by every account I
have gathered, who made the introductions rather than any formal Karth-Ven officer: Veska Karth-Ven,
senior combat instructor and keeper of the Vask's cooperative-era archives, a woman Lauris's own
later writing describes with a respect she extends to almost no one else in her long life; Tiramen
Karth-Ven, the Vask's adaptive-methodology specialist, whose particular gift, Mira told her plainly on
the first day, was building training that did not yet exist for problems that did not yet have names;
and Voreth Karth-Ven, the junior instructor of the three, closest to Lauris in relative age, assigned
within the week as her primary sparring partner.

I have read Lauris's account of that first meeting with Voreth more than once, because it is, among
her Kares Prime entries, one of the rare ones that permits itself something close to humor. Voreth,
by her own record, looked at the density figure in her transfer file, looked at the fourteen-year-old
standing in front of her, and said only: *The number and the girl do not yet agree with each other.
We will see which one is lying.* Lauris's own closing note for the day: *Neither of us was lying. It
took Voreth the better part of a year to stop expecting one of us to be.*

Veska's welcome, by contrast, was formal in the way Lauris herself would come to favor throughout her
own long life -- plain, unadorned, and, I think, genuinely meant. She is recorded as telling the
assembled Hold, on Lauris's first evening at Karth-Ven, that the Vask had trained combatants who
exceeded every projection the archive held, and had never yet trained one whose projection had
exceeded the archive's own capacity to make one. *We do not know what we are calibrating for,* Veska
is recorded saying. *We will calibrate anyway. That is what the floor is for.*

I do not know, transcribing this now, whether the floor Veska spoke of ever did finish learning
Lauris's shape, or whether -- as I suspect, having watched her fight on ground far younger than that
mountain's own stone -- no floor on any world was ever built long enough for the task.
