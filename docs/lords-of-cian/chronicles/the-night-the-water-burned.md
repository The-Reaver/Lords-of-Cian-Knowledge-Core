# The Night the Water Burned

*Locked canon, Batch 245, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1071`); corrected Batch 321, 2026-10-02 (Long-Mask-era
post-Mafesto gear -- the Sovereign Eyes, Breath Collar, and Ironfall Boots -- doesn't exist yet in
this Rebellion-era, still-live-Trinity window; swapped for Mafesto's own built-in HUD/armor/boots).
Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle
LXI, wave 21, first entry in the wave. A detailed naval Trinity combat showcase against a fireship
ambush. Not a territory Annals entry. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Six hulks came down the strait with the tide, low in the water and already burning — hulls packed
with pitch-soaked timber and set loose from a hidden cove to drift on the current straight into the
anchored convoy *The Ledger* was escorting through the narrows. No flag on any of them, no crew
visible, nothing to negotiate with or frighten off. Just fire, aimed by current and wind at ships
that couldn't outrun either in the dark.

Dol Maren read the drift pattern before anyone else understood what they were looking at — six
separate burn-rates, six slightly different headings, converging on a spread wide enough to catch
whichever way the convoy tried to scatter. "They're not random," he said. "Somebody timed the release
for this exact tide."

Kanja went over *The Ledger*'s rail into the smoke without waiting for the order to form. Mafesto's own
helm overlay cut through what his bare vision couldn't — six drift vectors
rendered clean against the dark and the glare, updating faster than the smoke could hide them, and he
read the gap between the nearest two hulks a full breath before either would have closed it. Mafesto's
Kinetic Transfer System took the first hard impact meant for the overlay itself: a burning spar,
already collapsing off one hulk's rigging, absorbed and redirected into the water instead of across
the deck of the convoy ship it would have struck. Obsidian Malice discharged twice, precise strikes
against two tow-lines still faintly visible where the hulks had been staged in pairs before release —
severed clean, breaking the coordinated spread into six separate, more survivable problems instead of
one converging trap.

That left three still on line for impact. Onyx of Oblivion carried him aboard the nearest through
Whisper of Shadows before the heat made the deck impassable, and Soulbound Edge found the lashed
tiller under a foot of already-charring planking and cut it free in one motion — enough to haul the
burning hulk hard off its heading by main strength before the fire reached anything that would have
made letting go the only option. The second, he simply put his shoulder into, boots planted, and rode
the collision down to a glancing scrape along *The Ledger*'s reinforced flank instead of a square hit.
The third burned out on its own drift, twenty yards short, having lost its line the moment its
partner's tow-cable went.

On deck, Mafesto's own sealed plating held the worst of the smoke out of his lungs long enough
to keep working, and when two men used the chaos to try to board from a skiff hidden behind the
fourth hulk's smoke, a single stomp through Mafesto's boots sent a tremor through the deck that put
both of them down before either drew a blade.

By the time the last hulk guttered against open water, the convoy was scorched but whole — one hull
holed above the waterline, no lives lost on either ship. Pell Ostra, already moving to check the
convoy's cargo holds for anything the heat had reached, allowed it was the first time she'd seen fire
used as a weapon with no one aboard to aim it. "Whoever built that spread wanted a massacre they
didn't have to watch."

"Then they'll be disappointed," Kanja said, "and we'll be asking around the cove at the mouth of this
strait until we know who."
