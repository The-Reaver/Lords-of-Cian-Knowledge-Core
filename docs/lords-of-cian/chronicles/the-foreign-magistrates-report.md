# The Foreign Magistrate's Report

*Locked canon, Batch 260, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1261`). Crow King Alias Chronicle LXIX, wave 23, closing
it. A neutral Sovereign Trust magistrate formally documents the phenomenon for an official
cross-border record. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

The magistrate had no jurisdiction over anything Kanja had done and no intention of pretending
otherwise. She'd come from the Sovereign Trust side of a border neither side of the Rebellion fully
controlled, not to judge, she said at the outset, but to write something down properly for once --
an official record, filed under her own seal, distinct from the folk exaggeration that had already
reached three settlements in three different, contradictory shapes.

"I've read the version where you're seven feet tall and made of smoke," she told him, without much
ceremony. "I've read the version where the crown is real gold and grants you the voices of the dead.
I'd like to write down what actually happened at the places I can verify, so that when historians on
my side of this border ask what the Crow King was, there's at least one document that isn't a
campfire story."

He gave her three hours, at a card-table's length of distance, and answered everything she asked
plainly -- the marsh, the Braid, the mountain flags, the mistake with the farming family, the
regional breach that cost two lives and was never fully recovered. She wrote all of it down,
including the failures, without softening either direction.

"Most legends don't survive the addition of their own failures," she said, closing her ledger. "This
one might. That's unusual enough that I intend to note it specifically."

"Why does it matter to you what gets written down?" he asked her.

"Because in another hundred years somebody on my side of this border will need to decide whether a
story like this one is possible, and they'll have nothing to go on but whatever survives in writing.
I'd rather they have something closer to true than something closer to comfortable."

The report went into the Sovereign Trust's own formal archive under her seal -- the first time the
Crow King's actual, unembellished history existed as an institutional document on either side of the
conflict that had given him the name.
