# The Student Who Walked Away

*Locked canon, Batch 220, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-980`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle LI, wave 17. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The third student — the one with a sailor's instinct and no patience for the mathematics under it —
made his first solo call two weeks after Sephtis judged him ready. He called clear water for a
supply run through a narrow coastal cut. The water was clear. The call was right.

It was also, he would say later, the worst thing that could have happened to him, because it meant the
next wrong call felt like a debt coming due rather than an ordinary risk of the craft.

The wrong call came a month after that — a minor one, a squall he read as passing north that instead
clipped the edge of a fishing settlement's harbor, damaging two boats and injuring no one. By any
measure Sephtis or the successor would apply to their own long records, it was a small, survivable
miss, the kind both of them had made and outlived a dozen times over.

He didn't see it that way.

"I can't do the part where I'm wrong in front of people," he told the successor, three days after the
squall, before she'd said a single word of correction to him. "I thought I could. I watched you do it
and it looked like courage. It isn't courage. It's just something you can carry that I can't."

"You're wrong about one thing," she said. "It never stops feeling like this. Not for me. Not for
Sephtis, after everything he's called right. You're not failing at carrying it easily. Nobody carries
it easily. You're finding out whether you can carry it at all."

He thought about that for a long moment.

"I can't," he said finally. "Not the way the fishermen looked at me after. Not the way I felt after.
I'd rather build something honest with my hands and know exactly what I built than call the sky and
spend the rest of my life not sure if I'm brave or just lucky."

Sephtis, when told, didn't argue the decision or try to talk him back into it.

"That's not a failure of teaching," he said. "That's the same lesson landing on two different kinds
of person and taking root in only one of them. The craft was never going to be for everyone who could
learn its mathematics. It was only ever going to be for the ones who could survive being wrong in
front of people who needed them to be right."

The student left the school with no shame attached to his name, and took up honest work with his
hands within the month, exactly as he'd said he would.

— competence at the mathematics is not the same as the emotional capacity to be visibly, publicly
wrong with lives on the line, the same cost Sephtis's own "The Weight Sephtis Carried Alone" (MCD-427)
first named as the real job. Distinct from every prior forecasting-failure entry: the loss here is not
a missed call's consequence but one capable student's honest, undramatic recognition that the craft's
emotional cost isn't his to carry, a genuinely new register for this sub-series. Names Sephtis and his
successor; the departing student is described by trait, not individually named. No new named
characters.*
