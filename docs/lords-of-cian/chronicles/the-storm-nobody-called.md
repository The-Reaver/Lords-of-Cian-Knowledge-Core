# The Storm Nobody Called

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-580`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXV, wave 12 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The allied squadron's commander had never trained his crews to fight inside a storm the way Kanja's
own doctrine had — his scouts had requested the sky-reading in the hope of borrowing an advantage, not
built an entire tactical approach expecting to receive it, and when the storm broke over his own
engagement unaided, it arrived as a hazard rather than a weapon, exactly as it would have for any
fleet that had never studied the Trinity's methods.

Ships that had never drilled for low-visibility coordination scattered further than they needed to.
Two collided in confusion neither crew would have risked in clear water. The Trust patrol they were
engaged with, better drilled for storm conditions on its own home coast, pressed the advantage the
squadron's own disorder handed it.

It was, in nearly every particular, the mirror of what Kanja's crew inflicted on enemy fleets when the
doctrine worked as intended — except here it was landing on an ally, with no forecast bought in
advance to soften it, no Trinity boarding action to turn the chaos back around.

The squadron held the line anyway, at a cost heavier than the engagement should have demanded, through
raw seamanship and a refusal to break formation that owed nothing to any storm-timing doctrine at all.

"We survived it," the squadron's second officer said afterward, assessing damage that a forecast could
have halved. "We didn't win it the way you win these. We just didn't lose."

"That's still worth something," Kanja told him later, having heard the full account. "A storm doesn't
care whose side called it. Yours held through one that was working against you as hard as it usually
works for me. That's not a smaller kind of skill. It might be a harder one."
