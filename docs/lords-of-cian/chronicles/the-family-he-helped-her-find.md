# The Family He Helped Her Find

*Locked canon, Batch 224, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1020`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LV, wave 19. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

She'd been freed off a fishing-fleet raid eleven years earlier, a grown woman by then, and she found
the crescent-sailed ship in harbor by asking three different dockmasters where it was rumored to
resupply, which told Kanja before she said a word that she'd worked hard to find him on purpose.

"I'm not here to be rescued," she said, once she was aboard and no longer needing to raise her voice
over harbor noise. "I'm here because you people keep records nobody else keeps. My brother was sold
off a different ship four years before I was taken, different buyer, different route. I've never
stopped trying to trace where he went. I thought if anyone's ledger crossed his, it might be yours."

Garren Hask didn't promise anything before he'd looked — he never did — but three days of cross-
referencing his own tally against the fragmentary manifests other freed crews had smuggled out over
the decades turned up a name that matched close enough to be worth the risk of hope: a settlement
two weeks' sail south, built partly by survivors of a raid the Scourge himself had run eight years
after her own.

"I can't promise it's him," Hask told her, closing the ledger over the page he'd found. "I can
promise the name's real and the settlement's real, and that's more than either of us had yesterday."

They sailed her there themselves, out of the way of any planned raid, and stood back at the edge of
the settlement while she walked in alone to ask after the name on the ledger. Kanja never learned
whether the reunion that followed was the one she'd hoped for — he made a point of not asking, and she
made a point of not telling him, thanking him only with a look before she disappeared into the
settlement to find out for herself.
