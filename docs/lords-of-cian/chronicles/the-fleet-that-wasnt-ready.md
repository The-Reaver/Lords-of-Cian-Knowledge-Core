# The Fleet That Wasn't Ready

*Locked canon, Batch 258, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1223`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle
LXXXV, wave 29, first entry in the wave. A genuine vulnerability register — the fleet caught mid-refit
and under-strength. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

*The Ledger* was three weeks into a hull refit when the attack came, half her deck planking stripped
for replacement, a third of her crew ashore on rotation, and Dol Maren's careful repair schedule
utterly unprepared for the six Trust raiders that closed on the anchorage at first light, well outside
the pattern any prior enemy had ever attacked on.

There was no clean Trinity showcase available for what followed, and Kanja knew it within the first
thirty seconds — *The Audit* and *The Receipt* were two days out on separate patrol routes, too far to
reach the anchorage before the raiders closed, and *The Ledger* herself couldn't maneuver properly
with her own deck torn open for repair.

"We fight from where we are," Kanja said, "with what we've got, and it's not going to look like it
usually does."

It didn't. Obsidian Malice discharged from a half-repaired deck that groaned under every recoil,
Mafesto absorbing impacts that came from awkward angles a fully crewed, fully rigged flagship would
never have allowed to develop. Onyx of Oblivion carried him ship to ship across gaps the crew's own
emergency rigging had to improvise on the spot, Cadence Ruin disrupting the raiders' coordination just
enough to buy Dol Maren's skeleton repair crew time to shore up the weakest section of hull before a
second raider could exploit it.

It wasn't a clean victory. Two raiders escaped rather than the usual complete disabling, and *The
Ledger* took real damage on top of the refit already underway, setting the repair schedule back weeks.
Pell Ostra, pulled off her own work to help hold a breach, said as much plainly once the last raider
had broken off. "We won. Doesn't feel like the wins usually do."

"Because it isn't," Kanja said. "Every fight before this one, we picked the ground or the timing
worked in our favor by luck as much as skill. This one, we just weren't ready, and we got through it
anyway because the crew held even when the ship couldn't."

Garren Hask logged it honestly, refusing to smooth the entry into a cleaner victory than it had been —
two raiders escaped, real damage taken, the refit set back, and the fleet's own vulnerability, laid
bare for the first time in years, recorded plainly alongside everything else.
