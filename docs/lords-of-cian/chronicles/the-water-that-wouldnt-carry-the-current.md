# The Water That Wouldn't Carry the Current

*Locked canon, Batch 196, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-697`). Bane Alias Chronicle XXXII, wave 11 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The flooded lowland fight at Verrow Marsh was the first engagement where Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer
System simply didn't do what it had always done. The armor's whole doctrine — absorb an incoming
blow, ground the force through conductive contact, redirect what the body alone couldn't survive —
depended on solid transfer through solid ground, and Verrow Marsh offered nothing but knee-deep
water and silt that swallowed momentum instead of carrying it anywhere useful at all.

A Suppression detachment had chosen the ground deliberately, someone among them evidently having
studied the Black Trench closely enough to notice what every other engagement since had missed: that
the Trinity's grounding mechanism needed something to ground *into*, and a marsh gave it nothing but
mud that absorbed force the same inert way it absorbed footsteps.

Bane felt the first mistimed block land wrong — force that should have transferred clean through his
stance instead staying with him, jarring up through the frame in a way the armor hadn't made him
feel since before it was bonded. He adjusted inside two exchanges, not by fighting the marsh's
physics but by abandoning the doctrine that assumed solid ground entirely: less absorption, more
raw displacement, trusting his own strength and Onyx's reach over a grounding system the terrain had
quietly taken away from him.

"They built this ambush around a weakness," Pell Ostra said afterward, once the detachment had
broken and the marsh had gone quiet again, studying the mud with the same attention she gave a
charge line. "That's not luck. Somebody's actually studying how the armor works, not just what it
does."

"They're right to. It is a weakness." Bane didn't dismiss the observation, checked the frame where
the mistimed block had jarred it, the ache of raw absorbed force still sitting in the joint under the
plate. "The system needs ground to work the way it's built to work. No ground, no transfer. I got
through it today because I stopped trying to use a system that had already stopped working and
fought without it instead. That's not a fix. That's just what's left when the fix isn't available."

He had the crew study the marsh engagement for weeks afterward, the first real gear limitation
anyone had catalogued since the original Deficiency work — proof that whoever was building
countermeasures against him had moved past guessing and into genuine study, and that the Trinity, for
all its power, was not invulnerable to ground it simply couldn't use.
