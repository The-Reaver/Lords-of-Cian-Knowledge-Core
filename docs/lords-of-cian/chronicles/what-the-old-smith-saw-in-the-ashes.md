# What the Old Smith Saw in the Ashes

*Locked canon, Batch 189, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-554`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XVIII, closing
the fifth wave. Rebellion era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign. Not a territory Chronicle. New
standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The campaign's senior smith, present for nearly every burning, every rebuild, every crisis the
Rolling Foundry Campaign had thrown at Kanja across its full length, had watched the toll accumulate
in ways the crew's own younger hands rarely noticed — the same tireless reputation the legend
described, carrying a weariness underneath it that never made it into any story told about the
campaign.

"You don't sleep much, these days," she said, finding him alone at the fifth site's own forge late
into the night the day after the five-site attack, the fires long banked but Kanja still awake beside
them. "I've watched you work every crisis this campaign's thrown at us without once looking like it
costs you anything. Tonight's the first time I've seen the cost show."

"It's always cost something. I've just been careful about when it's allowed to show." Kanja didn't
deny the weariness, didn't perform strength he didn't currently have. "Five sites in one hour was
closer to the edge than I let the crew see at the time. I don't regret that choice — panicking the
crew wouldn't have saved anything faster. I'm allowed to let it catch up with me now, once there's
nobody left who needs to see otherwise."

"You don't have to carry that alone, you know. Not every night."

"I know. I think I forget it more often than I should." He allowed the admission more openly than he
usually did, the old smith's own decades of quiet trust making the honesty easier than it would have
been with almost anyone else. "Most people see the burnings, the rebuilds, the legend of it holding
together no matter what's thrown at it. Nobody sees this part unless I let them, and I don't let
many people see it at all."

"I've watched you build this whole campaign out of exactly this kind of cost, paid quietly, over and
over, so the rest of us never had to carry it ourselves." The old smith settled beside him at the
banked forge, no further words needed for a while. "I don't think the legend usually makes room for
this part. I think it should. This is the part that actually built everything else."
