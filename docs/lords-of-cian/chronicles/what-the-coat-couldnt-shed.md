# What Mafesto Couldn't Shed

*Locked canon, Batch 262, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1322`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle LXXVI, first
entry in the twenty-sixth wave. Rebellion era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign (MCD-241). A
gear-vulnerability entry showing real Mafesto plating damage and improvised field repair. Not a
territory Annals entry. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. Corrected Batch 321, 2026-10-02: the
damaged item was originally written as the Forge-Coat's Dark-Drakma leather, gear that doesn't
exist until the Long Mask (ages 33-314); the damage is now Mafesto's own Void-Lattice plating,
which is live and wearable at age 27.*

---

The crack happened the ordinary way most real damage happened — not in a dramatic exchange but in
an unglamorous accident, a collapsing scaffold catching Mafesto's shoulder plating against a
jagged support beam during an otherwise unremarkable rebuild, splitting the Void-Lattice panel
along a hairline fracture long enough that its light-absorptive quality simply stopped functioning
across the whole section. Torchlight caught the exposed metal where it should have vanished into
it.

"There's no spare panel for this," the senior smith's successor said, examining the cracked
plating in the terrace's evening light, tracing the fracture's edge with a professional's eye
rather than a tailor's. "Whatever first gave Mafesto its light-eating quality took years to work
out. We don't have years."

Kanja had taken minor scuffs and dents to Mafesto's plating before, nothing deeper than the
surface. He hadn't gone without a whole panel's worth of it, and the difference showed the very
next night, a routine patrol nearly catching sight of him at a range the plating should have made
impossible, torchlight glinting where the dark should have swallowed it whole.

The fix, when it came, wasn't a repair in any sense the original craft would have recognized — the
successor working from what she actually had on hand rather than what the ideal treatment called
for, a stopgap compound of rendered fat, ground charcoal, and a thin cut of salvaged Dead Drakma
dust pressed into the fracture by hand until it held a rough approximation of the light-eating
surface, ugly and uneven and nowhere near as effective as the original, but enough to blunt the
worst of what the crack had exposed.

"It's not what it was," she warned him, checking the patch under torchlight one more time before
calling it finished. "Maybe two-thirds of the original function, if that. It'll need real repair
the first chance we get somewhere that actually has the materials."

"Two-thirds is enough to not get seen by accident," Kanja said, testing the shoulder's give.
"That's the only promise I actually needed tonight."

He wore the patched plating for six weeks before a stop finally carried the right materials for a
real repair, and in that time learned something about the gear he'd built his whole persona's
caution around — that its silence and its shadow were a manufactured advantage, not an inherent
one, and that the man wearing it was never quite as invisible as Mafesto alone made him feel.
