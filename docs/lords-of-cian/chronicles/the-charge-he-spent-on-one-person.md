# The Charge He Spent on One Person

*Locked canon, Batch 127, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-429`). Captain Alias Chronicle V. Rebellion era, a
detailed Trinity showcase built around a single crew member's rescue. Not a territory Chronicle.
New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters beyond the
already-locked Pell Ostra.*

---

Pell Ostra was setting the last demolition charge of the operation when the structure above her
began collapsing early, six minutes ahead of the timeline she'd calculated with her usual exacting
precision, and the crew's entire extraction plan had never accounted for anyone still being inside
the building when it happened.

Kanja was through the collapsing entryway before the dust had finished settling enough to see by.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System absorbed the falling debris directly, converting each impact into
stored charge rather than let a single collapsing support beam slow him down, the exoskeleton reading
structural failure the same way it had once read chemical fog and open darkness — not by sight, which
the dust denied him, but by the pressure and weight of each obstacle transmitted straight through the
plating. Obsidian Malice discharged upward once, a single precisely angled strike clearing a
collapsed section that would have taken minutes to move by hand, buying a path through rubble that
should have taken far longer than the ninety seconds it actually cost him.

He found her pinned beneath a support beam, conscious, calm in the specific way of someone who talks
to demolition charges rather than people and had apparently decided to talk herself through this too.
"The whole east wall's coming down in about forty seconds," she said, matter-of-fact even now. "You
should probably hurry."

He didn't discharge Obsidian Malice a second time — the war club's recharge cycle wouldn't have
cleared in time, and there wasn't room in the collapsing space for a second strike regardless. He
lifted the beam directly instead, Mafesto's absorbed charge from the entry converted now into raw
physical output, and carried her clear through the same path Obsidian Malice had opened, emerging
into open air twelve seconds before the east wall came down behind them in full.

"You used the war club's whole charge clearing a path for one person," the crew chief said afterward,
watching Ostra get checked over by the field medic, already talking through the mechanics of what had
gone wrong with the timing calculation instead of her own close call. "That's not how the doctrine
usually works. Usually you're conserving the charge for the bigger fight."

"There wasn't a bigger fight today. There was one person under a beam with forty seconds left." Kanja
checked his own hands, dust-covered but steady. "The doctrine's for when I have to choose between
outcomes. Today there wasn't a choice to make. There was just Pell, and forty seconds, and every
piece of equipment I had that could close that gap in time."
