# The Fleet She Called Alone

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-588`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XLIII, wave 15 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

By the time a Trust successor fleet moved to reclaim a stretch of contested water Kanja's allies had
held for a generation, Sephtis had stepped back from active forecasting almost entirely, his eyes no
longer sharp enough to read the finer detail of a distant chart the way he once had. The forecast for
the coming engagement — an entire fleet's worth of timing, coordination, and storm-based tactics,
not a single prediction handed off for one battle — fell to the second sky-reader alone, with no one
left to check her work before the fleet committed to it.

She spent eleven days building the forecast the way Sephtis had taught her decades earlier, and three
more days doing something he had never needed to teach her at all: presenting it to a fleet commander
without an older, more trusted name standing behind her to vouch for it.

"He always signed off on the timing before," the fleet's senior captain said, studying her charts with
open skepticism. "I don't see his mark on this one."

"You won't," she said. "This one's mine. If it's wrong, it's mine to answer for. I'd rather you trust
the work than the name attached to it, but I understand if you need time before you're willing to."

He gave her three days to prove the method rather than the reputation, tracking her shorter-range
predictions against his own instruments before committing the fleet to her full timing. She was right
each time, and the fleet committed to the storm-timed engagement on her forecast alone — the first
time in the doctrine's history that an entire fleet action ran on the second sky-reader's judgment
with no one senior to catch an error if she'd made one.
