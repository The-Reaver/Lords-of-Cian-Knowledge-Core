# The District Behind the Quarantine Line

*Locked canon, Batch 256, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1158`). Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle LXXIV, wave 25. A fever quarantine seals an entire district; the method is worked for weeks entirely from outside the sealed line through a single trusted courier, unable to enter at all.*

---

The fever reached the weaving district four days before the crew did, and the Trust physicians sealed the whole quarter within a week — a hard line, guarded, no one in or out except a narrow medical exemption, while the district's own wage dispute sat exactly where it had been the morning the sickness started. Kanja and Ezio arrived to find a line they weren't permitted to cross and a case they had no way to abandon, since the workers inside were, by every account reaching them, being paid nothing at all during the quarantine on the administrator's argument that no production meant no wages owed — an argument Ezio suspected, correctly, was being used to erase a debt that had predated the fever by months.

There was no way to take testimony directly. What there was, eventually, after two days of asking at the checkpoint, was a woman — a midwife with standing medical exemption to cross the line for deliveries the quarantine couldn't wait out — willing to carry questions in and answers out, memorized rather than written, since the guards searched anything carried on paper for fear of the fever traveling on it.

It made the work slower and stranger than anything the method had done before. Ezio couldn't watch a testifier's face for the small tells that usually told him where to press. He built the case instead from what the courier could reliably remember and repeat across a dozen crossings — worker names, remembered shortfall figures, the administrator's own stated justification relayed back word for word — cross-referenced against a partial production log a sympathetic clerk inside the line managed to have the courier memorize a page at a time.

"I can't verify half of what I'd normally check," Ezio admitted, the fourth night, laying out what the memorized testimony had produced so far. "I'm building a case on secondhand memory instead of documents I've held myself."

"Then build it as carefully as the case allows and say plainly what it doesn't allow," Kanja told him. What they produced, after nineteen days of relayed questions and answers, was a finding explicitly marked as provisional in its weakest points — the pre-fever shortfall was documented with full confidence, since it predated the quarantine and matched records the crew could check directly once the line finally lifted; the administrator's no-work-no-wage argument during the sealed weeks themselves was recorded as disputed but unresolved, honestly flagged rather than guessed at, pending direct testimony once the district reopened.

When the quarantine finally lifted six weeks later, the crew returned and closed the provisional gaps in two days flat, the memorized secondhand case having held up against every document they could now check directly. The administrator's wage freeze during the sealed weeks turned out to be exactly the pretext Ezio had suspected — no legal basis, invented to cover months of unrelated theft the fever had briefly let him hide behind. But the finding's honesty about what it hadn't been able to verify while the line stood, Ezio said afterward, was what let the district trust the fuller version once it finally arrived.
