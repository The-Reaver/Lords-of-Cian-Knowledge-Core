# The Insurance They Tried to Buy

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Scourge Alias Chronicle LIV, wave 18, closing the
wave. Long Mask era, age 135, V3 gear. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose. No new named characters.*

---

The broker who came aboard under a parley flag didn't represent a single captain or a single ship —
he represented a consortium of six trading houses that ran captives alongside legitimate cargo, and
he'd come to propose what he called, without any apparent embarrassment, insurance.

"A standing payment," he said, "quarterly, discreet, routed through accounts that won't trace to us
or to you. In exchange, our six houses' vessels sail your waters unmolested. We're not asking you to
stop the trade elsewhere. Only to leave ours alone."

"You're asking me to sell the one thing that isn't mine to sell," Kanja said.

"Everyone has a price. We've simply offered to pay it in a currency more useful than the satisfaction
of burning our ships."

"You've mistaken the reputation for a service I provide. It isn't a service. It's a fact about what
happens to a slaving vessel that crosses these waters, and facts don't take payment to remain facts."
He let the silence sit a moment before he went on. "But I'll give you something better than the
insurance you came for. Six months. Wind your houses down, convert what you can to legitimate trade
the way others already have, and I'll treat the transition itself as none of my business."

"And if we don't?"

"Then in six months you'll have your answer to the question you actually came here to ask, which
wasn't about price. It was whether the reputation is real."

Four of the six houses folded their operations within the season, converting hulls and crews to
freight running rather than test the deadline. The remaining two didn't survive to see it expire.
