# Two Sites, One Night

*Locked canon, Batch 194, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-634`). The Trench Monarch Alias Chronicle XXIX, wave 10 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The two warnings arrived within minutes of each other, carried by separate runners, describing two
separate threats on opposite ends of the canal district: a punitive raid forming against Ferrymouth,
still recovering from the Deepcut loss, and a hostage-taking already underway at Marrow Landing,
where a foreman's own family had been seized to force a retraction of the five-district agreement.

There was not enough crew, and not enough time, to answer both.

"Ferrymouth can't hold without us," Danne Sok said, already reaching for gear. "Not after Deepcut.
They'll lose the site the same way, maybe worse."

"Marrow Landing has a family in a room with a knife to it right now. That's not a site. That's
people, tonight, with no time for a tally or a negotiation to save them." Kanja was already moving,
the decision made faster than he was comfortable with, and said so plainly. "I don't have a way to
make this the right choice. I only have a way to make it the choice I can live with. We go to Marrow
Landing. Ferrymouth is a site. It can be rebuilt if it falls. A family in that room right now cannot
be rebuilt if we're too late."

Marrow Landing was saved — the hostage-takers found less prepared than the threat's phrasing had
implied, Onyx ending it in under two minutes, the family unharmed. Ferrymouth, undefended, fell to
the punitive raid within the hour, its own five-district signatory documents burned in the same fire
that took half the dredge line's equipment.

"We saved the people," Sok said afterward, watching the smoke rise from Ferrymouth's direction while
Marrow Landing's freed family was still being tended to. "That has to count for something."

"It does. It has to. I'm not going to pretend the choice was wrong, because I don't believe it was."
Kanja didn't look away from the smoke either. "But Ferrymouth's people are going to wake up tomorrow
to a burned line and no crew there to explain why, and some of them are going to hear that we chose
somewhere else over them, and they won't be wrong to feel that, even if I'd make the same choice
again tonight. I don't get to have saved Marrow Landing and also not have cost Ferrymouth something
real. Both of those things happened at once, because I could only be in one place."

"You're not going to tell them it worked out for the best."

"No. It didn't work out for the best. It worked out for the best of two costs, which isn't the same
thing, and I think Ferrymouth deserves to hear that difference from me directly, not hear a version
where I found a way to make the loss sound like it didn't happen."

He went to Ferrymouth himself the next morning, and told them exactly that.
