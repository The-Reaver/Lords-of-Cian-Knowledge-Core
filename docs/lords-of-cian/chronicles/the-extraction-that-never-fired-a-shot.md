# The Extraction That Never Fired a Shot

*Locked canon, Batch 134, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-449`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle VII, first entry in
the third wave. Rebellion era, age 23. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated
in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The prisoner convoy moved under heavy guard through open country with no marsh, no tree line, no
terrain feature of any kind for the Hymn-Engine's usual tricks to hide behind — a deliberate route
choice by a Directorate escort commander who'd studied the Crow King's every prior encounter and
concluded, correctly, that the deception relied on cover the open road simply didn't offer.

He hadn't accounted for deception that didn't need cover at all.

Three days before the convoy moved, false signal-fires had already begun appearing at irregular
intervals along a dozen unrelated patrol routes miles from the actual transfer corridor — the Hymn-
Engine's resonance seeded not into the terrain the convoy would cross, but into the reporting network
the escort commander himself relied on to confirm his route was secure. By the morning of the
transfer, half the Directorate's regional patrol structure was chasing signals that led nowhere,
convinced the real threat lay somewhere it demonstrably wasn't.

The convoy itself never saw an ambush. It didn't need to. Two guards posted at the rear wagon simply
found their own relief patrol arriving four hours early, credentials checked and confirmed against a
schedule that had been quietly altered days before through the same reporting network already
flooded with false signals — and by the time the actual relief patrol arrived on schedule, the
prisoners were gone, the wagon empty, and no combat had occurred anywhere along the convoy's route at
all.

"You didn't fight a single guard," the freed prisoners' own leader said afterward, still working out
how the extraction had actually happened. "I don't understand how that's possible against an armed
escort."

"Because the escort never knew there was anything to fight. The Hymn-Engine doesn't need a marsh or a
tree line to work. It needs a network that trusts its own reports, and every network trusts its own
reports until it's given a specific reason not to." Kanja didn't linger, already moving the freed
prisoners toward the extraction route. "The commander who planned this route was right that the open
ground gave me nothing to hide behind. He just didn't realize the ground was never what I needed to
hide in."
