# The Nine Bridges in One Night

*Locked canon, Batch 197, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-735`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XL, wave 14 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Nine bridges connected the Trust's forward supply depot to its front-line garrisons, spread across
forty miles of river country, and stranding the depot required all nine down within the same night —
before dawn patrols could confirm the first failures and warn the rest to reinforce or evacuate ahead
of the pattern being understood.

No single engagement had ever asked the doctrine to move this fast across this much ground.

Kanja split the work with rebel sapper teams at six of the nine crossings, each team carrying detailed
diagnostic notes he'd prepared in advance from days of prior reconnaissance — exact resonance
signatures, exact discharge timing, technique reduced to instructions precise enough that trained
sappers without his own biology could execute them correctly under a hard deadline. He took the
remaining three himself, the structurally complex crossings no written instruction could fully
substitute for.

The first six came down within two hours of each other, sapper teams working from his notes with a
confidence the weeks of prior training had earned them. Kanja's own three followed in sequence, each
one diagnosed and dropped in the gaps between covering the sapper teams' exposed positions, the full
night's work completed with less than an hour of darkness to spare before the earliest patrols would
have found the first crossing already gone.

"Nine bridges, one night, six of them without you standing there," the rebel sapper commander said at
dawn, surveying reports arriving from all nine positions within minutes of each other. "I didn't think
the doctrine traveled without you carrying it personally."

"It doesn't, not entirely — there's still a difference between what I can do and what I can teach
someone else to do reliably from notes. But six competent teams executing a documented technique is
worth more, some nights, than one man trying to reach nine crossings across forty miles before dawn.
I built the doctrine. I don't think it was ever meant to stay something only I could use."

The depot woke to a supply network with no bridges left connecting it to anything, nine separate
failures too widely spread and too precisely timed to read as anything but a single coordinated
intention.
