# The Signal That Meant Run

*Locked canon, Batch 257, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1184`). Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle LXXIII, wave 25, opening the wave. A mass strategic evacuation entry -- the resistance must flood a civilian-occupied tunnel section to deny the enemy a breakthrough, raising the stakes of self-inflicted demolition beyond any prior entry.*

---

The order came down from the resistance command Kanja himself answered to, and for once the engineering problem was the easy part. A Trust breakthrough was hours from reaching a critical junction, and the only certain way to deny it was to flood the connecting gallery before the enemy could use it -- the same gallery three hundred sheltering civilians had been using as their safest ground for eleven days.

"Same tunnel I built the crossing through," he said, reading the order twice. "Now I'm supposed to drown it."

Unlike the causeway he'd once demolished to deny a relief column -- empty of anyone but soldiers when the charges went in -- this gallery had families in it, elderly people who couldn't move fast, children who wouldn't understand the countdown even if someone explained it. Flooding it on the timeline command wanted meant flooding it with people still inside unless the evacuation matched the engineering perfectly, and there was no margin left to get both wrong.

He split the work into two clocks running against each other. Mafesto's kinetic transfer mapped the flood's actual timing against the sluice gate's true capacity, buying every extra minute the physics would honestly allow rather than the minute command had estimated from a rough guess. Obsidian Malice stood ready at the gate itself, a single discharge from full breach the instant the last person cleared, and not one second before regardless of how close the Trust column got.

Efa Gol ran the evacuation itself, calling the count aloud from person to person rather than trusting a headcount that could be wrong by exactly the number that mattered. Three hundred and eleven people came through the exit gallery in nineteen minutes, the last of them a boy carried out by Corren Halst with seconds of margin nobody wanted to have cut that close twice.

Kanja breached the gate the moment Gol's final count reached him, the crossing he'd once used to move an entire crew now doing the opposite work, and stood in the water's rising sound long enough to be sure no cry came back up through it before he let himself believe they'd made it.

"You built that gallery to save people," Gol said afterward, still catching her breath. "Tonight it did, right up until the second before you unbuilt it."

"That's the whole job," Kanja said. "Knowing exactly which one it needs to be, and when."

Reuses already-locked crew members Efa Gol and Corren Halst. No new named characters. Opens wave 25.*
