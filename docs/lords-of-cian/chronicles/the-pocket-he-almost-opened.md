# The Pocket He Almost Opened

*Locked canon, Batch 259, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1243`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LXXVIII, wave 26,
closing the wave. Age 300, V4 gear. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose.*

---

Of the Forge-Coat's forty-two compartments, forty-one had a known purpose someone else on the crew
could name from memory. The forty-second — known only as the Captain's pocket, never inventoried,
never opened in front of anyone across the whole of the Long Mask — had held whatever it held in
total, undiscussed privacy for two hundred and sixty-seven years by the night the operation on the
Ferrenline docks went as badly as anything had gone in over a decade.

Three crew lost. Garren Hask, well past the point in his own long life where he still went out on
raids himself, had ridden along anyway against every piece of advice given him, and had come closer
to being the fourth than anyone aboard was comfortable admitting afterward. Kanja sat with him
through the worst of the aftermath, in the hold, in the dark, his hand resting more than once on the
seam of the forty-second pocket without quite finishing the motion that would have opened it.

Hask noticed. Of course he noticed; noticing was most of what thirty decades of ledger-keeping had
trained him to do. He didn't ask what was in it. He asked, instead, whether Kanja needed to open it
tonight, or whether tonight was simply the kind of night that made a closed thing feel like it wanted
opening regardless of whether opening it would actually help.

The hand came away from the seam. Whatever the forty-second pocket held, it stayed held — not out
of discipline exactly, not tonight, but because Garren Hask had asked the one question that mattered
more than curiosity: not *what is it*, but *do you need it*, and the honest answer, even on the
worst night in years, was still no. Hask never asked again. The pocket stayed exactly what it had
always been -- unopened, unexplained, and, for one long dark hour on the Ferrenline docks, closer to
being neither than it had come in two hundred and sixty-seven years.
