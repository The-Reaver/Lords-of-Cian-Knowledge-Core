# The Gate That Was Also a Door

*Locked canon, Batch 197, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-714`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XIX, wave 7 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The compound's single exit was a portcullis chain-raised behind a plain stone archway, and the two
hundred refugees sheltering inside had exactly one route out — through that archway, past that chain
— before the approaching column arrived. Bring the chain down wrong and the falling gate sealed the
archway shut behind it. Bring down the archway instead and the same result followed by a different
door.

Kanja read both structures before touching either.

The chain carried the resonance he expected — iron under load, counterweighted, answering cleanly to
a single frequency the moment the Aegis-Talisman's lens settled against a link. The archway answered
nothing at all, plain dressed stone with no tension running through it, exactly as expected. The
distinction mattered more here than it ever had in open combat, where collapsing the wrong structure
cost ground. Here it would have cost two hundred lives their only way out.

"Chain only," he said, more to himself than to the rebel guide beside him, confirming the boundary
one more time before committing. "The archway doesn't carry anything I can use, which means it's not
carrying anything for me to accidentally bring down either."

The discharge took the chain apart in a single strike, the portcullis dropping its own weight into
the mechanism above the arch rather than across it, and the stone doorway itself never so much as
shifted. Two hundred people went through a clean opening that, from the approaching column's
perspective a quarter hour later, looked exactly like a gate that had simply failed on its own.

"You could have brought the whole wall down faster," the guide said, watching the last of the
refugees clear the archway. "Would've saved you the extra care."

"Faster isn't the same as right. I know the difference between a door that's part of the fight and a
door two hundred people are still walking through. I built this doctrine to tell those two things
apart. Today was exactly why that mattered."
