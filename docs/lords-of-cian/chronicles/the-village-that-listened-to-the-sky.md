# The Village That Listened to the Sky

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-570`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXV, wave 9 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The warning reached the fishing settlement nine days before the storm did, carried by a courier who
identified himself only as working for "the sky-readers" and left a single sheet of instructions:
haul every boat above the third tide-mark by the seventh day, secure the grain stores, move the
elderly and the very young inland before the fifth night.

The settlement's headman had no way to verify any of it. He had heard of the Storm That Walks the way
most coastal settlements had — as a naval legend, a fighter's alias, nothing that had anything to do
with a fishing village too small to matter to any war. He nearly dismissed the warning as some
merchant's scheme to clear the harbor for his own use.

He hauled the boats anyway, on the seventh day, mostly because the cost of being wrong about ignoring
it was higher than the cost of being wrong about believing it.

The storm arrived on the ninth day, within the hour the courier's sheet had specified, stronger than
anything the settlement's own oldest fishermen could remember surviving. Every boat above the third
tide-mark rode it out intact. The two boats a pair of skeptical younger fishermen had left at the
second mark, confident the warning was exaggerated, were destroyed.

"Who sent you," the headman asked the courier, when he returned afterward to confirm the outcome, "and
why does a war fought over territory and Houses care whether a fishing village three days from
anything survives a storm?"

"He doesn't fight for territory," the courier said. "He fights so people like you don't have to
choose between trusting a stranger's warning and losing everything you have. The sky-reading was never
just for ships that shoot back."

*Continuity notes (not narrative): a purely humanitarian, non-military entry — a fishing settlement
