# The Division That Marched Itself Away

*Locked canon, Batch 187, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-546`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle XIII, first entry
in the fifth wave. Rebellion era, age 23. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a
detailed, large-scale, multi-day deception showcase per Abad's craft instruction. Narrated in
neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

An entire Directorate division, nearly three thousand men, had been ordered to sweep a broad stretch
of open country believed to be hiding a rebel staging ground — a force too large for the Hymn-Engine's
usual localized tricks to redirect all at once, and the largest single target the Crow King had ever
attempted to move.

Kanja didn't try to redirect it all at once. He redirected its officers instead.

Over four days, a sequence of false field reports — each individually plausible, each building on the
last, each seeded into exactly the communication channels the division's own command structure relied
on — convinced the division's senior officers that the staging ground had shifted three separate
times, the division marching further from its actual target with every reported sighting. The rank
and file never once doubted their own orders; the deception worked entirely on the men writing them.

By the fourth day, the division was two full days' march from anywhere it needed to be, its
supply lines stretched thin chasing a target that had never existed where the reports placed it. The
actual rebel operation it had been sent to disrupt completed without ever coming within a day's march
of the division's own position.

"Three thousand men, moved four days in the wrong direction, and not one of them ever saw anything
resembling a trick," the division's own eventual after-action report noted, reaching commanders who
never fully understood what had actually happened. "The reports themselves were the weapon. Nobody
sabotaged the division. The division simply followed its own orders exactly as given."

"That's rather the whole design," Kanja told his own crew afterward, reviewing the division's stretched
supply lines and reversed course. "The rank and file don't need to be deceived if the people writing
their orders already are. Move the officers, and the men follow honestly, exactly as they're trained
to. That's not a flaw in their discipline. It's what makes the discipline itself the actual target."
