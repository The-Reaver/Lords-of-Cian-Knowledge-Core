# The Siege That Needed No Braid

*Locked canon, Batch 260, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1278`). Crow King Alias Chronicle LXXXVI, wave 29. The
craft plays a background logistics/morale role during a large siege rather than being the featured
trick. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

Nobody would tell this siege's story with the craft anywhere near its center, and that was, by this
point, exactly how the fourth generation preferred it. The fighting itself belonged to other hands
entirely -- lines held, walls breached, a campaign fought and mostly won by ordinary soldiers and
ordinary siegecraft over the better part of three weeks. What the craft did happened almost entirely
behind that, unremarked, the kind of work nobody writes songs about.

Garren Hask needed the besieging force's own supply figures to hold together across three weeks of
sustained pressure -- real grain, real water, real reserves that had to be tracked, rationed, and
reported accurately up a chain that had grown too large for any one ledger-keeper to manage alone.
The fourth generation didn't fabricate anything this time. He simply applied the same exacting
discipline he'd once used to build a convincing lie to keeping an enormous, genuinely true accounting
honest and current across a dozen separate supply lines, catching two real shortfalls three days
before they would have mattered and flagging a third that Hask's own count had missed.

"You built your whole reputation on numbers nobody could trust," Hask said, comparing figures late one
night by lamp-light. "I didn't expect you'd be just as good at numbers everybody could."

"It's the same discipline," the fourth generation said. "Exact recall doesn't care whether what it's
tracking is true or invented. I think I spent longer than I needed to believing the trick was the
point. The discipline underneath it is what actually does the work, whichever direction I point it."

The siege ended in three weeks the way sieges end -- slowly, expensively, decided by supply and
attrition more than any single dramatic moment -- and nothing about the craft's role in it would ever
be sung about or turned into legend. Nobody involved seemed to mind.

one single centered operation. Reuses the fourth generation's numeric-recall discipline (`MCD-1077`)
applied to genuine, non-deceptive logistics tracking for the first time, paralleling "What the Grain
Convoy Never Reported" (`MCD-1272`)'s inversion of the same method. Garren Hask (`CC-115`) reused for
continuity. No new named characters. No new proper nouns -- the besieging force and the siege itself
stay unnamed.*
