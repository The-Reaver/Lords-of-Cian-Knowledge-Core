# The Night Five Provinces Moved at Once

*Locked canon, Batch 201, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-858`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle XLIII, wave 15 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Nothing in the Crow King's five years of operations had approached the scale of what the crew
attempted across a single night spanning five separate provinces at once — not a single division
redirected by targeting its officers, but five entirely independent operations, each built on a
different application of the craft the run had spent years developing, coordinated to strike within
the same six-hour window so that no province's garrison could spare attention to help any other.

The apprentice ran the reporting-network deception in the north, redirecting a patrol column exactly
the way the earliest extraction had years before. Her student, further south, held a scaled-down
Braid across a walled settlement's own stone acoustics, the architectural trick first discovered at
Coalgate now taught deliberately rather than found by accident. A third team, working from a written
forgery modeled on the method once built for a deaf commander, moved an entire garrison's reserves
through nothing but a single falsified report. A fourth held a river crossing using water's own
acoustic folding. Kanja himself, in the fifth province, walked alone through a single gate with a
single sentence, exactly as he once had for one hostage — except this time for an entire compound's
worth of prisoners, the smallest-scale trick of the run deployed at its largest stakes.

Every operation held. By dawn, five provinces' worth of Directorate response had been misdirected,
delayed, or quietly neutralized without a single sustained engagement anywhere across the whole
night — the first time every register of the craft the crew had spent five years developing had been
deployed simultaneously, by five different hands, none of them needing Kanja present to execute what
he'd once had to build alone.

"Five years ago this was one man and a scarecrow," the apprentice said afterward, meeting the others
back at the rally point, her own student beside her, still catching her breath from six hours of
sustained performance. "Tonight it was five separate versions of everything we've learned, running
at once, and none of them needed you standing over their shoulder."

"That was always the point," Kanja told her, meaning it. "I didn't build this to be the only person
who could do it. I built it to become exactly this — something more people could carry than just me,
doing more at once than I could ever manage alone."
