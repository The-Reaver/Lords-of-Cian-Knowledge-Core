# The Vote Not to Ask

*Locked canon, Batch 256, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1159`). Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle LXXV, wave 25, closing the wave. The workers themselves vote, by clear majority, not to pursue a claim the ledger's own numbers say they would plainly win -- and Kanja defers to the vote rather than pressing the case.*

---

Ezio had the numbers finished before the third day was out, cleaner than most: a straightforward shortfall, well documented, an administrator with no real defense once the production logs went up against the payroll. By every measure the method used to judge a strong case, it was one of the strongest they'd built in weeks. What stopped it wasn't the arithmetic. It was a vote the workers held themselves, on their own initiative, the night before the finding would have gone to the administrator.

The man who'd wronged them was, by every account, dangerous in ways the ledger's own protections had never fully reached — not the ordinary retaliation the crew had learned to counter with MCD-895's standing relief practice, but a documented history of driving families entirely out of the district through means the workers described only obliquely, afraid even discussing it plainly might be overheard. A narrow majority decided, after a long night of open argument Kanja and Ezio weren't invited to, that winning the wage claim wasn't worth what they believed it would cost.

Ezio wanted to press it anyway. The numbers were sound, the crew's presence itself was protection the workers might be underweighting, and a case this clean, abandoned, meant a shortfall stayed unrecovered and a wrongdoer walked untouched. He said as much, plainly, to Kanja rather than to the workers directly.

"You're not wrong about the numbers," Kanja told him. "You're wrong about whose numbers matter here."

"Their safety's a number too. We could weigh it."

"We could. It's not ours to weigh for them." He gathered the district the next morning, not to argue the vote but to confirm it had been theirs freely — no threat forcing the choice that he could find, no administrator's hand visible on the outcome, just a community deciding, with fuller knowledge of their own situation than any ledger could hold, that the risk outweighed the recovery. He told them plainly the finding was ready whenever they wanted it, that the crew wouldn't return to press it uninvited, and that changing their minds in a month or a year would find the same finding waiting exactly as documented today.

The case closed unpursued, the shortfall recorded but the claim withdrawn at the workers' own request — the first time in the method's history the numbers had said one thing clearly and the people they were meant to serve had said, with equal clarity, that the numbers weren't the whole answer.

"We've never walked away from a case we could win," Ezio said, packing the tent down early.

"We've never had people this certain about what winning would cost them, either. The ledger doesn't get to overrule that just because it's got better arithmetic."
