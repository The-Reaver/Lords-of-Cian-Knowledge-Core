# The Return to the Wetlands

*Locked canon, Batch 260, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1277`). Crow King Alias Chronicle LXXXV, wave 29, opening
it. Decades later, the lineage passes near the original Voskharen Wetlands compound where the
Hymn-Engine was first invented. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

They weren't there for the wetlands themselves. An unrelated operation had them passing within half a
day of the old Voskharen Wetlands compound, and it was the apprentice, still rasp-voiced after all
these years, who suggested the detour before anyone else thought to.

The marsh had reclaimed almost everything. The drainage channels the crew had hand-dug that night were
silted over and indistinguishable from channels the wetland had cut on its own since. The command
platform where the first scarecrow had stood was gone entirely, swallowed by reeds, no trace left that
anyone had ever built anything there at all.

Kanja stood at roughly where he remembered it having been, though he admitted he couldn't be entirely
certain of the exact spot anymore. "I didn't plan any of it," he said. "Voris's three-ring encirclement
should have worked. I built the whole thing in the time it took to realize it hadn't, yet."

"Do you wish there was something left to see?" the third generation asked.

He thought about it longer than the question probably warranted. "No," he said finally. "I think I
like that the marsh took it back. It was never supposed to be a monument. It was a scared young man
buying his people a few hours, with nothing planned past that night. The wetlands forgetting the
exact spot feels more honest than a marker would."

The fourth generation, who had never seen the place and had built his own entirely different version
of the craft without ever needing to, walked the old channel lines anyway, tracing them by feel more
than sight. "It's strange," he said. "Everything I do comes from this place, and I've never been here
before today, and it doesn't look like anything anymore."

"That's probably right," the apprentice said. "The place was never really the point either."

They left before nightfall, the marsh closing back over whatever was left to close over, and none of
them tried to mark the spot a second time.
