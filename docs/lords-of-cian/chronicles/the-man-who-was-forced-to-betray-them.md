# The Man Who Was Forced to Betray Them

*Locked canon, Batch 161, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-507`). Captain Alias Chronicle XIV. Rebellion era. Not a
territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed combat-plus-moral-complexity showcase per
Abad's craft instruction. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

A trusted crew member had been feeding minor operational details to a Directorate handler for months,
not out of any change of loyalty but because the handler held his sister's location and safety as
leverage — small betrayals, each one individually survivable, that had nonetheless let an ambush form
around a supply run before Kanja's own instincts caught the pattern too consistent to be coincidence.

He confronted the man directly rather than the handler first.

"I know what's been happening," Kanja said, before the man could offer any defense. "I know roughly
how long, and I have a reasonable guess why. I'd rather hear it from you than assume the worst version
of the story."

The man didn't deny it, the relief of finally being caught plainer than any fear of the consequence.
"They have my sister. Every report I don't send, they remind me exactly what happens to her. I've
hated every single thing I've told them. I didn't see another way to keep her alive."

Kanja didn't have him restrained, didn't call the crew's security lead. Instead, within the hour, the
Trinity moved on the handler's own safehouse — a detailed, fast strike, Mafesto reading the building's
occupants before the door was ever breached, Obsidian Malice disabling the guards posted at the
sister's holding room without giving them time to signal a threat against her, Onyx of Oblivion
clearing the handler's own escort in the same sequence. The sister was freed inside fifteen minutes,
the handler himself taken alive for what he knew rather than killed outright.

"You could have punished me first and rescued her after," the crew member said afterward, still
processing that neither had happened. "Most people would have."

"Most people would have missed that you were never actually the threat. The leverage was the threat.
Removing the leverage removes the reason you'd ever betray anyone again." Kanja watched brother and
sister reunited, something quietly satisfied in it despite the danger the whole affair had posed.
"You're still part of this crew. What you did under that kind of pressure doesn't erase who you were
before it, or who you can be now that the pressure's gone."
