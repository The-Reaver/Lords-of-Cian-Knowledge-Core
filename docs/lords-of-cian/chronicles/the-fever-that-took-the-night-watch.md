# The Fever That Took the Night Watch

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. Captain Alias Chronicle, wave 9. Rebellion era. Not
a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters (the sick
sailor and the settlement healer are deliberately left unnamed).*

---

The fever moved through the aft watch faster than anyone expected, three sailors down within two
days and a fourth, the youngest of them, burning hot enough by the third night that the settlement
healer brought aboard to treat him stopped pretending the outcome was certain.

There was no enemy to fight, no ambush to outmaneuver, no Trinity function built for what a body
does when a fever won't break. Kanja spent the night on the floor beside the boy's cot anyway,
doing the only useful thing available to him — cooling cloths changed every few minutes, water held
to cracked lips, the unglamorous physical labor of nursing that had nothing to do with density or
gear or reputation at all.

"You don't need to be here for this," the healer said, well past midnight, clearly startled to find
the ship's own captain doing work any deck hand could have managed.

"I don't need to be. I want to be. He's mine to look after, same as anyone on this ship, and this
is the part of looking after someone that doesn't get written into any ledger or any story
afterward — it's just hours, and hands, and staying." He wrung out another cloth without looking up.
"I've spent most of my life being useful because I'm strong enough to change the outcome of things.
Tonight I'm not. Tonight the only thing I can actually do is exactly what you're doing, and stay
long enough that if it goes badly, he's not alone for it."

The fever broke before dawn, the boy's temperature dropping through the worst hour and holding
steady after. Kanja didn't leave until he saw it hold on its own for a full hour more, past the
point anyone needed him to.

"You could have slept," Garren Hask said the next morning, finding him still there, exhausted in a
way no battle had left him.

"I could have. But he's part of what I built this crew to be, same as anyone who's ever fought
beside me. Some nights that means standing between him and a blade. Some nights it just means
staying until a fever breaks. I don't think either one matters less than the other."
