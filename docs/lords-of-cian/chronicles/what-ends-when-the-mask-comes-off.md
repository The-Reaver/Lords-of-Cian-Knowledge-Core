# What Ends When the Mask Comes Off

*Locked canon, Batch 156, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-493`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle XII, closing the
fourth wave. Long Mask era, age ~300, deep into the persona's later decades. Not a territory
Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters beyond the already-locked Garren Hask.*

---

Garren Hask had outlived every reasonable expectation of a dock-smith's ordinary lifespan by decades,
this world's own long baseline making his continued presence at Kanja's side less remarkable than it
would have read in a shorter-lived setting — old now by any measure, still sharp, still keeping his
own private ledger of a persona he'd watched grow from a single burned-wharf origin into something
that had outlasted entire generations of the sailors who feared or trusted it.

"You've worn this mask longer than most people live an entire life," Hask said, one of the quieter
evenings that had grown more frequent between operations in recent years. "Do you ever think about
what happens to it, eventually? Not to you. To the Scourge itself, whenever this particular chapter
finally closes."

"More than I used to, honestly." Kanja considered the question with real weight rather than
deflecting it. "I don't know exactly when or how this ends. I know it will, someday — nothing wears a
single shape forever, and I don't think this one should either. What I don't know is what the water
looks like without black sails on it. That's been true longer than I've been willing to admit."

"Does that frighten you?"

"Less than I'd have expected, when I first started sitting with the question. Every alias I've ever
carried ended eventually, one way or another, and something else always took its place — not because
the old one failed, but because it had done what it needed to do, and something new was needed
instead." He looked out over water that had carried the reputation for longer than either of them
could easily reckon with. "I don't think the Scourge ending means anything was wasted. I think it
means, whenever it happens, it'll have earned its own ending the same way it earned everything else —
honestly, on its own terms, not mine to force before its time."

"I don't know that I'll be here to see it, whenever it comes." Hask said it plainly, without
self-pity, simply an honest accounting of a life that had already run far longer than most. "I've kept
this ledger since before there was a persona to keep it about. I'd like to think it'll still matter to
someone, after."

"It already matters. It'll go on mattering long after either of us is done with this particular
chapter." Kanja set a hand briefly on the old man's shoulder. "Whatever comes after the mask, this
ledger's part of what made it worth wearing in the first place."
