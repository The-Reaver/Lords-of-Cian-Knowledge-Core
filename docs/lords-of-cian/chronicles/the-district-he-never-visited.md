# The District He Never Visited

*Locked canon, Batch 153, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-484`). The Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle XII, closing
the fourth wave. Rebellion era, several years after the Furnace District Strike, age 19 event. Not a
territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters.*

---

Word of the tally method reached a mining district hundreds of miles from the Furnace District
secondhand, through travelers' accounts rather than any direct visit — a rough, incomplete picture of
what had actually happened, missing most of the specifics, carrying only the method's core shape: an
unarmed man with a ledger, patient documentation instead of violence, numbers that couldn't be
credibly denied.

A local organizer there built her own version of it from that incomplete account, adapting what she'd
heard to her district's own mining wages and working conditions, never once meeting Kanja or receiving
any direct guidance from his own crew.

"You did all of this from a story someone told you, without ever confirming the details firsthand?"
a fellow miner asked her, watching her own four-day tally near its completion, mirroring the Furnace
District's structure almost exactly despite the distance and the years between them.

"I didn't need to confirm every detail. The shape of it was enough — patient, honest numbers instead
of a fight nobody could win outright. I filled in what I didn't know with what made sense for our own
situation." She checked her own tally sheets, the district's wage figures laid out with the same
exacting care the original method was said to have used. "I don't know if I got it exactly right. I
know the numbers here are true, and I know that's the part that actually mattered in the story I
heard."

The district's own administrator, confronted days later with a tally he couldn't credibly dismiss,
settled the dispute in terms the miners judged fair — the method working a second time, hundreds of
miles and several years removed from its origin, built entirely from an incomplete secondhand account
rather than any direct transmission.

Word of the second success eventually made its way back to the Furnace District itself, reaching
Kanja indirectly the same way the method had reached the mining district — a story passed along
rather than witnessed.

"Someone built the whole thing from what they'd merely heard about," Ezio Valcari observed, hearing
the account. "Not even what you actually did. Just the shape of it, secondhand."

"Then the shape was the part that mattered, not the specifics of how I happened to do it." Kanja set
the account aside, something quietly satisfied in it. "I'd rather the method spread that way than not
spread at all waiting for a perfect transmission nobody could ever guarantee."
