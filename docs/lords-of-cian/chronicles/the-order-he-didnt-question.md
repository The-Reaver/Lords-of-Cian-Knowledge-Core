# The Order He Didn't Question

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-589`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XLIV, wave 15 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Kanja had, in every prior engagement built on Sephtis's own forecasts, retained the option of asking
follow-up questions the old man's decades of experience could answer on the spot — a margin of doubt
he could resolve before committing the Trinity to a timing window. With Sephtis retired and the second
sky-reader's forecast the only one available, that option didn't exist. He would commit fully or not
at all, on trust alone.

He committed fully.

Mafesto crossed open water on a timing window the second sky-reader had called to the hour, the
Kinetic Transfer System banking a storm surge that arrived within minutes of her predicted center.
Obsidian Malice discharged against the successor fleet's forward battery exactly when her forecast's
visibility window opened, no adjustment needed, no correction called for. Onyx moved through a
confusion that matched her predicted conditions precisely enough that Whisper of Shadows and Cadence
Ruin both worked exactly as the engagement had been planned around, without a single deviation from
the timing she alone had built.

The fleet action closed in the doctrine's favor, decisively, on a forecast that no one but its author
had verified before the Trinity moved.

"You trusted it completely," the fleet's senior captain said afterward, still faintly astonished.
"No second opinion. No fallback plan if she'd been wrong."

"I trusted her," Kanja said. "Not blindly — she earned that the same way Sephtis did, the slow way,
over years I watched her work. But once I trusted her, questioning every call the way I used to
question his would have told her I never actually believed she'd earned it. Sometimes trust means
committing without the safety net, or it isn't really trust at all."
