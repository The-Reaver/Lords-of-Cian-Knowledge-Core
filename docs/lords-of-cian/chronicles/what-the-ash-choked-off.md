# What the Ash Choked Off

*Locked canon, Batch 285, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1506`). Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XCV, wave 32. A coastal volcanic ash-fall -- a hazard this alias's ninety-plus prior entries have never used -- fouls Obsidian Malice's discharge housing mid-engagement, the sub-series' first genuine equipment failure for this alias, forcing Mafesto and Onyx of Oblivion to carry a fight without their third piece.*

---

The mountain had been venting fine grey ash over the Lawless Reaches' coastal shelf for three days before the wind turned and put the whole plume out over open water, and it was into that falling ash, not a storm, that a Directorate patrol boat led its own opportunistic strike against a small allied resupply convoy anchored blind in it.

The ash did what fog and moonless night had done before it in this alias's run -- stripped sight from everyone on the water in equal measure -- and for the first several minutes it seemed like one more variation on a problem the Trinity had already solved more than once. Onyx of Oblivion's Whisper of Shadows carried Kanja across the anchored convoy's deck rails the same way it had carried him through smoke and darkness a dozen times, reading the fall of ash-muffled footsteps rather than any shape the eye could catch.

Obsidian Malice was the piece that failed. Kanja felt it first as a half-second's hesitation where a discharge should have been instant -- ash, drawn into the housing on an inhale of the weapon's own charge cycle, packing fine and grey against the internal mechanism the way it had never been designed to shed. The second attempt didn't fire cleanly at all, the arc guttering short a body's length from where it needed to land.

He didn't fight the housing. He set it, dead weight now, against his hip, and let Mafesto and Onyx do what three pieces together had never strictly required either of them to prove they could do alone. Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System took a boarding axe's full swing and put it back through the man who'd thrown it, then a second, then a third, redirected force compounding across the deck rail in a rhythm Kanja had never needed to rely on so completely before. Onyx's Whisper of Shadows carried him below and around rather than through, denying the patrol boat's crew a single clean look at where he actually was, buying the seconds Mafesto needed to close the gap on the last three men standing.

It ended with the patrol boat's crew disarmed and the convoy's own cargo intact, ash still sifting down over all of it in a fine, silencing grey that muffled even the sound of the fighting's end. Kanja worked Obsidian Malice's housing open on the convoy's deck afterward, packed to the seam with the same fine grey powder, and said nothing for a while, turning it over in his hands the way a man turns over a tool that has simply, honestly failed him for the first time.

"It'll clean," he said finally, to no one in particular. "Just not before the next storm asks it to work again."
