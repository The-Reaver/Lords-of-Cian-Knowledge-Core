# The Armada at Dead Reckoning

*Locked canon, Batch 186, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-543`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle XIII, first entry in
the fifth wave. Long Mask era, age ~150, the Golden Terror period. Not a territory Chronicle. New
standalone material — a detailed, large-scale fleet battle per Abad's craft instruction. Onyx of
Oblivion remains sealed at L9 throughout the Long Mask; this showcase uses Mafesto, Obsidian Malice,
and the built gear system only. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The Directorate had finally committed a full armada to ending the Scourge's operations permanently —
twelve warships under a single unified command, more firepower assembled in one place than any
prior engagement had faced, sent to the shoal-strewn waters near Dead Reckoning specifically because
its navigational hazards had always favored local knowledge over raw numbers.

The Scourge brought eight ships against twelve, and the shoals themselves as a weapon neither side
fully controlled.

The Forge-Coat's grounding weave absorbed the armada's opening long-range volleys while the Sovereign
Eyes read the shoal patterns invisible to the armada's own unfamiliar navigators, guiding the smaller
fleet through channels the larger force couldn't safely follow. Three armada vessels ran aground
within the first hour, their own numbers advantage undone by ground none of their captains had ever
sailed. Obsidian Malice discharged twice against the armada flagship's own steering, disabling rather
than sinking it, while the Ironhand Gauntlets and Ironfall Boots carried the Scourge through a
boarding action against the command vessel that ended its coordination entirely.

With the flagship disabled and three more ships grounded, the remaining armada scattered rather than
press an engagement that had already cost it more than intended — the Scourge's own fleet, still at
full strength, letting the retreat go rather than press an advantage that would have cost lives on
both sides beyond what the battle's actual purpose required.

"Twelve ships, and we're the ones still whole," the flagship's own sailing master said afterward,
surveying an engagement that should have overwhelmed them by simple numbers. "The Directorate will
call this an ambush."

"Let them call it whatever explains the loss most comfortably. We didn't hide anything from them. We
simply knew this water better than they did, and they committed twelve ships to waters they'd never
actually sailed." The Scourge watched the scattered armada's retreat. "Numbers only win if the ground
cooperates with them. Today it didn't."
