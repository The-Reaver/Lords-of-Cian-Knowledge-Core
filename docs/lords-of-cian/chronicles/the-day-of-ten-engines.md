# The Day of Ten Engines

*Locked canon, Batch 188, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-549`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XIII, first
entry in the fifth wave. Rebellion era, age 25. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material —
a detailed, large-scale combat showcase per Abad's craft instruction. Narrated in neutral
third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

Ten siege engines advanced on a rebel-held causeway simultaneously, each one tensioned and counter-
weighted according to the same underlying physics the resonance doctrine had spent years reading —
but never before attempted against so many targets at once, under fire, with a garrison's survival
measured in minutes rather than the careful, unhurried diagnosis the doctrine usually demanded.

Kanja read them in sequence, not simultaneously — a deliberate choice, each engine's own resonance
diagnosed individually in the seconds available before committing, refusing to let the pressure of ten
targets at once collapse into the same hurried misdiagnosis that had once cost two men their safety.

The first three engines came down clean, precisely targeted discharges collapsing their own
counterweight systems before they reached effective range. The fourth resisted the pattern slightly —
a modified tensioning Kanja hadn't encountered before, costing precious seconds to properly diagnose
rather than assume familiar. He took those seconds anyway, the causeway's defenders holding just long
enough for the correction to matter. Engines five through nine fell in rapid, confident sequence once
the modification's pattern was understood. The tenth, its crew already retreating rather than face
what had happened to the other nine, was left standing, its threat effectively ended without a
discharge needed at all.

"Ten engines, nine strikes," the causeway's own garrison commander said afterward, the assault broken
entirely. "That's not a number I'd have believed possible from a single resonance doctrine, no matter
how skilled."

"It's not a number I'd have attempted before I learned what happens when I don't take the time to
verify a reading properly." Kanja surveyed the disabled engines, no friendly casualties despite the
scale of the assault. "Ten targets under pressure is exactly the kind of situation where the old
mistake becomes tempting again — assume the pattern, move fast, trust the read without checking it.
I'd rather move a fraction slower and be right nine times out of nine than move faster and risk being
wrong even once."
