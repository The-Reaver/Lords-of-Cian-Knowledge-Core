# The Saboteur Among the Apprentices

*Locked canon, Batch 159, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-501`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XIV. Rebellion
era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a
detailed detection-and-combat showcase per Abad's craft instruction. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose. No new named characters.*

---

A Directorate infiltrator had spent weeks embedded among The Anvil's apprentice cohort, learning
enough of the forge's own rhythms to plant a slow structural weakness in a support beam rather than
attempt anything as detectable as open sabotage — a failure timed to occur during a full working
shift, when the beam's collapse would do maximum harm to the apprentices gathered beneath it.

Kanja caught the wrongness in the beam's own sound three days before the timed failure.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System registered a resonance in the support structure subtly inconsistent
with the beam's known load history — not yet failing, but weakened in a pattern no ordinary wear
produced. He spent a full day quietly tracing the modification back to its source rather than
confronting the cohort openly, watching for who returned to check on the compromised beam without
being asked.

The infiltrator returned twice, confirming himself before Kanja ever needed to make an accusation.

The confrontation itself, when it came, was brief — Obsidian Malice's presence alone enough to end any
thought of resistance, the infiltrator disarmed and restrained before the sabotage could ever be
triggered. No apprentice in the cohort ever learned how close the beam had come to failing over their
own heads.

"You could have simply reinforced the beam and said nothing," the crew's own security lead observed
afterward, reviewing how the whole matter had been handled. "Why trace it back instead of just fixing
the immediate danger?"

"Because fixing the beam without finding the source leaves the source free to try again, somewhere
I'm not listening as closely." Kanja watched the infiltrator taken into custody, the apprentices below
still unaware anything had ever been wrong. "I'd rather spend an extra day being patient than leave a
threat like this one loose in a place this many people trust to be safe."
