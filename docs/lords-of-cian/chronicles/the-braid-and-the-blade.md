# The Braid and the Blade

*Locked canon, Batch 157, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-495`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle XI. Rebellion era,
age 23. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed hybrid deception-plus-combat
showcase per Abad's craft instruction, combining the Hymn-Engine with full Trinity engagement for the
first time in this alias's run. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The fortified garrison at Reth Hollow had survived every prior deception attempt against it by
splitting its response into small, independently verified units — exactly the kind of redundancy the
Braid's interleaved false signals had defeated once before. This time, the Crow King didn't intend
the deception to end the engagement on its own. He intended it to set the terms of a fight he was
already prepared to finish by force.

Three interleaved false signals drew the garrison's response units into a single consolidated
position — not scattered thin the way a pure evasion would have left them, but concentrated exactly
where Kanja wanted them, believing they'd successfully converged on the source of a threat that was,
in truth, walking directly toward them in full armor.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System absorbed the consolidated position's opening volley the moment the
deception's purpose became clear to the garrison too late to matter, converting the concentrated
fire into more stored charge than any scattered engagement could have offered. Obsidian Malice
discharged once against the position's central command structure, the strike calculated against a
target the Braid had deliberately walked into range. Onyx of Oblivion finished what remained, Cadence
Ruin breaking a formation that had never actually been coordinated in the first place — only
believed itself to be, right up until the moment it wasn't.

"You didn't use the Braid to avoid the fight," the garrison's surviving officer said afterward,
disarmed and clearly working through what had actually happened. "You used it to build the fight you
wanted."

"Evasion is one use of the method. It's never been the only one. Sometimes the honest answer isn't
avoiding a confrontation — it's choosing exactly where and how it happens, on terms that cost fewer
lives than letting the enemy choose instead." Kanja surveyed the position, the garrison's own
consolidated strength turned into the very vulnerability that ended it. "The lie and the blade aren't
different tools. They're the same tool, used at different moments of the same plan."
