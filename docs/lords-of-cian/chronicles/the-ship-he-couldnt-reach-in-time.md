# The Ship He Couldn't Reach in Time

*Locked canon, Batch 155, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-488`). The Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias
Chronicle X, first entry in the fourth wave. Rebellion era, age 21, Ghost Harbor. Not a territory
Chronicle. New standalone material — the first genuine loss-at-sea entry in this alias's run.
Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The distress signal reached the fleet too late by any honest measure — a merchant vessel caught in a
sudden squall miles beyond the fleet's fastest possible response, the mathematics of distance and
weather working against every advantage Kanja's own speed and skill could otherwise offer.

He pushed the flagship harder than caution allowed anyway, Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System
converting the storm's own battering into stored charge to keep the ship moving past what its crew
would ordinarily risk, closing distance that should have taken twice as long.

It wasn't enough. The merchant vessel went down eleven minutes before the flagship reached the
wreckage, its crew scattered across open water in conditions that made a clean rescue of the
survivors difficult enough without the added weight of having missed the ship itself entirely. Twelve
of the eighteen crew were recovered alive. Six were not.

"Eleven minutes," Dol Maren said quietly, watching the recovery effort's grim final accounting.
"There wasn't a faster ship on this water tonight. There wasn't a version of tonight where you
reached them in time."

"I know the mathematics were never in my favor. That doesn't make six names easier to accept." Kanja
watched the last of the search pattern complete, no further survivors to be found. "I've built this
entire reputation on being the fleet that reaches people in time. Tonight the reputation ran into the
actual limits of distance and weather, the same limits every fleet that ever sailed this water has
run into. I don't think the stories usually make room for that part."

"Will you tell it that way? Honestly, the way it happened?"

"I don't see another version worth telling. If the only stories people hear are the ones where I
arrive in time, they'll believe the fleet is something it isn't — infallible instead of simply fast
and willing." Kanja set his hand briefly against the flagship's rail, the weight of the night settling
somewhere it wouldn't fully leave. "Six people are dead because the sea is bigger than any speed I can
manage. That's not a story I intend to soften for anyone's comfort, including my own."
