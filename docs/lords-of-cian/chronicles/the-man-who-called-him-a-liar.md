# The Man Who Called Him a Liar

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-564`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XIX, wave 7 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The Trust's own commissioned weather service had spent two years insisting, in every report it
circulated, that a single unaffiliated forecaster could not reliably out-predict an institution built
on generations of recorded observation — and that the "Storm That Walks" legend was built less on
genuine method than on the ordinary statistical luck of a man who fought often enough on open water
that some engagements were bound to coincide with weather anyway.

Its director said as much publicly, at a captains' assembly called to steady rattled Trust morale,
and framed it as a wager: name a date, any date within the month, and let the unaffiliated forecaster
commit to a specific prediction the Trust's own service would independently verify.

Kanja accepted on Sephtis's behalf without consulting him first, which Sephtis later called "the one
time I've resented being trusted."

He delivered anyway — a storm system, its arrival window narrowed to six hours, called eleven days in
advance and posted publicly before the Trust's own service had detected so much as a pressure change.
The system arrived within forty minutes of the predicted window's center.

"Luck," the director said, standing in weather he had told two hundred captains would not come.

"Then wager again," Kanja said. "Name your own terms this time. Choose the storm you think I can't
call."

The director didn't take a second wager. What he did, three weeks later, after his own staff had
quietly begun cross-checking Sephtis's public forecasts against their own instruments and finding the
comparison consistently unflattering, was resign the directorship and request, through an intermediary
rather than in person, whether the method behind the predictions was something that could be taught
rather than merely watched.

"Tell him yes," Sephtis said, when the request reached him. "Tell him it took me years and I'm still
learning it. Tell him the door's open regardless."
