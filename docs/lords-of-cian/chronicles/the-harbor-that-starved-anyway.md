# The Harbor That Starved Anyway

*Locked canon, Batch 258, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1208`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle
LXX, wave 24, first entry in the wave. Sustained drought/famine relief through trade logistics, zero
combat. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The drought had broken two harvests running before the fleet ever heard about it, and by the time
Garren Hask's contacts in the coastal trading towns brought word south, the harbor town in question
was already three weeks into rationing that wasn't going to stretch through the season it still had
left. No siege, no blockade, no enemy fleet to disable — just bad rain for two years and a granary
that had run dry a month before the next crop could possibly come in.

There was no combat in what followed, and Kanja said as much to a crew that had spent a decade
learning to read enemies and threats before anything else. "Nobody's attacking this town. The
drought doesn't care about the doctrine. What it needs from us is grain, and grain doesn't come from
a Trinity showcase."

The fleet spent six weeks running trade routes rather than patrol routes — Dol Maren negotiating hull
space with merchant captains who'd never dealt with the ghost fleet on these terms before, Danne Sok
charting the fastest supply lines from three separate ports willing to sell at fair price once Garren
Hask's ledger made clear exactly what the fleet was buying and for whom, and Efa Gol running the
actual logistics of getting grain from three different holds into one starving harbor without a
single armed escort needed the entire way, because nobody with a reason to interfere ever surfaced.

It wasn't glamorous work, and Pell Ostra said so plainly on the fourth week, hauling sacks alongside
dockhands who had no idea a Trinity-bearing weapon of a man was doing the same labor beside them
unremarked. "Feels strange, doesn't it. All that gear, and the thing that actually saves this town is
a grain sack."

"Feels right, though," Kanja said, hauling the next one. "The doctrine was never really about the
fight. It just usually shows up wearing one."

By the season's end the harbor had grain enough to reach the next harvest with a margin to spare, and
Garren Hask logged the whole operation the same plain way he logged everything — six weeks, three
ports, one town fed, not a single blade drawn.

Fire," `MCD-956`) — this is sustained civilian humanitarian infrastructure work with zero combat or
threat of it anywhere in the entry. Reuses Dol Maren, Danne Sok, Efa Gol, Pell Ostra, Garren Hask.
The harbor town is deliberately unnamed. No new named characters. First entry in wave 24.*
