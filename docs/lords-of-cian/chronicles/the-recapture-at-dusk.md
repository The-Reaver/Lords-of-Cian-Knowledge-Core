# The Recapture at Dusk

*Locked canon, Batch 259, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1238`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LXXIII, wave 25,
first entry. Age 172, V3 gear. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The rendezvous point had worked eleven times before on this same overland route — a dry riverbed
two miles inland, easy to defend, easy to reach, easy to leave from before dawn. The twelfth time,
it worked for someone else first. A second slaving operation, entirely unconnected to the depot
they'd just cleared, had been watching that same riverbed for its own reasons — a resupply cache,
not a rescue route, coincidence rather than betrayal — and its scouts found forty-three freed
captives moving through open ground hours before the crew's own escort could close the distance.

By the time Kanja reached the riverbed, it was empty of everyone but three guards left to strike the
cache and cover the retreat, and Garren Hask's ledger, when it was finally written that night, would
carry a line it hadn't carried in years: not *freed*, but *lost again*, hours after the first
rescue had already been logged as a success.

The second rescue cost more than the first had. The recapturing operation had a full day's head
start and knew the terrain properly, having worked it for longer than the crew ever had; finding
their new holding site took two days of exactly the patient tracking work the alias's reputation
had never needed before, since reputation alone stops nobody who's never heard of it. When the
site was finally found, it fell in under an hour — the guards there had prepared for pursuit, not
for the specific and total collapse of resistance the Trinity and the loadout together produced —
but the captives had spent two extra days chained, cold, and frightened in a second holding pen
that the first rescue should have made unnecessary.

The lesson Garren Hask entered beside the corrected line wasn't about the crew's own tactics. It was
about the route itself: a corridor safe enough for one operation to use freely was, by that same
logic, safe enough for a second to be watching unnoticed. The rendezvous point was retired after
that night. It had worked eleven times. It had also, this once, been the failure's whole cause.
