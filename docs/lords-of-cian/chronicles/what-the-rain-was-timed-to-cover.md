# What the Rain Was Timed to Cover

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-582`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXVII, wave 13 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The siege had stalled for weeks over a single obstacle: a flooded river separating the rebel force
from a garrisoned crossing point, its ford unusable at normal water levels and its bridge held by
enough men to make a direct assault costly. Sephtis's forecast offered something no naval engagement
had called for before — not a storm at sea, but heavy inland rain timed to swell the river past its
banks for a specific eighteen-hour window, submerging the ford entirely but also, if the timing held,
raising the water enough to float flat-bottomed transport craft over a stretch of riverbed too
shallow to cross any other way.

It was the doctrine applied to a body of water no ship would ever sail, and it required trusting a
forecast built from patterns Sephtis had spent years reading over open sea, extended now to rainfall
and river drainage he'd had far less time to study.

"You're less certain of this one," Kanja's siege engineer observed, reading the forecast's hedged
language.

"I am," Sephtis admitted. "Rivers aren't the ocean. I've had years on one and months on the other. I
believe the window. I wouldn't stake as much on the hour as I would at sea."

Kanja staked the crossing on it anyway, narrowing the assault's timing to the forecast's broader range
rather than its precise center, building enough slack into the plan to survive the rain arriving early
or late within the predicted day.

It arrived on schedule, if not to the hour Sephtis would have wanted, and the river rose exactly
enough to carry the transport craft over ground that had blocked every crossing attempt for three
weeks.

---

*Continuity notes (not narrative): the doctrine's first application to a land-adjacent, inland
engagement — a river crossing rather than open sea — deliberately dramatizing Sephtis's own reduced
confidence outside his core domain and the plan's need to build in slack accordingly. Opens the
thirteenth wave. No new named characters; the siege engineer is unnamed.*
