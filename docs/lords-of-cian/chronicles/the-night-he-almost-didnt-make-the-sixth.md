# The Night He Almost Didn't Make the Sixth

*Locked canon, Batch 202, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-873`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XXVIII, wave 10 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Six forge sites came under coordinated assault in the same hour this time, one more than the
Directorate had ever attempted before — a deliberate escalation, built on everything the failed
five-site strike had taught them, timed to outlast whatever pace even Kanja could sustain.

He made the first four with room to spare, Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System converting the pace
between sites into stored charge the way it always had, Obsidian Malice discharging exactly when each
site needed it and not a moment before. The fifth cost him more than the first four combined — a
breach already three walls deep by the time he reached it, Onyx clearing it in a sequence that left
less charge banked for what came after than any crossing yet in the campaign.

He reached the sixth site with almost nothing left to answer it with.

The breach there had already reached the forge floor itself. Mafesto's charge was down to a fraction
of what the fight in front of him actually needed. For the first time in the campaign's entire run,
the Trinity's combined output was not, by any honest measure, enough to guarantee the outcome — and
for several seconds that the crew who witnessed it would never fully describe the same way twice, it
did not look like it was going to be enough.

He held anyway. Not with reserve charge he didn't have, but with technique alone — Onyx's Veil Piercer
finding the one gap a exhausted body could still exploit, a single decisive strike thrown with
everything genuinely left rather than anything held back for later. The breach broke a half-second
before it would have reached the last defended room.

All six forges stood. Kanja did not, immediately after — he sat down where he'd finished the fight and
did not move for several minutes, the crew giving him the space to do it without comment.

"That was closer than five ever was," the campaign's senior smith said afterward, finding him still
recovering. "I watched you almost not make it."

"I almost didn't." He didn't dress it up as anything less than it had been. "Five, I had room in.
Six, I found out exactly where the room runs out. I don't know what seven looks like, and I'm not in
a hurry to find out."
