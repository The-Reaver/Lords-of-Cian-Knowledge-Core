# The Boarding in the Blind Dark

*Locked canon, Batch 264, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1374`). Captain Alias Chronicle LXXIV, wave 25. A detailed full-Trinity boarding action fought in a moonless storm at sea, the sub-series' first true blind-dark combat showcase.*

---

There was no moon that night and no lantern light worth risking, not against a slaver vessel running dark specifically to avoid being spotted before it made open water, and Corren Halst's plan depended entirely on getting the Second Chance alongside without a single visible signal passing between the two boarding teams. It was, Pell Ostra said flatly before they went in, the kind of job that separated crews who trained together from crews who'd merely fought together.

They went in on touch and sound alone. Callum Breck ran the approach on hand-signals nobody could see, felt instead through rope-tension code passed boat to boat, a language the crew had built over years for exactly this kind of night. Onyx's Whisper of Shadows carried Kanja across the gap between hulls reading footfall vibration through the deck itself rather than sight, every enemy position mapped before his boots ever found the rail. Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System took the first drawn blade by feel, redirecting a lunge he never saw coming by the pressure of displaced air alone; Obsidian Malice discharged once, precisely angled by memory of the hold's layout from the boarding brief rather than any line of sight, opening the chained hold without touching a single captive chained inside it.

Efa Gol coordinated the whole boarding from the Second Chance's own dark deck by sound alone, counting engagement noises against the boarding roster she'd memorized rather than written down, since no lantern could risk giving away the approach. When she called the hold clear, she called it before anyone topside could confirm it visually — she'd simply counted the right number of freed voices against the right number of chains going slack, and trusted the number.

Dawn found forty-one freed captives blinking against the first real light they'd seen in weeks, and a crew that hadn't lit a single lantern until the fighting was entirely done. "I've fought in worse conditions," Kanja admitted afterward, "but I've never trusted a crew's coordination more than I did tonight, precisely because none of us could see each other doing it."

---

*Continuity notes (not narrative): the sub-series' first detailed combat showcase fought in total darkness, extending the blind-dark register already established for the Storm That Walks alias ("The Vault That Held No Light" precedent notwithstanding, this is Captain's own first use) into a boarding-action context, and the first entry to show Efa Gol coordinating a live engagement by sound-count alone rather than sight or written ledger. No new named characters -- Corren Halst, Pell Ostra, Callum Breck, Efa Gol, and Kanja all reused; the slavers and captives are deliberately unnamed. Second entry, wave 25.*
