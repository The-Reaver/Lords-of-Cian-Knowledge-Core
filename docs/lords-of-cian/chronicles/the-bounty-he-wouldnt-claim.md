# The Bounty He Wouldn't Claim

*Locked canon, Batch 258, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1205`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle
LXVII, wave 23, first entry in the wave. A declined bounty and a resolved root cause rather than a
resolved fight. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The merchant guild's agent came aboard *The Audit* with a sealed offer and an escort of two armed
clerks, plainly expecting negotiation rather than the flat answer he got inside of a minute. Ten
thousand in guild scrip, paid on proof, for the elimination of a rival pirate crew that had been
raiding the guild's grain convoys for two seasons running — a crew Kanja's fleet had already crossed
paths with twice without engaging.

"You want us to kill them," Kanja said, not a question.

"We want the raiding to stop," the agent said. "How it stops isn't our concern. Your fleet's
reputation for efficiency speaks for itself."

Kanja handed the sealed offer back unopened past the first page. "My fleet's reputation is for
disabling ships and freeing captives, not filling contracts. If you want the raiding to stop, I'll go
talk to them. If talking doesn't work, we'll disable their ships the same way we've disabled every
other ship that's forced our hand. What I won't do is take your coin to hunt men down because it's
cheaper than fixing whatever's driving them to raid your convoys in the first place."

He found the rival captain three days later at a contested anchorage, and the conversation that
followed had nothing to do with threats. The raiding, it turned out, traced back to the guild's own
price-fixing on grain sold to the crew's home port — a town the guild had been quietly starving out
of a fair market for two years, driving desperate men to raiding as the only trade left open to them.
Kanja laid the guild's own ledgers, obtained through Garren Hask's contacts, in front of both
captains side by side, and the rival crew agreed to stand down in exchange for the fleet forcing the
guild's hand on fair pricing rather than a bounty on their heads.

"Ten thousand in scrip walked off my deck today," Kanja told Efa Gol that evening, "and what we got
instead was cheaper for everyone but the guild."

"They won't thank you for it."

"They don't need to. The town gets fed either way."

*Continuity notes (not narrative): a new economic register — declining a bounty/contract offer
rather than accepting payment for combat, and resolving the underlying grievance (guild
price-fixing) rather than the surface conflict (piracy), distinct from every prior negotiated
surrender or terms-without-blood entry, which involved combatants directly rather than an economic
root cause. Reuses Efa Gol and Garren Hask's ledger network. The merchant guild agent and rival
captain are deliberately left unnamed. No new named characters. First entry in wave 23.*
