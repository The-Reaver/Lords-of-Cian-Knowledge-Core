# The Cut That Freed Them

*Locked canon, Batch 195, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-664`). The Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle XXIX, wave 10 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Eleven prisoners were sealed behind a load-bearing wall the Trust had built specifically to be
unbreachable without bringing the whole holding cell down on the people inside it — a design
that assumed anyone trying to free them would have to choose between leaving them sealed and
killing them in the attempt.

Kanja spent twenty minutes reading the wall before he touched it.

"It's not one solid structure," he told the crew chief, tracing a line invisible to anyone who
hadn't spent a lifetime learning to see load paths in stone. "It's built to look unbreakable, but
whoever designed it still had to leave a seam where the newer section meets the original
foundation wall, because you can't tie fresh masonry into old stone without one. That seam is the
only place in this whole wall that isn't carrying weight it can't afford to lose."

"You're going to cut through a load-bearing wall without bringing it down."

"I'm going to cut through the one part of it that was never actually load-bearing to begin with. Two
very different things." Kanja marked the seam's exact path with chalk, a single continuous line no
wider than a finger. "One clean strike, precisely on that line, and this opens like a door instead
of collapsing like a wall. A wrong strike, even an inch off that seam, brings the whole thing down
on everyone behind it."

Obsidian Malice's discharge, when it came, was the most restrained use of the weapon anyone on the
crew had ever witnessed — no wasted force, no wide arc, a single controlled strike following the
chalked line with a precision that read more like surgery than demolition. The section gave way
cleanly, a doorway where a wall had been a moment before, the rest of the structure settling exactly
where it had always stood.

Eleven prisoners walked out through an opening the Trust's own engineers had sworn couldn't exist
without killing them to make.

"How did you know exactly where to cut?" one of the freed prisoners asked, staring back at the
still-standing wall around the clean new opening.

"Because whoever built it still had to build it like a wall, not like a puzzle. Every wall has a
seam somewhere, whether the people who built it wanted it to or not." Kanja checked the opening's
edges one final time before moving the group toward the exit route. "I didn't break their design. I
just found the one part of it that was never actually part of the design at all."
