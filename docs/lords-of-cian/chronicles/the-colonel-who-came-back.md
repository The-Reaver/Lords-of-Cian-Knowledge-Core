# The Colonel Who Came Back

*Locked canon, Batch 254, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1104`). Bane Alias Chronicle LXXIV, wave 25. Introduces
Colonel Serrin Draeth, Bane's first recurring (rather than one-scene) antagonist -- a Directorate
officer returning for a personal rematch rather than under orders. Thread left open.*

---

Bane had beaten Colonel Serrin Draeth once before, months earlier, in an engagement neither of them
had thought worth naming at the time — a supply-line skirmish Draeth's company lost badly enough that
he'd been quietly reassigned to a garrison post afterward, out of the field. Bane hadn't thought about
the man since.

Draeth had thought of nothing else.

He came back attached to a relief column he had no real authority over, on a pretext that didn't
survive close scrutiny, and made for Bane's position directly rather than toward whatever objective
the relief column was supposedly there to support. It wasn't strategy. It was a man who'd spent
months rebuilding a single confrontation in his head until it became the only thing that mattered
more than the war around it.

Bane read it inside a minute of Draeth opening his mouth.

"You came a long way to fight someone who already won the last time you tried it."

"You don't get to walk away from that skirmish like it meant nothing."

"It meant six of your men went home instead of into the ground. That's what it meant. I don't know
what else you've built it into since." Bane didn't draw Onyx. "I have an operation to finish tonight
that has nothing to do with you. You can spend the time you've got left here trying to make this
about you, or you can go back to whatever post you actually still hold and keep your rank."

Draeth didn't back down, but Bane didn't give him the fight he wanted either — moved past him toward
the actual objective, let his own people handle the relief column's confused, leaderless remainder,
and left Draeth standing in the field having accomplished nothing but confirming, publicly, that he'd
abandoned his post for a grudge no one above him had sanctioned.

"He'll be back," Corren Halst said, watching Draeth's retreat from a distance.

"Probably." Bane didn't look especially concerned about it. "Some people can't let a loss stay a
loss. I'm not going to give him a second one worth obsessing over. That's usually the only thing that
actually starves it."
