# The Night They Took Him Instead

*Locked canon, Batch 277, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1439`). Trench Monarch Alias Chronicle C, wave 34, first
entry. Rebellion era, pre-Black-Trench -- Onyx of Oblivion solo, Mafesto dormant and Obsidian Malice
undeployed (`MCD-232`). The alias's first genuine capture-and-escape register.*

---

He'd walked the same stretch between Warehouse Twelve and the third canal bend alone a hundred
times without incident, which was precisely why the trap worked. A thrown sack of quicklime dust,
not a weapon anyone had thought to guard against, caught him full in the face in the dark; the blow
that followed, aimed at the back of his skull while he was still blind and coughing, landed clean
enough to put him down before Onyx could read an attack he genuinely never saw coming.

He woke bound at the wrists and ankles in the back room of a shuttered tannery, blindfolded, the
quicklime's sting still raw enough that opening his eyes did nothing useful even once the blindfold
slipped. Two voices outside the door discussed, without much urgency, whether to wait for a third
man before deciding what ransom the Trench Monarch's own crew might pay to get him back, or whether
killing him outright and letting the district draw its own conclusions was worth more than any sum
they could ask for.

Onyx alone couldn't cut rope it couldn't reach, bound as his hands were behind him, but Whisper of
Shadows didn't need his hands to work -- a controlled application against the door hinge itself,
patient rather than rushed, the two men outside never registering that the silence in the room had
changed shape before the door came off its frame from the inside rather than being forced through
it. What followed was short and one-sided: two men who'd planned for a bound prisoner and found
instead one already free, the fight ending before either of them fully understood the door hadn't
simply failed on its own.

He walked back to Warehouse Twelve on his own feet, quicklime burns still raw down one side of his
face, and told the crew the truth of it plainly rather than let the story grow past what had
actually happened. "I didn't see it coming. That's worth saying out loud, not burying under how it
ended."

Danne Sok said nothing for a long moment, then asked only whether the route needed changing.

"The route's fine. I need to stop assuming familiar ground means safe ground." Kanja pressed a wet
cloth to his jaw and didn't flinch away from the sting. "That's the part of tonight actually worth
remembering."
