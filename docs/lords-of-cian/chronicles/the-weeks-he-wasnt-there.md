# The Weeks He Wasn't There

*Locked canon, Batch 264, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1385`). Captain Alias Chronicle LXXXV, wave 29. The sub-series' deepest self-sufficiency entry yet: the crew runs everything, unsupervised, across a full six-week absence.*

---

Six weeks was longer than Kanja had ever been away from Pier Nine at once, and he'd chosen the timing deliberately — not because the crew needed watching less than ever, but because he'd started to suspect, after "The Command He Left Behind" and everything the rotating-chair structure had since proven, that the only real way to know whether an institution could stand without him was to genuinely stop checking on it for longer than felt comfortable.

He told no one exactly when he'd return. That was deliberate too.

What Corren Halst's council ran in those six weeks, compiled afterward into a report Hask insisted on keeping for the ledger's own permanent record, read like an ordinary season rather than a crisis: two merchant escorts completed without incident, the magistrate's liaison arrangement renewed for another term without needing his signature, a dispute between two junior crew members mediated and resolved entirely by the council process, Mira's first solo intake shift run flawlessly, Efa Gol's registry work continuing uninterrupted, the ledger's new keeper closing out a full month's accounts without a single figure needing correction. Nothing dramatic happened, which was itself the entire finding.

When he finally returned, unannounced, he found the pier running exactly as he'd left it — not frozen in his absence, not straining to cover for him, simply continuing, the way a well-built ship keeps its heading in a captain's absence because the rigging was set correctly before anyone let go of the wheel.

"Six weeks," Corren Halst said, handing him the report without ceremony. "You could read this whole thing and not find one place where we needed you specifically. I want you to actually sit with that, not just nod at it."

He read it twice, the way he'd once read Hask's flood report twice. "I already know what this crew is capable of without me in the room. I've known for years. I think what I needed wasn't proof. I think I needed to actually let six weeks pass and feel what it was like not to be needed, so the knowing stopped living only in my head."

No new named characters -- Corren Halst, Garren Hask, Efa Gol, and Mira all reused. First entry, wave 29.*
