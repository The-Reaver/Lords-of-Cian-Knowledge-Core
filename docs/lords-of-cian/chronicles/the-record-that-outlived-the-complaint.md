# The Record That Outlived the Complaint

*Locked canon, Batch 256, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1168`). Industrial Myth Alias Chronicle LXXXIV, wave 28, closing the wave. A claimant withdraws her complaint entirely but asks the documentation to remain on file, unresolved, for whoever comes after her -- the method's first witness-without-a-verdict case.*

---

She withdrew on the third morning, before Ezio had finished the cross-referencing, and she was plain about why: her husband's family, who owned a stake in the same mill she'd testified against, had made clear through channels quiet enough to be undeniable and unwitnessed enough to be unprovable that pursuing the claim would cost her more in the marriage than the wages were worth recovering. Nothing in what she described rose to the threat the district that voted not to ask had faced weeks earlier — no danger to safety, just a domestic cost she'd weighed and decided, entirely on her own terms, not to pay.

Ezio closed the ledger on the finding without argument, the same restraint the crew had already learned to hold. What she asked for instead, before she left the tent for what both of them understood was the last time, was stranger than a simple withdrawal: that the testimony she'd already given — dates, figures, the shortfall as far as it had been documented — stay in the crew's own records rather than be struck out entirely, unresolved and unpursued but not erased, in case anyone else at the mill ever brought the same complaint and needed to know she hadn't been the only one, or the first.

"We don't usually keep a case we're not finishing," Ezio said, uncertain of the shape of the request.

"We don't usually get asked to." Kanja had him write it exactly as she'd framed it: not a finding, not an accusation formally lodged against the mill, but a documented account, dated, unresolved by the testifier's own choice, held in the crew's records rather than the mill's for whoever might one day need it. It changed nothing about the mill's exposure that day. It meant that eight months later, when a second worker at the same mill brought an unrelated but strikingly similar shortfall claim, Ezio found her earlier account waiting in the file — not evidence he could use directly, since she'd never authorized it as testimony against a live case, but confirmation that the pattern predated the second worker by the better part of a year, context that shaped how carefully Ezio built the case that followed.

"She'll never know it helped," Ezio said, once the second case closed.

"Maybe. Or maybe she hears, someday, that the mill finally answered for something, and wonders if her own account sitting quiet in a drawer somewhere had anything to do with it. We don't get to promise her that. We just get to keep the drawer honest in case it ever matters."
