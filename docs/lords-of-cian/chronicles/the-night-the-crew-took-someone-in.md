# The Night the Crew Took Someone In

*Locked canon, Batch 224, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1021`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LVI, wave 19. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

She'd been freed two years earlier off a mining-camp raid, resettled with the others in a coastal
town built for exactly that purpose, and she'd spent both of those years learning knot-work, tide
tables, and cargo-lashing from any sailor who'd teach her, which was most of them, because she asked
better questions than most sailors bothered to ask themselves.

She found the ship at anchor and asked to see the captain directly, not through Efa Gol or through
Hask's ledger the way requests usually came.

"I'm not asking to be looked after," she said. "I've had two years of people looking after me and I'm
grateful for every day of it. I'm asking to work. I want a berth."

Kanja studied her a long moment — not doubt, just the weight of the question underneath the one she'd
asked. "You understand what this crew does. Not the legend. The actual work — long weeks, real risk,
nights that don't end the way anyone hopes going in."

"I understand it better than most who ask," she said. "I was cargo on a ship like this once. I know
exactly what it costs to be the reason a crew like yours has to fight."

He didn't decide it alone. He put it to the crew that night over the kind of meal that happened maybe
once a season — everyone aboard, no watch skipped without relief, Garren Hask breaking out a cask
he'd been saving without anyone quite remembering what occasion he'd been saving it for.

The vote wasn't close. She took her oath before the whole crew rather than privately, the way new
hands rarely did, because Efa Gol insisted the first one raised this way deserved to be seen doing it.

"Welcome aboard," Kanja said, and for once there was nothing underneath the words but exactly what
they said.
