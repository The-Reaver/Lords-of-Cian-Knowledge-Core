# Taifa Chronicle III: What Traveled Besides the Alarm

*Locked canon, Batch 177, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-524`). Homage-era Detroit, Taifa/Osei. Territory
Chronicle — Osei as protagonist, Kanja an unnamed background guest with no command, credit, or
resolution authorship. New standalone material. No new named characters.*

---

Every use of "Kin at a Distance" that Osei had ever witnessed or tested had carried danger — a raid,
a threat, need arriving whole and urgent across unconnected cities. He'd never once considered whether
the same bond carried anything else, until the night his own daughter was born and he felt, distinctly
and without meaning to send anything at all, something warmer reach every sworn hand scattered across
the claimed land's cells.

Word came back within days — sworn members in three separate cities, none of them told directly,
reporting an unexplained lift in spirit the same night, a feeling they'd assumed was simply their own
until they compared notes and found it shared.

"I didn't send that deliberately," Osei told his own closest sworn cell, still working through what
had actually happened. "I didn't know the bond carried anything but warning. I assumed, all this time,
that it only spoke in emergencies."

"Maybe it doesn't distinguish between an emergency and something that matters just as much for a
different reason," one of the cell's elders offered, having felt the same unexplained warmth himself
that night without understanding its source until now. "Joy that real, in someone bound as deeply as
you are to the rest of us — maybe that travels the same road the danger does. It's not danger. It's
just weight, moving the same way."

"I don't know that I ever wanted the bond to carry anything but warning. I built it, in my own mind,
around the worst moments — the raid, the threat, the need for hands to converge." Osei sat with the
unexpected discovery, something in it clearly moving him more than he'd anticipated. "I think I
missed, this whole time, that the same connection that lets us protect each other in crisis might
also just be us, genuinely bound, feeling each other's lives regardless of the reason."

The elder considered that a long moment. "Would you want it to stop, now that you know it isn't only
danger?"

"No. I don't think I'd want that at all." Osei looked toward where his daughter now slept, the
distant, shared warmth of that night still settling into something he was only beginning to fully
understand about the oath he'd built his life around. "If this bond is going to ask this much of
everyone sworn to it, I'm glad to learn it gives something back besides the weight of every crisis I've
ever called them to."

An unnamed man who had passed through the claimed land that same week, present for the quiet
celebration that followed, left before anyone thought to ask who he was.
