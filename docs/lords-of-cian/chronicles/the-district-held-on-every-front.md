# The District Held on Every Front

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle XLIV, second
entry in the fifteenth wave. Rebellion era, age 20, the Sewer War of Killane, its final and largest
engagement. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named
characters.*

---

The Trust's final push came at every known tunnel mouth simultaneously — five separate points of
entry across the whole district, a coordinated assault meant to overwhelm a defense that could not
possibly hold all five fronts with anything less than perfect knowledge of the ground beneath every
one of them.

Kanja had that knowledge, and he used the tunnel network itself as the answer no single defensive
line could have provided.

Mafesto carried him between fronts through connecting passages the Trust's own maps didn't show,
appearing at the second breach within minutes of holding the first, Obsidian Malice's discharge
clearing a chokepoint before doubling back through a service gallery to reinforce a third front
already buckling under pressure. Onyx handled the gaps in between — Cadence Ruin breaking a
formation at the fourth breach while Kanja himself was still three tunnels away, timed and trusted
rather than personally wielded in that moment, the sword's own edge doing work no single body could
have reached in time to do by hand.

"Five fronts, one man moving between them," the crew chief said afterward, still working out how
the defense had actually held. "How did you know which one to answer first?"

"I didn't choose by instinct. I chose by the map." Kanja traced the district's own tunnel layout,
now permanently etched in memory after six weeks of reading every foot of it. "I know this ground
better than the people attacking it, because I spent six weeks learning it the honest way, one
section at a time, while they were still drawing plans from outdated surveys. Every front I reached
in time, I reached because the tunnels themselves told me the fastest route before the fighting ever
started."

The assault broke completely within the hour, all five fronts held, the Trust's coordinated
overwhelm defeated not by superior force at any single point but by a defender who could be, in
effect, present at all five points close enough together that the network itself erased the
distance between them.

"This is the whole war, in a way," the crew chief said, once the district was finally, fully secure.
"Not brute strength. Just knowing the ground better than anyone trying to take it from you."

"That's the only kind of titan I ever wanted to be." Kanja surveyed the held district, six weeks of
patient, careful, unglamorous knowledge finally paying for itself in full. "The kind the ground
itself trusts more than the men trying to break it."
