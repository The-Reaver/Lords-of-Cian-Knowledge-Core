# The Forge at the Edge of the Map

*Locked canon, Batch 202, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-886`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XLI, wave 14 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The site sat four days beyond the campaign's furthest previously charted stop, reachable only by a
route no prior leg of the tour had risked — no reinforcement possible, no second detachment of crew
within reach, nothing between the forge and whatever came for it but Kanja himself and the dozen
apprentices who had insisted on making the crossing anyway.

The Trust force that found it had clearly counted on exactly that isolation, a full company sent
specifically because the site's own remoteness meant no rescue could arrive in time regardless of how
the engagement went.

Kanja fought it as what it was — not a battle with a fallback position, but one where every choice had
to account for the fact that nothing beyond his own resources was coming to correct a mistake. Mafesto's
Kinetic Transfer System banked every exchange's charge with unusual discipline, nothing spent that
didn't need spending, the company's numbers making patience as important as force. Obsidian Malice
discharged only twice across the entire engagement, both times decisive, each one ending a segment of
the company's advance rather than merely wounding it. Onyx worked the apprentices' own defensive
positions between engagements, Cadence Ruin buying the margin needed to rotate exhausted fighters to
safer ground without ever leaving the forge itself undefended.

The company broke after four hours, its numbers halved, its remaining officers concluding — correctly
— that whatever isolation had promised them, it hadn't delivered the advantage they'd counted on.

"No one was coming if that had gone wrong," one of the apprentices said afterward, still shaking, once
the site was secured. "I don't think I understood that until it was already happening."

"I understood it going in. That's why I fought it the way I did — nothing wasted, nothing spent that
didn't need spending." Kanja checked each apprentice in turn before he checked himself. "Most of this
campaign, I've had room to be generous with the charge because something else was usually close enough
to catch a mistake. Out here, there wasn't anything else. Just made sure there didn't need to be."
