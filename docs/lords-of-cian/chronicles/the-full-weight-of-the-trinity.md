# The Full Weight of the Trinity

*Locked canon, Batch 128, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-432`). Bane Alias Chronicle VIII. Rebellion era, age 19,
shortly after the Black Trench. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed
armor-and-weapon combat showcase per Abad's craft instruction. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose. No new named characters.*

---

The garrison at the Kessic Overwatch had been built to survive exactly the kind of assault a rebel
force could normally mount against it — triple-walled, three interlocking kill-zones, a command
tower reinforced against anything short of a direct Titan-scale strike. It had not been built to
survive one man who had bonded a full Trinity two years before anyone in the garrison knew his name.

Bane went over the outer wall in full armor, and what followed was not a battle so much as a
demonstration of exactly how much a Trinity could do when nothing was held back.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System absorbed the first kill-zone's volley crossfire directly into the
exoskeleton's plating rather than let momentum slow him — six simultaneous impacts converted to
stored charge in the time it took the garrison's second rank to loose their own volley, which found
him already past the zone entirely. Obsidian Malice discharged once against the inner gate, a
single strike concentrating two years of dormant charge into a blow that didn't so much break the
gate as make the question of its structural integrity briefly irrelevant. Onyx of Oblivion came free
of its sheath only once the gate was down, Cadence Ruin disrupting the coordinated rhythm of the
garrison's trained formation-fighting into six men each fighting alone instead of one unit fighting
together, Veil Piercer finding the gaps their own armor had never been designed to protect because no
attacker before him had ever needed to find them.

The command tower's captain met him on the top level with a loaded crossbow and the particular
calm of a man who had already accepted he wouldn't survive the next thirty seconds and had decided
to spend them well. He fired. Bane didn't dodge — Mafesto's plating absorbed the bolt's impact and
converted it, unremarkable, into the same stored charge everything else had become, and the captain
watched his last defense become fuel for the very thing it had been aimed at.

"How," the captain said. Not a question with an answer he expected to hear. Just the word, spoken
into a room he'd already accepted he wasn't leaving.

"Two years of preparation nobody outside my own crew knew was happening. A Trinity most of the
Directorate still doesn't believe is real, because believing it means admitting how unprepared every
garrison like this one actually is." Bane lowered Obsidian Malice, its cycle not yet fully spent.
"You built walls against an army. I'm not an army. I'm one man who spent two years making sure that
wouldn't matter."

The garrison surrendered within the hour, the captain among them — the Trinity's charge deliberately
left unspent rather than turned on men who no longer had anything left to fight with.
