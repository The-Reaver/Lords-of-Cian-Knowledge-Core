# What the Shadows Wouldn't Hide

*Locked canon, Batch 254, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1094`). Bane Alias Chronicle LXIV, wave 22. First
dedicated showcase of Onyx of Oblivion's Whisper of Shadows power for this alias, and its own first
established limit.*

---

The records depot held eleven captured couriers scheduled for transfer at dawn, and the only way to
reach them before then was to walk through three patrol lines without being seen doing it.

Danne Sok went in low and slow, the way he always did. Bane went in a different way entirely.

Whisper of Shadows didn't hide him. Nothing about his shape changed, nothing bent light or swallowed
sound. What it did was quieter and stranger than that — it took the specific, deliberate attention a
trained guard puts on a moving shape at the edge of a lantern's reach, and let it slide past him the
way a tired eye slides past a shadow that was probably always there. Three separate sentries looked
directly at the stretch of wall he crossed and found nothing worth reporting, because there was
nothing there that asked to be found.

It worked on every human eye in the depot. It did not work on the hound.

The animal came up off its haunches near the second gate, hackles raised, nose working at a scent
that had never learned to be talked out of noticing him. It didn't bark yet — just stood, rigid,
tracking something the guards beside it couldn't see. Bane went still. Whisper of Shadows had nothing
to offer against a nose; whatever it did to a man's attention, it did nothing to a dog's.

Danne Sok solved it before Bane had to. He'd already palmed a strip of dried meat off his own belt
for exactly this kind of contingency, tossed it wide into the dark beyond the gate, and the hound went
after it instead, dragging its handler's attention with it just long enough.

They had the couriers moving inside four minutes, out the depot's rear culvert inside eleven, and
were three miles gone before the dawn transfer crew found an empty cell block and no explanation any
of the human witnesses could offer that held together.

"You went right past two men who were looking straight at you," Danne Sok said once they'd put enough
distance behind them to talk normally again.

"It's not that they can't see me." Bane didn't look back at the depot. "It's that it convinces them
there's nothing worth seeing. A dog never needed convincing in the first place."

*Continuity notes (not narrative): first dedicated Whisper of Shadows showcase for the Bane alias
(one of Onyx of Oblivion's five named powers, `ARS-020`) — all five powers were shown together for
the Trench Monarch in "What the Sword Remembers," but Whisper of Shadows had not yet had a dedicated

human attention, not animal senses. No new named characters — Danne Sok reused; the guards and
couriers are unnamed. Opens wave 22.*
