# What the Quartermaster Kept Account Of

*Locked canon, Batch 132, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-445`). The Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias
Chronicle IX, closing the third wave. Rebellion era, age 21, Ghost Harbor. Not a territory
Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters
beyond the already-locked Garren Hask.*

---

Garren Hask kept a separate ledger for the ghost fleet's own year at sea, distinct from the crew
accounts he'd kept since the earliest days on Pier Nine — a running tally not of wages or debts owed,
but of every ship the fleet had encountered and precisely what had been done to it: disabled, not
sunk; boarded, not plundered; crews put ashore safely, not left to the sea. By the year's end, the
tally ran to hundreds of encounters, and the pattern it revealed was one Hask himself hadn't fully
appreciated until he added the final entry and read the whole account back in sequence.

"You could have sunk most of these," he said, showing Kanja the finished ledger. "Every one of them
was armed, hostile, or working directly for the Directorate. Nobody watching from outside would have
questioned it if you had."

"I know what I could have done. I've been keeping my own count too, just not on paper." Kanja studied
the tally, the pages of restraint laid out in Hask's own precise hand. "Every ship left afloat is a
crew that goes home with a story about what actually happened, instead of a story someone else
invents to fill the silence a sinking leaves behind. I'd rather be known for what's true than feared
for what isn't, even when what's true costs more effort than the alternative."

"That's not how most reputations like yours get built. Most of them get built on the version of
events that's easiest to repeat, not the one that's most accurate."

"Then I'll take the harder path, as long as your ledger keeps the accurate one somewhere it can be
found." Kanja closed the book gently, handing it back. "Keep the account, Hask. Someday the story
people tell about the Sovereign Ghost will drift from what actually happened, the way every story
eventually does. I'd like there to be a true version somewhere underneath it, in case anyone ever
bothers to look."
