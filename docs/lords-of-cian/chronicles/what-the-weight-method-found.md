# What the Weight Method Found

*Locked canon, Batch 257, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1191`). Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle LXXX, wave 27. Mafesto's density-sensing register, introduced against an asphyxiant vapor (MCD-1068), finds a new non-combat application detecting contraband hidden by mass alone.*

---

The wall read wrong before Kanja had any idea why. A storage niche that should have held nothing but packed earth and old timber backing carried a density signature that didn't match either, a subtle mass discrepancy he wouldn't have noticed at all if he hadn't spent months learning to read exactly that kind of variance since the gas pocket that first taught him to sense weight through stone instead of just heat and pressure.

"Something's in there that isn't supposed to be," he told Garren Hask, palm still resting against the wall. "Not big. Dense, though. Metal, probably, packed in deliberately."

It wasn't a trap and it wasn't a hazard. Cracking the niche open revealed a cache of counterfeit Sovereign Trust currency, professionally struck, hidden mid-wall by someone using the tunnel network's own construction as cover for smuggling operations that had nothing to do with the siege at all. Whoever had built the cache had trusted that no one checking a wall for structural integrity would think to check it for hidden mass.

They hadn't accounted for a method built to catch a killing gas by its weight alone being just as capable of catching anything else dense enough to leave a signature.

"You found that by feel?" Hask turned one of the counterfeit coins over, genuinely impressed despite the circumstances. "No cracks, no seam anyone could see."

"The wall didn't weigh what it should have. That's all I needed to know something was wrong with it." Kanja set the coin back with the rest of the cache for the council's own investigators to handle. "Started as a way to find something that could kill people without announcing itself. Turns out most things people hide don't want to announce themselves either."

The cache traced back to a smuggling ring operating three districts over, using the tunnel network's reputation for secrecy against the very people who'd built that secrecy for entirely different reasons. Kanja handed the whole find to the new workers' council rather than pursuing it himself -- a civil matter for their investigators, not a rescue or a fight, just one more thing the tunnels had quietly been hiding that honest reading finally brought into the light.
