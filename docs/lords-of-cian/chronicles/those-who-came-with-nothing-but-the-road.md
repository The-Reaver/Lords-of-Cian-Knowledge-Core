# Those Who Came With Nothing But the Road

*Locked canon, Batch 219, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-976`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle LVI, wave 19. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

They came up the coast road in a straggling column of nearly four hundred, families from three
districts further south who'd fled fighting that had nothing to do with the campaign at all — a
local warlord's collapse, and the scramble of smaller powers filling the gap he'd left — arriving at
one of the campaign's forge sites with nothing but what they'd carried and nowhere further to walk.

They weren't apprentices. They weren't a settlement the crew had any standing obligation toward.
And the raiders trailing three days behind them, picking off stragglers for whatever little the
column still carried, didn't care about that distinction either.

"They're not ours," one of the site's foremen said, watching the column's exhausted edge stumble
into the clearing around the half-built terrace. "Nobody's going to fault us for finishing the work
here and letting the district authorities sort the rest."

"I'd fault us." Kanja was already moving, directing apprentices toward what shelter the site could
offer rather than the forge itself. "They walked into the only defended ground for two days in
either direction. That makes them ours for as long as the raiders behind them think otherwise."

The raiders arrived expecting stragglers too spent to run and a forge crew too focused on its own
schedule to notice four hundred extra bodies underfoot. What they found instead was Mafesto's
Kinetic Transfer System absorbing their opening strikes at the tree line before the column's own
panic could scatter it into worse danger, Obsidian Malice discharged low across the approach to
break the raiders' formation before it ever reached the huddled families, and Onyx of Oblivion
moving the fight bodily away from the clearing, Cadence Ruin drawing every attacker toward open
ground where the sixty apprentices, armed with nothing but forge tools and told plainly to stay
behind the line, never had to be anything but witnesses.

It broke in under ten minutes. Not one refugee was touched.

"We're not part of this campaign," the column's eldest said afterward, still not quite steady on
her feet. "We didn't come here to be defended. We came here because there was nowhere else left to
walk to."

"Doesn't much matter why you came. You were on ground I was standing on when trouble found you."
Kanja looked over the exhausted column, already being fed and sheltered by apprentices who'd never
asked whether that was part of the job either. "This floor's rule was never that it only rebuilds
what it broke, or only defends what it built. It's that whatever's standing on this ground when the
trouble arrives, doesn't stand alone in it. You weren't ever going to be the exception to that just
because I hadn't met you yet."
