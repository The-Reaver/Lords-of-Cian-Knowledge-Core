# The Second Student

*Locked canon, Batch 197, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-719`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XXIV, wave 8 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The student who had once confessed, unprompted, to nearly using the doctrine against an unarmed
overseer out of anger rather than necessity had spent the months since proving, quietly and without
being asked to prove anything, that the confession had changed something real in how he worked.

Kanja set him a task without supervision for the first time: a single supply-line gate, isolated,
low-stakes enough that a mistake would cost inconvenience rather than lives, and told him nothing
else.

He watched from a distance, close enough to intervene, far enough not to be a presence the student
could lean on.

The student took nearly twice as long as Kanja himself would have — verifying the reading, verifying
it again, tracing the full tension distribution before ever raising a hand toward the structure — and
when the gate finally came down, it came down exactly as intended, no more damage than the task
required, no haste anywhere in it.

"You took your time," Kanja said afterward, not as criticism.

"You told me once that being right nine times out of nine mattered more than being fast. I didn't
have your instincts to fall back on if I rushed and got it wrong. So I didn't rush." The student
studied the collapsed gate with something that looked almost like disbelief at his own competence. "I
kept waiting for you to step in."

"I know. That was the point of not doing it." Kanja considered him for a moment. "You didn't just
learn the technique. You learned to be honest with yourself about your own limits while using it,
which is the part I actually couldn't teach you directly. That had to come from you deciding to want
it."

"Does that mean I'm finished learning?"

"It means you're finally ready to keep learning without me standing over you while you do it. Those
aren't the same thing, and I don't think they ever will be, for either of us."
