# What Grew Back on the Kessic Flats

*Locked canon, Batch 255, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1142`). Trench Monarch Alias Chronicle LXXXV, wave 29.
Rebellion era, pre-Black-Trench. Months after the ledger with no owner's name on it, something on the
Kessic flats finally takes root.*

---

The page Kanja had written in his own hand, with no owner's name at the top and no repayment schedule
beneath it, had sat in Garren Hask's separate ledger for the better part of a year before anyone
thought to check on the grove itself again.

The reclamation work had gone on the whole time regardless — crew members rotating through in
whatever hours the tally table didn't need them, clearing salted rows, carrying fresh soil in from
upriver, grafting whatever cuttings of the old root stock the woman's extended family had managed to
preserve elsewhere. Nobody had expected results quickly. Sixty years of grafted lineage didn't return
in a season, and nobody involved had ever promised her it would.

She came back to Warehouse Twelve unannounced, the way she had the first time, and found Kanja at the
tally table between disputes.

"The third row took," she said, without preamble. "Not all of it. Six trees, out of what used to be
forty. But they took, and they're not dying, and I wanted you to see the leaves before I let myself
believe it."

He went with her that same afternoon, the first time he'd walked the flats since the flood itself. Six
young grafts stood where forty mature trees once had, thin and unremarkable against the scarred silt
around them, nothing that would have caught a stranger's eye. To her, standing beside them, they
plainly meant everything the blank page in Hask's ledger had promised to hold space for without
pretending to resolve.

"It's not sixty years," she said. "It's not even close."

"No," Kanja agreed. "It's six trees. That's what it is, honestly, and I won't tell you it's more than
that."

"I know what it is." She touched one of the new grafts, careful, the way someone touches a thing they
still aren't sure will survive being touched. "I didn't come back today because I thought you'd fixed
it. I came back because six trees is still more than the ledger had a year ago, and I wanted the page
updated to say so."

Hask amended the entry himself that evening, in the same plain hand as the original: not a closed
debt, not a resolved account, just an honest addition beneath what had already been written — *six
grafts survived, third row, one year on. The rest remains owed.* Kanja read it once before it went
back in the drawer, and didn't ask Hask to make it sound more finished than it was.
