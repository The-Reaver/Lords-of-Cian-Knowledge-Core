# The Second Who Became the First

*Locked canon, Batch 260, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1262`). Crow King Alias Chronicle LXX, wave 24, opening
it. Commandant Voris's former second, now promoted in his place, faces the craft for the first time
without Voris's own study to lean on. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

Voris had stepped back from active pursuit years before, not in disgrace and not in defeat, but the
way a man sets down a study he's finally satisfied he's finished. His second took the command in his
place and, with it, inherited a filing cabinet's worth of reports, two scarecrows kept in a corner
for reasons the old commandant had never fully explained, and no clear answer for what he was
supposed to do the first time the Crow King's name came across his own desk instead of his
predecessor's.

He did what seemed reasonable: he read every report Voris had ever filed, cover to cover, twice.

It didn't prepare him the way he'd hoped. A supply column vanished from a guarded waystation under
his own command, and every procedure the reports recommended -- vary the patrol timing, don't trust
open sightlines, watch for anything too rhythmically perfect -- had already been followed to the
letter, and it hadn't mattered, because the crew hadn't used any of the tricks the reports described.
They'd used something newer, something Voris's own study had never had the chance to catalogue.

He went to Voris directly, uninvited, and found the old commandant exactly where rumor said he'd be
-- retired from the chase but not from thinking about it, a corner of his quarters still holding both
scarecrows.

"I followed everything you wrote," the new commandant said, "and it still happened."

"It will keep happening," Voris said, without any satisfaction in it. "I told you what I understood,
which was never the whole thing, only as much as one man chasing one trick for years could see.
You're not failing where I would have succeeded. You're standing exactly where I stood, except the
craft has grown since I stopped studying it and you haven't had the years I had to catch up."

"Then what do I do?"

"Build your own understanding instead of finishing mine. Mine was never going to be complete. It was
only ever going to be a start."

The new commandant left without a solution, which was, Voris told his second afterward, closer to
useful than any answer he could have handed over instead.
