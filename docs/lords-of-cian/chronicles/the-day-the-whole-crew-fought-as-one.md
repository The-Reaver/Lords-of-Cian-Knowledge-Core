# The Day the Whole Crew Fought as One

*Locked canon, Batch 138, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-461`). Captain Alias Chronicle VII, first entry in the
third wave. Rebellion era. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed
armor-and-weapon combat showcase per Abad's craft instruction, built around coordinated command
rather than solo heroics. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

Every prior Captain engagement had turned on Kanja's own gear and Trinity carrying the moment — a
solo defense, a single-person rescue. This engagement turned on something else entirely: a
Directorate strongpoint too large and too well-distributed for any one fighter, however equipped, to
take alone, and a crew large enough and drilled well enough that the actual work belonged to all of
them together, with Kanja directing rather than carrying it.

He read the strongpoint's full layout through Mafesto's sensors before a single order went out,
mapping three simultaneous approach vectors and assigning each to a crew element suited to it — Efa
Gol's decoy force drawing the garrison's attention toward the weakest-defended gate, Pell Ostra's
demolition team breaching the eastern wall on a precisely timed charge, Callum Breck leading the main
assault through the gap the demolition opened, each element moving on Kanja's own signal rather than
his own blade.

Obsidian Malice discharged only once in the entire engagement, clearing a secondary chokepoint that
threatened to trap Breck's assault element rather than opening the main breach itself — that work
belonged to Ostra's charges, exactly as planned. Onyx of Oblivion stayed sheathed until the
strongpoint's interior command post made a coordinated defense worth disrupting, Cadence Ruin
breaking it apart in seconds once committed, but the bulk of the fighting belonged to ordinary crew
members executing a plan built around their own strengths rather than around his.

"You barely fought today," Hask observed afterward, tallying casualties that came in lighter than any
prior engagement of comparable scale. "Efa Gol's decoy did more work than your own blade did."

"That was the entire point. I've got a Trinity most of this crew doesn't. That's not a reason to make
every fight about what I can do alone — it's a reason to make sure the fights where the crew's own
skill matters more go exactly as well as they can, with my own gear held in reserve for wherever it's
actually needed most." Kanja watched the strongpoint's garrison, disarmed and accounted for, far more
of the crew walking away from this one than usual. "The name means something different when the crew
wins a fight together instead of watching me win it for them. I intend to keep it meaning that as
often as I can arrange it."
