# The Tunnel He Cut Through Stone

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle XX, second entry
in the seventh wave. Rebellion era, age 20, the Sewer War of Killane. Not a territory Chronicle.
Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The existing tunnels ran out forty feet short of the safehouse cellar they needed to reach, and the
forty feet in between was solid bedrock — no clay layer, no old sewer works, nothing but stone that
would ordinarily take a proper mining crew the better part of a month to clear.

They had six hours before a Trust patrol rotation made the surface approach impossible.

Obsidian Malice had never been built as an excavation tool, but a discharge that could unmake a
shield wall in a single strike didn't much care what kind of resistance it was aimed at, and Kanja
spent the first two hours of the six alternating between controlled strikes at the working face —
each one aimed and angled the way a quarryman reads a stone's own grain before committing a chisel
to it, never a wasted blow, never a strike that risked bringing the whole ceiling down with it —
and Mafesto grounding the recoil each strike sent back through his own frame, the Kinetic Transfer
System converting what should have been a bone-jarring shock into nothing worse than heat in the
joints.

The patrol found the surface access an hour into the dig, two soldiers investigating a scouting
report of unusual sound carrying up through the ground. Kanja met them without stopping the work
entirely — Onyx handling the brief exchange in under a minute while Obsidian Malice's charge
continued to bleed back to full between strikes, the digging never fully paused, only briefly
shared with a fight neither soldier survived engaging.

By the fifth hour the stone gave way to the cellar's own foundation, and the forty feet that should
have taken a month took six hours, the tunnel's walls rough but sound, checked and shored as
carefully as any dug through softer ground.

"You cut solid rock with a war club," the safehouse's own keeper said, staring at the raw new
passage in something close to disbelief.

"I cut solid rock with a tool built for something else, because reading the grain right matters
more than what the tool was originally meant for." Kanja checked the tunnel's ceiling one last time
before calling it finished, tapping for hollow spots the way any quarryman would. "Obsidian
Malice doesn't know the difference between a shield wall and a stone face. It only knows how to
discharge what it's given, cleanly, wherever I aim it. The difference between quarrying and combat
was never really in the tool. It's in whether the hand holding it knows what it's actually looking
at."
