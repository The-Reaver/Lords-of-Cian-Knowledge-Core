# What the Kinetic Transfer System Held Up

*Locked canon, Batch 264, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1377`). Captain Alias Chronicle LXXVII, wave 26. A detailed rescue-engineering showcase: a collapsing tenement, Mafesto's gear used purely to hold structure rather than fight anyone.*

---

There was no enemy in the tenement collapse, no ambush waiting in the dust, nothing for Onyx to read or Obsidian Malice to discharge against — only a badly rebuilt post-war building giving way at its worst possible hour, with a stairwell full of people and a support beam already three-quarters through its own failure when word reached Pier Nine. Kanja was there before the district's own emergency response finished assembling, and what the moment called for wasn't a Trinity combat showcase at all. It was engineering, applied under a collapsing ceiling with people still inside it.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System had always been built around redirecting force — a blade's momentum thrown wide, a ramming cutter's shock sent sideways along pilings. Nobody had ever tried using it to hold a failing structure up rather than turn an attack aside, and there was no time to test the idea gently. Kanja braced the gauntlet directly against the beam's own failing joint and fed the building's collected downward force sideways through the system rather than letting it complete its collapse, buying — by Hask's later careful reconstruction of the timing — perhaps ninety seconds more than the beam would otherwise have held.

Ninety seconds was enough. Corren Halst ran the evacuation line through the stairwell herself, counting bodies out the way Efa Gol would have counted coin, while Callum Breck held the ground floor exit clear of the panicked crush that could have cost more lives than the collapse itself. The building came down in full less than a minute after the last person cleared the door, Kanja pulling free of the joint himself only when the redirected force finally exceeded what even the Kinetic Transfer System could keep bleeding sideways.

"That's not what that gear was built for," Corren Halst said afterward, watching him work dust and grit out of the gauntlet's housing.

"Most of what it's ended up doing for this crew wasn't what it was built for," Kanja said. "It was built to save one man's life at the Black Trench. Everything since has just been finding out how many other ways that same idea can be useful."
