# The Dark Water Ambush

*Locked canon, Batch 126, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-426`). Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle V. Rebellion
era, a new night engagement in the Gale Straits corridor. A detailed Trinity showcase in close,
zero-visibility shipboard combat. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in
neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The night raid came without running lights from three directions at once, timed for the exact hour
the fleet's own watch rotation ran thinnest, and the first Kanja knew of it was Mafesto's Kinetic
Transfer System registering an impact against the flagship's own hull before any lookout had called
a warning.

He was over the rail and onto the nearest raiding vessel before the second boat had fully closed.

Mafesto's exoskeleton read the darkness the way it had once read chemical fog — not through sight,
which the moonless night denied everyone equally, but through impact and pressure transmitted
directly into the plating, each contact converted to stored charge regardless of whether he could
see the hand that delivered it. Obsidian Malice discharged blind, aimed by the same pressure-reading
that guided Mafesto's absorption, each strike landing precisely where an attacker's own committed
weight gave away a position no eye could have confirmed in the dark. Onyx cut through the boarding
party's second wave with Whisper of Shadows doing, in true darkness, exactly what it had always done
in shadow and fog — erasing whatever certainty an attacker carried about where he actually stood.

The raid's third vessel broke off before closing at all, its own crew unwilling to press an
engagement they could hear going badly for their allies but couldn't see well enough to understand
how, and by the time dawn confirmed the extent of the failed ambush, two of the three raiding vessels
had been disabled without a single accurate report from anyone who'd actually fought in the dark
about what, exactly, had stopped them.

"Nobody on either raiding boat got a clean look at you," the flagship's own watch officer said,
reviewing what few garbled accounts had reached them from captured crew. "Half of them think you
fought with your eyes closed on purpose."

"Didn't need them open. The dark took away the one advantage they thought they had — surprise — the
moment I stopped needing to see them to know where they were." Kanja checked Obsidian Malice's
housing by feel rather than light, satisfied with a discharge cycle that hadn't required sight to
land true. "They planned an ambush around darkness being their weapon. Turned out it was never
theirs to keep."
