# The Cell They Thought Would Hold Him

*Locked canon, Batch 284, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1499`). Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XCVII, first entry
in the thirty-third wave. Rebellion era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign (MCD-241). A detailed,
battle-intense Trinity combat showcase closing on a deliberate capture-and-escape. Not a territory
Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Nobody caught the Lord of Embers by accident. Kanja let the patrol take him a half-mile outside a
garrison town whose commander had spent three months quietly diverting requisitioned iron to a
storehouse nobody official knew existed, and the only way to learn exactly where that storehouse
was, and who else was drawing from it, was to let himself be walked through the garrison's own gate
in irons and see what got said around a man everyone assumed was helpless.

It was the first time the alias's run had asked patience to carry the opening act of an engagement
instead of the Trinity. Mafesto stayed dormant against his forearm the whole first night, giving off
nothing an interrogator's hands would find. Kanja answered questions with half-truths precise enough
to sound broken and vague enough to give away nothing, and listened while two guards argued, three
cells down, about whether the storehouse's third shipment had cleared inspection yet — the detail the
whole capture had been staged to surface.

By the second morning he had the storehouse's location, its guard rotation, and the name of the
supply officer skimming from both ends of it, all without raising his voice or his hands once. Then
he broke the cell door outward with a grounded pulse timed to the changing of the guard, and the part
of the plan that had never needed patience took over completely.

Two corridors of garrison regulars stood between the cell block and the gate. Obsidian Malice cleared
the first in a single controlled discharge shaped to drop men without killing them, precision he'd
had eighteen months of the campaign's own restraint to refine; Onyx of Oblivion's Whisper of Shadows
carried him past the second corridor's watch before an alarm bell finished its first swing, and Veil
Piercer found the supply officer personally, still holding the ledger he'd been falsifying, before
the man understood the prisoner from three cells down was already standing in his office. Mafesto,
grounded and current again, took the garrison gate off its hinges on the way out rather than waste
time with the bar.

The storehouse was seized and its iron returned to the requisition rolls within the week, the supply
officer's own falsified ledger the evidence that convicted him. Garren Hask, cross-checking the
recovered stock against the campaign's own missing-material reports, found the number matched almost
exactly. "You could have taken that garrison apart from the outside in an afternoon," he said. "You
spent two days in a cell instead."

"An afternoon would have told me there was a storehouse," Kanja said. "Two days told me who else was
using it, and I wasn't finished listening for that answer when the fight started."
