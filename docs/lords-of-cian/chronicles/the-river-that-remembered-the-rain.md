# The River That Remembered the Rain

*Locked canon, Batch 259, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1245`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle LXXX, wave 27. Age
95, V3 gear. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The compound sat on a river delta's lowest ground, chosen deliberately for the same reason it now
threatened to drown everyone inside it — flat, hidden among the reed channels, invisible from the
water routes any naval patrol would use, and built with no thought at all for the one monsoon season
in ten that turned the delta's slow channels into a rising flood inside of six hours.

This was that season. The rain had started at dusk and hadn't slowed once by the time he reached the
compound on foot, wading the last quarter mile through water already at his knees and rising, the
holding cells themselves — chained captives on a floor built two feet below the compound's own
courtyard — already taking water faster than the structure's drainage could move it back out.

There was no fight in it at all, in the end. The guards had abandoned their posts an hour earlier,
saving themselves rather than their prisoners, and the actual contest was the water itself: forty
minutes to break every chain before the rising level reached the height that mattered, working by
feel in the dark as much as by sight, the Ironhand Gauntlets' reinforced knuckle plates making short
work of locks that would have cost precious minutes to pick properly.

Nineteen freed, the last of them lifted clear with water already at his own chest, and a delta that
kept rising for another two hours after the compound was empty, erasing by morning almost every
trace that a holding cell had ever stood there at all. Garren Hask's ledger recorded nineteen names
and a note about the site itself, flagged for no crew to ever bother searching again — the flood had
finished a job no raid needed to.
