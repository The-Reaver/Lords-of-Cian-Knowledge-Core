# The Storm They Called a Bluff

*Locked canon, Batch 280, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1467`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle CI,
wave 34. A new deception register: a rival power fabricates a storm warning to lure the fleet away from
the convoy it actually intends to raid. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose.*

---

The warning reached them through a fishing captain the fleet had helped once, years back, passed along
in good faith: a major storm system building fast to the north, the kind of thing that would have sent
any prudent captain running for open water well clear of the coast. Danne Sok's own instincts balked at
it before his charts could confirm or deny anything.

"There's no pressure drop," he said, checking the glass a third time. "No swell change. Nothing the sky
is actually telling me. Just a very confident man telling me what the sky's about to say."

It took him half a day to be certain the warning was fabricated — planted deliberately by a rival
trading concern that wanted the fleet's protection pulled away from a grain convoy it intended to raid
under cover of a threat that was never going to materialize. By the time Danne Sok brought it to Kanja,
the timing window to reach the convoy honestly was already tight.

"They didn't lie about a ship or a position," Kanja said. "They lied about the weather itself, betting
we'd trust it without checking. That's a new one."

The Ledger made the convoy's position with barely an hour to spare, catching the raiding party mid-
approach and ending the attempt without a shot fired once the raiders realized exactly whose sails had
appeared where a storm was supposed to have sent them running instead. No one was hurt; the raiders
withdrew rather than test a fight they clearly hadn't planned for.

"Next time someone hands you weather instead of a target," Kanja told Danne Sok afterward, "check it
twice before you believe it once."

"I already do," Danne Sok said. "This one was just good."
