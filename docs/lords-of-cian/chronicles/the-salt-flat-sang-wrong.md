# The Salt Flat Sang Wrong

*Locked canon, Batch 260, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1271`). Crow King Alias Chronicle LXXIX, wave 27,
opening it. A desert salt flat's heat-shimmer and long sound-carry force a new, wide-open
zero-cover adaptation of the craft. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

There was no marsh to hide the movement in, no urban stone to fold an echo through, no tree line to
break a sightline. The crossing they needed lay across a dry salt flat, flat enough to see a man
walking on it from two miles off on a clear morning, and the garrison holding the far side had chosen
the position specifically because nothing could approach it unseen.

What the flat gave them instead was something none of the crew had used deliberately before: sound
carried across it farther and stranger than it should have, bending oddly through air baked uneven by
the heat rising off the white crust, and the same heat threw shimmer across the horizon that made a
distant shape's true distance and true count almost impossible to judge by eye.

The third generation built the deception around both at once. A dozen voices, spaced far apart and
pitched to exploit the flat's own strange carry, threw a false count of a much larger column moving
along the horizon's shimmer-line -- a shape that, viewed through heat distortion at that range, could
have been forty men or four hundred, and the garrison's own watch, unable to resolve it either way,
reported the larger number rather than risk being wrong about a threat they could see coming from two
miles out but couldn't accurately measure.

The real crossing, six people moving low and unhurried well outside the shimmer-line's distortion,
passed while the garrison's attention held on a horizon that was lying to their own eyes as much as to
their ears.

"No cover anywhere on that flat," the third generation said afterward, still faintly sunburned. "I
kept waiting to need some, and then I realized the heat itself was doing half the work for us. The
land was already lying before we said a word."

---

*Continuity notes (not narrative): establishes desert/salt-flat terrain as a new environmental
category for this alias, distinct from marsh, highlands, urban, river-crossing, total darkness, and
mountain -- the first entry to combine long-distance acoustic carry with heat-shimmer visual
distortion in a wide-open, zero-cover setting, rather than the concealment-dependent tricks of every
prior terrain type. No new named characters. No new proper nouns -- the flat, the garrison, and the
crossing all stay unnamed.*
