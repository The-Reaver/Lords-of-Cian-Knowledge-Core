# The Season Bane Didn't Fight

*Locked canon, Batch 254, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1118`). Bane Alias Chronicle LXXXVIII, wave 30. Largest-
scale non-combat restorative-labor register in the run -- a full liberated district rebuilt ahead of
winter over several weeks, no combat across the entire entry.*

---

There was no operation to plan once the district's last garrison had stood down the month before.
What there was, instead, was a hard winter coming fast and a district with half its granaries
collapsed, its roofs stripped for fuel during the occupation, and not nearly enough hands left to fix
either before the first real frost.

Bane's column stayed six weeks. No engagement, no extraction, no gear drawn the entire time.

He spent most of it doing the kind of work that didn't ask anything of Onyx or Mafesto or Obsidian
Malice at all — setting roof beams alongside people who'd never have guessed, watching him haul
timber in a work shirt, that he was the reason their garrison wasn't standing anymore. Efa Gol ran the
granary repairs with the district's own carpenters, teaching nothing they didn't already half know,
mostly adding hands to a job that needed more of them than the district had left. Callum Breck, still
sparing with words even now, spent most of the six weeks on roof work specifically, high above the
noise, doing more with a level and a plumb line than most of the district's own tradesmen managed with
twice the conversation.

Nobody asked Bane to lead the work. He didn't. He took direction from a district elder who'd never
led anything larger than a granary crew before and had no idea what to do with a man who deferred to
her without needing to be told twice.

By the sixth week, every roof in the district's core held, the granaries were rebuilt and half-full
from an early harvest brought in under cover before the frost, and the column moved on the same
quiet way it had arrived — no ceremony, no farewell speeches, just a district that would make it
through winter that might not have otherwise.

"Six weeks and nothing to show for it in a ledger anyone but Hask keeps," Danne Sok said, once they
were back on the road.

"There's a whole district that isn't going to freeze this winter," Bane said. "I'd rather spend six
weeks on that than six hours on something that makes a better story."
