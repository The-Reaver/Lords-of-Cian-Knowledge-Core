# The Tide That Turned Twice

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-565`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XX, wave 7 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Sephtis's forecast this time wasn't a storm at all — a tidal surge, driven by a distant weather
system's pressure drop rather than any local wind, timed to arrive at a river-mouth garrison two
hours after the ebb tide should have left its approach channel unusable to anything but the smallest
boats.

The garrison's commander had built his defense around exactly that channel's unreliability, confident
no serious force would attempt a crossing at low water. He hadn't accounted for a surge arriving
against the ebb, raising the channel's depth by four feet for a window of less than ninety minutes
before the tide reasserted itself and the channel emptied again.

Kanja's boarding force went in on the surge's rising half, Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System banking
charge from the current's own push against the hull rather than any strike, and came out on the
falling half before the channel closed behind them — the tide turning against the garrison's own
expectation twice within two hours, first rising when it should have been ebbing, then dropping fast
enough to strand three of the garrison's own patrol boats on the exposed mud the moment the window
closed.

Obsidian Malice discharged once, into the garrison's watchtower gate rather than any vessel, and Onyx
cleared the interior in the confusion of men who had planned their entire defense around water depths
that no longer matched the chart they'd trained on.

"You didn't attack us," the garrison's second-in-command said afterward, watching his own patrol
boats sit stranded on mud that should have been water for another two hours. "You attacked the tide
table."

"The tide table was already going to be wrong today," Kanja said. "I just knew it before you did."
