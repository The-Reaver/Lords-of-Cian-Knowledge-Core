# What the City Walls Repeated

*Locked canon, Batch 201, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-832`). The Crow King Alias Chronicle XVII, wave 6 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

Coalgate was a walled settlement built centuries before any of its current garrison, its narrow
stone streets laid out by masons who had cared about drainage and shade far more than acoustics,
and had left behind, without meaning to, corridors that caught and threw sound back on itself in
ways no open marsh or highland ever could.

Kanja's people found this by accident, during a routine reconnaissance pass through the market
district — a single voice, pitched correctly against the angle of two facing stone walls, arriving
at the far end of the street sounding like three or four voices speaking at once, the buildings
themselves doing work no chant had ever needed to do on open ground.

Nineteen singers, positioned at specific corners the reconnaissance had already mapped, produced by
the night of the operation a sound the garrison's watch commander logged, with total confidence, as
"no fewer than two hundred armed men moving through the eastern quarter" — a number the walls
themselves had manufactured out of nineteen honest voices and the simple, ancient geometry of
narrow streets built for shade.

"Two hundred men don't just vanish," the watch commander insisted the next morning, after a full
company had been redirected overnight to a quarter that held, by dawn, no evidence any of them had
ever been there at all. "I heard them. My whole watch heard them."

"You heard the city," the crew's own logistics officer said afterward, reviewing the operation with
open admiration for a trick nobody in the crew had actually built from nothing. "We didn't invent
anything here. We just finally noticed what Coalgate's own stonemasons left lying around for
anyone patient enough to listen for it."

"That's most of the craft, honestly," Kanja told her, already sketching notes on which other walled
settlements might hold the same accidental architecture. "The marsh taught us to work with what a
place already does naturally instead of forcing something it resists. Coalgate just does it
louder than most places ever will."
