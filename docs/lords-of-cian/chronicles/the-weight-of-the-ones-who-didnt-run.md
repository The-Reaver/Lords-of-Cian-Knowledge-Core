# The Weight of the Ones Who Didn't Run

*Locked canon, Batch 254, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1113`). Bane Alias Chronicle LXXXIII, wave 28. A detailed
full-Trinity combat showcase defending a dense urban tenement district whose residents refused
evacuation, the first entry to withhold Obsidian Malice entirely as unsuitable for the terrain.*

---

The district's council had refused evacuation twice before Bane's column ever reached it — narrow
stacked tenements, families who'd already lost everything once to a Directorate relocation order and
weren't going to lose it a second time to their own side's caution. By the time the Directorate
counter-assault came, the fight would be happening with civilians embedded through every street it
touched, not cleared ahead of it the way every prior full-Trinity showcase had assumed.

Bane fought it that way rather than argue the district out of its own decision first.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System had to answer differently here than it ever had in open ground.
Redirecting a soldier's momentum into a stone tenement wall risked bringing the wall down on the
families behind it; every deflection had to be aimed along the street itself rather than into the
structures flanking it, a constraint that slowed the tempo of every engagement to something more
careful than fast.

Onyx did the work that made the careful version possible. Cadence Ruin picked apart formations before
they closed rather than in the middle of a clash where a misjudged blast radius would have mattered;
before any strike landed, its reading told him which doorways along a contested block held people
still sheltering inside, so that every redirected blow had somewhere safe to go.

Obsidian Malice never left its sheath. The terrain didn't allow for it — no discharge in these streets
could be aimed carefully enough to guarantee it hit only what it was meant to, and Bane made the call
early, before the fighting started, to fight the entire engagement without it rather than risk what
one wrong angle could do to a street full of people who'd chosen to stay.

The district held. Six hours of close, careful fighting through streets that never fully cleared of
the families living in them, and not one civilian casualty by the time the Directorate assault broke
and withdrew.

"You never drew it once," Efa Gol said afterward, nodding at the weapon still sheathed at his side.

"Some ground doesn't get the fastest answer I've got. It gets the one that doesn't risk the people
who stayed to defend it themselves."
