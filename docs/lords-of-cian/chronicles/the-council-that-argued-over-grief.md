# The Council That Argued Over Grief

*Locked canon, Batch 264, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1371`). Captain Alias Chronicle LXXI, wave 24. The dispute council splits over how the crew should formally carry a loss it didn't cause but couldn't prevent.*

---

The council session Garren Hask called after the flood wasn't about discipline — nobody aboard had done anything wrong, and the timeline Kanja had turned over a dozen times said as much every time. It was about something the charter's three written clauses had never anticipated: what the crew owed to a grief that belonged to a stranger, for a death that had happened on their watch without being their fault.

Efa Gol argued for silence — grieve it privately, she said, the way the crew had always grieved its own losses, without turning a stranger's tragedy into an institutional occasion that risked looking more like ceremony than sorrow. Callum Breck argued the opposite, harder than anyone expected from him: that a name spoken only in private was a name that could be forgotten, and Joran deserved better than a grief nobody outside the boats that failed to reach him would ever hear about.

Corren Halst tried to find the middle and couldn't, not cleanly. "We're not his family. We don't get to decide how loud his death gets mourned. But we were the last hands in that water, and pretending we're not part of the story feels like its own kind of dishonesty."

They voted, and it split three-three, Kanja abstaining deliberately rather than break the tie with his own preference — the first time since the charter's writing that a vote had genuinely deadlocked rather than resolved. Hask, presiding, didn't force a resolution that night. "Some questions this charter's going to have to learn to sit with unanswered for a while," he said, closing the session without a decision. "I'd rather we take longer and get it right than vote fast and get it wrong."

It sat unresolved for eleven days, argued in smaller conversations across the whole crew, before Mira herself — asked gently, once, what she thought her father would have wanted — gave them an answer none of the council's arguments had reached on their own.

Kanja's deliberate abstention is new -- he has cast votes as one equal seat before but never withheld one to avoid tipping a genuine deadlock. No new named characters beyond Mira and the late Joran (both established `MCD-1370`, reused here) -- Efa Gol, Callum Breck, Corren Halst, and Garren Hask all reused. Second entry, wave 24.*
