# The Second Tunnel

*Locked canon, Batch 120, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-407`). Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle IV, first
entry in the second wave. Rebellion era, a new infrastructure operation in a different city from
the Sewer War of Killane. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material. Narrated in neutral
third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The second city's own pre-modern infrastructure had been sealed and forgotten longer than Killane's,
and the crew nearly abandoned the operation twice in the first week when the old maps proved almost
entirely unreliable, drawn from a century-old survey that had never accounted for two subsequent
generations of unrecorded municipal expansion built directly on top of it.

"We could still do this from the surface," the crew chief said, on the third day of dead ends, "take
the risk of being seen instead of guessing our way through a maze that might not even connect where
we need it to."

"Give me one more day." Kanja was already tracing a wall seam with his fingers, reading the mortar's
age and composition the way the old Killane engineer had once read a pipe joint. "This section's
newer than the rest — see the mortar color shift, right here. Somebody widened this passage
deliberately, sometime after the original survey. That's not collapse or erosion. That's
construction. Which means somebody had a reason to come this way that the old map never recorded."

The widened passage led, a day later, to exactly the connection the operation needed — a forgotten
maintenance corridor cut decades after the original tunnels, built by workers solving the same
practical problem the crew was now solving, using the same practical logic. The information had
never been written down anywhere. It had only ever been legible to someone who understood
construction well enough to read a wall the way most people read a page.

"How did you know to look for that?" the crew chief asked, once the connection was confirmed and the
operation's real timeline recovered three days it had nearly lost to the outdated maps.

"Because a map only records what somebody bothered to write down, and workers solving a practical
problem underground rarely stop to file a report about it." Kanja checked the passage's structural
soundness before letting the rest of the crew through. "The tunnel doesn't lie, even when the paper
does. You just have to know how to ask it the right questions."

The operation reached its objective four days behind the original schedule and three days ahead of
where the crew chief's surface alternative would have landed them, undetected the entire way —
proof, the crew chief said afterward, that the alias's real gift was never strength at all, but a
kind of literacy nobody else on the operation had thought to bring.
