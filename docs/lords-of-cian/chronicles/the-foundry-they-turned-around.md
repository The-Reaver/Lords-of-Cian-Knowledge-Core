# The Foundry They Turned Around

*[DRAFT — awaiting batch/rule-ID assignment]. The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XXIX, wave 9.
Rebellion era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign. A detailed showcase of the Trinity defending a
captured Trust foundry mid-conversion. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person
prose. No new named characters.*

---

The captured foundry had belonged to the Trust three weeks earlier — seized intact during an
engagement further up the coast, its furnaces still running, its stock rooms still full, and the crew
had spent every day since converting its layout, its output, its entire purpose from garrison
supply to district tool-making. It was, by the time the counterstrike came, only half-turned: half its
furnaces still bore the Trust's own brand marks, half its stock still marked for requisitions that
would never be filled.

The Trust detachment sent to retake it came expecting a foundry still functionally their own,
defended by whatever militia the campaign could spare. They found the Trinity waiting instead.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System absorbed the detachment's opening volley directly into the
foundry's own furnace-floor stonework, converting kinetic force into stored charge without so much as
scorching the half-converted equipment around it. Obsidian Malice discharged along the foundry's main
gantry, collapsing the detachment's approach route without touching a single furnace it fed through.
Onyx cleared the loading bay in a sequence tight enough that not one incendiary charge — the
detachment's clear intent to simply burn what it couldn't reclaim — ever reached fuel enough to
catch.

By the time the detachment broke off, the foundry stood exactly as converted as it had been that
morning — no further along, no worse off, every furnace still running toward its new purpose rather
than its old one.

"They'd rather have burned it than let us keep it," the conversion crew's own lead said, surveying
the undamaged floor. "Didn't expect that."

"I did." Kanja checked the nearest furnace's draft, satisfied it hadn't lost heat through the fight.
"A foundry that used to make weapons for people who burned districts doesn't get destroyed just
because we can't finish turning it fast enough to suit somebody's timeline. It gets defended until
it's finished becoming something better. That's a slower kind of rebuilding than the ones this
campaign usually does. Doesn't make it a lesser one."
