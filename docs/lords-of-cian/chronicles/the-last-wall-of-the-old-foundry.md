# The Last Wall of the Old Foundry

*Locked canon, Batch 197, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-738`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XLIII, wave 15 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The condemned foundry had been converted, years earlier, into a holding facility for conscripted
laborers too injured or too old to be worth transferring to an active production site — left in a
structure the Trust itself had already written off as unsafe, its support framework rusted well past
any standard the original foundry-masters would have tolerated, the workers inside kept less by
guards than by the simple fact that the building itself would likely kill anyone who tried to force an
exit through anywhere but the single supervised door.

Kanja read the framework before deciding anything.

"It's already failing on its own," he said, the resonance signature returning something closer to a
structure mid-collapse than one merely under load. "Whatever I do here has to work with that, not
against it. A hard discharge risks bringing down sections that are barely holding as it is."

It required a different kind of precision than any healthier structure had ever demanded — not
targeting a specific failure point, but stabilizing the framework's remaining sound sections just long
enough to open a controlled exit route through the compromised ones, the discharge tuned to guide an
already-failing collapse rather than cause a fresh one.

Mafesto held the ceiling's worst section steady through raw absorbed force while the workers filed out
through the opening Obsidian Malice's discharge had carved with more restraint than power, Onyx
counting each person clear before the last supports were finally allowed to give way behind them.

The foundry came down completely within minutes of the last worker reaching open ground — not
destroyed by the engagement, simply finally allowed to finish the collapse it had already been years
into becoming.

"They kept people inside something that dangerous rather than spend the coin on somewhere safe," the
oldest of the freed laborers said, watching the last of the old foundry settle into rubble. "Guarded
door. Rotting walls. Didn't much matter which one killed us first, most days."

"Neither one gets the chance now." Kanja studied the wreckage a moment longer. "This is the only
foundry I've ever brought down that was already trying to fall on its own. I just made sure it did it
without anyone left standing inside when it finally did."
