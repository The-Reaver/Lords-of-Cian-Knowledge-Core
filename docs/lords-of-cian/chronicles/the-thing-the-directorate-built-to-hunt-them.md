# The Thing the Directorate Built to Hunt Them

*Locked canon, Batch 199, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-793`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle XXXVIII, wave 13 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

It didn't fly Trust colors because it wasn't a ship in any sense the fleet had fought before — a
Directorate-built war-construct, iron-hulled and unmanned below the waterline, designed by engineers
who had studied every prior Sovereign Ghost engagement and built something specifically to answer each
trick the fleet had ever used.

"No crew to demoralize," Dol Maren reported, watching it close from a half-mile out, its approach
mechanically precise in a way no human helmsman's ever was. "No captain to surrender. Whatever this is,
it doesn't get afraid."

"Then fear was never going to be the weapon that stops it." Kanja studied the construct's hull, dense
with iron in a configuration too deliberate to be ordinary shipbuilding. "They built this to counter
the anchor-chain weapon specifically. Look at the plating pattern — designed to disperse a magnetic
pulse rather than let it seize."

It nearly worked. The anchor-chain device's usual paralysis found no purchase against a hull engineered
precisely to defeat it, and the construct pressed its attack with a relentless, fearless persistence
that forced the engagement into pure combined-arms combat far faster than any human crew would have
allowed.

Mafesto absorbed its opening ram attempt, the Kinetic Transfer System converting a collision that
would have staved in an ordinary hull into stored charge redirected straight back through Obsidian
Malice's next discharge — a strike aimed not at the construct's armored hull but at the exposed
mechanism driving its rudder, the one component its engineers hadn't thought to armor as heavily,
having assumed no boarding crew would survive long enough to find it.

Onyx found that assumption wrong in under a minute, Whisper of Shadows carrying him past the
construct's mechanical defenses to the rudder housing itself, Veil Piercer opening it to Obsidian
Malice's final discharge — the construct listing, disabled, its relentless approach finally broken not
by fear, which it had never possessed, but by the same patient search for the one vulnerability every
built thing carried somewhere.

"They built something that can't be frightened," Kanja said afterward, studying the disabled hull with
genuine respect for the engineering even as he catalogued its failure. "That's a real answer to half
of what makes this alias work. It just forgot that the other half was never about fear at all. It was
always about finding the weak point nobody else bothered to look for."

The Directorate never deployed a second construct. Whatever the engineers had learned from the first
one's failure, they'd apparently concluded the fleet's actual advantage — patient, adaptable skill —
wasn't something a single machine, however well designed, could be built to counter.
