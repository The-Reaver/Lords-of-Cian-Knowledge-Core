# Lauris Chronicle XCV: The Hand That Wasn't Hers

*Locked canon, Batch 300, 2026-09-18 (`MCD-1712`). Ninety-fifth entry in Lauris Letitia's own
Chronicle series, part of Strand L. Opens a new debt at Site K-Theta's relocated concealment site
(`MCD-190`/`193`): on a routine rotation visit, Lauris finds competent, unexplained signs that
someone other than herself has recently maintained it. Deliberately does not confirm or rule out a
connection to Val Mirel Kareth's own parallel-aligned War-Order operatives (`MCD-198`/`212`), and
does not advance or touch the knowledge-staging detail `MCD-193` reserves for a future book. No
new named characters. Narrated by Fermand Aurelias.*

-----

*Archive fragment, present-day: "Someone was here before me. Not against the concealment -- for
it. I did not leave what I found. I do not know who did. I am recording the fact of the visit
without pretending I understand what it means."*

I have accompanied Lauris to Site K-Theta's relocated cave system on perhaps a dozen occasions
across the years I have kept her archive, always on the same careful, staggered rotation she
applies to every site of this kind, and I have come to know its rhythms well enough to recognize
when something about the approach differs from what I expect. This visit differed before we had
even reached the concealment's outer perimeter.

The discrepancy was small, and I want to record it exactly as she described finding it, because I
think the smallness is precisely why it unsettled her. A section of the concealment's outer
structural bracing -- stonework she herself reinforced during the original relocation, and has
maintained on every subsequent visit since -- had been repaired. Not damaged and left, which would
have been alarming in an entirely different register. Repaired, cleanly, using a technique
consistent with her own original method, by someone who was neither her nor, so far as she could
determine from every mark left behind, any member of the two hundred sheltered within.

She confirmed this carefully before drawing any conclusion, which I record because I think it says
something true about how seriously she takes a finding of this kind. She checked the interior
population count against her own last visit's record -- unchanged, no discrepancy, no sign of
distress or disturbance among the sheltered subjects themselves. She examined the repair itself at
length, comparing its technique against her own remembered method closely enough that she was
confident, by the end, that whoever performed it understood not merely that the bracing needed
maintaining, but precisely how her own original work had been constructed -- a level of technical
familiarity she does not believe could be reconstructed from casual observation of the site alone.

*

"Someone found this place," she told me, once we had withdrawn to a safe distance to speak
plainly. "Someone found it, understood immediately what it was for, and did not disturb it, expose
it, or report it. They repaired a structural weakness I had not yet gotten to myself and left
without taking anything, without leaving any message, and without, so far as I can determine,
letting the population inside know they had ever been there at all."

I asked the question I suspect any chronicler in my position would have asked first: whether she
believed the visitor connected to Val Mirel Kareth, whose own War-Order operatives have tracked
Lauris's operational profile at a distance for roughly eighteen hundred years without initiating
contact, and whose alignment with Lauris's own work Sephtis has described to her, more than once,
as parallel rather than coordinated. She did not answer quickly.

"I cannot confirm it," she said. "I cannot rule it out, either. The competence is consistent with
the kind of operative the War-Order trains. So, for that matter, is the restraint -- finding
something this significant and choosing not to act on it beyond quietly protecting it further is
exactly the posture Sephtis has described to me as the War-Order's own standing doctrine toward
operatives outside its direct command. But competence and restraint are not a signature. I have no
name to attach to this, and I am not currently willing to invent one simply because a name would
make the discomfort easier to hold."

*

I asked, further, whether this changed anything about her own standing arrangement -- whether a
find of this kind was, perhaps, the operational reason Sephtis's broker-contact protocol has been
waiting, unused, to answer. She was quiet longer over this question than over any other in the
account. "It might be," she said, eventually. "I do not think I am ready to decide that it is. I
have spent a great many years now content to let Val Mirel and I occupy the same ground without
occupying it together. I do not know that one repaired wall changes that arrangement's terms. I
know that it is the first time I have had concrete evidence, rather than a standing assumption,
that the ground is genuinely shared."

She has left the repair as she found it, added a note to her own archive's site record, and
continued her rotation without altering its schedule or attempting to identify the visitor further.
She has not, as of this account, invoked the broker-contact protocol.

Her closing note: *Someone was here, and meant no harm by it, and I still do not know who. I am
recording this as an open question rather than a closed relief, because I have learned this season
what it costs to mistake the first for the second.*
