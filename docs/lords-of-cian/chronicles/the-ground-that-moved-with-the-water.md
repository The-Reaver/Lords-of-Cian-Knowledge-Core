# The Ground That Moved With the Water

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-583`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXVIII, wave 13 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The garrison holding the bridge had built its defense around solid footing — packed earthworks,
fixed gun positions, a killing ground it had drilled to hold against any assault approaching from dry
land. It had never drilled to hold ground that was actively becoming a riverbank underneath it.

Mafesto crossed with the first wave of transport craft, the Kinetic Transfer System banking the
current's own push against the hull rather than fighting the flood directly, and reached the near
bank before the garrison's gun crews had finished adjusting their aim to account for footing that kept
shifting under their own feet as the rising water undercut the earthworks they stood on. Obsidian
Malice discharged into the base of the bridge's near-side gate tower, not to collapse it outright but
to crack its foundation enough that the rising water finished the work minutes later, the tower
settling at an angle that made its own gun positions useless.

Onyx moved through the resulting chaos — soldiers abandoning fixed positions that had stopped being
fixed, mud and rising water turning drilled formation into scattered footing no training had prepared
for — Veil Piercer finding gaps in a defense that was, quite literally, dissolving under the men
holding it.

"You didn't storm the bridge," the garrison's surviving officer said afterward, standing on ground
that would be underwater again within the day. "You made the ground itself fight against us."

"The ground was always going to move," Kanja said. "I just knew when, and you didn't."
