# What the Storm Exposed

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-574`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXIX, wave 10 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The embedded agent made his attempt two nights before the envoy's ship was scheduled to depart with
or without him — a break-in aimed not at Sephtis's charts directly, which were too voluminous and too
dependent on years of accumulated judgment to simply steal, but at the smaller, more portable record
of his method's underlying principles: the reasoning itself, condensed into a set of working notes
meant only for training the second sky-reader.

He chose the night deliberately — a storm Sephtis had predicted days earlier, correctly assuming the
weather would draw attention and cover elsewhere while he worked.

He hadn't accounted for the Trinity treating a predicted storm as routine rather than distracting.
Mafesto intercepted him at the tent's outer perimeter before he'd finished forcing the lock, the
Kinetic Transfer System banking the storm's own wind load rather than expending effort fighting it,
freeing full attention for the intruder instead of the weather. Obsidian Malice discharged once,
into the ground at the agent's feet rather than at the man himself, close enough to end the attempt
without a fatal strike. Onyx confirmed no second intruder was working a diversion elsewhere, Whisper
of Shadows moving the storm's own confusion in Kanja's favor rather than the agent's.

"You picked the one night we're never less alert," Kanja told him, disarmed and held in the rain. "The
storm isn't when we relax. It's when we're already braced for something to try to use it."

The notes were recovered undamaged. The agent, questioned separately from the envoy's formal
delegation, confirmed the theft had been ordered rather than freelanced — confirmation enough for
Kanja to have the envoy's ship searched before its departure, and enough for the foreign government's
own later communications to drop the exclusivity demand entirely.
