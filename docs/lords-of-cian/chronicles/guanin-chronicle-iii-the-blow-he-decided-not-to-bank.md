# Guanín Chronicle III: The Blow He Decided Not to Bank

*Locked canon, Batch 164, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-511`). Homage-era NYC, Guanín/Eri Kotoko. Territory
Chronicle — Eri Kotoko as protagonist, Kanja an unnamed background guest with no command, credit, or
resolution authorship. New standalone material. No new named characters.*

---

A junior counting-house clerk, young and plainly out of his depth, insulted Eri Kotoko publicly at a
hearing — a petty, personal jab rather than any real institutional wrong, the kind of provocation
that, six years earlier, might have gone straight into the same patient ledger that had once produced
"The Unanswered Blow" against a far more deserving target.

Eri Kotoko let it pass without a word.

"You could have banked that," a younger organizer observed afterward, having watched the exchange
with some confusion at the lack of response. "I've heard the stories. I expected something."

"The stories are about a debt six years in the making, against a man who'd earned every bit of what
finally reached him. This was a nervous clerk saying something foolish because he didn't know how else
to seem important in a room full of people who intimidated him." Eri Kotoko said it without rancor,
genuine perspective rather than performed patience. "Not every provocation is worth carrying. Some of
them are just noise, and the discipline isn't only in knowing how to answer a real wrong precisely
when the moment comes. It's in knowing which moments don't deserve the weight of an answer at all."

"How do you tell the difference?"

"Scale, mostly. Intent, when I can read it. Whether the person doing it actually has power over
anything that matters, or whether they're just posturing because they're afraid of the room." Eri
Kotoko watched the young clerk, still visibly rattled by his own outburst, quietly retreat from the
hearing floor. "If I banked every slight the way I banked that one real debt, I'd spend my whole life
carrying weight that was never worth carrying in the first place. The gift isn't a reflex. It's a
choice, made carefully, about what actually deserves it."

The young clerk approached him privately after the hearing, awkward and apologetic, and Eri Kotoko
accepted the apology plainly, the incident already fully closed in a way six years of banked
provocation against Guanín's true debts had never been.

An unnamed man seated near the back of the hearing hall, present for the exchange and its quiet
aftermath, left without comment once the room had emptied.
