# Lauris Chronicle XXII: The Wall That Came Apart in Silence

*Locked canon, Batch 299, 2026-09-18 (`MCD-1639`). Twenty-second entry in Lauris Letitia's own
Chronicle series, a Strand D (Sealbound Directorate years) entry giving full-scene treatment to
Operation 2, the Sister-of-Voren Abduction (previously only summarized at `MCD-1543`): her
fourteen-kilometer night swim, the stacked-stone perimeter wall disassembled rather than forced to
avoid a percussive signature, and the roughly seven-minute extraction of Directorate archivist
Sister Vaneth of Voren from a fortified former Directorate watch-station island, in place of a
projected two-hundred-enforcer Directorate assault. Set within her ten Apprentice Contracts, with
her assigned mentor Vael Korr-Drennen (`MCD-176`) present only at the briefing -- an early data
point toward his own later retrospective assessment that she required no guidance from her second
contract onward. No new named characters; Sister Vaneth of Voren and Vael Korr-Drennen already
locked.*

-----

*Archive fragment, Apprentice Contracts, Operation 2, designation LL-001-S-002: "Two hundred
enforcers were offered. I required one wall, persuaded, for the length of time it took me to pass
through it, to remember that it had never truly been finished being built."*

Vael Korr-Drennen read the operational brief for Operation 2 twice before he set it down, which I
understand, from everything Lauris has since told me of him, to have been a considerable departure
from his usual habit. Eight hundred years a Directorate contractor teaches a man to skim. He did
not skim this one. A Directorate archivist, Sister Vaneth of Voren, taken from a Coastal Voskharen
records facility by Vorenist paramilitaries and held on a fortified island roughly fourteen
kilometers offshore -- the standing Directorate response to such a holding being, at minimum, two
hundred enforcers, a projected forty to sixty of their own dead, and a coin-flip's chance the
hostage would not survive the taking regardless.

"You intend to go alone," Vael said. Not a question, by then. She had told him as much in her first
sentence.

She has recorded his objection faithfully in her own archive, and I record it here in the same
spirit: he did not doubt her capability. He doubted the Directorate's willingness to explain, to a
board of inquiry, why its newest external asset had been permitted to attempt alone what the
standing doctrine called for two hundred men to attempt together. She told him the doctrine assumed
a wall could only be gone through one way. He signed the contract's amendment within the hour.

*

The swim itself she has never described to me as difficult, which I have learned to read, with
Lauris, as its own kind of information -- she reserves the word for very little. Fourteen
kilometers of open water, undertaken at night, at a pace that would have exhausted a Cian-born
swimmer inside the first three. Karesian density does not float. It also does not tire the way a
lighter body tires, and Ironstorm Blood's oxygen-binding capacity made the crossing, by her own
account, closer to a long walk than a swim in any sense a Directorate physician would have
recognized.

The island's perimeter was a stacked-stone wall of the old fortification style, load-bearing course
upon course, the kind of construction a demolition charge announces itself against from a
considerable distance and a battering ram announces against from a lesser one. Lauris did neither.
She read the wall the way I have since watched her read every structure she has ever stood in front
of -- not as an obstacle to be overcome but as an argument to be understood, each stone's placement
a claim about which of its neighbors it depended on to stay upright. She removed them in the
reverse order of the argument that had put them there, one at a time, each stone set down rather
than dropped, until the wall had not been broken so much as persuaded, briefly, to stop being a
wall. No percussive signature reached the garrison. No sentry's count of ordinary night sounds
changed by so much as one.

She found Sister Vaneth of Voren in the central keeping-house within, by her own recorded timing,
seven minutes of the breach -- an archivist by trade, calm in the particular way I have come to
associate with people who have spent long careers cataloguing worse things than their own capture,
and who recognized, in the manner of Lauris's approach, something she said afterward reminded her
of her own discipline. "You are careful with things," Sister Vaneth told her, in the brief exchange
Lauris's archive preserves. "I did not expect that from a rescue." Lauris did not answer this
directly; she has told me only that she found the observation accurate and has thought of it
several times since.

They were clear of the island roughly forty minutes later, the full engagement -- entry to
departure -- closing at forty-seven minutes by Lauris's own count. Of the approximately sixty
paramilitaries garrisoned there, not one was killed; the contract had specified acquisition, and
Lauris has told me she has never once, across her career, allowed a contract's specified terms to
drift wider than what they actually asked of her, a discipline she credits, not entirely in jest,
with keeping her conscience considerably lighter than her reputation would suggest it ought to be.

*

Vael's own report on Operation 2, brief and, I am told, uncharacteristically without qualification,
reads simply: *The wall was disassembled, not breached. The hostage was recovered, unharmed, in
under an hour. I did not advise the method used. I would not have known to.* It is not the sentence
he would eventually be remembered for -- that one came at the end of ten contracts, not the second
of them -- but I have come to think of Operation 2 as the quiet first draft of that later
conclusion, the moment a mentor eight hundred years into his own career first understood that the
thing he had been assigned to teach had, in some fundamental sense, already arrived knowing it.

Her own closing note: *A wall is only ever an argument someone has already won once. I am simply
better than most at finding the terms on which it was won.*
