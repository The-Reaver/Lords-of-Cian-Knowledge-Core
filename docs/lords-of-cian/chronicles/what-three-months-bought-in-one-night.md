# What Three Months Bought in One Night

*Locked canon, Batch 192, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-577`). The Storm That Walks Alias Chronicle XXXII, wave 11 of the ten-wave sixth-through-fifteenth run. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The storm arrived in the blockade's twelfth week, called eight days in advance the moment Sephtis's
method finally had a system worth charting, and the harbor's garrison — worn down by the same three
months of waiting that had strained Kanja's own crew, but without any forecast at all to plan against
— had let its own readiness slip in ways a shorter siege would never have allowed.

Mafesto led the breach through the harbor's outer chain boom, the Kinetic Transfer System banking the
storm surge's own force against the chain's anchor points until three months of patient positioning
paid off in a single controlled strike that opened the harbor's mouth to the full fleet behind it.
Obsidian Malice discharged twice against the garrison's shore batteries, timed to the storm's heaviest
rain band so that the gunners' own reduced visibility did more to blind them than the strikes
themselves. Onyx moved through the garrison's command post in driving rain that had, after twelve
weeks of nothing, become almost unfamiliar to Kanja's own crew — Whisper of Shadows and Cadence Ruin
both deployed at a scale the doctrine hadn't called for since the blockade began.

The harbor fell in under six hours, the entire three-month siege resolved in a single night the moment
the sky finally gave the doctrine something to work with.

"Every week we waited felt like nothing was happening," the quartermaster admitted afterward, watching
the garrison's colors come down in the storm's last hour. "I didn't understand until tonight that the
waiting wasn't wasted. It was the whole fight. Tonight was just where it finally showed."

"That's the doctrine most people never see," Kanja said. "It's not the storm. It's everything held
ready, patiently, for however long it takes the storm to actually come."
