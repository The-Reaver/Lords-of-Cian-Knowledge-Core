# What the Golden Terror Left Behind

*Locked canon, Batch 122, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-413`). The Scourge Alias Chronicle IV, first entry in
the second wave. Long Mask era, the Golden Terror period (ages 80-180, `ARS-348`). A detailed
showcase of the mature V3 loadout. Onyx remains sealed at L9 for the full 284-year Long Mask period
(`MCD-267`-area rule) and does not appear. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material.
Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The young crew member who'd spent two years secretly shadowing the Scourge's every documented
engagement, studying survivor accounts and salvaged fight-scene evidence with the single-minded
devotion of someone building a life's ambition on borrowed technique, finally got close enough to
ask the question directly during a lull in a coastal raid's aftermath.

"How do you move like that in the coat? I've tried copies of it. Every one weighs me down past
useful."

Kanja considered the question seriously, the V3 Forge-Coat's triple-layer composite still settled
around him — Dark-Drakma leather outer shell, articulated Dead Drakma plates, thermal lining graduated
precisely to protect the torso's vital structures first and the limbs' mobility second, a design
philosophy the coat's very weight distribution encoded. "You're not supposed to move like the coat
isn't there. You're supposed to learn to move like it's part of you, which takes longer than most
people are willing to spend on any one piece of equipment. Twenty years, near enough, between the
first version and this one, adjusting what it protects and how much it costs me in speed to protect
it."

"Twenty years for one coat."

"Twenty years for one relationship with a coat. It's not the same as owning it." He demonstrated,
briefly, the Ironhand Gauntlets' convex knuckle geometry catching a training strike and redirecting
its force rather than simply absorbing it, the Ironfall Boots' impact soles transmitting a
warning tremor through the deck before the follow-through even landed. "The Sovereign Eyes weren't
built to look frightening. That's an accident nobody designed on purpose, calibrated later once we
learned it worked. Everything else on this loadout, every piece, took years of getting it wrong
first. You're trying to copy twenty years of mistakes in an afternoon. It doesn't work that way."

"Then how do I actually learn it?"

"The same way I did. Wear something lighter. Get hurt in it a few times, in ways that teach you
exactly what it should have protected and didn't. Build the next version smarter because of it.
Repeat that for longer than feels reasonable." Kanja checked the coat's shoulder vents, the Smoke
System's capsules already reloaded for the return voyage. "There's no shortcut to the part where the
equipment stops being equipment and starts being an extension of judgment you've already earned
some other way. I can't hand you that in an afternoon. Nobody can hand anyone that."

The young crew member spent the following decade building an entirely original loadout of their own,
never quite matching the Scourge's own silhouette and, by their own later account, glad of it — proof,
they'd say afterward, that the actual lesson had finally landed.
