# The Night All Five Forges Burned at Once

*Locked canon, Batch 189, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-552`). The Lord of Embers Alias Chronicle XVI, first
entry in the fifth wave. Rebellion era, age 27, the Rolling Foundry Campaign. Not a territory
Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed, large-scale combat showcase per Abad's craft
instruction. Narrated in neutral third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

Five linked forge sites, spread across a full day's travel from each other but coordinated under the
Rolling Foundry Campaign's own supply chain, came under simultaneous Directorate assault in the same
hour — a coordinated strike specifically designed to deny Kanja the chance to defend more than one
site personally, forcing an impossible triage across distances no single fighter should have been able
to cover.

He didn't choose one site. He moved between all five.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System converted the frantic pace between sites into stored charge rather
than exhausting him, each arrival timed to the precise moment a site's own defenses were closest to
failing. Obsidian Malice discharged at the second site, breaking an assault that had already breached
the outer perimeter, before Kanja doubled back through terrain no ordinary rider could have crossed
in time to reach the third. Onyx of Oblivion cleared the fourth site's own attackers in a sequence
brief enough to leave for the fifth within minutes, Cadence Ruin disrupting a coordinated breach before
it could fully form.

By the time the hour ended, all five forges stood — none of them undefended long enough to fall,
though several came close enough that the campaign's own smiths would later describe the night as the
closest the Rolling Foundry Campaign ever came to losing everything at once.

"Five sites, one man, and you reached every single one before it broke," the campaign's senior smith
said, still processing the night's scale. "I didn't think that was possible, not even for you."

"I didn't think so either, honestly, until I was already moving and there wasn't time to reconsider
whether it was possible." Kanja surveyed all five forges, still standing, still working. "They built
their attack around the assumption I could only be in one place. I spent this whole campaign making
sure that assumption would eventually cost them everything, the one time they actually tested it at
full scale."
