# What the Rigging Remembered

*Locked canon, Batch 158, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-498`). The Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XI. Rebellion
era, age 25. Not a territory Chronicle. New standalone material — a detailed naval application of
the resonance doctrine per Abad's craft instruction, its first use at sea. Narrated in neutral
third-person prose. No new named characters.*

---

The Directorate blockade ship held its position through rigging tensioned to a precision no
conventional boarding action had ever managed to exploit — masts and stays under calculated load,
built by naval engineers who'd studied the Iron Bastard's land-based engagements closely enough to
believe a ship's rigging bore no resemblance to a bridge or a Crawler's alloy frame.

Applied carefully, after the lesson of a recent misdiagnosis had made him slower and more certain
before committing, Kanja found the rigging read almost exactly like every other tension-bearing
structure he'd ever diagnosed — the same underlying physics, however unfamiliar the shape carrying
it.

Mafesto's sensors mapped the full rigging network from the water below, identifying the load-bearing
sequence that kept the blockade ship's masts standing under sail. Obsidian Malice's discharge, timed
precisely against the mainmast's own resonant frequency, didn't sink the ship or endanger its crew —
it dropped the sails and rigging in a controlled, contained collapse that left the vessel dead in the
water without a single cannon fired or a hull breached, the blockade broken through immobilization
rather than destruction.

"You did to our rigging what you've apparently done to bridges and armored vehicles," the ship's
disarmed captain said, watching his own crew work to clear the fallen sailcloth. "I confess I didn't
expect the same principle to apply at sea."

"Tension is tension, whether it's holding up a bridge or holding a sail to the wind. I nearly forgot
that myself, recently, in a way that cost people something real." Kanja surveyed the disabled ship,
no casualties on either side. "I'm more careful now about assuming I understand a structure just
because it resembles ones I've read before. This one, I took the time to actually listen to first."

The blockade broke within the hour, the immobilized ship towed clear by its own crew rather than
scuttled — the doctrine's naval debut costing considerably less than the land engagement that had
preceded it, the caution learned from that earlier failure paying forward directly into this one.
