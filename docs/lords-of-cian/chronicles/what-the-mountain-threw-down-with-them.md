# What the Mountain Threw Down With Them

*Locked canon, Batch 283, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1488`). Iron Bastard Alias Chronicle XCV, wave 32. A detailed, battle-intense full-Trinity combat showcase against raiders who weaponize the exact geological fracture identified in `MCD-1487`. Not a territory Chronicle.*

---

Someone had been listening at the elder's council after all, or paid a spy to, because the raiders who came down on the settlement three weeks later brought sappers and enough black-powder charge to know exactly where to place it. Not against the houses. Against the seam Kanja had named as the one place in that whole cliff face that would fail on its own timetable — and would fail a great deal sooner if someone helped it along.

He heard the drilling before he saw the raiders' vanguard clear the tree line, and understood the shape of the whole plan in the same breath: overrun the shoreline defenders while the mountain did the rest of the killing for them.

"Mafesto," he said, already moving, "ground everything they throw at us — I need both hands and everything Onyx has left to me for the rock, not the men in front of it." Kinetic Transfer System took the first volley of arrows and answered fire without asking him to spend attention on it. Onyx cleared the vanguard in three passes — Cadence Ruin breaking their line's rhythm, Veil Piercer finding the gaps their shields left open, Whisper of Shadows putting him behind the flank before they'd finished reacting to the first strike — while Kanja's own attention stayed split, one hand on the rock face itself, tracking the charge crew's drilling against the seam's own slow, honest strain in real time.

He couldn't stop the charge from firing. There wasn't time, and stopping it wasn't the read he needed anyway. What he could do — what the doctrine had never once been asked to do before — was choose where the mountain's answer landed. Doubled verification confirmed the seam's true weak point wasn't exactly where the sappers had drilled, close but not identical, a hand's width off toward the empty ravine side rather than the settlement's roofs. Obsidian Malice's discharge went into that true point a half-breath before the charge's own blast reached it, redirecting the coming failure's whole weight away from the houses and down into open ravine instead of down onto anyone's roof.

The rockfall that followed was real, loud, and enormous, and it killed no one but three raiders too slow retreating from their own trap. Kanja stood in the settling dust afterward with Mafesto's absorbed impact still humming faint in his frame, Onyx quiet again at his side, and Obsidian Malice's housing warm from a discharge that had never once been aimed at a person.

"You beat the mountain," a defender said, staring up at the new scar in the cliff face.

"I didn't beat anything. I just made sure it fell somewhere honest instead of somewhere cruel."
