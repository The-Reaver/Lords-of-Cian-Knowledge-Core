# The Two Tunnels That Never Should Have Met

*Locked canon, Batch 257, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1196`). Blue-Collar Titan Alias Chronicle LXXXV, wave 29, opening the wave. A multi-party criminal standoff entry -- two separate illicit operations dig into the same passage from opposite ends, forcing Kanja to defuse a confrontation without enforcing for either side.*

---

Neither crew had known the other existed until the wall between them gave way. One had been running a smuggling route for goods the siege's rationing made valuable enough to risk digging for; the other had been cutting toward the same unclaimed passage to move something considerably less forgivable if it were found. Both had assumed they were alone down there. Both were now facing each other across a breach neither had planned, weapons half-drawn, in a standoff that had nothing to do with the resistance or the Directorate at all.

Kanja arrived to a tunnel full of people who wanted him to be one thing or the other -- an enforcer come to shut down the smuggling route, or a lawman come to break the more dangerous operation entirely -- and he was neither, which took longer to establish than the actual danger warranted.

"I'm not here for either crew's business," he said, standing in the gap between two groups that would rather have been fighting each other than talking to him. "I'm here because a standoff this close to a working gallery kills people who have nothing to do with whatever this is."

That got their attention faster than any threat would have. The smuggling crew, weighing their actual crime against the danger of a fight in an enclosed space, backed off first, willing to abandon the passage rather than risk it. The second crew took more convincing -- Kanja made plain, without raising his voice, exactly what happened to any operation found using his tunnels for the kind of trade the resistance wouldn't tolerate, a warning specific and credible enough that they sealed their own end of the breach and left without further argument.

He didn't turn either crew over to anyone. The smugglers kept operating, watched more closely now; the second crew's passage was destroyed outright, its purpose reported to the council for follow-up he had no interest in personally pursuing.

Danne Sok, arriving after the worst of it had already passed, found him resealing the breach himself.

"You could've just let them fight it out," Sok said. "Neither one's exactly on our side."

"They're not on anyone's side but their own," Kanja said, driving the last support home. "That's exactly why I couldn't let it happen here. A fight that isn't about the war still gets people killed just as dead."
