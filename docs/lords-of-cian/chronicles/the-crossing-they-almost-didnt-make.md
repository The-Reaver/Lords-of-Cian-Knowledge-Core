# The Crossing They Almost Didn't Make

*Locked canon, Batch 215, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-936`). Bane Alias Chronicle LII, wave 18. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The Kessra ford was supposed to be waist-deep and slow this time of year, and it had been, right
up until the storm broke over the highlands two days upriver and sent the whole thing up four feet
overnight, brown and fast and carrying trees. Bane's column had ninety-some refugees strung along
the near bank when the water rose, pulled out of a burned settlement a day behind them and a
Directorate suppression company half a day behind that, and the ford that had been their whole
plan was gone.

There was no time to find another crossing. Halst said so plainly, and Bane didn't argue the point,
only the conclusion — they would make this one work, not find a better one.

Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System had never been built with a flood in mind. It read impact through
solid ground, footing, weight settling somewhere it could be measured, and the riverbed under four
feet of moving brown water gave it almost nothing to hold — the current shifted the stones
underfoot too fast for the system to trust any reading it took for longer than a step. Bane crossed
first anyway, testing each footing by feel as much as by whatever the armor told him, and set a
line — a length of salvaged rope anchored on a half-submerged boulder on the far side, the closest
thing to solid ground the crossing had.

What got ninety people across wasn't a power. It was the line itself, and the order they crossed
in, and every hand available holding it steady against a current trying to take it downstream —
Pell Ostra rigging a second anchor point when the first began to work loose from the boulder's
crumbling face, Efa Gol running the near bank end to end keeping the queue moving fast enough that
no one stood exposed longer than they had to. A child slipped two-thirds of the way across and the
current took her ten feet downstream before Bane reached her, Mafesto's system reading nothing
useful the entire time he swam, no ground to transfer force against, nothing but his own strength
against the water.

The suppression company reached the near bank forty minutes after the last refugee reached the
far one, the rope already cut and gone, the ford still four feet over what any map called it. They
did not attempt the crossing themselves.

"That's twice now the Trinity's had nothing to give me," Bane said that night, wringing river water
out of a coat that had been dry that morning. "Marsh, and now this. I'm starting to think it was
never meant to replace knowing how to swim."
