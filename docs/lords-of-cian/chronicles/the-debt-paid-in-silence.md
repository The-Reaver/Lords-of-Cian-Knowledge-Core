# The Debt Paid in Silence

*Locked canon, Batch 258, 2026-09-11 (`MCD-1224`). Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea Alias Chronicle
LXXXVI, wave 29. An anonymous benefactor, once helped by the fleet, repays them without
acknowledgment. Not a territory Chronicle. Narrated in neutral third-person prose.*

---

The supplies simply appeared one morning at a resupply point Danne Sok had marked only in his own
private charts, never shared beyond the fleet itself — fresh water, preserved meat, and bolts of
sailcloth enough to matter, left with no note, no flag, no indication of who had found the location or
why they'd chosen to help rather than report it.

It happened again six weeks later, at a different unmarked point, this time with medical supplies the
fleet had genuinely been running low on. Efa Gol, whose job it was to be suspicious of exactly this
kind of unexplained generosity, spent two weeks trying to trace the source and came up with nothing
beyond a pattern: whoever it was knew the fleet's unmarked resupply points, knew what the fleet
actually needed rather than what would look impressive to give, and wanted nothing back for it.

Garren Hask found the answer eventually, not through investigation but through an old entry in his own
ledger — a merchant captain, freed years back from a Trust prison hold the fleet had liberated in
passing during an early wave, who had since built a modest but genuine fortune in honest trade and
apparently never stopped keeping track of the fleet that had given him the chance to build it.

Kanja found him at his own dockside warehouse, older, greyer, and visibly uncomfortable at being
discovered rather than proud of it. "I didn't want thanks," the man said. "Didn't want a name in
Hask's book either, if I'm honest. You gave me a life back and never once came looking for repayment.
Seemed like the only honest way to pay it forward was to do the same — quietly, and let it be enough
on its own."

"It is enough," Kanja said. "You didn't owe us this."

"No," the man agreed. "That's rather the point of it."

Kanja didn't press him to stop, and didn't ask him to reveal himself going forward either — the
supplies kept appearing, unmarked, at unmarked points, for years after, and Garren Hask logged each
delivery the same plain way he logged everything, name withheld at the man's own quiet request, the
debt recorded as paid in full though neither side had ever called it a debt to begin with.
