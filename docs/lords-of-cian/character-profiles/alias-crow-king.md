# The Crow King — Profile & Game Plan

**Status:** walkthrough drafted
**Track:** Alias Chronicle
**Gate cleared:** NO — no Chronicle prose may be drafted or presented until this file reaches "game plan approved."

This file is the standing gate artifact for this character, per the Character Chronicle Launch
Protocol locked in `CLAUDE.md`. It is the single source of truth for who this character is before
any Chronicle is written or rewritten — not a summary produced after the fact.

Per the Alias track's own standing note (`chronicle-tracks-status.md`): the Crow King is a
register/mask Kanja wears, not a distinct psychology. This walkthrough therefore covers two layers
throughout — (a) Kanja's own underlying biography/gear/stats where they bear directly on this
alias, and (b) what is specific to this alias's own narrative register, gear-era, themes, and
established recurring cast.

## 1. Rules Walkthrough

### Biography / stats

- `MCD-230` — The Crow King is acquired at age 23, the seventh of Kanja's Twenty-Two Victories
  (ages 18–30) and one of six classified "Unwinnable" by the Directorate (each earning a
  Directorate-classified alias). Acquisition order: the Trench Monarch (18), Bane (19, Black
  Trench), the Industrial Myth (19, first iteration), the Blue-Collar Titan (20), the Sovereign
  Ghost of the Great Sea (21), the Scourge (22, Ash-Wharf), **the Crow King (23)**, the Iron
  Bastard (25), the Lord of Embers (27), the Storm That Walks (29), and Captain (never a
  Directorate classification).
- `MCD-236` — Origin battle: **the Night of the Crow King** (Unwinnable). Escaping Commandant
  Voris's mathematically airtight three-ring encirclement (signal-jammed, thermal-sensored, zero
  escape vectors) of his Voskharen Wetlands compound by inventing the Hymn-Engine on the spot: a
  synchronized ~19 Hz sub-bass work-chant sung by 300+ fighters producing false thermal-sensor
  readings across the marsh, under cover of which the compound evacuated through hand-dug
  drainage channels. Left a coat-and-feather-crown scarecrow on the empty command platform — the
  origin of both the Hymn-Engine and the alias's own name/iconography.
- `ARS-310` / `MCD-246` — The Hymn-Engine is one of four components of Kanja's **Pre-Awakening
  Theatrics System** (Forge-Coat, Hymn-Engine, Dead Drakma Decoys, Smoke-Pots), which he used to
  sustain **the Scourge** persona across the entire 284-year Long Mask (ages 30–314). The
  Hymn-Engine's own technology therefore outlives the Crow King alias itself as a discrete
  Directorate classification and continues in active use for centuries under a different alias.
- `MCD-299` — The crew's acoustic Hymn-synchronization is the direct descendant of Rexmar/Jicome
  battlefield-coordination tradition (organizational rather than numerical doctrine, every fighter
  trained in both positional and kinetic combat) — not a Kanja invention from nothing.
- `MCD-246` — At age 30, the Trinity (Mafesto, Onyx of Oblivion, Obsidian Malice) is surrendered to
  the vault at L9 in the Karkosa Complex under Kanja's father's peace deal with the Sovereign
  Trust; only the Talisman of Mao, the Aegis-Talisman, and the Rexmar Machete remain through the
  Long Mask. **This is a hard structural boundary for this alias's corpus** — see the flagged
  chronology note below.
- `MCD-989` (Crow King Alias Chronicle LI) — Explicitly marks "the craft's first post-Rebellion
  deployment... marking the Hymn-Engine's transition into the Long Mask era," confirming the
  Hymn-Engine technology (as distinct from the discrete age-23 "Crow King" classification) remains
  in active use well past age 30.
- `MCD-248` (doctrine-establishing operations, ages 36–44, Long Mask era) — the Hymn of Saltmarsh
  (age 38) locks the Hymn-Engine's "precision beam, not wall of sound" doctrine as its standard
  deployment for the next 250 years. Filed under general Scourge/Theatrics-System material, not
  under the Crow King's own Chronicle numbering.
- `MCD-237` — The Three-Day Blackout: nine coordinated Hymn-Engine teams overload the feedback
  loops of all nine Blight Frequency relay towers on Jicome's stretch of the Southern Seaboard simultaneously
  (with Anansi and Sephtis), scrambling continental biological suppression for three days — the
  first mass demonstration the Blight was artificial technology, and framed as planting the seed
  of the later Pi-Awakening.
- `MCD-264` / `MCD-265` — Later Long Mask-era Hymn-Engine applications: the Calibration Theft (age
  108) redesigns the Hymn-Engine using stolen technical manuals; Maw-11 (age 140) is liberated via
  an inside job combining Ghostwind infiltration with a Hymn-Engine counter-frequency device.
- `ARS-398` — Mechanically explains why the Hymn-Engine works against SBD Blight Frequency
  Projectors: the Talisman of Mao's Blight Immunity generates a near-perfect anti-phase
  cancellation against the SBD signal (a degraded copy of T.D.K.'s Ionic Rite frequency), and the
  Hymn-Engine produces "a cruder functional approximation using synchronized human voices."
- `ARS-356` — Confirms the Hymn-Engine and Dead Drakma Decoys "remain distinct, still-undetailed
  elements" of the Pre-Awakening Theatrics System even after that document's full detailing of the
  Forge-Coat and Smoke System — the Hymn-Engine's own physical mechanism (if any exists beyond
  synchronized voices) has never been separately specified.

**Flagged chronology note (for the Game Plan stage, not resolved here):** No Crow King Chronicle
statement gives an explicit in-story age except `MCD-236` (age 23) and `MCD-989` ("shortly after
age 30"). Several later-wave entries are explicit "full-Trinity combat showcases" (Mafesto, Onyx of
Oblivion, Obsidian Malice) — e.g. `MCD-417` (wave 2), `MCD-495` (wave 4), `MCD-1045` (wave 20),
`MCD-1078` (wave 21), `MCD-1484` (wave 34). Since the Trinity is surrendered at age 30 (`MCD-246`)
and stays sealed throughout the entire 284-year Long Mask (per the Scourge's own locked precedent
in the alias-track batch log), every full-Trinity showcase almost certainly sits within the pre-30
Rebellion window regardless of its wave number — **wave number is drafting-batch order, not
in-universe chronology.** Pure-deception/coordination entries with no Trinity combat could sit
anywhere across the alias's full span (age 23 through the Long Mask's end at 314). This
era-spanning structure is unusual among the eleven aliases (most stay within one narrower
era/register) and should be addressed explicitly in the pacing convention, not left implicit.

### Relationships

- **Commandant Voris** — the alias's defining opponent, built entirely through the Chronicle
  corpus itself (no dedicated `CC-` character rule exists for him). Humiliated at the origin battle
  (`MCD-236`); keeps the original scarecrow against regulation as "the only honest teacher of his
  career" (`MCD-385`); personally tests his own "unpredictability" doctrine and discovers its
  institutional cost (`MCD-836`); recognizes restraint, not just cunning, as central to the method,
  secondhand (`MCD-857`); voluntarily steps back from active pursuit and consults for his own
  successor rather than re-engaging in the field (`MCD-1262`, consistent with, and extended by, his
  retirement at `MCD-860`); writes a final, honest retrospective for the Directorate's own historical archive
  concluding the method "stopped being one man's method before he finished studying it" (`MCD-1281`);
  receives one final scarecrow at full retirement and recognizes the craft has become "a discipline
  larger than any one practitioner" (`MCD-860`, "The Last Scarecrow," closing the ten-wave run
  covering waves 6–15).
- **The apprentice** (unnamed, first-generation direct student) — the original Hymn-Engine singer,
  permanently rasp-voiced from six weeks' rehearsal (`MCD-418`); becomes Kanja's apprentice, learning
  the craft's real discipline is restraint and listening rather than cleverness (`MCD-451`);
  personally redesigns the method after a Directorate cryptographer partially cracks it (`MCD-496`);
  takes on her own student (the "third generation") without asking permission first (`MCD-548`,
  explicitly contrasted later at `MCD-1046` with how the fourth generation is taught only after
  Kanja's endorsement); captured and interrogated, resists via silence rather than lies (`MCD-838`);
  present across nearly the entire run as the lineage's second voice and, later, its senior judgment
  figure alongside Kanja (`MCD-859`).
- **Third generation** (unnamed) — taught by the apprentice without prior authorization (`MCD-548`);
  begins teaching her own student in turn, a third generation of the craft (`MCD-854`); writes the
  lineage's underlying doctrine down as a single page for the first time (`MCD-1256`); eventually
  shifts her own primary role from frontline operations toward judgment — deciding who learns next
  and what the craft becomes (`MCD-1276`), an explicit mirror of Voris's own arc.
- **Fourth generation** (unnamed — "a two-year crew messenger-runner known for exact, unembellished
  recall") — taught only after Kanja's own direct endorsement, a deliberately more formal consent
  process than either generation before him (`MCD-1046`); develops a wholly distinct specialty —
  bureaucratic/logistics/numeric camouflage rather than vocal deception (`MCD-1077`); becomes the
  crew's supply-accounting backstop alongside Garren Hask (`MCD-1278`); reacts to Garren Hask's
  eventual death with a defining grief beat — "his numeric gift has no tool for an honest,
  uncorrectable loss with no error to find in it" (`MCD-1486`).
- **Fifth generation** ("the runner's son," unnamed) — the fifth-generation question is first raised
  (`MCD-1258`), advanced but left unresolved (`MCD-1280`), then finally answered: teaching begins
  with him decided collectively by Kanja and all three prior generations together, not by any one of
  them alone (`MCD-1479`); runs his own first independent field test, revealing a third distinct
  specialty within the lineage — behavioral/visual observation (`MCD-1483`).
- **A former Directorate officer** ("the Hunter," unnamed) — first tries to deceive Kanja directly
  and nearly succeeds, caught only by the fourth generation's cross-check (`MCD-1265`); years later,
  having left Directorate service, returns asking to be taught rather than to counter the craft;
  Kanja imposes a full year's probation before any teaching is considered (`MCD-1482`).
- **Reused crew, for continuity rather than new relationship material:** Efa Gol (`CC-130`)
  recognizes her own long-standing decoy/diversion specialty as the same underlying discipline
  wearing a different form (`MCD-1079`); Callum Breck reflects on Kanja's own vulnerability (a
  near-deception, temporary deafness) as a direct parallel to his own post-Black-Trench silence
  (`MCD-1267`); Corren Halst narrates a retrospective closer (`MCD-848`) and is later sought out by
  a historian documenting the craft's true history (`MCD-1274`); Garren Hask's genuine supply
  accounting is the fourth generation's proving ground (`MCD-1278`) and his death (`MCD-1422`,
  Captain Alias Chronicle XCII) is felt directly by the fourth generation (`MCD-1486`).
- **Halveth Ashcombe** (`CC-149`) — a Directorate counter-intelligence analyst antagonist. His
  eventual neutralization (career destroyed, forced into disgraced reassignment, not killed) by a
  coordinated Crow King "Braid" operation is locked as fact (`MCD-1857`) but **explicitly flagged in
  the ledger itself as not yet dramatized** — see Reserved/unresolved threads below.

### Abilities / gear

- **The Hymn-Engine, core mechanism:** synchronized voices producing false sensor/detection
  readings — originally false thermal readings via a ~19 Hz sub-bass chant sung by 300+ fighters
  (`MCD-236`). Evolves into **"the Braid"**: three interleaved false patterns sung simultaneously so
  a triple-redundant sensor grid cross-confirms three fabricated truths instead of exposing one lie
  (`MCD-384`). Standardized as "precision beam, not wall of sound" doctrine at age 38 (`MCD-248`).
  Later combined with a full-Trinity offensive setup for the first time in `MCD-495` ("The Braid
  and the Blade").
- **Non-vocal backup/parallel channels**, each introduced by a specific environmental or physical
  failure: percussive tap-signal relay (introduced after Kanja's own throat wound, `MCD-915`,
  becomes a standing required-backup practice — "every operation now requires a rehearsed backup
  voice"); a full multi-person relay chain replacing voice entirely when a storm makes vocal
  projection impossible (`MCD-987`); a visual signal-flag pattern for high-altitude vocal
  degradation (`MCD-1259`); a crude, redundant two-signal tap layer built to survive cold-numbed
  hands (`MCD-1478`); written/visual deception adapted for a deaf opponent (`MCD-843`).
- **Non-deceptive/non-vocal applications of the underlying discipline:** verified-truth-as-leverage
  used without any lie at all (`MCD-450`, `MCD-844`, `MCD-1268`, `MCD-1270`); pure
  eavesdropping/reconnaissance with zero deception (`MCD-842`); pure coordination discipline with
  zero deception during a dam-failure evacuation (`MCD-993`) and a landslide rescue (`MCD-1260`);
  bureaucratic/documentary deception via falsified but internally consistent paperwork (the fourth
  generation's own specialty, `MCD-1077`, later inverted for humanitarian grain redistribution,
  `MCD-1272`); behavioral/visual observation (the fifth generation's specialty, `MCD-1483`).
- **Full-Trinity combat integration** (see the chronology flag above — implicitly pre-age-30):
  Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System, Obsidian Malice's discharge, and Onyx of Oblivion's Cadence
  Ruin / Veil Piercer / Soulbound Edge appear alongside Hymn-Engine deception in `MCD-417` (forced
  encirclement combat when evasion fails outright), `MCD-495` (first offensive-setup combination),
  `MCD-1045` (total-darkness showcase — Onyx's non-visual sensing proven not to need light any more
  than the Hymn-Engine needed sight), `MCD-1078` (combat sustained in continuous motion for the
  first time), and `MCD-1484` (structural fire/collapse showcase, deliberately inverting `MCD-1045`
  by using overwhelming noise instead of total silence as the tactical hinge).
- **Documented, transmissible doctrine:** the underlying discipline ("listen before you act,
  restraint over cleverness") is written down as a single page for the first time (`MCD-1256`); the
  page is lost during a raid, survives independently for six years, and seeds a wholly separate,
  self-taught community with no knowledge of the Hymn-Engine, the Crow King name, or Kanja's
  identity at all (`MCD-1257`, `MCD-1409`–`1411`) — direct confirmation that the craft's real
  content was never a technique but a philosophy.
- **Institutionalization:** a structured six-week curriculum taught to twenty outsider scouts at a
  regional commander's request (`MCD-917`), later requiring its own private authentication phrases
  after imitation and friendly-misreading risks (`MCD-990`, `MCD-992`).
- **Established limits/failure states** (a long, deliberately unsmoothed list across the run): a
  single unbriefed private recognizing the chant by ear (`MCD-416`, the alias's first genuine
  near-failure); forced combat when evasion fails outright (`MCD-417`); real bystander harm from a
  redirected false signal (`MCD-494`, the alias's first genuine failure entry, prompting a
  bystander-mapping verification step); a Directorate cryptographer partially cracking the pattern
  (`MCD-496`); a fully genuine draw against blanket, undifferentiated skepticism (`MCD-853`); a deaf
  commander (`MCD-843`) and a blind commander with hyperacute hearing exposing a signal as "too
  rhythmically perfect for real fear" (`MCD-988`), both forcing real adaptation; a burned regional
  variant with two deaths and no clever counter (`MCD-995`, "the craft's first genuine, unrecovered
  regional breach"); a catastrophic dam failure taking twelve lives with no adversary to out-think at
  all (`MCD-993`); three distinct physical limits landed on lineage members personally — Kanja's
  throat wound (`MCD-915`), Kanja's temporary deafness (`MCD-1266`), the fourth generation's
  cold-numbed hands (`MCD-1478`); a coordination failure purely from the lineage's own growth in
  scale, not any opponent (`MCD-1480`); Kanja himself nearly deceived by his own method's own logic
  (`MCD-1265`); an internal-temptation near-failure (`MCD-1273`, a trained scout nearly sells the
  method for money, then self-reports); Halveth Ashcombe (`CC-149`) as a queued, not-yet-dramatized
  future defeated opponent (`MCD-1857`).

### Already-locked plot beats (book-level or Chronicle-level)

- Origin: **the Night of the Crow King** (age 23, `MCD-236`) — the Hymn-Engine invented live to
  escape Commandant Voris's encirclement; the coat-and-feather-crown scarecrow gives the alias both
  its name and its recurring iconography (further scarecrows recur at `MCD-383`, `MCD-860`).
- The alias's core technology becomes a permanent component of Kanja's broader Pre-Awakening
  Theatrics System (`ARS-310`), carried forward into **the Scourge's** 284-year Long Mask persona
  rather than retired with the discrete "Crow King" Directorate classification.
- Age 30: the Trinity is surrendered to the L9 vault (`MCD-246`) — the hard boundary separating this
  alias's Rebellion-era full-combat showcases from its post-30, Trinity-free deception/coordination
  showcases.
- Later-life beats tied to the same underlying technology but filed under general Scourge/Theatrics
  material rather than the Crow King's own Chronicle numbering: the Hymn of Saltmarsh (age 38,
  `MCD-248`); the Three-Day Blackout (`MCD-237`); the Calibration Theft (age 108) and Maw-11's
  counter-frequency liberation (age 140, `MCD-264`/`265`).
- Within the Crow King's own 102-Chronicle run: five generations of direct teaching lineage
  established in sequence (the apprentice → third generation → fourth generation → fifth
  generation), each transition explicitly built around a maturing consent process (`MCD-1046`,
  `MCD-1479`) rather than simple succession; the doctrine committed to a single written page
  (`MCD-1256`) that survives and propagates entirely independently of the lineage or the Crow King
  name itself (`MCD-1409`–`1411`); institutional/documentary corroboration of the craft's true
  history from both sides of the conflict — a crew-side historian (`MCD-1274`) and a fully-retired
  Voris's own honest Directorate archive report (`MCD-1281`) — which independently match almost
  everywhere it matters (`MCD-1282`); full institutional autonomy demonstrated for the first time,
  with the lineage running two simultaneous operations without Kanja's presence, foreknowledge, or
  approval (`MCD-1485`).

### Existing Chronicle corpus

102 Chronicles locked (Chronicles I–CII: `MCD-383`–`385`, `416`–`418`, `449`–`451`, `494`–`496`,
`546`–`548`, `831`–`860`, `915`–`917`, `987`–`995`, `1044`–`1046`, `1077`–`1079`, `1256`–`1282`,
`1409`–`1411`, `1478`–`1486`), across 34 completed waves of three entries each, plus one further
queued-but-undrafted beat (Ashcombe, `MCD-1857`). No Chronicle statement names a narrator directly;
per `VB-020`/`VB-026`, Kanja-POV material during the Rebellion (ages 18–30) uses the progressive
Onyx-of-Oblivion narrator handoff, while Long Mask-era material (post-30) uses Onyx's full
steady-state narration per `VB-020`/`021` — this alias's own era-spanning corpus will likely need
its narrator convention confirmed explicitly at the Game Plan stage, distinct from most other
aliases whose Chronicles sit cleanly within one narrator register.

Demonstrated characterization throughlines across the run, synthesized directly from the statement
text rather than invented:

- **Terrain/environment as an ever-expanding catalogue.** Marsh (origin) → highland checkpoint →
  urban/walled settlement → river crossing → desert/salt-flat → mountain/high-altitude → total
  darkness → continuous motion (a chase) → winter/blizzard → structural fire/collapse → open
  water/strait. Each new environment forces a genuinely new adaptation rather than repeating a
  trick that already worked once.
- **"The craft was never really vocal at its core."** Stated outright at `MCD-845` and extended
  twice more (`MCD-1079`, `MCD-1483`): its actual content is restraint and listening, expressed
  first through voice, then rhythm/percussion, then written numbers, then behavioral observation,
  then documentary record — one discipline in progressively different clothing, never a fixed
  trick.
- **Generational transmission as the alias's defining emotional register**, distinct from every
  other alias's family/crew-loyalty material. Teaching begins accidentally/informally (the
  apprentice, taught without anyone's prior sign-off); matures into an explicit, deliberately
  discussed consent process for every subsequent generation (`MCD-1046`, `MCD-1479`); survives being
  physically lost and outliving its own authorship entirely (`MCD-1257`, `MCD-1409`–`1411`); and
  eventually operates with full institutional autonomy, functioning without Kanja's presence,
  foreknowledge, or approval (`MCD-1485`).
- **Commandant Voris as a slow-burn mirror, not a static villain.** Humiliated pursuer (`MCD-236`)
  → obsessive private collector of his own defeat (`MCD-385`) → a man who tests his own doctrine on
  himself and learns its institutional cost (`MCD-836`) → a graceful, self-aware retiree who writes
  the honest final word on his own career-defining loss (`MCD-1281`–`1282`).
- **Genuine costs are never smoothed over.** Real bystander harm (`MCD-494`); a burned regional
  variant with two deaths (`MCD-995`); a dam-failure death toll of twelve with no villain to blame at
  all (`MCD-993`); a partial rescue failure, one victim of four not recovered in time (`MCD-1260`);
  Garren Hask's death landing on the fourth generation as a loss "the craft has no tool for"
  (`MCD-1486`).
- **Restraint over cleverness/performance as a value, not just a tactic.** Declining an easy win
  against an already-beaten, legitimate target (`MCD-856`); refusing to use gathered intelligence
  for personal vengeance (`MCD-1263`); a self-imposed, location-based ethical constraint against
  using the craft on a neutral shrine both warring sides have left unspoken-for (`MCD-1269`); a
  near-betrayal self-reported unprompted before anyone else discovered it (`MCD-1273`).
- **Institutional legitimacy, seen from both sides.** A neutral Sovereign Trust magistrate's formal
  archival record of the Hymn-Engine's true, unembellished history including its failures
  (`MCD-1261`); a liberated town's own council writing the craft's underlying restraint-based
  testimony principles into its own local governing custom, in its own words rather than copying the
  crew's (`MCD-1270`).
