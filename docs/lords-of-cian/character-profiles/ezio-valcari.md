## 1. Rules Walkthrough

Every locked rule touching Ezio Valcari, pulled from `canon-ledger.json` and organized thematically.
Ezio is a genuine backfill case in the same sense Daba was: he has never had his own Chronicle
series, but he is an extensively demonstrated recurring supporting character across 31 Kanja
Industrial Myth Alias Chronicles (plus one Lord of Embers crossover) and 4 of Lauris Letitia's own
Character Chronicles. The profile below must synthesize that demonstrated characterization, not
invent a competing one.

### Biography / stats
- `CC-027` / `WC-016` — publicly known as a theorist/spymaster, but classified as an elite
  close-quarters combatant capable of killing a room in three seconds. This capability is a closed
  secret known only to Valen, Sephtis, Kanja, Anansi, and Lauris (`CC-111` amends the list to
  confirm Lauris knows). Valen actively protects Ezio's cover.
- `CC-026` / `WC-016` / `CC-108` — first cousin of Valen Sinisterblade, of House Valcari, sharing
  the "Sinister Bloodline" martial tradition; Valen personally and familially trained him in the
  Sinister Bloodline's War-Side tradition (extends `CC-035`), not merely a shared lineage.
- `CC-028` — 75 years old (present-day/Book 1 timeline), with 750 years of wisdom-equivalence
  gained through training under Sephtis.
- `MCD-373` — roughly sixteen years old at the Furnace District Strike (Kanja's own age 21 at that
  battle, `MCD-244`) — his earliest demonstrated appearance in the corpus.
- `CC-030` — one-third of "the revolutionary trio": Ezio (the Architect/strategy), Fermand/Hermes
  (the Unbroken/endurance and synthesis), Anansi (the Spark/morale and agitation).
- `VB-020` / `VB-023` — one of the Voice Bible's five designated narrators: narrates the
  investigation chapters (alternating with Fermand). His register is Zafón Gothic/Noir atmosphere
  fused with Twain-Marx wit; never breaks character to explain the investigation to the reader.
- `VB-024` / `CC-034` — Fermand narrates all Ezio and Lauris POV chapters in his own clinical,
  contraction-free register — and reserves his only warmth in the entire Voice Bible for "My dear
  Ezio."

### Relationships
- `CC-029` / `MCD-021` / `CC-073` — his patron is Lady Nadea Thren, a defected SBD analyst
  operating independently (not T.D.K.'s champion, not "Glass Ordained" — her defection dissolved
  the Mirrored Chorus division, `MCD-284`). She is genuinely, secretly in love with him; Ezio
  figured out years ago that she is "the Viper" and stays anyway.
- `CC-031` / `MCD-032` / `MCD-194` — Lauris Letitia is his Attia (Sergeant-at-Arms), a former SBD
  external contractor he personally identified and recruited: he observed her Directorate career
  from a distance for roughly two decades, arranged the recruitment meeting through Aerelin's
  mediation, briefed her on Anu Un Ra's history via Sephtis's archives, and offered her the role.
  She accepted on three non-negotiable conditions (her independent Karesian-preservation agenda
  takes precedence when it conflicts with Lords of Cian operations; her relationship with Aerelin's
  network stays on her own terms, not central command's; her combat-deployment authority is
  absolute) — all still hold as of the present-day timeline.
- `MCD-194` — at that same recruitment meeting, Lauris's biological senses registered Ezio's body
  as inconsistent with his "non-combatant theorist" public identity; the two explicitly agreed
  never to discuss it, and the arrangement still holds — she has never raised it since.
- `CC-032` / `MCD-203` / `MCD-204` / `ARS-359` — roughly fourteen months after Lauris's
  recruitment, Ezio commissioned Kanja to forge Attia's Rite as the symbolic instrument of her
  Attia role; Kanja forged it in eleven days, and it was delivered privately to Lauris with Ezio
  present only as nominal commissioner. (`ARS-210`'s original listing of Attia's Rite as part of
  Ezio's own Concealed Arsenal is a superseded duplication error, corrected in favor of Lauris's
  ownership — see Abilities/gear below.)
- `MCD-205` — his present-day working relationship with Lauris runs through communication
  protocols compressed to a single word or gesture: he provides strategic direction, she provides
  survivable execution.
- `MCD-1626` — Fermand's reserved warmth ("My dear Ezio") is given narrative texture directly in
  Lauris Chronicle IX, "My Dear Ezio" — a stakes-free, non-combat evening between the two of them.
- `MCD-1564` / `MCD-1661` — in Lauris's own Chronicle series he functions as the party she reports
  operational debts back to (Chronicle IV, the Operation 38 facility) and as someone she
  deliberately does not brief when honoring a personal, off-the-books favor for Aerelin
  (Chronicle XLIV) — both consistent with `MCD-194`'s recruitment-condition carve-outs.
- `ARS-344` — Valen (already established as Ezio's cousin/combat mentor) is also locked as Kanja's
  own Master-at-Arms ("the White Lotus"), the one who ordered Kanja to document Mafesto's function
  starting at age 26 — a structural link between Ezio's own combat-tradition family and Kanja's
  crew infrastructure.

### Abilities / gear
- `CC-027` / `WC-016` / `ARS-405` — his classified combat profile: can kill a room in three seconds.
  Never dramatized directly on the page anywhere in the corpus to date — it stays a stated fact,
  not a shown one.
- `ARS-404` — the Archive-Key: twelve hair-thin Living Drakma filaments bio-tuned to his own
  resonance, housed in the hollow shaft of the Cipher Cane (a plain cane reading publicly as a
  theorist's affectation). Defeats locks/mechanisms by touch-reading their structure through
  micro-vibration feedback, and interfaces with Directorate Drakma data-plates to read sealed
  classified SBD records back to him the same way. The same cane performs Structural Interrogation
  (mapping a room's density/structure in two taps).
- `ARS-405` — the Socratic Trap: his signature interrogation method, not a weapon — oblique,
  apparently academic questions that lead a target down a logical path where only a truthful final
  answer avoids self-contradiction, closed with a deliberate silence. In-canon characterized as
  having extracted more intelligence than any blade in the crew's arsenal.
- `ARS-210` / `ARS-359` — superseded/corrected: Attia's Rite is not his own weapon. The original
  listing was a duplication error; it is Lauris's breaker, forged to mark his own promotion of her
  to Sergeant-at-Arms, not something he wields.
- `ARS-399` — his forensic accounting is locked as one of the two known real-world weaknesses of
  the SBD's Scrip-Tether system (alongside the Sovereign Umbrella's radius-nullification):
  publicly proving the debt's underlying math is fraudulent.

### Already-locked plot beats (book-level or Chronicle-level)
- `MCD-231` / `MCD-244` / `MCD-234` / `MCD-286` — the evidence-first, unarmed documentation method
  he is most associated with predates his own recruitment onto the page: Kanja's proof-not-violence
  doctrine begins at the Scrip-Forge Raid (age 18) and reaches its purest form at the Furnace
  District Strike (age 21), where Ezio (Kanja's own account puts him at ~16 here) compiles 4,000
  workers' testimony into a documented, deliberately unpayable debt structure.
- `MCD-070` — Book 1, "The Deposed King": Ezio and Fermand investigate the Fulfillment Ceremony's
  double regicide, hired by Ozmund. Three acts: Investigation → 10-Day Interregnum → Karkosa Heist.
- `CULT-044` / `CULT-053` / `CULT-071` / `CULT-075` / `CULT-080` / `CULT-081` / `MCD-1853` — the
  Book 1 investigation's actual texture: an eleven-minute death-certificate filing anomaly he
  finds first; a dead Fraction analyst's buried evidence of SBD leadership's advance knowledge; a
  Caucus senator's silent calculation that his investigation won't reach the relay (it does); a
  clearinghouse archive Keeper's theologically-motivated bureaucratic obstruction; and the SBD
  wet-work team's actual tradecraft (planted false-conspiracy evidence, a memory-suppressing
  compound) that misdirects his investigation for the first third of the book.
- `MCD-098` / `MCD-221` — Book 5 Gate Battle: Gate team is Orlok, Anansi, Ezio, Fermand, Legbara
  Kalunga. "Ezio's cover dies at the gate; the gate lives" — his cover identity, not necessarily
  Ezio himself.
- `MCD-279` — Book 2's climax: Bolo Troth's attempt to retrieve Archon Meridian collides with
  Ezio's own investigation; Book 2 ends with Ezio's discovery that the Crown-Scar is a root-access
  tether T.D.K. could reactivate to seize direct control of Ozmund's mind.
- `MCD-1864` — Callas Modrin (`CC-156`, a Batch-312 pre-Book-1 villain) is eventually exposed by
  Ezio personally, via the same evidence-first methodology, in a bureaucratic-judo defeat with no
  combat. The rule itself states plainly: **"No Chronicle prose has been drafted; this is a queued
  future beat for whenever Ezio's own Character Chronicle series launches"** — this is a
  ready-made, already-reserved Chronicle candidate.

### Reserved / unresolved threads
- His full classified combat capability (`WC-016`/`CC-027`) has never been shown on the page
  anywhere in the corpus — deliberately withheld across every appearance to date, including his 31
  Industrial Myth appearances, which are consistently unarmed/non-combat.
- Nadea Thren's secret love for him (`CC-073`) is locked as real and unresolved — not to be paid
  off or dramatized as reciprocated/rejected without Abad's direction.
- `MCD-1171` — Pell Ostra's private margin-notes (kept from Ezio) identify an unnamed recurring
  observer connecting two otherwise unrelated frauds; the observer's identity is deliberately left
  open — a live thread that could eventually intersect Ezio's own investigative arc.
- The entire Book 1 investigation (`CULT-044` etc.) and the Book 2 Crown-Scar discovery (`MCD-279`)
  are explicitly Book-1/Book-2-era plot material — reserved, not pre-Book-1 territory. Any launch
  wave for Ezio needs its own era constraint the way Ozmund's launch got one (strictly
  pre-Fulfillment-Ceremony); the natural equivalent boundary here is pre-Book-1 (before the double
  regicide), matching Ezio's own already-demonstrated corpus, which runs from his teens through his
  adult spymaster years without ever touching the investigation itself.
- His "cover dies at the gate" (`MCD-098`) in Book 5 is a live open question about what exactly his
  cover identity is and how it differs from Ezio himself — not defined anywhere yet, and not this
  track's to resolve.

### Existing Chronicle corpus
Unlike Ozmund or Kanja-version (both launched from near-zero), Ezio already has substantial
demonstrated characterization on the page — 31 Industrial Myth Alias Chronicles plus 4 Lauris
Character Chronicles, spanning from his teens through adulthood:

- **Origin appearance** (`MCD-373`, Industrial Myth III, "The Boy Who Kept the Numbers Honest"):
  ~age 16, already performing the method's real corrective function — cross-checking worker
  testimony against production records and quietly adjusting entries *upward* when a frightened
  worker under-claims, rather than taking testimony at face value.
- **Growth into an institution-builder**: dispersing decoy ledger copies to defeat a raid
  (`MCD-404`); articulating *why* the numbers-first method outlasts fear-based leverage (`MCD-439`);
  running fully epistolary campaigns against evasive administrators (`MCD-749`); keeping the method
  running without Kanja present (`MCD-761`); training a second-generation of independent district
  auditors (`MCD-896`, `MCD-931`, `MCD-1399`); training Kanja's own testimony-gathering successor
  alongside his own arithmetic-auditor track (`MCD-1443`).
- **Genuine friction with Kanja, not just partnership**: the method's first real disagreement
  (`MCD-925`, "What Ezio Refused to Enter") — Ezio wants to use unrelated personal scandal as
  leverage, Kanja refuses and draws a hard boundary, and Ezio stays genuinely unpersuaded.
- **His own tested integrity**: catches a testifier weaponizing the ledger to frame an innocent
  clerk (`MCD-924`); is offered and genuinely tempted by a bribe himself, and discloses both the
  offer and his own temptation before deciding (`MCD-1446`) — extending the crew's internal
  transparency discipline that Kanja himself models in `MCD-1152`.
- **His own vulnerability shown once**: injured off-page in `MCD-1156`, forcing Kanja to run the
  cross-referencing arithmetic alone for the first time — established as slower and less precise
  than Ezio's trained skill, the corpus's clearest statement of Ezio's own specific expertise.
- **Consistent traits across the full corpus**: methodical, exacting, initially reluctant to share
  or delegate his method (`MCD-766`) but grows past it; treats numbers as something that "don't
  flinch either way" (impartial even against a sympathetic claimant's own wishes, `MCD-1442`);
  capable of real moral nuance (reconciling two honestly conflicting testimonies rather than
  picking a winner, `MCD-1148`; inventing a new record category for grief that has no wage
  equivalent, `MCD-764`); consistently unarmed and non-combat in every one of his 31 appearances to
  date.
- **Lauris's own track** (`MCD-1564`, `MCD-1626`, `MCD-1661`, `MCD-1669`): confirms his role as the
  one she reports operational debts to, the one relationship allowed genuine on-page warmth via
  Fermand's narration, and `CC-134`'s explicit framing that among the crew's defining emotional
  burdens, "Ezio carries deception."
