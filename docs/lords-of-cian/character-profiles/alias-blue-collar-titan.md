# The Blue-Collar Titan — Profile & Game Plan

**Status:** walkthrough drafted
**Track:** Alias Chronicle
**Gate cleared:** NO — no Chronicle prose may be drafted or presented until this file reaches "game plan approved."

This file is the standing gate artifact for this alias, per the Character Chronicle Launch
Protocol locked in `CLAUDE.md`. Per the standing note in `chronicle-tracks-status.md`: **the
Blue-Collar Titan is not a distinct character or a distinct psychology.** It is one of Kanja's
eleven Directorate-classified aliases/masks, grounded in his own single underlying psychology.
This profile documents the alias's own narrative register, gear-era, themes, established voice
quirks, and recurring cast — the material a Psychological Profile discussion (Section 2) and Game
Plan (Section 3) would need, once Abad reaches this alias in the backfill queue. It is not a
second psychology competing with Kanja's own core profile.

## 1. Rules Walkthrough

### Biography / stats

- `MCD-230` — Kanja's Twenty-Two Victories (ages 18-30) break down as 10 Conventional Victories, 6
  Unwinnable Victories (each earning a Directorate-classified alias), and 6 Operational/labor-
  logistics Campaigns. Aliases in acquisition order: the Trench Monarch (18), Bane (19, Black
  Trench), the Industrial Myth (19, first iteration, Furnace District Strike), **the Blue-Collar
  Titan (20, Sewer War of Killane)**, Sovereign Ghost of the Great Sea (21, Ghost Harbor), the
  Scourge (22, Ash-Wharf), the Crow King (23), the Iron Bastard (25), the Lord of Embers (27), the
  Storm That Walks (29) — and Captain, never a Directorate classification at all.
- `MCD-234` — The Sewer War of Killane, the alias's founding battle: an 80-fighter, six-week
  infiltration through Killane's forgotten pre-modern sewer layer that diverted 600 of the city's
  1,200-soldier garrison chasing sabotaged infrastructure, while copying the Southern District's
  Scrip-Registry ledger undetected for four months. This battle also marks fifteen-year-old Ezio
  Valcari's first on-page appearance, already eight months into work as a junior intelligence
  courier.
- `MCD-286` — Distinguishes the Sewer War of Killane's evidence method (four months of undetected
  Scrip-Registry copying) from the separate, earlier Scrip-Forge Raid's charcoal-rubbing evidence
  chain (age 18) — the two are not the same operation and shouldn't be conflated.
- `GEO-006` — Killane is locked on the Atlas as a Corehold-class fortress-city Hold, cell C09,
  Jicome's southern district.
- `MCD-232` / `MCD-233` — Context immediately preceding this alias in the timeline: the Trinity
  (Mafesto/Onyx/Obsidian Malice) went live as a complete combined system for the first time at the
  Black Trench (age 19, Bane); Iron Shallows (age 19, no alias earned) established the terrain-
  physics doctrine the Blue-Collar Titan's own infrastructure-reading repeatedly extends.
- `MCD-246` — At age 30, Kanja surrenders the full Trinity to the Karkosa L9 vault under his
  father's peace deal; the 284-year Long Mask that follows runs on the Talisman of Mao/Aegis-
  Talisman/Rexmar Machete instead (the Scourge's own gear-era). **This bounds the Blue-Collar
  Titan strictly to the Rebellion era (ages 18-30) with full Trinity access** — it has no Long
  Mask or Captain-era counterpart appearances.
- `MCD-293` — Kanja's resting density itself escalates across the five books (6,000x to 12,000x),
  with his Spike/Awakening ceiling doing most of the escalation on top of that rising floor
  (18,000x to 42,000x+). No alias-specific density figure is separately locked for the Blue-Collar
  Titan; it inherits Kanja's own Rebellion-era baseline.
- `MCD-291` — Mafesto's Bio-Drakma skeleton doesn't passively absorb kinetic impact, it *conducts*
  it downward into the ground — grounding-dependent, finite routing capacity, and his soft tissue
  stays vulnerable when grounding is unavailable or impact exceeds capacity. This corrected
  mechanic is the one repeatedly dramatized in this alias's infrastructure-combat showcases.
- The corpus spans, chronologically: the six-week siege itself (the bulk of waves 1-15's material),
  entries reaching back to the crew's Black-Trench-era formation, and a long postwar tail running
  years to decades after the siege's own ceasefire (e.g. `MCD-1037`, ~20 years later; `MCD-1201`,
  "decades later") — the alias's timeline is not confined to the six weeks of the war itself.

### Relationships

- `MCD-234` — Corren Halst, Danne Sok, and Maret Vos were each freed independently *before* Maw-9
  (at the Black Trench, age 19), forming the nucleus of Kanja's earliest crew; all three recur
  heavily across this alias's corpus.
- `CC-115` / `CC-116` — Garren Hask, the crew's counter/ledger-keeper, recurs repeatedly (`MCD-1176`,
  `1179`, `1188`, `1189`, `1191`, `1192`, `1199`, `680`) as the record-keeping counterweight to the
  alias's growing legend; his closing cost-accounting ledger (`MCD-680`) closes the ten-wave run
  spanning waves 6-15.
- `CC-120` / `CC-121` — Dol Maren (he/him, per the Batch 226 reconciliation), the crew's engineer/
  shipwright, appears in surface/outdoor infrastructure showcases (`MCD-667`) and paired above-
  ground/below-ground coordination entries (`MCD-1193`, `MCD-1454`).
- Efa Gol (established elsewhere at `MCD-233`/`CC-130`-ish material) first appears in this alias's
  own run at `MCD-1012` ("What Efa Gol Watched From Above") and recurs (`MCD-1184`, `1192`, `1453`,
  `1458`, as security lead).
- Pell Ostra recurs as the alias's own reflective/contrasting voice (`MCD-671`, comparing this
  alias to Bane and the others) and later in an adaptive-gear entry (`MCD-1455`).
- Maret Vos (he/him, per the same reconciliation) recurs as an engineering peer offering genuine
  technical refinement (`MCD-1013`) and later as co-expert-witness alongside Kanja at the alias's
  hundredth Chronicle (`MCD-1457`).
- **The smiths' guild.** An unnamed, skeptical guild master — established via `MCD-1069`'s own
  cross-reference as a woman — sets Kanja a genuine journeyman's trade test before agreeing to
  cooperate (`MCD-409`); he passes but admits an unprompted rushed reheat, which wins more trust
  than flawless work would have. The guild later votes him honorary membership explicitly crediting
  his accumulated *honest failures* (the eastern gallery collapse, `MCD-485`; the admitted reheat,
  `MCD-409`) alongside his successes (`MCD-539`).
- **A second, distinct stonemasons' guild** (`MCD-1069`) is deliberately kept separate from the
  smiths' guild to avoid contradicting it. An unlicensed stonemason, barred from principal
  licensure by charter despite six years of unlicensed structural work, is sponsored not by
  Kanja's personal vouching but by insisting the guild test her on its own genuine competence
  standard — she passes on a diagnosis three certified journeymen missed. The relationship
  continues: Kanja later deliberately withholds his own inspection of her first fully solo project
  (`MCD-1177`), and is rewarded when she treats him as a peer for the first time rather than a
  sponsor.
- **The workers' council** (`MCD-1183`) — founded by synthesizing three earlier institutions (the
  safety-training program, `MCD-657`; the tradesmen's association, `MCD-662`; the wage-dispute
  resolution, `MCD-1070`). Kanja declines a unanimous offer of a permanent voting seat, accepting
  only a non-voting advisory role. The council later files a formal grievance against him
  (`MCD-1197`) over a unilateral evacuation decision (`MCD-1068`); he submits fully to its hearing
  process rather than defending himself outside it — its first real test of authority over its own
  founder. By `MCD-1200`, the smiths' guild, the tradesmen's association, and the workers' council
  are all shown functioning fully independently of him.
- **A previously-spared Trust fortification/combat engineer** — a full four-entry arc: designs
  traps against Kanja and defects after his own craftsmanship is recognized (`MCD-668`); fights him
  in the alias's first genuine skill-versus-skill duel, ending in mutual recognition rather than a
  kill (`MCD-670`); is tested and backed (not spoken for) by Kanja when his former command tries to
  reclaim him eight months later (`MCD-1190`); returns years later as a civilian collaborator
  rather than an adversary, receiving the crew's expertise on ordinary terms, no favor granted for
  old respect (`MCD-1402`).
- **A three-generation orphan-mentorship lineage** — a grieving orphaned boy who demands to be
  taught to kill is redirected into trade competence instead (`MCD-653`); eleven years later, now a
  journeyman, he independently teaches the same lesson to a new orphan of his own (`MCD-1036`); as
  a master, he later corrects Kanja's own proposed shoring method with superior field evidence, and
  Kanja accepts without hesitation (`MCD-1195`).
- **The Widow** who lost her husband to the work — confronts Kanja directly and refuses
  reconciliation, testing his composure under personal blame with no resolution offered (`MCD-677`).
  Her grown son later returns asking to be trained in the same trade, choosing his own path
  separately from his mother's still-unresolved refusal (`MCD-1185`) — she herself never
  reconciles.
- **The Founders' Guild** — a rival post-war trade collective falsely implying Kanja's personal
  endorsement to poach membership from the tradesmen's association he deliberately founded
  without his own name attached; Kanja refuses in front of its own recruits to confirm or deny the
  implied endorsement, and the false claim drops within a season on its own (`MCD-1401`).
- `VB-060` — the general presence-doctrine trait ("already-finished negotiation") applies to Kanja
  under any alias, this one included, though no Blue-Collar Titan entry has yet been singled out in
  the ledger as its specific on-page demonstration the way "The Pivotal Piece" (Bane, `MCD-365`)
  was.

### Abilities / gear

- **Full Trinity, Rebellion-era, applied specifically to infrastructure/rescue work:**
  - Mafesto's Kinetic Transfer System (`ARS-010`, corrected via `MCD-291` to grounding/conductance)
    is repeatedly redeployed as terrain/hazard-*sensing*, not just combat absorption: pressure/
    current sensing in flooding (`MCD-440`), density/weight-sensing for counterfeit detection
    (`MCD-1068`, `MCD-1191`), vibration-signature tracking of a mechanized Directorate boring
    engine (`MCD-1400`), thermal-gradient reading for a geothermal hazard (`MCD-1181`), seismic
    prediction (`MCD-1175`), and a slow, sustained-timescale reading register for frost-heave
    (`MCD-1451`).
  - Obsidian Malice (`ARS-030`) is repeatedly redeployed as a construction/demolition tool rather
    than purely a combat discharge weapon: quarrying bedrock (`MCD-655`), a precision vent-strike
    redirecting fire away from workers (`MCD-1035`), a precision drive-shaft strike disabling
    machinery intact instead of shattering it (`MCD-1400`) — and is deliberately *withheld* when
    spark or alloy-corrosion risk makes it dangerous (mineral-vapor hazard, `MCD-1068`; acid
    hazard, `MCD-1178`).
  - Onyx of Oblivion (`ARS-020`, powers: Cadence Ruin, Whisper of Shadows, Veil Piercer, Soulbound
    Edge, the Black Ledger) gets infrastructure-register showcases distinct from its combat use
    elsewhere: silent reconnaissance with zero engagement (`MCD-658`), a precision-cutting
    application of Soulbound Edge in place of a forceful strike to avoid spark (`MCD-1068`), and
    fighting entirely through touch/vibration/sound in total darkness after a collapse kills every
    light source (`MCD-652`).
  - The alias's signature combined-Trinity doctrine — all three systems handing an engagement to
    each other in sequence rather than any one acting alone — is stated verbatim in a Trust
    after-action report: **"he does not fight like a man carrying three weapons. He fights like
    one weapon that happens to be wearing a man"** (`MCD-375`; reused verbatim again at `MCD-1863`
    for the still-undrafted Kruger Sennit raid).
- **Field repair of Trinity gear through genuine smithing technique, explicitly *not* any
  Trinity-native self-regenerative property** (`MCD-408`) — Obsidian Malice's housing, cracked
  under an over-tolerance strike, is repaired via controlled reheat and Dead Drakma filing work
  mimicking the original Mao forging sequence; the repair holds through eleven subsequent
  engagements.
- **Bare manual labor, no gear at all** — personally setting shoring timber (`MCD-376`), nine days
  of ordinary carpentry alongside civilian carpenters (`MCD-1188`), an entirely unforced peacetime
  well-build (`MCD-1199`).
- **Learned (not innate) tactile knowledge** — an elderly Killane digger teaches Kanja decades of
  non-equipment tunnel-reading (`MCD-442`); this becomes a portable skill that later saves Kanja
  himself when he's blinded mid-rescue (`MCD-1182`) and that he later teaches formally at a
  Sovereign Trust engineering college (`MCD-487`).
- No Moonvault gifts, Ozmund-specific gear (`ARS-375`-`ARS-381`), or later Long-Mask-era gear
  (Forge-Coat, Sovereign Eyes, etc., `MCD-291`/`292` notwithstanding as cross-referenced physics)
  belong to this alias — checked clean, no gear overlap.

### Existing Chronicle corpus

**This is a backfill case: 102 Chronicles are already locked** (Chronicles I through CII,
`MCD-374`-`376`, `407`-`409`, `440`-`442`, `485`-`487`, `537`-`539`, `651`-`680`, `891`-`893`,
`1005`-`1013`, `1035`-`1037`, `1068`-`1070`, `1175`-`1201`, `1400`-`1402`, `1451`-`1459`), across
34 waves through Batch 286. **Note:** `chronicle-tracks-status.md` currently lists this alias at
"93" Chronicles — that figure is stale, predating waves 32-34 (Batches 276-286, +9 entries); the
true current count is 102. Worth correcting in that table whenever this alias's row is next
touched.

Synthesis of what the corpus has already established (grounding for a future Psychological
Profile discussion, not a competing invention):

1. **Construction literacy over reputation or violence, repeatedly.** The alias solves problems by
   reading physical evidence competently, not by fighting or by trading on fear: a mortar-color
   shift (`MCD-407`), weighing timber bundles against grain and moisture (`MCD-654`), a factual
   error in a forged repair order (`MCD-651`), elevation/spacing inconsistencies in a forged
   blueprint (`MCD-669`), fracture-pattern forensics (`MCD-675`), mass-discrepancy detection
   (`MCD-1191`).
2. **Honest admission of mistakes wins more trust than flawless work.** `MCD-409` (an unprompted
   admitted rushed reheat), `MCD-539` (the guild honors his *honest failures* explicitly, not just
   his successes), `MCD-665` (the alias's first real technical mistake with actual injury, publicly
   owned and corrected), `MCD-893` (a no-fault forensic account delivered honestly to grieving
   families rather than manufacturing a villain), `MCD-1187` (a defensible past compromise's
   hidden cost is written into training material rather than quietly fixed).
3. **A self-governing institution that outgrows and eventually checks its own founder.** The
   workers' council arc (`MCD-1183` founding → `MCD-1197` formal grievance against Kanja himself,
   his first real institutional check → `MCD-1200` full independent operation) is the corpus's
   clearest single throughline.
4. **An identity-reversal register: ordered to undo his own work.** Demolishing a causeway he
   personally built (`MCD-891`), then a much higher-stakes version evacuating 311 civilians while
   flooding a gallery he built (`MCD-1184`).
5. **Refusal to fabricate, even for a cause he personally supports.** Won't fake a structural
   condemnation for a hated but sound monument (`MCD-538`); refuses a staged Trinity demonstration
   for morale and substitutes a real unscripted repair instead (`MCD-1186`); answers a Directorate
   propaganda lie with better-sourced truth rather than denial in his own voice (`MCD-1176`).
6. **Structural honesty extended into non-technical domains.** Land disputes settled by physical
   survey rather than ruling for either side (`MCD-1179`), labor economics (`MCD-1070`), cross-
   border technical diplomacy (`MCD-1458`), and his own stated limits ("I can't fix this,"
   `MCD-1452`; a structural failure beyond even his skill, `MCD-485`).
7. **Generational/legacy transmission as a defining through-line.** The three-generation orphan-
   mentorship chain (`MCD-653` → `MCD-1036` → `MCD-1195`), every founded institution deliberately
   carrying no trace of his name so it outlasts his reputation (`MCD-662`), Danne Sok as the
   alias's own oral historian decades later (`MCD-1201`), and the corpus's explicit closing framing
   (`MCD-1200`, "What Outlived the Alias") of institutions functioning fully without him.
8. **A genuine fallibility arc distinct from raw power.** Physical exhaustion forcing him to apply
   his own safety rule to himself (`MCD-1189` → `MCD-1456`), a flawless rescue effort that still
   arrives too late (`MCD-656`), a structural failure beyond even his skill (`MCD-485`), and
   hazards where the Trinity itself must be deliberately withheld rather than relied on (`MCD-1068`,
   `MCD-1178`).
9. **Escalating environmental/hazard variety rather than escalating opponents.** Seismic
   (`MCD-1175`), corrosive/acid (`MCD-1178`), geothermal (`MCD-1181`), asphyxiant vapor
   (`MCD-1068`), fire/arson (`MCD-1035`), frost-heave (`MCD-1451`), drought (`MCD-1454`), illness/
   ventilation (`MCD-1452`), a mechanized boring-engine combat showcase (`MCD-1400`) — the corpus
   is deliberately built to widen physical/engineering variety over time rather than raise
   opponent tiers.
10. **Recurring crew depth is used deliberately instead of new named characters.** Nearly every
    entry explicitly notes "no new named characters... reuses already-locked crew" — Corren Halst,
    Danne Sok, Maret Vos, Garren Hask, Efa Gol, Dol Maren, and Pell Ostra carry almost the entire
    102-entry run; new figures (skeptics, collaborators, victims) are deliberately left unnamed,
    one-scene, and collision-checked rather than expanding the named cast.
