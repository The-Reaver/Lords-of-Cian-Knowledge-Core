# Kanja Haku Rexmar — Profile & Game Plan

**Status:** walkthrough drafted
**Track:** Kanja version (new track, Onyx-narrated)
**Gate cleared:** NO — no Chronicle prose may be drafted or presented until this file reaches "game plan approved."

This file is the standing gate artifact for this character, per the Character Chronicle Launch
Protocol locked in `CLAUDE.md`. It is the single source of truth for who this character is before
any Chronicle is written or rewritten — not a summary produced after the fact.

**Note on this track specifically:** this is a *new*, additional Chronicle track — Kanja himself as
his own protagonist, narrated properly by Onyx of Oblivion using the same **progressive
narrator-handoff mechanic** (`VB-026`) the original 8 manuscript Chronicles already demonstrate:
normal, neutral third-person prose early in the Rebellion, Onyx appearing only as a short
end-of-chapter coda, growing chapter by chapter until Onyx has fully become the narrator by the
Rebellion's end (age 30, the Trinity's surrender, `MCD-246`). This is deliberately distinct from the
existing 11-alias **Alias Chronicle track** (990+ entries), which by explicit design (and confirmed
by grep across the whole corpus) uses flat, neutral third-person prose throughout, with zero Onyx
narrator presence at any era — `VB-026` itself says the progressive handoff does not apply to the
Phase 2 territory Chronicles, and in practice it has never been applied to the Alias Chronicles
either. That track is "the regular accounting" and stays exactly as it is; nothing here touches it.
This new "Kanja version" track is where Onyx's proper narrator voice actually gets built going
forward, starting from Chronicle I of this track.

## 1. Rules Walkthrough

### Biography / stats

- `CC-001` — Full name Kanja Haku Rexmar; "Haku" is a middle name taken from the ancestor who
  defeated T.D.K. (Anu Un Ra) roughly 5,000 years ago.
- `MCD-294` / `MCD-295` — Bloodline: the Rex bloodline (Jicome's volcanic highlands, positional
  "hold ground, fortify, convert terrain into a killing field" combat doctrine, a biologically
  inherited metallic-composition sensitivity from millennia of tactile contact with raw Drakma) and
  the Mar bloodline (Jicome's coastline/archipelago, kinetic "strike, withdraw, reposition" combat
  doctrine, storm/current-reading senses) — the Haku convergence expresses both simultaneously.
  `MCD-311` locks that this is a biological engineering discipline applied to violence (reading a
  target's structural failure point, not forms), not a trained martial art, which is why an
  eighteen-year-old Kanja with no formal military training could produce results the Sovereign Trust
  couldn't counter.
- `MCD-101` / `CC-004` — His mother is Val Saeryn Kareth, Ninth Wing frontline commander of the
  Kares Prime Kareth war-sisterhood; her sister Val Mirel Kareth is Ozmund's mother, making Kanja
  and Ozmund cousins through their mothers' generation (`WC-006`).
- `MCD-025` — His father, Maro Rexmar, is murdered alongside Ozmund's father Aethelgard Verehimu at
  the Fulfillment Ceremony — Book 1's opening event. This sits chronologically *after* everything in
  this walkthrough's Rebellion/Long-Mask material; see Reserved threads below.
- `MCD-1568` (amended) / `MCD-1570` — Some years before the Rebellion formally begins, during
  Kanja's otherwise-unrecorded formative years, he enters a deliberate, mutual mentorship with Daba:
  Kanja doesn't arrive a blank slate in tactical warfare — the Rexmar-Haku convergence already
  carries an inherited, instinctive mastery of tactical warfare, the biological legacy of Haku's own
  campaigns against Anu Un Ra (`WC-005`, `MCD-305`) — and Daba's guerrilla teaching sharpens and
  refines that baseline rather than originating it. In exchange, Kanja teaches Daba his own Rexmar
  forging tradition, which becomes the concrete mechanical source of 1804's disproportionate
  lethality (`MCD-1570`) — distributed, cell-by-cell craftsmanship rather than a central armory.
- `MCD-231` — His first actions at age 18: the Scrip-Forge Raid (exposing Drakma-content
  debasement through evidence, not violence — his signature method from his very first engagement)
  and the Dredge-Line Ambush (flooding a canal district to drown a 200-soldier column's density
  advantage in industrial sludge — earning the Trench Monarch alias).
- `MCD-230` — The Twenty-Two Victories (ages 18–30, 12 years): 10 Conventional Victories, 6
  Unwinnable Victories (each earning a Directorate-classified alias — see Already-locked plot beats),
  and 6 Operational/labor-logistics Campaigns.
- `MCD-232` — The Battle of the Black Trench, age 19: the Trinity's first combat deployment as a
  complete system (Mafesto had been bonded roughly two years earlier and worn dormant; Onyx and
  Obsidian Malice go live alongside it here for the first time). Nine hours in chemical fog, 93 of
  120 fighters survive against 1,400+ Brigade losses. Earns the "Bane" classification.
- Density mechanics during the Rebellion/Long Mask: `CC-002`/`WC-004` lock the Density Spike as
  post-Awakening only, exclusive to the Rexmar and Verehimu bloodlines — meaning it is **not active**
  at any point covered by this walkthrough. `ARS-310` names the entire alias-era gear/tactics system
  the "Pre-Awakening Theatrics System" for exactly this reason: everything Kanja does from age 18
  through age 314 is Trinity gear, inherited tactical instinct, and craft/reputation, not raw
  density-scaled physical power.
- `MCD-246` — At age 30, Kanja surrenders the entire Trinity (Mafesto, Onyx, Obsidian Malice) to
  the vault at L9 in the Karkosa Complex under his father's peace deal with the Sovereign Trust,
  retaining only the Talisman of Mao, the Aegis-Talisman, and the Rexmar Machete. The 284-year Long
  Mask that follows comprises 67 Tier-1 battles, 10 Tier-2 campaigns, and roughly 50 Tier-3 ledger
  entries (120+ operations total), tracked by Onyx as a running seconds-count from the Sovereign
  Pier treaty. The Talisman of Mao's Blueprint Eye lets him map financial/legal structures the way
  he once mapped physical ones, making "the man" rather than the Trinity the crew's core weapon for
  this whole era.
- `CC-005` / `CC-006` — The Long Mask persona runs 284 years, ending when the Gravity-Fetter pendant
  is severed at the Gilded Lighthouse, triggering the Pi-Awakening between his 314th and 315th
  birthdays ("Day 0" = age 314).
- `WC-005` (amended) — The Pi-Awakening is a bloodline-wide biological threshold at exactly 314
  years, not unique to Kanja individually — a shift from passive to active biology, gaining remote
  Drakma communication and environmental tuning. Haku himself experienced it roughly 5,000 years ago,
  forcing Anu Un Ra's retreat (`MCD-305`); Kanja's own Awakening at 314 is the next occurrence T.D.K.'s
  containment was specifically built to prevent.
- `MCD-144` / `MCD-227` / `MCD-293` — Density figures across the series, post-Awakening: Book 1
  baseline 6,000x resting / ~18,000x combat ceiling; the fixed five-book escalation schedule running
  2,000–4,000x (Book 1) through 20,000–42,000x+ (Book 5, S-tier collision, "Limitless at impact");
  and, distinct from the Spike ceiling itself, his *resting* density also escalates across the
  series (6,000x → 12,000x by Book 5, `MCD-293`) — unlike Ozmund's fixed 15,000x resting baseline
  across all five books.

### Abilities / gear

- **The Trinity**: Mafesto (Kinetic Transfer System — redirects/absorbs momentum through grounded
  contact), Onyx of Oblivion (`ARS-020`, sentient Living Drakma longsword bonded age 17, five named
  powers: Cadence Ruin, Whisper of Shadows, Veil Piercer, Soulbound Edge, the Black Ledger), and
  Obsidian Malice (`ARS-030`, war club forged at the Mao Volcano, 3–5 second active recharge or a
  much slower two-year passive dormant-charge accumulation as an alternate discharge mode, plus a
  defensive "Last Ward" mode, `ARS-390`). First deployed together at the Black Trench (age 19);
  surrendered as a complete system to the L9 vault at age 30 (`MCD-246`) for the entire 284-year Long
  Mask that follows.
- **Long-Mask-era kit**: the Talisman of Mao (Blueprint Eye — maps financial/legal structures),
  the Aegis-Talisman, and the Rexmar Machete (`MCD-246`) — the three pieces retained after the
  Trinity's surrender, the actual gear this new track's material (if set during the Long Mask) would
  draw on rather than the Trinity.
- **The seven-piece post-Mafesto gear system** (`ARS-344` onward): the Valen Protocol begins within
  the Rebellion itself, age 26 (Battle of the Forge Quarter) — Valen Sinisterblade orders Kanja to
  document what Mafesto's armor does that his own body can't, as a hedge against its eventual loss.
  The actual replacement gear (Forge-Coat, Sovereign Eyes, Breath Collar, Ironhand Gauntlets, Ironfall
  Boots, Smoke System, Mend-Line) isn't built until after the Trinity's surrender at 30, and powers
  the entire Long Mask (`ARS-310`'s "Pre-Awakening Theatrics System").
- Density mechanics generally (see Biography/stats): none of this era's combat is density-powered;
  everything is craft, Rexmar-Mar tactical instinct, and Trinity/replacement gear.
