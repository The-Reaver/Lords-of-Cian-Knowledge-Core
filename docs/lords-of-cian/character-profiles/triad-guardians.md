## 1. Rules Walkthrough

Every locked rule touching the Triad, pulled from `canon-ledger.json` and organized thematically

### Biography / stats

**Varkul (TRIAD-1)**
- `MCD-040` — Species: Tide-Back Courser, the land/water branch of the Dhar-Kael clade.
- `CC-049` — Designated TRIAD-1; epithet "Drown-Warden"; homages the mythic stallion Svadilfari;
  morphs between a ~4,025 lb Land Form and an 88%-larger Hydro-Titan Form on submersion.
- `CC-094` — Land Form ~4,025 lb, 12.6 ft; Hydro-Titan Form ~7,567+ lb minimum, 23.7 ft.
- `MCD-020` — The morphing is a natural adaptive response to habitat, not a transformation power;
  both forms canon.
- `ARS-412` — Bioluminescent flank markings pulse with threat proximity; hooves reinforced with
  biological Drakma-carbonate; three-layer bio-armor (keratinous plate, shock-absorbing cartilage,
  Drakma-mineralized bone) that only Drakma-forged weapons or Titan-class impacts fully penetrate.
- `SBD-044` — Density-unrated, pure biology (not "as dense as Vargo Vakas," as one SBD directive
  claims); the world regards him as the strongest non-human presence in the setting; his true
  ceiling is deliberately never shown maxed.
- `SBD-020` — The SBD files him as "Miremaw Varkul," classified a "living preservation clause."
- `SBD-044` — SBD threat rating OMEGA-PRIME.
- `MCD-140` — Counted (as "Drown-Warden Varkul") among the nineteen Avatars under the Talisman of
  Mao's Sovereign Umbrella.

**Sorya (TRIAD-3)**
- `MCD-040` — Species: Apex-Feline, the shadow/memory branch of the clade.
- `CC-050` — Designated TRIAD-3; homages Muninn (Memory).
- `CC-096` — ~15% larger than Panthera atrox: ~1,150-1,300+ lb, 13.2 ft; melanistic coat with
  rosettes visible only in direct light; emerald eyes. Female (pronouns "her" throughout `CC-097`/
  `CC-136`).
- `SBD-022` — SBD threat rating OMEGA-PRIME (conditional); framed by the SBD as a "living archive."
- `ARS-403` / `ARS-414` — Sephtis's private name for her is "the True Log," his highest
  compliment, a deliberate echo of his own codex.
- `MCD-140` — Counted among the nineteen Avatars.

**Varruk (TRIAD-2)**
- `MCD-040` — Species: Oath-Raptor, the sky branch of the clade.
- `CC-051` — Designated TRIAD-2; homages Huginn (Thought).
- `CC-098` — ~15% larger than Argentavis magnificens: ~195 lb, 27.6 ft wingspan, hollow-bone build;
  the lightest of the Triad by design rather than weakness. Male ("he"/"his" throughout).
- `ARS-413` — Plumage pale cream to buff-white, deliberately stained rust-orange by iron-rich dust
  bathing as scent-masking.
- `MCD-277` — First appearance dated to the Dog Watch (Kanja age 248), 42 years before the Triad's
  bonding with Pyro (~290). The only Guardian with a dated first appearance.
- `SBD-021` — SBD threat rating OMEGA-PRIME.
- `MCD-140` — Counted among the nineteen Avatars.

**The Triad as a unit**
- `MCD-040` — The Dhar-Kael ("the Bound Ones"): three companion species bonded symbiotically with
  Karesian biology; imprint happens during a juvenile window; the bond is permanent, biological,
  non-transferable; three survivors remain, and the clade is extinct when they die.
- `ARS-200` — Varkul, Varruk, Sorya are the last three Dhar-Kael (consistent with `MCD-040`).
- `MCD-041` — The Vael Kem (the Hollow Tide) consumed Dhar-Kael as food and drove them to
  near-extinction; the Rexmar bloodline's military tradition was forged in that war, "protecting what
  cannot protect itself."
- `WC-017` — Origin as stated by the World Codex: Pyro's mother's final act inverted the Living Gate
  into a forge, "producing the Triad Guardians" before Pyro was born in the severed instant.
- `MCD-131` — The Triad are what Pyro's mother "pushed outward" through the Gate's inversion before
  her fusion into its architecture completed; permanently bonded to Pyro.
- `CC-046` — The Codex's version: Pyro and the three Dhar-Kael were produced as her final act, with
  the Dhar-Kael bond itself completed beforehand (citing `MCD-136`).
- `MCD-022` — The Dhar-Kael bond was completed by Pyro's mother, the last keeper, *before* T.D.K.'s
  Living Gate curse.
- `MCD-136` — She was both the last keeper of the Dhar-Kael tradition and an SBD-recruited
  veil-reader; she completed the bond before the curse took hold.
- `MCD-132` — Pyro's birth and his mother's transformation are two effects of one single event,
  "born in the severed instant."
- `MCD-270` — The Birth of Fire (Kanja age 290): Pyro's natural birth and the imprinting bond
  completing between him and the *juvenile* Varkul, Varruk, and Sorya; Kanja kept 12 km away so the
  Talisman's output wouldn't disrupt the bonding frequencies.
- `CULT-199` — The SBD's own protocol holds that the Guardians "operate on principles incompatible
  with SBD technology."
- No rule locks an age, lifespan, or maturation rate for any of the three. Their age at Book 1 is
  derivable only from Pyro's (bonded at his birth): `CC-101` gives Pyro as "roughly 24-36 at Book 1."

  Dhar-Kael.

### Abilities / gear

**Varkul**
- `CC-094` — Land Form: fights by blunt-force mass displacement rather than mauling; scent-first
  target acquisition; generates the **Harrow Presence**, an instinctive discipline-collapse effect on
  nearby groups that the SBD misclassifies as frequency-craft but is pure biology. Hydro-Titan Form:
  **Hydro-Inertia** (near-unstoppable momentum), **the Breach** (full-body aerial strikes from
  water), **Snap-Turn**, **Wake Distortion**, the **Living Depth Charge** (mass-displacement swamping).
- `MCD-020` — The **Harrow Ring** is hydrodynamic physics.
- `ARS-412` — **Density Compression Charge**: temporarily raises his own mass mid-charge (effective
  ~2-8 tons), colloquial terminology, not a formal density rating; distinct from Hydro-Inertia.
- `ARS-412` — **The Guardian Clause**: a real in-universe restriction confining the Harrow Ring
  strictly to Pyro-vowed defense; gives concrete content to the SBD's "Harrow Ring Clause
  Conditions."
- `CC-095` — **Indomitable Will** (refusal to acknowledge pain or fatigue). Vulnerabilities: Blight
  Frequencies (disrupt Hydro-Titan expansion and Harrow Ring formation), Abyssal Bile-Salts (suppress
  the bond's pain/fatigue override), extended separation from Pyro.

**Sorya**
- `CC-096` — Six capabilities: **Witness-Scouting** (eidetic recall across every sensory channel),
  **Vow-Taste** (physically senses a broken vow before the betrayer acts), **Shard-Recall** (projects
  sensory memory-fragments into a target's mind as evidence, not telepathy), **Mimic Speech**
  (reproduces overheard phrases in the original voice), **Long-Haul Endurance**,
  **Stealth-Dominance** (silent despite 1,000+ lb).
- `CC-050` — Vow-Taste is her primary betrayal-detection ability.
- `CC-136` — A real combat/ambush layer beneath that profile: **Black-Rosette Vanish**, **Throatline
  Shear**, **Green-Eye Fixation Trap**, **Bone-Engine Pin**, **Silent-Break Commit**.
- `ARS-414` — **The Witness Shriek**: an emergency-only multi-frequency vocalization causing ~3
  seconds of total sensory confusion.
- `CC-097` — Vulnerabilities: Blight Frequencies (degrade Witness-Scouting/Vow-Taste), Abyssal
  Bile-Salts (stall Shard-Recall's delivery, not the memory); **the Oath Paradox** (two valid,
  conflicting oaths) is the only thing that breaks her operational calm.
- `SBD-022` — The SBD's own file names only three of her capabilities: Witness-Scouting,
  Shard-Recall, Vow-Taste.

**Varruk**
- `CC-098` — Five capabilities: **Pattern-Scouting** (full strategic picture from fragments),
  **Angle-Whisper** (projects compressed geometric certainty, not words or images, into a bonded
  mind), **Cadence Break** (renamed from "Cadence Ruin" to clear Onyx's blade power; low-altitude
  passes and targeted-frequency screams that disrupt enemy coordination), **Storm-Lane Travel**
  (hurricane weather as express routes), **Guidance by Refusal** (signals danger only by refusing to
  land on or fly a path, never by warning).
- `CC-051` — Aerial reconnaissance and pattern reconstruction via Angle-Whisper.
- `ARS-413` — Cadence Break's vocalization operates at ~200 m radius against communication and
  targeting at once; **the Stare**, a separate named behavior (being observed "deeper than visual
  contact").
- `CC-099` — Vulnerabilities: Blight Frequencies (degrade Pattern-Scouting/Angle-Whisper);
  functionally grounded in enclosed spaces; **Cadence Saturation** (self-limiting: overuse of his own
  frequency projection disorients him through reverberation in his hollow bones).
- `SBD-021` — The SBD's own file lists Pattern-Scouting, Angle-Whisper, Guidance by Refusal,
  Cadence Break.
- `SBD-045` — The SBD's contested five-tactic "Offensive Capability Suite" (Wake-Scissor,
  Pressure-Seam Drop, Lantern-Denial Pass, Oarline Misfire Window, Refusal-Trap Coercion), rated
  CONFIRMED on a partially corrupted informant stream; reliability contested; `CC-098`/`099` remain
  his real profile.

### Connective-tissue findings

Listed for decision, not resolved. Each quotes both sides with rule IDs.

1. **Were the Triad *created* by the Gate's inversion, or are they a pre-existing species that was
   only bonded then?**
   - `WC-017`: the mother's final act "inverted the Gate into a forge, producing the Triad Guardians
     (Varkul, Varruk, Sorya) before Pyro was born in the severed instant."
   - `MCD-131`: "The Triad Guardians (Varkul, Varruk, Sorya) are what she pushed outward through that
     inversion before the fusion completed."
   - `CC-046`: "Pyro and the three Dhar-Kael guardians ... were produced as her final act (the
     Dhar-Kael bond itself having been completed beforehand, MCD-136)."
   - Against: `MCD-040`: "a clade of three companion species ... Imprint happens during a juvenile
     window ... Three survivors remain"; `MCD-041`: the Vael Kem "consumed Dhar-Kael as a food source
     and drove them to near-extinction"; `MCD-277`: "places Varruk's first appearance 42 years before
     the Triad's bonding with Pyro (~290)." A species hunted to near-extinction across history, with
     one member already seen 42 years earlier, cannot also have been produced by a forge at the birth.

2. **When did the bond complete?**
   - `MCD-022`: "The Dhar-Kael bond was completed by his mother (the last keeper) BEFORE T.D.K.'s
     Living Gate curse." `MCD-136`: "she completed the Dhar-Kael bond before the curse took hold."
   - `MCD-270`: at the Birth of Fire, "the Dhar-Kael imprinting bond completing between him and the
     juvenile Varkul, Varruk, and Sorya." Bonding an unborn child before the curse vs. imprinting
     completing at the birth is never reconciled; `CC-046`'s parenthetical restates the "beforehand"
     side without addressing `MCD-270`. The curse's own installation date (how long before the birth)
     is not locked.

3. **"Juvenile" at age 290 vs. Varruk seen at age 248.** `MCD-270` calls all three "juvenile" at the
   bonding; `MCD-277` dates Varruk's first appearance 42 years earlier. No Dhar-Kael lifespan or
   maturation rate is locked anywhere, so whether a 42+-year-old Oath-Raptor can still be "juvenile"
   is open. Varkul's and Sorya's first appearances are undated; whether they were with the fleet
   before 290 is unstated.

4. **What the Living Gate physically is, and therefore where the Triad emerged.**
   - `MCD-269`: "an ancient Karesian cavern-system heritage site already known as 'the Living Gate'
     (the same Living Gate later activated as T.D.K.'s containment mechanism at Pyro's birth, age
     ~290)."
   - `CC-046`: T.D.K. "installed a voice-keyed 'Living Gate' curse into Kanja's first and only wife";
     `MCD-131`: "T.D.K.'s Living Gate (voice-keyed breach-lattice) was deployed as a containment
     mechanism." A place, a curse inside a person, and a deployed lattice; whether the birth happened
     at the cavern site is not locked. `MCD-270`'s "Kanja ... 12km away" implies a fixed location.

5. **What exactly is false in Dexton's Triad-origin claim.** `SBD-041` locks as false "the Triad
   raised from birth by Pyro's mother in her own Shattered Kingdoms homeland." Yet `MCD-022`/`MCD-136`
   lock her as the Dhar-Kael's last keeper who completed their bond, and `MCD-277` has her aboard
   Kanja's fleet from age 96. Which element is false (that she raised them, that it was from birth,
   that it was in a homeland, that the homeland was in the Shattered Kingdoms) is not specified. Her
   homeland is not locked anywhere.

6. **Varkul's two names.** "Miremaw Varkul" (`SBD-020`, `SBD-044`) vs. "Drown-Warden Varkul"
   (`CC-049`, `CC-094`, `MCD-140`). No rule says whether "Miremaw" is his true name, a second epithet,
   or an SBD designation (relevant given `CULT-199`'s Continuity Lock, which strips proper names from
   SBD files). Sorya and Varruk carry no epithet.

7. **Ability names after the renames.** Clean in every rule statement: "Cadence Break" (`CC-098`,
   `SBD-021`, `ARS-413`, `ARS-395`) and "Cadence Saturation" (`CC-099`). Zero "Riptide Break"/
   "Riptide Saturation" in any rule statement or entry; the name survives only in the ledger's
   batch notes (batches 341 and 344), `CLAUDE.md` (line ~3882), and `merge_batch343_cc_wgd_char_fixes.py`
   (lines 260-308). "Cadence Ruin" for Varruk survives only inside "renamed from" notes; every other
   "Cadence Ruin" in the ledger and in 85 Chronicle files is Onyx's own power. Two small residues:
   - `CC-099` says Cadence Saturation was "renamed from 'Cadence Ruin'-adjacent naming," but the
     Batch 343 script shows its original name was already "Cadence Saturation" (it went to "Riptide
     Saturation" and back). Its name never actually changed.
   - Batch labels disagree: `CLAUDE.md` calls the two rename batches 340 and 342 and the
     reconciliation 343; the ledger's `batches_completed` records them as 341, 343, and 344, and the
     script files are `merge_batch343_cc_wgd_char_fixes.py` and `merge_batch345_cult_ash_sbd_fixes.py`.

8. **Which SBD descriptions carry an overclaim flag, and which don't.**
   - Flagged: `SBD-045` (Varruk's suite, "the reliability of this suite as described is contested");
     `CC-136` (Sorya's combat layer, "the SBD's own dossier on it, however, overclaims certainty");
     `SBD-044` (only the "as dense as Vargo Vakas" claim is rejected).
   - Not flagged: the OMEGA-PRIME ratings themselves (`SBD-020`'s classification, `SBD-021`,
     `SBD-022`, `SBD-044`) and the capability lists in `SBD-021`/`SBD-022`. `SBD-022` names only three
     of Sorya's six `CC-096` capabilities, with no rule saying whether the gap is SBD ignorance or

9. **Three "clauses" and the undefined Harrow Ring.** `SBD-020`: a "living preservation clause";
   `ARS-412`: "The Guardian Clause ... confining the Harrow Ring strictly to Pyro-vowed defense";
   `CULT-200`/`MCD-1727`: "Harrow Ring Clause Conditions." No rule says what the Harrow Ring does:
   `MCD-020` only says it "is hydrodynamic physics," and `CC-095` mentions "Harrow Ring formation."
   `CC-094` separately introduces the **Harrow Presence** ("an instinctive discipline-collapse
   effect ... pure biology") in Land Form. Whether the Ring and the Presence are one ability (in two
   forms) or two is unstated, and whether the SBD's "living preservation clause" relates to the
   Guardian Clause is unstated.

10. **SBD policy vs. the future-opponents hook.** `SBD-020`: "SBD is barred from capturing, bonding,

    the Harrow Ring cannot be turned on the SBD unless it is Pyro-vowed defense.

## 2. Psychological Profile

Built collaboratively with Abad, not handed to him finished. Draft proposals go here marked
**PROPOSED**, and are corrected in place (not appended-and-superseded) once discussed —
this file always reflects current understanding, not a batch-log history of how we got there.

Drafted 2026-10-04 against `ledger_version` 37.8. Every rule ID below was checked against the

**What "psychology" means here.** The Triad are animals. Varkul is pure biology and
density-unrated (`CC-094`, `SBD-044`); none of the three speaks. Sorya's Mimic Speech repeats
overheard phrases in the original voice (`CC-096`), and Varruk's Angle-Whisper projects geometry
with no words or images in it (`CC-098`). This profile therefore describes drives and behavior:
what each Guardian protects, ranks, reacts to, and avoids, as its locked rules show. It gives them
no inner monologue, no human reasoning, and no motive their rules do not already carry.

### One shared profile or three

**Recommended: one shared profile in three registers.** Canon builds the Triad as one unit:
- one clade with three branches (`MCD-040`), and "the last three" Dhar-Kael (`ARS-200`);
- bonded together, to one child, in one instant (`MCD-270`, `MCD-131`, `CC-048`);
- regarded by the world as "a single combined posture package" (`SBD-044`);
- a coordinated extraction in which Varkul and Varruk act inside Sorya's Witness Shriek
  (`ARS-414`);
- three bond hierarchies built to the same shape, each with Pyro first (`CC-095`, `CC-097`,
  `CC-099`).

No Guardian has any locked history of its own apart from the other two, except Varruk's Dog Watch
sighting (`MCD-277`). Three separate profiles would invent three separate inner lives that canon
does not give them. Each facet below states what the three share, then the register each Guardian
carries it in: Varkul holds, Sorya remembers, Varruk finds the way.

The alternative is three individual profiles, one per Guardian. It suits a later wave, once
entries have given each of them demonstrated behavior of their own.

### (A) Questions to rule before this profile can be confirmed

Three of the Section 1 findings decide facts the profile stands on. Each facet further down is
written under the recommended answer and says where it depends on one.

**TA1. Were the Triad made at the Gate, or are they an ancient clade bonded there? (finding 1)**
- **Option 1 (recommended): an ancient clade, bonded and released at the Gate.** The three are
  the last survivors of a clade the Vael Kem hunted to near-extinction (`MCD-040`, `MCD-041`,
  `ARS-200`). Pyro's mother was the last keeper of their tradition (`MCD-040`, `MCD-136`); this
  option asserts nothing about where, or by whom, the three were kept before 290 (TB1). After
  Pyro's natural birth, within the same event (`MCD-022`, `MCD-132`), she inverted the Gate into a
  forge (`MCD-131`). What the forge made was the bond: it sealed the imprint between the three
  juveniles and the newborn (`MCD-270`), and she pushed them outward through the inversion before
  her fusion completed (`MCD-131`, already worded this way). Varruk's sighting 42 years earlier
  (`MCD-277`) stands. Scope: `WC-017`'s "producing the Triad Guardians" and `CC-046`'s "produced"
  become "sending out the Triad Guardians, bonded to him." `CC-046` is a Codex rule, already
  flagged as in-world misdirection on the mother's death (`MCD-133`).
- **Option 2: quickened at the Gate.** The keeper carried the clade's last line, and the forge
  brought three new juveniles to life from it. This keeps "produced" literally. Cost: it breaks
  `MCD-277` (Varruk seen at Kanja 248, 42 years before), `MCD-022` and `MCD-136` (a bond completed
  beforehand, with animals not yet living), and arguably `MCD-040`'s "three survivors remain."
- **Option 3: a mixed origin.** Varruk is ancient, and Varkul and Sorya were quickened at the Gate.
  It keeps `MCD-277` and splits a unit canon treats as one.

**TA2. How the bond was completed, and how a Guardian seen at Kanja 248 is still "juvenile" at 290
(findings 2, 3).**
- **Option 1 (recommended): the keyed bond and the open window.** As last keeper, the mother keyed
  the three to the child she was carrying before the curse took hold (`MCD-022`, `MCD-136`). The
  imprint itself completed within the birth event, after the natural birth, as she pushed them out
  through the inverted Gate, and the forge sealed it permanently (`MCD-270`, `MCD-132`,
  `MCD-131`). The juvenile window of `MCD-040` is the unbonded state: a Dhar-Kael stays juvenile
  until it imprints, however long that takes, and matures only after. All three could therefore be
  "juvenile" at 290 whatever their age, Varruk included. New fact: imprint ends the juvenile window.
  Consequence: the Triad grew up alongside Pyro, small and young at the Pyro Incident (Kanja 296,
  `MCD-277`) and grown by the Last Breakfast. Scope: clarifying clauses on `MCD-040`, `MCD-022`,
  `MCD-136`, and `CC-046`.
- **Option 2: a long-lived clade with a long juvenile stage.** Juvenility lasts a century or more,
  with no link to imprinting. No new mechanism is needed, and how the three came to maturity by
  Book 1 stays unexplained.
- **Option 3: a different Oath-Raptor at the Dog Watch.** Amend `MCD-277` so the bird seen at 248
  is not Varruk. That bird would be a fourth Dhar-Kael, against "three survivors remain"
  (`MCD-040`) and `ARS-200`.

**TA3. What Sorya's Oath is, and what the Triad know of Pyro's history (findings 11, 12).**
- **Option 1 (recommended): the Oath is the keeper's vow.** New fact: the Oath is the vow Pyro's
  mother made when she keyed the bond (`MCD-022`, `MCD-136`), her intent for the child: to keep
  him. `CC-097` puts Sorya's deepest loyalty in "the Oath itself, not to Pyro," and this reading
  keeps that distinction, because the keeper's intent for the child can part from what the child
  himself wants. "Pyro first, the Oath second" then means the Oath overrides Pyro only when keeping
  him safe cuts against what he wants (`CC-097`). Possible scope: a clarifying clause on `CC-097`
  defining the Oath as the keeper's vow. This is also Sorya's bond expression, the one missing from
  the three (finding 12): Varkul holds through will (`CC-095`), Varruk through the path (`CC-099`),
  Sorya through the vow. With eidetic recall across every sense (`CC-096`), she carries the keeper
  and the night of the Gate up to the moment she was pushed out, which is why she is the one who
  can show Pyro his history from outside his own view (`ARS-414`). New fact: she knows Kanja is the
  father. New fact: Kanja swore Pyro no vow, so his silence breaks nothing she can taste (`CC-050`,
  `CC-097`); it would become her Oath Paradox only if he ever swore one that collided with the
  keeper's. `CC-079`'s list of knowers is open-ended ("multiple guardians"), so no amendment is
  needed. She was pushed out before the fusion completed (`MCD-131`), so what her memory holds of
  the fusion itself stays reserved.
- **Option 2: the Oath is any sworn vow made in her presence.** A general enforcer's instinct, with
  no tie to the keeper or to Pyro's history. Cost: the Oath is cut loose from the keeper, and
  Sorya's Pyro-bond expression stays undefined.
- **Option 3: a vow sworn at the Gate by Kanja.** Kanja was held 12 km away (`MCD-270`), so the vow
