# Character Chronicle Gameplan

Written 2026-09-15. This is the planning document for the next open-ended
Chronicle track: giving every real, canon-detailed character in the Lords of
Cian universe who doesn't have one yet their own dedicated Chronicle series,
the same way the 20 Phase 2 homage-era territories and Arturo Salvatierra
Duho already have. This is a planning/sequencing document only — no new
canon rules, no Chronicle content. Per the project's own non-negotiable
rule, nothing gets drafted without its own draft-then-explicit-approval pass
when Abad is ready for each character.

## Where this sits relative to everything else already built

Two Chronicle tracks already exist and continue independently, at whatever
pace Abad points at next:

- **Kanja's eleven Alias Chronicles** (Bane, the Trench Monarch, the
  Industrial Myth, the Blue-Collar Titan, the Sovereign Ghost of the Great
  Sea, the Scourge, the Crow King, the Iron Bastard, the Lord of Embers, the
  Storm That Walks, Captain) — 31 waves each as of Batch 275, 93 Chronicles
  per alias, 1,023 total, still short of the long-term 50-100-per-alias
  target.
- **The 20 Phase 2 homage-era territories'** (Xaragua, Areíto, Yara, Guanín,
  Borikén, Sankofa, Aztlán, Atunbi, Ijoko, Orin, Ide, Kwan, Umoja, Jibaro,
  Uhuru, Kazi, Taifa, Hekalu, Nyansa, Kiti) own Chronicle series, 2-13
  entries each depending on territory, plus Arturo Salvatierra Duho's own
  6-Chronicle arc as the NYC-equivalent city's (Batey's) standing figure.

This document opens a **third track**: mainline-canon characters (crew,
co-leads, villains, Avatars, and named minor figures) who are deeply
detailed in the Character Codex and other source material but have never
had a Chronicle of their own — only appearances as supporting cast inside
Kanja's own story.

## Structure decision (Abad, 2026-09-15)

**Each Tier 1+ character gets their own protagonist Chronicle series**,
mirroring the territory-leader model exactly: their own numbered Chronicle
series (e.g. "Ozmund Chronicle I," "Ezio Chronicle I"), themselves as the
POV/protagonist, with Kanja appearing only as an unnamed or background
presence where it genuinely fits — not the "deep-dive entry inside Kanja's
own Alias Chronicles" alternative that was also on the table.

Open sub-question, not yet resolved: whether each series uses that
character's own already-established narrator (Fermand for Ezio/Lauris,
Onyx for Kanja himself — Ozmund and the others don't have a confirmed
narrator assignment yet) or runs close-third like the territory Chronicles
do. Worth settling before the first draft, not blocking the gameplan itself.

## Starting point (Abad, 2026-09-15)

**Tier 1 first** — the 8 major co-leads: Ozmund, Ezio, Lauris, Fermand,
Valen, Sephtis, Anansi, Orlok. Tier 2 (Avatars/Titans), Tier 3
(antagonists), Tier 4 (Five Champions), Tier 5 (crew), and Tier 6 (minor)
all stay queued, not sequenced yet, per the same "no track starts
automatically" pacing discipline already governing the Alias Chronicle and
territory-Chronicle tracks.

Not yet decided: which of the 8 Tier 1 characters gets the first series,
or whether they launch together the way earlier Phase 2 city builds did.
Next concrete step is Abad pointing at one (or several) of the eight.

## Lauris Letitia: four-strand pacing convention (Abad, 2026-09-17)

Resolved the "not yet decided" pacing question above, at least for Lauris -- and set a candidate
pattern other Tier 1 characters may or may not follow once their own series start. Rather than one
flat numbered sequence, her series braids four parallel strands, one entry per strand per wave, all
still numbered in a single continuous sequence (Chronicle I, II, III...) rather than separately
sub-numbered per strand:

- **Strand K -- Kares Prime (the deep past).** Flashback entries into her homeworld life,
  pre-departure -- the vulnerable, unfinished version of her before ~4,000 years of grief calcified
  into "witness." Mirrors the Arturo-prequel move.
- **Strand D -- the Directorate years.** Full-scene treatment of specific operations from her
  40-contract Sealbound career, continuing what Chronicle I started -- her isolation and slow
  radicalization against the institution she worked for.
- **Strand L -- the Ledger (present-day debts).** Her standing operational debts as an ongoing arc:
  the Drowning Vault's 120 (reserved for Book 5 per MCD-216, not to be touched early), the withheld
  Operation 38 facility, the K-Theta cave-system subjects (reveal to Kanja reserved for a future
  book per MCD-193), the deferred Verith/Val Mirel question. Deepen, don't resolve, matching the
  project's established pattern for reserved threads.
- **Strand W -- Witness (present-day, Lords of Cian).** Quiet, stakes-free entries showing her
  ordinary present-day texture among the crew -- the emotional-counterweight register CC-134
  establishes, without a mystery attached.

**Two-voice narrator structure, unique to this series:** every entry opens with a short archive
fragment in Lauris's own spare, clipped voice (matching the quotes catalog style at MCD-1558)
before Fermand Aurelias's ornate Baroque-noir narration proper (CC-034/VB-024) picks up the scene.
No other Tier 1 character keeps a literal written archive a narrator is established as transcribing
from (MCD-211), so no other series can use this device the same way.

**Wave one, Batch 293, 2026-09-17 (`MCD-1562` through `MCD-1565`):** the first four-strand wave --
"The Vein Between Two Vasks" (K), "The Eighty Interviews" (D), "The Last of the Seven" (L), "Two
Archives, One Question" (W). Full details in CLAUDE.md's batch log. Further waves start whenever
Abad points at it, same pacing discipline as the other two tracks.

## Standing conventions carried over from the other two tracks

- Draft, present in full, wait for explicit approval, then lock — no
  exceptions for this track either.
- Collision-check every new proper noun against the full live ledger before
  drafting.
- No real-world proper nouns (learned the hard way from the NYC/LA/Chicago/
  Detroit leak, Batch 290) — anything new invented for these characters'
  own supporting casts follows the same real-vocabulary-reuse naming
  discipline already established.
- Sync new Chronicle files to the Google Drive mirror using the
  established per-character/per-territory folder pattern once a character's
  own folder exists.
